These functions work with every numeric type, using the same call for `f64`, `Dual`, or `Var`.
Pass matrices as flat row-major slices; callers supply outputs for functions ending in `_into`.
On a tape, `dot` and each inner product in `gemv_into` and `gemm_into` form one statement; `solve_into`, `checkpointed_loop`, and `Var::external` each form one call statement, while `cholesky_into`, `qr_into`, and `least_squares_into` record their algorithmic operations directly.
Item-name links point to the version 0.1.0 API reference on docs.rs.

## Linear systems and factorizations

| Item | Kind | Role | Key specifications | Discussion | Runnable example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Length mismatch gives `DimensionMismatch`, overflow of $n^2$ gives `SizeOverflow`, a pivot that is not positive in at least one lane gives `DomainError` (argument `matrix`, `OutsideDomain`), and allocation failure gives `AllocationFailure` | [Differentiating factorization algorithms directly](oxref:ad-differentiated-factorizations), [Series-spring compliance sensitivities](oxref:ad-series-springs-sensitivity) | [Stiffness sensitivities of series-spring compliance with solve_into and cholesky_into](oxref:ad-linear-solve-adjoint), [Compare tape word counts for solve_into and cholesky_into](oxref:ad-solve-statement-count) |

Allocates two `rows × cols` scratch arrays (working matrix and reflections). | [`least_squares_into`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-ad/0.1.0/scioxide_ad/fn.least_squares_into.html) | Function | Least-squares solution $X = \arg\min \lVert AX - B \rVert_2$ | Computes $X = R^{-1}Q^\mathsf{T}B$ by thin QR (assumes independent columns without rank detection). ## Summary

Writing matrix and inner products with `dot`, `gemv_into`, and `gemm_into` needs only one tape statement per inner product.
Use `solve_into` for linear-system solutions, applying factors to derivative components without differentiating elimination.
For equation-solution sensitivities, find the solution separately and pass it to `implicit_solution_into`.
When long-loop gradients exceed memory, use `checkpointed_loop` and `CheckpointSchedule`; supply derivatives with `map_scalar` or `ExternalRule` for parts not expressible with `Real`.
