Failures in `scioxide-core` return one opaque type, `SciOxideError`.
Read categories through the `SciOxideErrorKind` of `kind()` and details through the `SciOxideErrorView` of `view()`.
Do not depend on internal representations or `Display` strings.
Errors carry only what failed (category, `Argument` / `Quantity`, details), without caller identifiers.
[Error categories and detailed views](../../algorithms/errors-and-diagnostic-views/) explains category-specific details with examples.
[Error contracts and partial results](../../../concepts/errors-and-partial-results/) explains `Err` and unsuccessful `Ok`.

## Summary

On failure, classify through `kind()` and read arguments, quantities, and reasons through `view()`.
Branch on `SciOxideErrorKind` and reason enums, not `Display` strings.
For failed iterations, `ConvergenceDiagnostics` exposes iteration counts, the stopping limit, and nested records.
Name arguments and quantities in your own methods with compile-time validation through `argument!` and `quantity!`.
