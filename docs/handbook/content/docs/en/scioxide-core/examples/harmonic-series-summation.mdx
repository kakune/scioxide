The harmonic series $H_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n} 1/k$ diverges as $n \to \infty$.
Yet summing in $k = 1, 2, \ldots$ order with single precision (`f32`) eventually stops increasing altogether.
This example explains why through floating-point spacing and compares reordered sums, compensation with `ScaledAccumulator`, and exact accumulation with `RangeAccumulator`.

## Euler–Maclaurin reference values

Using Euler's constant $\gamma \approx 0.5772156649015329$, $H_n$ expands as

$$
H_n = \ln n + \gamma + \frac{1}{2n} - \frac{1}{12 n^2} + \frac{1}{120 n^4} - \frac{1}{252 n^6} + \cdots
$$


Truncating through $1/(120 n^4)$ leaves error of the order of the next term $1/(252 n^6)$, at most $4 \times 10^{-9}$ for $n \ge 10$.
Computed in double precision, its error is much smaller than spacing in single precision (about $9.5 \times 10^{-7}$ near $H_n \approx 14$), making it a suitable reference.
Here $H_{10^6} \approx 14.3927267$ and $H_{3 \times 10^6} \approx 15.49134$.

## Why forward summation stops

`f32` has 24 significand bits including the implicit leading bit; values in $8 \le S < 16$ have spacing $\mathrm{ulp}(S) = 2^{3-23} = 2^{-20}$.
Under round to nearest, ties to even, $S + x$ rounds to $S$ when $x < \mathrm{ulp}(S)/2 = 2^{-21}$.
Forward `f32` summation reaches about 15.40 at $k \approx 2 \times 10^{6}$.
This exceeds mathematical $H_{2^{21}} \approx 15.133$; [Errors as term count increases](#errors-as-term-count-increases) explains why.
Terms satisfying $1/k < 2^{-21}$, equivalently $k > 2^{21} = 2{,}097{,}152$, all leave the sum unchanged.
The $k = 2^{21}$ term is exactly half a spacing, so the sum's lowest bit decides whether ties-to-even rounds upward or back.
Thus forward `f32` summation stops at $15.403683$ around $k = 2^{21}$.

Three approaches prevent this stagnation.

- **Reverse summation**: Add in $k = n, n-1, \ldots, 1$ order.
  Small terms enter while partial sums are small, avoiding stagnation and greatly reducing rounding error.
- **Compensated summation** (`ScaledAccumulator`): Neumaier's method retains a sum and correction.
  Each addition's lost low-order contribution remains in the correction.
  The correction has finite precision too, so the sum is not exact.
- **Exact summation** (`RangeAccumulator`): Accumulate terms as integer digits without rounding, then round once at the end.

Every method still first rounds each $1/k$ term to `f32`.
`RangeAccumulator` adds those rounded terms exactly, leaving the sum of term-rounding errors in its difference from $H_n$ (at most about $u H_n$, with $u = 2^{-24}$).
At $H_n \approx 15.5$ this is $u H_n \approx 9.2 \times 10^{-7}$, nearly 1 ulp.

The following block defines helpers shared by two cells.
`term` returns term $1/k$ rounded to `f32`; `harmonic_reference` returns the expansion above through $1/(120 n^4)$; `ulp_at` returns spacing at a `f32` value.

```rust
//| id: core-harmonic-series-model
//| kind: definitions
//| title: "Harmonic terms and reference values"
//| crates: [scioxide-core]
use scioxide_core::RealScalar;

/// Term 1/k rounded to f32 (k < 2²⁴ is exactly representable in f32).
pub(crate) fn term(k: usize) -> f32 {
    1.0_f32 / k as f32
}

/// Reference: Euler–Maclaurin expansion in double precision using intrinsic f64 methods.
pub(crate) fn harmonic_reference(n: usize) -> f64 {
    let x = n as f64;
    x.ln() + 0.577_215_664_901_532_9 + 0.5 / x - 1.0 / (12.0 * x * x) + 1.0 / (120.0 * x.powi(4))
}

/// Spacing at f32 value (difference to its upper neighbor).
pub(crate) fn ulp_at(value: f32) -> f64 {
    f64::from(RealScalar::next_after(value, f32::INFINITY) - value)
}
```

## Comparing a selected term count

For selected $n$, compute four `f32` sums and report reference differences in local `f32` spacing (ulp).
Input is $\log_{10} n$ (1–6.5 in steps of 0.5), with $n$ obtained by rounding $10^{\log_{10} n}$ to an integer.
The first table shows $n$, reference, and ulp size; the second each method's sum and reference difference.
Computing $n = 10^{6.5} \approx 3.16 \times 10^{6}$ takes time, so execution uses a button.

```rust
//| id: core-harmonic-series-summation
//| ref: core-harmonic-series-summation
//| title: "Summing the harmonic series in single precision four ways"
//| run: button
//| crates: [scioxide-core]
//| uses: [core-harmonic-series-model]
//| inputs:
//|   log_n: {"type":"range","label":"Term count exponent log₁₀ n [-]","description":"1 gives 10 terms, 6 gives 10⁶, and 6.5 about 3.16 × 10⁶.","min":1,"max":6.5,"step":0.5,"value":6.5}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"million","label":"n = 10⁶","description":"Forward summation has not stopped but already differs substantially.","inputs":{"log_n":6}}
//|   - {"id":"ten","label":"n = 10","description":"Few terms; every method agrees within 1 ulp.","inputs":{"log_n":1}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"reference","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":3162278}
//|       - {"artifact":"reference","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":15.5440194,"atol":1e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"sums","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":15.403683,"atol":1e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"sums","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"min":-160000,"max":-140000}}
//|       - {"artifact":"sums","path":"/rows/3/2","range":{"min":-1,"max":1}}
//|   - case: million
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"reference","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":14.392726722865724,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"sums","path":"/rows/2/2","range":{"min":-3,"max":3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"sums","path":"/rows/3/2","range":{"min":-1,"max":1}}
//|   - case: ten
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"sums","path":"/rows/3/1","approx":{"value":2.9289682539682538,"atol":3e-7}}
//|       - {"artifact":"sums","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"min":-1,"max":1}}
use scioxide_core::{RangeAccumulator, RealScalar, ScaledAccumulator};

let n = RealScalar::round(RealScalar::powf(10.0_f64, log_n)) as usize;
let forward = (1..=n).fold(0.0_f32, |sum, k| sum + term(k));
let backward = (1..=n).rev().fold(0.0_f32, |sum, k| sum + term(k));
let mut compensated = ScaledAccumulator::<f32>::ZERO;
let mut exact = RangeAccumulator::<f32>::new();
for k in 1..=n {
    compensated.add_value(term(k)).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    exact.add(term(k)).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
}
let compensated = compensated.round().map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let exact = exact.finish().map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;

let reference = harmonic_reference(n);
// f32 spacing at the sum, used as the difference unit.
let ulp = ulp_at(exact);
let fixed7 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 7 });
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let reference_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Term count n [terms]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Reference H_n [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed7 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "f32 spacing at the sum [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    reference_columns,
    vec![(n as u32, reference, ulp)],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "reference" })
);

let fixed2 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 2 });
let sum_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Method" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "f32 sum [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed7 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Sum − reference [ulp]", "type": "number", "format": fixed2 }
]);
let row = |name: &'static str, value: f32| {
    let value = f64::from(value);
    (name, value, (value - reference) / ulp)
};
emit_table_with_columns!(
    sum_columns,
    vec![
        row("Forward summation", forward),
        row("Reverse summation", backward),
        row("ScaledAccumulator", compensated),
        row("RangeAccumulator", exact),
    ],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "sums" })
);
```

At the default $n = 3{,}162{,}278$, the forward sum has stopped at $15.403683$, about 0.14 below reference $15.544019$ (approximately 150,000 ulp).
Reverse summation differs by about 1300 ulp; `RangeAccumulator` by 0.3 ulp.
`ScaledAccumulator` differs by about 180 ulp.
Lost low-order contributions enter the correction, but it also accumulates at `f32` precision, leaving its own rounding error.

At $n = 10^{6}$, forward summation has not stopped yet, but already differs by about 37,000 ulp.
Reverse error is about 80 ulp, `ScaledAccumulator` about 1 ulp, and `RangeAccumulator` 0.2 ulp.
At $n = 10$, all four sums are within 1 ulp of the reference.

## Errors as term count increases

The following cell compares forward, reverse, and `RangeAccumulator` sums with references at twelve points $n = 10, 10^{1.5}, \ldots, 10^{6.5}$ and at $n = 2^{21}$.
Forward and `RangeAccumulator` sums record intermediate states during one pass; reverse summation is recomputed for every $n$.
There are no inputs.
The chart plots absolute errors in ulp on logarithmic axes, marking the forward stopping point $n = 2^{21}$ vertically.
Exact-zero differences are omitted because they cannot appear on logarithmic axes.
The table shows forward sums at the two terms immediately before $k = 2^{21}$, at $k = 2^{21}$, and at the final count, alongside `RangeAccumulator` errors for those counts.

```rust
//| id: core-harmonic-series-growth
//| ref: core-harmonic-series-growth
//| title: "Term counts and method errors"
//| run: button
//| crates: [scioxide-core]
//| uses: [core-harmonic-series-model]
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"frozen","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":2097150}
//|       - {"artifact":"frozen","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":15.4036818,"atol":3e-7}}
//|       - {"artifact":"frozen","path":"/rows/2/1","approx":{"value":15.403683,"atol":1e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"frozen","path":"/rows/3/0","equals":3162278}
//|       - {"artifact":"frozen","path":"/rows/3/1","approx":{"value":15.403683,"atol":1e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"frozen","path":"/rows/3/2","range":{"min":0,"max":1}}
use scioxide_core::{RangeAccumulator, RealScalar};

let mut checkpoints: Vec<usize> = (2..=13)
    .map(|i| RealScalar::round(RealScalar::powf(10.0_f64, 0.5 * i as f64)) as usize)
    .collect();
let last = *checkpoints.last().ok_or("The term-count sequence is empty")?;
// Also record n = 2²¹, where forward summation stops.
checkpoints.push(2_097_152);
let ulps =
    |value: f32, n: usize| (f64::from(value) - harmonic_reference(n)).abs() / ulp_at(value);

let mut forward = 0.0_f32;
let mut exact = RangeAccumulator::<f32>::new();
// Index 0 is forward, 1 reverse, and 2 RangeAccumulator.
let mut curves = [Vec::new(), Vec::new(), Vec::new()];
let mut frozen = Vec::new();
for k in 1..=last {
    forward += term(k);
    exact.add(term(k)).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let exact_sum = exact.finish().map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    if [2_097_150, 2_097_151, 2_097_152, last].contains(&k) {
        frozen.push((k as u32, f64::from(forward), ulps(exact_sum, k)));
    }
    if checkpoints.contains(&k) {
        let backward = (1..=k).rev().fold(0.0_f32, |sum, j| sum + term(j));
        for (curve, value) in curves.iter_mut().zip([forward, backward, exact_sum]) {
            let error = ulps(value, k);
            if error > 0.0 {
                curve.push([k as f64, error]);
            }
        }
    }
}
emit_line_chart!(
    &vec![
        serde_json::json!({ "name": "Forward summation", "points": curves[0] }),
        serde_json::json!({ "name": "Reverse summation", "points": curves[1] }),
        serde_json::json!({ "name": "RangeAccumulator", "points": curves[2] }),
    ],
    options = &serde_json::json!({
        "xLabel": "Term count n [terms]",
        "yLabel": "|Sum − reference| [ulp]",
        "xType": "log",
        "yType": "log",
        "annotations": [{ "kind": "line", "axis": "x", "value": 2_097_152.0, "label": "n = 2²¹" }]
    })
);
let fixed7 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 7 });
let fixed2 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 2 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Term count k [terms]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Forward sum [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed7 },
    {
        "key": "2",
        "label": "RangeAccumulator |sum − reference| [ulp]",
        "type": "number",
        "format": fixed2
    }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    frozen,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "frozen" })
);
```

Forward error grows overall with $n$ but not monotonically, peaking near 280,000 ulp at $n = 2^{21}$.
As terms approach the sum's spacing, each is rounded to an integer number of spacings before addition.
Terms in $2^{20} < k < 2^{21}$ exceed half of the spacing $2^{-20}$ but are below one spacing, so each adds exactly one spacing.
Their mathematical sum is about $\ln 2 \approx 0.693$, while the `f32` sum increases by about 1.
Consequently, at stopping point $n = 2^{21}$, forward sum $15.403683$ exceeds $H_n \approx 15.133$ by about 0.27.
Thereafter the sum remains fixed while only $H_n$ grows; error changes sign at $n \approx 2.75 \times 10^{6}$ and at final $n \approx 3.16 \times 10^{6}$ the sum is about 0.14 low (150,000 ulp).
Beyond this point, the discrepancy keeps growing with $\ln n$.

Reverse error grows slowly, reaching about 1300 ulp at $n \approx 3.16 \times 10^{6}$.
`RangeAccumulator` has only term-rounding error and stays within 1 ulp at every recorded $n$.

The table shows the final 1 ulp forward increase at term $k = 2{,}097{,}151$, reaching $15.4036827$.
Term $k = 2^{21} = 2{,}097{,}152$ is exactly half a spacing and rounds back under ties to even.
Approximately 1.06 million subsequent terms leave the sum unchanged, including final $k = 3{,}162{,}278$.
The same rows' `RangeAccumulator` errors remain 0.2–0.3 ulp across forward stagnation.

## Summary

Forward `f32` sums cannot change when adding below half a spacing; harmonic summation therefore stops increasing at $k > 2^{21}$.
At $n \approx 3.16 \times 10^{6}$, reversing order reduces error from about 150,000 to 1300 ulp.
`ScaledAccumulator` compensation reduces it further, but the single-precision correction still leaves about 180 ulp at the same $n$.
`RangeAccumulator` sums supplied terms exactly and rounds once, leaving only the errors from rounding individual terms to `f32`.
Use `RangeAccumulator` for exact sums or merging partitioned sums; use `ScaledAccumulator` for low-storage compensation over wide-range values.
