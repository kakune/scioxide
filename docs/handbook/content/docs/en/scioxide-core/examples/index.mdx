`scioxide-core` examples address issues hard to detect from final results alone: intermediate range, small terms lost to rounding, cancellation, and solve accounting.
Every example compares SciOxide computations with independently obtained analytical solutions, expansions, or alternative methods.

| Example | Topic | Main APIs | Independent check |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Protect intermediate values before the final result](range-safe-arithmetic/) | Sums between large terms, norms with out-of-range squares, binomial probabilities | `checked_sum`, `checked_scaled_norm`, `checked_float_product_factors` | $N s$ rounded through one multiplication, $\sqrt{n}\,x$, exact logarithmic sums |
| [Diatomic rotational partition functions and Boltzmann distributions](rotational-partition-function/) | Rigid rotors CO, HCl, HF; energies referenced to dissociation limits | `weighted_logsumexp`, `logsumexp`, `softmax_into`, `checked_sum_of_products` | Mulholland high-temperature expansion, three-term low-temperature sums, continuous approximation of the most populated level |
| [The common-ion effect and solubility of sparingly soluble salts](common-ion-solubility/) | Solubility equilibria of AgCl, AgBr, AgI, and BaSO₄ | `checked_quadratic_roots`, `Tolerance::is_residual_ok`, `checked_sum_of_products` | Rationalized solution formula and residual contracts |
| [The harmonic series in single precision and summation methods](harmonic-series-summation/) | Stagnation of harmonic summation in `f32` | `RangeAccumulator`, `ScaledAccumulator` | Euler–Maclaurin expansion |
| [Norms of wavefunction tails](wavefunction-tail-norms/) | Samples of decaying and growing harmonic oscillator solutions | `checked_scaled_norm`, `ScaledNorm::merge`, `logsumexp` | Log-sum-exp from component logarithms |
| [Spin degeneracy and paramagnetism](ising-paramagnet/) | Independent spins in an external magnetic field | `checked_binomial`, `weighted_logsumexp`, `CombinationEnumerator`, `combination_rank` | Exact solution $(2\cosh \beta h)^N$ and enumeration of every microstate |
| [Kepler's equation and solve accounting](kepler-solve-accounting/) | Fixed-point iteration and nested Newton solves | `SolveState`, `SolveScope`, `invoke_provider`, `ResidualTolerance` | Bisection reference values |

When reading results, examine the stated error scale (ulp, relative difference, or residual) together with the contract or approximation predicting it.
[The mathematics of the shared foundation](../algorithms/) collects contract definitions.

## Summary

Arithmetic examples use defaults whose direct evaluation becomes zero, `inf`, or `NaN`, or silently loses significant digits.
Compare naive computations with range-safe arithmetic in the first example, exponential sums and probabilities in the partition-function and spin examples, and many differently scaled terms in the harmonic-series example.
Solubility covers small-root cancellation, wavefunctions cover norms with out-of-range squares, and Kepler's equation covers iteration and evaluation counts.
Before applying these methods to your own problem, check that table errors fit the contracts or approximation scales stated in the text.
