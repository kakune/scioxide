In statistical mechanics, the number of microstates sharing an energy (degeneracy) weights the partition function.
Degeneracy grows combinatorially with system size, exceeding 32-bit integers at 35 spins.
This example considers noninteracting spins in an external field (an ideal paramagnet), computing degeneracies through `checked_binomial` and partition functions through `weighted_logsumexp`.
An exact solution allows direct error measurement.

## Independent spins in an external field

Let $N$ spins $s_i \in \{+1, -1\}$ lie in uniform magnetic flux density $B$ without mutual interactions.
For spin 1/2 with g-factor 2 (such as unpaired electrons in paramagnetic salts), the moment equals Bohr magneton $\mu_B$, with single-spin energy magnitude $h = \mu_B B$.
The Hamiltonian is

$$
\mathcal{H} = -h \sum_{i=1}^{N} s_i
$$


With $k$ down spins, total magnetization in $\mu_B$ units is $M_k = N - 2k$, energy is $E_k = -h M_k$, and $g(N, k) = C(N, k)$ microstates share that energy.
For inverse temperature $\beta = 1/k_B T$, the partition function is

$$
Z = \sum_{k=0}^{N} C(N, k)\, e^{\beta h (N - 2k)}
$$

Independent spins factorize it as $Z = (e^{\beta h} + e^{-\beta h})^N = (2\cosh \beta h)^N$.
The probability of level $k$ and the mean magnetization are

$$
P(k) = \frac{C(N, k)\, e^{\beta h (N - 2k)}}{Z}, \qquad \langle M \rangle = \sum_k P(k)(N - 2k) = N \tanh \beta h
$$


$P(k)$ is binomial with single-spin down probability $p_{\downarrow} = 1/(1 + e^{2\beta h})$ and mean $\langle k \rangle = N p_{\downarrow}$.

Only dimensionless product $\beta h = \mu_B B / k_B T$ enters the computation.
Since $\mu_B / k_B = 0.671714$ K/T, $B = 1$ T and $T = 1$ K give $\beta h \approx 0.672$.
For $\beta h \ll 1$, $\langle M \rangle \approx N \beta h$, making magnetization proportional to $B/T$ (Curie's law).
For $\beta h \gg 1$, $\langle M \rangle \to N$, and magnetization saturates.

## Exact degeneracy calculations

Degeneracy $C(N, k)$ peaks near $k = N/2$; limits for fitting `usize` are as follows.

| Width of `usize` | Largest fitting $\max_k C(N, k)$ | Supported $N$ |
|---|---|---|
| 32 bits (wasm32) | $C(34, 17) = 2{,}333{,}606{,}220$ | $N \le 34$ (`SizeOverflow` at $C(35, 17) = 4{,}537{,}567{,}650 > 2^{32}$) |
| 64 bits | $C(67, 33) \approx 1.42 \times 10^{19}$ | $N \le 67$（$C(68, 34) \approx 2.85 \times 10^{19} > 2^{64}$） |

`checked_binomial(n, k)` uses symmetry to replace $k$ with $\min(k, n - k)$, then updates integer $C(n - k + i, i) = C(n - k + i - 1, i - 1) \cdot (n - k + i) / i$ in $i = 1, 2, \ldots, k$ order.
Each stage first divides numerator and denominator by $d = \gcd(n - k + i,\ i)$.
The previous value times $n - k + i$ equals $i \cdot C(n - k + i, i)$ and is divisible by $i$; since $(n - k + i)/d$ and $i/d$ are coprime, the previous value is divisible by $i/d$.
Division is therefore exact, and multiplication gives exactly the next value $C(n - k + i, i)$.
It grows monotonically with $i$, reaching $C(n, k)$ at the final stage.
No intermediate exceeds the final result, so `SizeOverflow` occurs only when the final value exceeds `usize`.

Partition computation converts this integer to `f64` as a weight.
For $N \le 56$ satisfying $C(N, \lfloor N/2 \rfloor) < 2^{53}$, every $C(N, k)$ is exactly representable in `f64`, making conversion error-free.

`scioxide-core` provides no logarithmic binomial-coefficient function.
For larger $N$ with degeneracies exceeding `usize`, use `ln_binomial` in `scioxide-special` or accumulate recurrence $\ln C(N, k+1) = \ln C(N, k) + \ln\bigl((N - k)/(k + 1)\bigr)$ through `RealScalar::ln`.
The latter's rounding error grows proportionally to the $k$ stages.
When spins are independent as here, use factorized $\ln Z = N \ln(2\cosh\beta h)$ without degeneracies.

## Partition functions and probabilities in logarithmic form

The largest $C(N, k)\, e^{\beta h(N-2k)}$ term is of order $e^{|\beta h| N}$, leaving `f64` range when $|\beta h| N > 709.78$.
For example, $B = 10$ T, $T = 0.2$ K, and $N = 34$ give $\beta h N \approx 1142$.
Passing exponents $v_k = \beta h (N - 2k)$ and weights $w_k = C(N, k)$ separately to `weighted_logsumexp(v, w)` obtains $\ln Z$ without forming $e^{v_k}$ or products $w_k e^{v_k}$ in `f64`.
With positive weights, error stays within 16 ULP at the result itself (a tested accuracy guarantee; [Logarithmic sums, weighted sums, and normalization](../../algorithms/logarithmic-reductions/)).
At $\ln Z \approx 10$, this corresponds to absolute error about $3 \times 10^{-14}$.

Compute probabilities $P(k)$ by normalizing $\ln C(N, k) + \beta h (N - 2k)$ through `softmax_into`.
Compute $\ln C(N, k)$ through `RealScalar::ln`.
Compute $\langle M \rangle$ through `checked_sum_of_products`, rounding the product sum once.
The error in $\langle M \rangle$ is therefore determined almost entirely by rounding of $P(k)$.

For an independent reference, rewrite exact $\ln Z = N \ln(2\cosh \beta h)$ as $N(|\beta h| + \ln(1 + e^{-2|\beta h|}))$ to avoid $\cosh$ overflow and compute with intrinsic `f64` methods `exp`, `ln_1p`, and `tanh`.
These independent computations bypass SciOxide and each have their own rounding error.

Inputs are spin count $N$, flux density $B$ [T], and temperature $T$ [K].
The first table shows $\ln Z$, $\langle M \rangle$ [$\mu_B$], and a direct sum for comparison, formed by summing terms in `f64`, with absolute and relative exact-solution differences.
The second shows $\beta h$, whether `checked_binomial(35, 17)` fits this environment's `usize`, and the number of levels whose $P(k)$ underflows to zero.
The chart plots $P(k)$ logarithmically, marking exact mean $\langle k \rangle$ vertically.
Zero $P(k)$ values are omitted from logarithmic plots.

```rust
//| id: core-ising-paramagnet
//| ref: core-ising-paramagnet
//| title: "Partition functions from degeneracy-weighted logarithmic sums"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core]
//| inputs:
//|   n_spins: {"type":"range","label":"Spin count N","description":"Degeneracies fit usize for N ≤ 34 in wasm32.","min":2,"max":34,"step":1,"value":16}
//|   field_tesla: {"type":"range","label":"Magnetic flux density B [T]","description":"Negative B favors down spins.","min":-10,"max":10,"step":0.5,"value":1}
//|   temperature: {"type":"range","label":"Temperature T [K]","description":"βh = 0.671714 B/T. Lower temperatures and stronger fields increase βh.","min":0.1,"max":10,"step":0.1,"value":1}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"cold-strong-field","label":"N = 34, 10 T, 0.2 K","description":"At βhN ≈ 1142, direct f64 term summation overflows.","inputs":{"n_spins":34,"field_tesla":10,"temperature":0.2}}
//|   - {"id":"reversed-field","label":"B = −1 T","description":"Magnetization reverses sign and the probability peak shifts toward larger k.","inputs":{"field_tesla":-1}}
//|   - {"id":"zero-field","label":"B = 0 T","description":"Z = 2^N and ⟨M⟩ = 0.","inputs":{"field_tesla":0}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":14.457265385799,"rtol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/0/3","range":{"min":0,"max":5e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/1/1","approx":{"value":9.377699506276,"rtol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/1/3","range":{"min":0,"max":5e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"environment","path":"/rows/0/4","equals":0}
//|   - case: cold-strong-field
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":1141.9138,"rtol":1e-8}}
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/0/3","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-11}}
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/2/1","equals":null}
//|       - {"artifact":"environment","path":"/rows/0/4","range":{"min":20,"max":26}}
//|   - case: reversed-field
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":14.457265385799,"rtol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/1/1","approx":{"value":-9.377699506276,"rtol":1e-12}}
//|   - case: zero-field
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":11.090354888959125,"rtol":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/1/1","approx":{"value":0,"atol":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/1/4","equals":null}
use scioxide_core::{
    SciOxideErrorKind, RealScalar, checked_binomial, checked_sum_of_products, softmax_into,
    weighted_logsumexp,
};

let n = n_spins as usize;
// βh = μ_B B / (k_B T). μ_B / k_B = 0.671714 K/T (spin 1/2 with g = 2).
let x = 0.671_714 * field_tesla / temperature;
// Degeneracy C(N, k). Below 2^53 for N ≤ 56, so f64 conversion is exact.
let mut degeneracy = Vec::with_capacity(n + 1);
for k in 0..=n {
    degeneracy.push(checked_binomial(n, k).map_err(|e| e.to_string())? as f64);
}
// Total magnetization M_k = N − 2k [μ_B] and exponent βh M_k.
let magnetization: Vec<f64> = (0..=n).map(|k| n as f64 - 2.0 * k as f64).collect();
let exponents: Vec<f64> = magnetization.iter().map(|m| x * m).collect();

// ln Z: pass weights C(N, k) separately from exponents βh M_k without forming e^{βh M_k}.
let ln_z = weighted_logsumexp(&exponents, &degeneracy)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?
    .log_magnitude()
    .ok_or("The partition function became zero")?;
// P(k): normalize ln C(N, k) + βh M_k. Round the product sum for ⟨M⟩ once.
let logits: Vec<f64> = degeneracy
    .iter()
    .zip(&exponents)
    .map(|(&g, &e)| RealScalar::ln(g) + e)
    .collect();
let mut probability = vec![0.0; n + 1];
let mut scratch = vec![0.0; n + 1];
softmax_into(&logits, &mut probability, &mut scratch).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mean_m =
    checked_sum_of_products(&probability, &magnetization).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;

// Comparison: form C(N, k) e^{βh M_k} with intrinsic f64 methods and sum; non-finite values become None.
let direct: f64 = degeneracy
    .iter()
    .zip(&exponents)
    .map(|(g, e)| g * e.exp())
    .sum::<f64>()
    .ln();
let direct = RealScalar::is_finite(direct).then_some(direct);
// Exact references using intrinsic f64 methods: ln Z = N(|βh| + ln(1 + e^{-2|βh|})), ⟨M⟩ = N tanh βh.
let exact_ln_z = n as f64 * (x.abs() + (-2.0 * x.abs()).exp().ln_1p());
let exact_m = n as f64 * x.tanh();
let row = |name: &'static str, value: Option<f64>, exact: f64| {
    let difference = value.map(|v| (v - exact).abs());
    // Relative difference is undefined when the exact solution is zero.
    let relative = difference.filter(|_| exact != 0.0).map(|d| d / exact.abs());
    (name, value, exact, difference, relative)
};
let digits = serde_json::json!({ "significantDigits": 14 });
let sci2 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 2 });
let lost = "Non-finite";
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Quantity" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Computed value", "type": "number", "format": digits, "nullText": lost },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Exact solution", "type": "number", "format": digits },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Absolute difference", "type": "number", "format": sci2, "nullText": "—" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Relative difference [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2, "nullText": "—" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    vec![
        row("ln Z [-]", Some(ln_z), exact_ln_z),
        row("⟨M⟩ [μ_B]", Some(mean_m), exact_m),
        row("ln Z (direct f64 term sum) [-]", direct, exact_ln_z),
    ],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "partition" })
);

// Show by error category whether C(35, 17) = 4537567650 fits this environment's usize.
let (boundary_status, boundary) = match checked_binomial(35, 17) {
    Ok(value) => ("Ok", Some(value as f64)),
    Err(error) if error.kind() == SciOxideErrorKind::SizeOverflow => ("SizeOverflow", None),
    Err(error) => return Err(error.to_string()),
};
let underflow = probability.iter().filter(|&&p| p == 0.0).count();
let fixed4 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 4 });
let environment_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "βh = μ_B B / k_B T [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "1", "label": "usize width [bits]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "checked_binomial(35, 17) result" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "C(35, 17)", "type": "integer", "nullText": "Does not fit usize" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Levels where P(k) becomes zero [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Level count N + 1 [levels]", "type": "integer" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    environment_columns,
    vec![(x, usize::BITS, boundary_status, boundary, underflow, n + 1,)],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "environment" })
);

// Reference line: exact mean ⟨k⟩ = N / (1 + e^{2βh}), using intrinsic f64 methods.
let mean_k = n as f64 / (1.0 + (2.0 * x).exp());
let points: Vec<[f64; 2]> = probability
    .iter()
    .enumerate()
    .filter(|&(_, &p)| p > 0.0)
    .map(|(k, &p)| [k as f64, p])
    .collect();
emit_line_chart!(
    &vec![serde_json::json!({ "name": format!("N = {n}, βh = {x:.3}"), "points": points })],
    options = &serde_json::json!({
        "xLabel": "Down-spin count k [spins]",
        "yLabel": "Level probability P(k) [-]",
        "yType": "log",
        "xMin": 0.0,
        "xMax": n as f64,
        "annotations": [{ "kind": "line", "axis": "x", "value": mean_k, "label": "Exact mean ⟨k⟩" }]
    })
);
```

At the defaults ($N = 16$, $B = 1$ T, $T = 1$ K, $\beta h = 0.672$), both $\ln Z = 14.457265385799$ and $\langle M \rangle = 9.377699506276\,\mu_B$ match exact solutions to displayed digits.
Terms fit `f64` here, so direct summation gives the same value.
Native evaluation of 200 selected inputs ($N$ of 2, 9, 16, 25, 34; $B$ of $\pm 10$, $\pm 3$, $\pm 0.5$, 1, 4 T; $T$ of 0.1, 0.5, 1, 3, 10 K) gave relative $\ln Z$ differences at most $2.2 \times 10^{-16}$ (about 1 ULP) and relative $\langle M \rangle$ differences at most $1.2 \times 10^{-15}$.
These are indistinguishable from reference rounding error and, for $\ln Z$, within the 16 ULP specification.

At $N = 34$, $B = 10$ T, and $T = 0.2$ K, $\beta h N \approx 1142$; direct sums become non-finite, but $\ln Z = 1141.9138$ from `weighted_logsumexp` agrees with the exact solution.
Magnetization saturates at $\langle M \rangle = 34\,\mu_B$.
Then $P(k)$ underflows to zero in 23 of 35 levels, which are omitted from the chart.

The chart shows the binomial probabilities $P(k)$, peaking near the vertical line for exact mean $\langle k \rangle = N/(1 + e^{2\beta h})$.
At $B = -1$ T, $\langle M \rangle$ reverses sign and the peak shifts toward larger $k$.
At $B = 0$, $\ln Z = N \ln 2$ and $\langle M \rangle = 0$, with a symmetric peak centered at $k = N/2$.
The second table's `checked_binomial(35, 17)` produces `SizeOverflow` in browser wasm32 but returns 4537567650 in native 64-bit environments.

## Verification by enumerating all microstates

Small systems can enumerate and sum every one of $2^N$ microstates.
Without grouping degeneracies, this independently checks both binomial coefficients and weighted log-sum-exp.
For each $k = 0, \ldots, N$, enumerate down-spin position combinations lexicographically through `CombinationEnumerator`, checking three properties.
Inputs are spin count $N$ and dimensionless $\beta h$.

- Enumeration count equals `checked_binomial(N, k)`.
- Each combination's `combination_rank` equals enumeration position.
- The `logsumexp` of all-state exponents $\beta h M$, with magnetization counted from individual spins, agrees with exact $\ln Z$.

The first table shows count and rank comparisons; the second differences between $\ln Z$ and exact solutions.

```rust
//| id: core-ising-microstates
//| ref: core-ising-microstates
//| title: "Checking partition functions by enumerating all microstates"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core]
//| inputs:
//|   n_spins: {"type":"range","label":"Spin count N","description":"Enumerate all 2^N states.","min":2,"max":14,"step":1,"value":10}
//|   beta_h: {"type":"range","label":"βh = μ_B B / k_B T [-]","description":"About 0.67 for B = 1 T and T = 1 K.","min":-3,"max":3,"step":0.1,"value":0.7}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"largest","label":"N = 14, βh = −2.5","description":"Even 16384 states agree with the exact solution to about 1 ULP.","inputs":{"n_spins":14,"beta_h":-2.5}}
//|   - {"id":"zero-field","label":"βh = 0","description":"All exponents are zero, giving ln Z = N ln 2.","inputs":{"beta_h":0}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"counts","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":1024}
//|       - {"artifact":"counts","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":1024}
//|       - {"artifact":"counts","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":0}
//|       - {"artifact":"counts","path":"/rows/0/3","equals":0}
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/0/0","approx":{"value":9.20417409918451,"rtol":1e-13}}
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"min":0,"max":3e-14}}
//|   - case: largest
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"counts","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":16384}
//|       - {"artifact":"counts","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":0}
//|       - {"artifact":"counts","path":"/rows/0/3","equals":0}
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-13}}
//|   - case: zero-field
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/0/0","approx":{"value":6.931471805599453,"rtol":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"partition","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"min":0,"max":2e-14}}
use scioxide_core::{CombinationEnumerator, checked_binomial, combination_rank, logsumexp};

let n = n_spins as usize;
let mut exponents = Vec::new();
let (mut count_mismatch, mut rank_mismatch) = (0_u32, 0_u32);
for k in 0..=n {
    let mut storage = vec![0; k];
    let mut states =
        CombinationEnumerator::first_into(n, &mut storage[..]).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let mut position = 0;
    loop {
        let down = states.indices();
        if combination_rank(n, down).map_err(|e| e.to_string())? != position {
            rank_mismatch += 1;
        }
        // Count magnetization from individual spin directions (−1 at down positions, +1 elsewhere).
        let m: i32 = (0..n).map(|i| if down.contains(&i) { -1 } else { 1 }).sum();
        exponents.push(beta_h * f64::from(m));
        position += 1;
        if !states.advance() {
            break;
        }
    }
    if position != checked_binomial(n, k).map_err(|e| e.to_string())? {
        count_mismatch += 1;
    }
}
let count_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Enumerated states [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "2^N [states]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "k values with counts different from C(N, k) [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "States with ranks different from enumeration positions [count]", "type": "integer" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    count_columns,
    vec![(exponents.len(), 1_usize << n, count_mismatch, rank_mismatch)],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "counts" })
);

let ln_z = logsumexp(&exponents).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
// Exact references using intrinsic f64 methods.
let exact = n as f64 * (beta_h.abs() + (-2.0 * beta_h.abs()).exp().ln_1p());
let digits = serde_json::json!({ "significantDigits": 14 });
let sci2 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 2 });
let partition_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "ln Z (logsumexp of all states) [-]", "type": "number", "format": digits },
    { "key": "1", "label": "ln Z (exact solution) [-]", "type": "number", "format": digits },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Absolute difference [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    partition_columns,
    vec![(ln_z, exact, (ln_z - exact).abs())],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "partition" })
);
```

At the defaults ($N = 10$, $\beta h = 0.7$), there are $2^{10} = 1024$ states and zero count or rank mismatches.
Even summing 1024 ungrouped states, `logsumexp` agrees with exact $\ln Z = 9.2042$ to displayed digits.
At $N = 14$ and $\beta h = -2.5$ (16384 terms), absolute difference $7.1 \times 10^{-15}$ equals 1 ULP of $\ln Z \approx 35.1$.
At $\beta h = 0$, every state exponent is zero and $\ln Z = 10 \ln 2 = 6.9315$.

## Applicability and limits

These computations avoid overflow in $\ln Z$ and $\langle M \rangle$ whenever exponents $\beta h (N - 2k)$ are finite.
The following restrictions remain.

- Degeneracy is limited by `usize` ($N \le 34$ in wasm32, $N \le 67$ with 64 bits).
- Small $P(k)$ outside the representable range of `f64` become zero or subnormal.
  Their contributions to means and variances are negligible, but retain logarithms through `log_softmax_into` when tail probabilities themselves matter.
- With interacting spins in an Ising model, $Z$ no longer factorizes, and degeneracies must be recounted by energy level.
  Exact-solution checks here apply only to this independent system.

## Summary

`checked_binomial` degeneracies have no intermediate above the result, so `SizeOverflow` occurs only when the result exceeds `usize`.
Since `scioxide-core` provides no logarithmic binomial coefficients, for $N \ge 35$ in wasm32 use `ln_binomial` from `scioxide-special` or accumulate a logarithmic recurrence yourself.
Pass weights and exponents separately to `weighted_logsumexp`, obtain probabilities through `softmax_into` and expectations through `checked_sum_of_products`, keeping finite results even when low-temperature, strong-field direct sums become `inf`.
At the defaults, $\ln Z$ and $\langle M \rangle$ match exact solutions to displayed digits; all 200 tested inputs stayed within relative difference $1.2 \times 10^{-15}$, also agreeing with microstate enumeration to about 1 ULP.
