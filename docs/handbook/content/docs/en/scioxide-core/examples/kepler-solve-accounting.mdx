Practical numerical methods often first approach a solution with a global but slow method, then finish with a locally fast method.
When the second stage is a nested solve, distinguish how many iterations and evaluations each level performs.
Using Kepler's equation, this example separates accounting through `SolveState`, `SolveContext`, and `SolveScope` from `scioxide-core`.
scioxide-rootfind's [Kepler's equation and eccentricity](../../../scioxide-rootfind/examples/kepler-equation/) covers the model and method comparisons; this page focuses on accounting.
Defaults ($e = 0.6$, $M = 1.2$ rad) match that page.

## Kepler's equation

Elliptical orbital positions follow from eccentric anomaly $E$ [rad], obtained from mean anomaly $M$ [rad], an angle advancing proportionally to time.
For dimensionless eccentricity $e$ ($0 \le e < 1$), $E$ solves

$$
R(E) = E - e \sin E - M = 0
$$


Since $R'(E) = 1 - e\cos E \ge 1 - e > 0$, $R$ is strictly increasing and solution $E^*$ is unique.
Because $R(0) = -M \le 0$ and $R(2\pi) = 2\pi - M \ge 0$, condition $M \in [0, 2\pi]$ puts the solution in $[0, 2\pi]$.

Residuals also bound solution error.
The mean value theorem gives $R(E) = R(E) - R(E^*) = R'(\xi)(E - E^*)$ for some $\xi$; since $R' \ge 1 - e$,

$$
|E - E^*| \le \frac{|R(E)|}{1 - e}
$$


Residual at most tolerance $\tau$ gives error at most $\tau/(1 - e)$.
This bound holds for exact $R$; floating-point residuals include rounding, so applying it to a computed residual is not a verified error bound ([Root uniqueness and error bounds](oxref:rootfind-kepler-uniqueness)).
As $e$ approaches 1, the same residual gives a larger error bound.

## Two-stage method

1. **Fixed-point iteration (parent)**: $E_{k+1} = g(E_k)$, $g(E) = M + e\sin E$, $E_0 = M$.
   Since $|g'(E)| = e|\cos E| \le e < 1$, $g$ is a contraction, converging from every initial value.
   Errors satisfy $|E_k - E^*| \le e^k |E_0 - E^*|$.
   Local contraction rate is $|g'(E^*)| = e|\cos E^*|$, giving slow convergence when $e$ and $|\cos E^*|$ are both near 1.
2. **Newton's method (child)**: $E_{n+1} = E_n - R(E_n)/R'(E_n)$.
   Near the solution, $E_{n+1} - E^* \approx \dfrac{R''(E^*)}{2R'(E^*)}(E_n - E^*)^2$ and $R''(E) = e\sin E$, so errors approximately square at each step.

Fixed-point iteration reveals residuals without additional evaluations.
Since $R(E_k) = E_k - g(E_k) = E_k - E_{k+1}$, $|R(E_k)|$ equals the difference magnitude of consecutive iterates.
$|g'| \le e$ implies $|E_{k+1} - E_k| \le e\,|E_k - E_{k-1}|$, so each fixed-point residual is at most $e$ times the previous one.

## Parent/child accounting

The parent starts through `SolveState::begin_solve`; the child starts from the parent's context through `SolveScope`.
Solve identity `SolveIdentity` contains problem identity, algorithm identity (a constant from `algorithm_id!`), and shape/configuration identities; parent and child use different algorithms.
Apply the limit/admission rules of [Solve lifecycle and computation count management](../../algorithms/solve-lifecycle-and-accounting/) and hierarchical accounting rules of [Nested solves, accounting, and diagnostics](../../algorithms/nested-solves-and-diagnostics/) as follows.

- Parent iterations use $K_{\mathrm{outer}}$; the child has its own $K_{\mathrm{inner}}$.
  Child iterations do not count as the parent's own iterations but appear in its `counts().nested()`.
- Count $g(E)$, $R(E)$, and $R'(E)$ through `invoke_provider` ($g$ and $R$ use `EvaluationRole::Function`; $R'$ uses `Jacobian`).
  The parent has one shared evaluation limit; child evaluations consume its remaining allowance.
- Before indivisible work, check required evaluations through `can_consume`.
  One fixed-point step needs one $g$ evaluation; one Newton step needs two evaluations, $R'$ and updated $R$.
  Insufficient allowance stops before counting an iteration, so every counted iteration completes.
- The child accepts Newton iterates into its `SolveState` through `commit_accepted(PartialResultTrust::DiagnosticOnly)`.
  Once residual $|R(E)|$ satisfies `ResidualTolerance` (absolute tolerance $\tau = 10^{-14}$ rad), it raises reliability through `assess_accepted(PartialResultTrust::ResidualChecked)` and exits.
  Child acceptance does not advance the parent's accepted state.
- The parent accepts the child's returned $E$ just once.
  Reliability is `ResidualChecked` if the child converged, or `DiagnosticOnly` if it reached a limit.

Implement the child Newton solve as `newton_scoped` in `_scoped` form, taking caller scope last.
Passing `&mut SolveContext` from the parent makes the child obey its shared evaluation limit.

Record attempt results in `AttemptRecord`.
Use `AttemptOutcome::Succeeded` when residual tolerance is met and `AttemptOutcome::Stopped` on limit exhaustion, retaining the last $E$ as progress in either case.
All residuals used in decisions come from counted evaluations.
The independent reference is obtained with 80 bisections on $[0, 2\pi]$ ($2\pi / 2^{80}$ is well below spacing in double precision).
Reference evaluations are independent checks and excluded from accounting.

use scioxide_core::{
    Admission, AlgorithmId, AttemptKind, AttemptOutcome, AttemptRecord, Budget, BudgetKind,
    EvaluationCounts, EvaluationRole, ExecutionContext, SciOxideError, PartialResultTrust,
    ProblemToken, RealScalar, ResidualTolerance, SolveBudgets, SolveIdentity, SolveScope,
    SolveState, algorithm_id,
};

const FIXED_POINT: AlgorithmId = algorithm_id!("handbook", "kepler_fixed_point", 1);
const NEWTON: AlgorithmId = algorithm_id!("handbook", "kepler_newton", 1);

/// Residual R(E) = E − e sin E − M.
fn residual(e: f64, m: f64, x: f64) -> f64 {
    x - e * RealScalar::sin(x) - m
}

/// Newton child: `_scoped` form with caller scope last.
/// Append evaluated residual magnitudes to `history`, returning (E, limiting resource kind).
/// A limiting resource of `None` means residual tolerance was achieved.
fn newton_scoped<'scope, 'capability>(
    (e, m, start): (f64, f64, f64),
    tolerance: ResidualTolerance<f64>,
    max_iterations: Budget,
    history: &mut Vec<f64>,
    scope: impl Into<SolveScope<'scope, 'capability>>,
) -> Result<(f64, Option<BudgetKind>), SciOxideError> {
    let identity = SolveIdentity::new(ProblemToken::new(1), NEWTON, 0, 0);
    let allowance = SolveBudgets::unbounded().with_limit(BudgetKind::Iteration, max_iterations);
    let mut state = SolveState::new();
    scope.into().run(&mut state, identity, allowance, |child| {
        let mut x = start;
        // Evaluate the starting residual once; if rejected, return without further work.
        if let Admission::Denied(report) = child.can_consume(BudgetKind::Evaluation, 1)? {
            return Ok((x, Some(report.kind())));
        }
        let mut f = child
            .invoke_provider(EvaluationRole::Function, || Ok(residual(e, m, x)))?
            .require()?;
        loop {
            history.push(f.abs());
            if tolerance.is_satisfied(f.abs(), 1.0)? {
                child.assess_accepted(PartialResultTrust::ResidualChecked)?;
                return Ok((x, None));
            }
            // Each step needs R′ and updated R. Count the iteration only when two evaluations are admitted.
            let admission = match child.can_consume(BudgetKind::Evaluation, 2)? {
                Admission::Granted(()) => child.record_iteration()?,
                denied => denied,
            };
            if let Admission::Denied(report) = admission {
                return Ok((x, Some(report.kind())));
            }
            let slope = child
                .invoke_provider(
                    EvaluationRole::Jacobian,
                    || Ok(1.0 - e * RealScalar::cos(x)),
                )?
                .require()?;
            x -= f / slope;
            f = child
                .invoke_provider(EvaluationRole::Function, || Ok(residual(e, m, x)))?
                .require()?;
            child.commit_accepted(PartialResultTrust::DiagnosticOnly)?;
        }
    })
}

let (e, m) = (eccentricity, mean_anomaly);
let limit = |count: f64| Budget::try_from(count as usize).map_err(|error| error.to_string());
let mut budgets = SolveBudgets::unbounded()

let mut parent = parent_state
    .begin_solve(ExecutionContext::sequential(), identity, &mut budgets)
    .map_err(|error| error.to_string())?;

let mut x = m;
let mut history = Vec::new();
let parent_stop = loop {
    let admission = match parent
        .can_consume(BudgetKind::Evaluation, 1)
        .map_err(|error| error.to_string())?
    {
        Admission::Granted(()) => parent
            .record_iteration()
            .map_err(|error| error.to_string())?,
        denied => denied,
    };
    if let Admission::Denied(report) = admission {
        break report.kind();
    }
    let next = parent
        .invoke_provider(EvaluationRole::Function, || Ok(m + e * RealScalar::sin(x)))
        .and_then(|invocation| invocation.require())
        .map_err(|error| error.to_string())?;
    // R(E_k) = E_k − g(E_k), giving residuals without extra evaluations.
    history.push((x - next).abs());
    x = next;
};

    (e, m, x),
    tolerance,
    limit(inner_max_iter)?,
    &mut history,
    &mut parent,
)
.map_err(|error| error.to_string())?;

    None => (
        PartialResultTrust::ResidualChecked,
        AttemptOutcome::Succeeded,
    ),
    Some(_) => (PartialResultTrust::DiagnosticOnly, AttemptOutcome::Stopped),
};

    .map_err(|error| error.to_string())?;
let accepted = parent.accepted_state().ok_or("No accepted state")?;
let iteration_budget = parent.budget_report(BudgetKind::Iteration);
let evaluation_budget = parent.budget_report(BudgetKind::Evaluation);
let counts = parent.counts();

    parent_state.epoch(),
    AttemptKind::Call,
    attempt,
    counts,
    Some(x),
);

let (mut lo, mut hi) = (0.0, 2.0 * core::f64::consts::PI);
for _ in 0..80 {
    let mid = 0.5 * (lo + hi);
    if residual(e, m, mid) < 0.0 {
        lo = mid;
    } else {
        hi = mid;
    }
}

    .copied()

Solution $E = 1.78614$ rad differs from bisection by less than bound $\tau/(1 - e) = 2.5 \times 10^{-14}$ rad.
Parent `own` records four iterations and four `Function` evaluations.
`nested` records two child iterations, three `Function` evaluations (starting residual and two updated residuals), and two `Jacobian` evaluations; `total` equals `own` plus `nested`.
Combined evaluation consumption is nine, leaving 21 of the shared allowance of 30.
`accepted()` has one `own` (parent acceptance of final $E$), while `nested` has two (Newton updates), confirming child acceptance does not advance parent acceptance.

At evaluation limit 6, four parent evaluations and one child starting residual leave one remaining.
Insufficient for a two-evaluation Newton step, the child stops with `evaluation` before counting an iteration: `nested` iterations are zero, evaluation consumption five, remaining one.
Attempt outcome is `Stopped`, parent reliability `diagnostic-only`, and $E$ remains the fixed-point result.
Last residual is $6.0 \times 10^{-5}$ rad and bisection difference $5.4 \times 10^{-5}$ rad, within bound $|R(E)|/(1 - e) = 1.5 \times 10^{-4}$ rad.

At $e = 0.95$, fixed-point convergence slows, leaving residual $4.9 \times 10^{-3}$ rad after four steps.
The Newton child takes three steps and eleven evaluations are consumed.

## Summary

Nested two-stage methods manage parent and child iterations under separate limits, readable through `own()` and `nested()` of `counts()`.
Both levels consume one parent evaluation allowance, limiting total problem evaluations in one place.
Checking required evaluations through `can_consume` before indivisible work ensures every counted iteration was executed.
Distinguish `Succeeded` and `Stopped` in `AttemptOutcome` outcomes; for `Stopped`, inspect reliability and the limiting resource kind before use.
Error bounds scale the residual by $1/(1 - e)$, so orbits with $e$ near 1 require correspondingly smaller tolerances.
