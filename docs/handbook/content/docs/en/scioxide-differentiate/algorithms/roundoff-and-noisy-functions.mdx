[Taylor expansion and step-size selection](../taylor-and-step-size/) shows truncation decreasing as $h^p$ when step $h$ shrinks.
In practice, however, sufficiently small $h$ makes errors increase again.
Function values contain rounding or noise amplified by $1/h$ ($1/h^2$ for second differences).
This page estimates that growth, balances it against truncation to derive optimal steps and attainable errors, and compares measurements.
It then explains steps for noise exceeding rounding and for functions available only as tables.
General floating-point and cancellation discussion appears in [Floating-point numbers and conditioning](../../../concepts/floating-point-and-conditioning/).

## Function-value rounding and the 1/h rule

Assume computed value $\hat f$ has relative error at most $\varepsilon_f$ against the true value.

$$
\hat f(t) = f(t)(1 + \theta_t), \qquad \lvert \theta_t \rvert \le \varepsilon_f .
$$

Write double-precision unit roundoff as $u = 2^{-53} \approx 1.11 \times 10^{-16}$.
$u$ is half machine epsilon $\varepsilon = 2^{-52}$, the gap from 1 to the next floating-point value.
Correct rounding gives $\varepsilon_f = u$; error within 1 ulp gives $\varepsilon_f \le \varepsilon$.
Values from lengthy computations or amplified argument rounding can have larger $\varepsilon_f$.
For example, in $\sin(\omega t)$, rounding $\omega t$ can add function-value error up to about $u \lvert \omega t \cos(\omega t) \rvert$.

For central numerator $\hat f(x + h) - \hat f(x - h)$, taking $\lvert f(t) \rvert \approx \lvert f(x) \rvert$ at $t \approx x$ bounds its error by $2 \varepsilon_f \lvert f(x) \rvert$.
Dividing by denominator $2h$ bounds derivative rounding as follows.

$$
E_{\mathrm{round}}^{0}(h) \le \frac{\varepsilon_f \lvert f(x) \rvert}{h}.
$$

The same reasoning bounds forward rounding by $2 \varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert / h$.
Both forward and central second differences have weights $(1, -2, 1)/h^2$ with absolute sum $4/h^2$, bounding rounding by $4 \varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert / h^2$.
The numerator subtracts nearby numbers; smaller $h$ loses more function-value digits through cancellation.
On logarithmic axes, this bound is a line of slope $-1$ ($-2$ for second differences).
Actual rounding sums errors of mixed signs, remaining below the bound and varying irregularly by step.
SciOxide [builds weights from rounded displacements](oxref:differentiate-rounded-displacements), so rounding of node positions themselves does not contribute to error.

## Total error and optimal steps

Adding truncation $C_t h^p$ and rounding $C_r \varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert / h^m$ ($p$ formula order, $m$ derivative order) estimates total error.

$$
E(h) \approx C_t h^p + \frac{C_r \varepsilon_f \lvert f(x) \rvert}{h^m}.
$$

The first term grows with $h$ and the second falls, giving $E(h)$ a U shape on logarithmic axes.
Solving $E'(h) = p C_t h^{p-1} - m C_r \varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert h^{-m-1} = 0$ gives optimal step $h^\ast$.

$$
h^\ast = \left( \frac{m C_r \varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert}{p C_t} \right)^{1/(p + m)} .
$$

At $h^\ast$, rounding is $p/m$ times truncation, giving attainable error below.

$$
E(h^\ast) = \left( 1 + \frac{p}{m} \right) C_t \, (h^\ast)^p \propto \varepsilon_f^{\,p/(p + m)} .
$$

Inserting the [preceding page's](oxref:differentiate-second-differences) leading $C_t$ and $C_r$ above gives this table for four formulas.

| Formula | $p$, $m$ | Total estimate $E(h)$ | Optimal step $h^\ast$ | Attainable error $E(h^\ast)$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First, forward | 1, 1 | $\tfrac{1}{2}\lvert f'' \rvert h + 2 \varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert / h$ | $2 (\varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert / \lvert f'' \rvert)^{1/2}$ | $2 (\varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert \lvert f'' \rvert)^{1/2}$ |
| First, central | 2, 1 | $\tfrac{1}{6}\lvert f''' \rvert h^2 + \varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert / h$ | $(3 \varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert / \lvert f''' \rvert)^{1/3}$ | $\tfrac{3}{2} (\varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert)^{2/3} (\lvert f''' \rvert / 3)^{1/3}$ |
| Second, central | 2, 2 | $\tfrac{1}{12}\lvert f^{(4)} \rvert h^2 + 4 \varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert / h^2$ | $(48 \varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert / \lvert f^{(4)} \rvert)^{1/4}$ | $\tfrac{2}{\sqrt{3}} (\varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert \lvert f^{(4)} \rvert)^{1/2}$ |
| Second, forward | 1, 2 | $\lvert f''' \rvert h + 4 \varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert / h^2$ | $(8 \varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert / \lvert f''' \rvert)^{1/3}$ | $3 (\varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert)^{1/3} \lvert f''' \rvert^{2/3}$ |

If $\lvert f \rvert$ and all derivatives are about 1 and $\varepsilon_f = u$, attainable scales are $10^{-8}$ for first forward, $10^{-11}$ first central, $10^{-8}$ second central, and $10^{-5}$ second forward.
At fixed derivative order $m$, higher formula order $p$ permits smaller errors for the same $\varepsilon_f$.

Factor $c$ in `FiniteDifference` [default step](oxref:differentiate-default-steps) $c \cdot \max(1, \lvert x \rvert)$ uses power $\varepsilon_f^{1/(p + m)}$ of $h^\ast$ in the table, replacing $\varepsilon_f$ with $\varepsilon$ and treating constants and ratio $\lvert f \rvert / \lvert f^{(k)} \rvert$ as 1.
This gives $\varepsilon^{1/2}$ first forward, $\varepsilon^{1/3}$ first central and second forward, and $\varepsilon^{1/4}$ second central.
For smooth, moderately scaled $f$, default errors therefore scale as $\varepsilon^{p/(p + m)}$: $\sqrt{\varepsilon} \approx 1.5 \times 10^{-8}$ for double first forward, $\varepsilon^{2/3} \approx 3.7 \times 10^{-11}$ first central.
Defaults need no knowledge of derivatives of $f$, but deviate from optima when ratio $\lvert f \rvert / \lvert f^{(k)} \rvert$ is far from 1.

## Measuring the U-shaped curve

The next cell computes the first derivative of $f(x) = \sin(\omega x)$ with forward and central differences over steps $h = 10^{-16}$ through $1$, plotting errors against exact $\omega \cos(\omega x_0)$ logarithmically.
Restricting $\omega$ to powers of 2 makes $\omega t$ exact, leaving only $\sin$ rounding in function values.
Dashed lines show table estimates $E(h)$ with $\varepsilon_f = u$.
Vertical lines mark default `FiniteDifference` steps.
The table compares estimated $h^\ast$ and $E(h^\ast)$, errors at $h^\ast$ and defaults, and excluded-point counts.

Below half $\mathrm{ulp}(x_0)$, $x_0 \pm h$ rounds back to $x_0$.
If only one direction rounds back, forward differences switch to the other side.
Function differences are then mostly rounding, giving errors of order $10^{-2}$–$10^{-1}$ at the chart's left edge.
Central differences do not switch one-sided and return `InvalidInput` (`UnsupportedConfiguration`) when either direction rounds back ([Nodes selected by fixed-step central differences](oxref:differentiate-bounded-fixed-nodes)).
Forward differences also error if neither side fits; exclude such steps and count them in “Excluded points”.
Count and exclude exactly zero errors from logarithmic axes too.
Exact values and estimates are references from f64 inherent methods.

```rust
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/0/3","range":{"min":1.3e-9,"max":3.84e-8}}
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/1/3","range":{"min":1.1e-12,"max":3.37e-11}}
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":1.4901161e-8,"rtol":1e-7}}
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/1/4","approx":{"value":6.0554545e-6,"rtol":1e-7}}
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/1/5","range":{"max":6.7e-11}}
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/0/6","equals":0}
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/1/6","equals":1}
//|   - case: fast-oscillation
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/1/2","approx":{"value":1.004e-10,"rtol":2e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/0/3","range":{"max":1.67e-7}}
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/1/3","range":{"max":1.004e-10}}
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/1/5","range":{"min":2.0e-10}}
//|   - case: large-point
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/1/3","range":{"max":2.03e-11}}
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/1/4","approx":{"value":1.8166364e-5,"rtol":1e-7}}
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/1/5","range":{"min":2.0e-10}}
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/0/6","equals":2}
//|       - {"artifact":"u-curve","path":"/rows/1/6","equals":2}
use scioxide_core::{SciOxideError, SciOxideErrorKind, RealScalar};
use scioxide_differentiate::DifferenceScheme::{Central, Forward};
use scioxide_differentiate::{DifferenceStep, FiniteDifference};

let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let omega: f64 = omega.parse().map_err(|_| "Cannot read ω")?;
// References: exact derivative and |f|, |f″|, |f‴| for estimates (f64 inherent methods).
let (s, c) = (omega * x0).sin_cos();
let exact = omega * c;
let (f0, f2, f3) = (
    s.abs(),
    (omega * omega * s).abs(),
    (omega.powi(3) * c).abs(),
);
let u = f64::EPSILON / 2.0;
let scale = x0.abs().max(1.0);
let f = |t: f64| Ok::<f64, SciOxideError>(RealScalar::sin(omega * t));
let mut series = Vec::new();
let mut rows = Vec::new();
for (name, scheme) in [("Forward difference", Forward), ("Central difference", Central)] {
    // Estimate E(h), optimal h*, and default c·max(1, |x0|), using the text's table formulas.
    let model = |h: f64| match scheme {
        Forward => f2 * h / 2.0 + 2.0 * u * f0 / h,
        Central => f3 * h * h / 6.0 + u * f0 / h,
    };
    let (optimum, default_h) = match scheme {
        Forward => (2.0 * (u * f0 / f2).sqrt(), f64::EPSILON.sqrt() * scale),
        Central => ((3.0 * u * f0 / f3).cbrt(), f64::EPSILON.cbrt() * scale),
    };
    // Error at step (None means default); unplaceable nodes give None.
    let error_at = |step: Option<f64>| -> Result<Option<f64>, String> {
        let mut method = FiniteDifference::default().with_scheme(scheme);
        if let Some(h) = step {
            method = method.with_step(DifferenceStep::absolute(h).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?);
        }
        let result = match method.derivative(f, x0) {
            Ok(result) => result,
            Err(e) if e.kind() == SciOxideErrorKind::InvalidInput => return Ok(None),
            Err(e) => return Err(e.to_string()),
        };
        Ok(Some(
            (result.values().ok_or("Derivative not published")?[0] - exact).abs(),
        ))
    };
    let (mut observed, mut theory, mut skipped) = (Vec::new(), Vec::new(), 0);
    for k in 0..=64 {
        let h = 10.0_f64.powf(-16.0 + 0.25 * k as f64);
        theory.push([h, model(h)]);
        // Count and exclude unplaceable steps and exactly zero errors from logarithmic axes.
        match error_at(Some(h))? {
            Some(error) if error > 0.0 => observed.push([h, error]),
            _ => skipped += 1,
        }
    }
    rows.push((
        name,
        optimum,
        model(optimum),
        error_at(Some(optimum))?,
        default_h,
        error_at(None)?,
        skipped,
    ));
    series.push(serde_json::json!({
        "name": name,
        "points": observed,
        "style": { "showSymbol": true }
    }));
    series.push(serde_json::json!({
        "name": format!("{name} estimate E(h)"),
        "points": theory,
        "style": { "lineType": "dashed", "showSymbol": false }
    }));
}
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Formula" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Estimated h* [x units]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Estimate E(h*) [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Error at h* [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3, "nullText": "No nodes" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Default step [x units]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Default-step error [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3, "nullText": "No nodes" },
    { "key": "6", "label": "Excluded points [count]", "type": "integer" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "u-curve" })
);
let annotations = serde_json::json!([
    { "kind": "line", "axis": "x", "value": f64::EPSILON.sqrt() * scale, "label": "Forward default step" },
    { "kind": "line", "axis": "x", "value": f64::EPSILON.cbrt() * scale, "label": "Central default step" }
]);
emit_line_chart!(
    &series,
    options = &serde_json::json!({
        "link": { "group": "differentiate-step" },
        "xLabel": "Step h [x units]",
        "yLabel": "Absolute derivative error [-]",
        "xType": "log",
        "yType": "log",
        "annotations": annotations
    })
);
```

At the left, errors vary irregularly below dashed slope $-1$; at the right they follow dashed truncation slopes 1 forward and 2 central.
The minimum lies near the crossing.
At defaults $\omega = 2$ and $x_0 = 1$, errors at $h^\ast$ are $2.39 \times 10^{-8}$ forward and $6.43 \times 10^{-12}$ central, below estimate $E(h^\ast)$ ($3.83 \times 10^{-8}$ and $3.37 \times 10^{-11}$).
Estimates assume rounding errors align, so actual errors are often smaller.
Some minimum-region points lie below $E(h^\ast)$ (central $8.3 \times 10^{-13}$), but accidental cancellation of rounding does not persist under slight step changes.
Default steps $1.49 \times 10^{-8}$ and $6.06 \times 10^{-6}$ have the same magnitude as $h^\ast$, and errors $3.11 \times 10^{-8}$ and $2.75 \times 10^{-11}$ stay below estimate $E(h^\ast)$.
At leftmost $h = 10^{-16}$, only $x_0 + h$ returns to 1; forward differences switch negative and plot error $0.17$, while central differences exclude it (1 exclusion).

At $\omega = 8$, $\lvert f''' \rvert / \lvert f \rvert$ grows and central $h^\ast$ shrinks to $1.64 \times 10^{-6}$.
Defaults do not know $\omega$ and remain $6.06 \times 10^{-6}$; central error $4.55 \times 10^{-10}$ becomes 4.5 times $E(h^\ast) = 1.00 \times 10^{-10}$.
At $x_0 = 3$, default steps triple to $1.82 \times 10^{-5}$ in proportion to $\max(1, \lvert x_0 \rvert)$.
But small $\lvert f \rvert = \lvert \sin 6 \rvert = 0.28$ and large $\lvert f''' \rvert$ shrink central $h^\ast$ to $2.30 \times 10^{-6}$, so default error $4.23 \times 10^{-10}$ is 21 times $E(h^\ast)$.
Since $\mathrm{ulp}(3) \approx 4.4 \times 10^{-16}$, the two smallest steps $10^{-16}$ and $1.8 \times 10^{-16}$ fit neither direction, excluding 2 points for each formula.

## Noisy functions

Values from truncated iterations, stochastic simulation, or measurements have errors far above rounding.
With absolute function-value error bound $\sigma$, replace $\varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert$ in the estimates by $\sigma$.
Central optimal steps and attainable errors then follow.

$$
h^\ast = \left( \frac{3 \sigma}{\lvert f''' \rvert} \right)^{1/3}, \qquad E(h^\ast) = \frac{3}{2} \left( \frac{\lvert f''' \rvert}{3} \right)^{1/3} \sigma^{2/3} .
$$

Defaults assume $\sigma \approx \varepsilon \lvert f \rvert$ and are too small for noisy functions, leaving error dominated by $\sigma / h$.
If noise and approximate $\lvert f''' \rvert$ are known, specify $h^\ast$ with `DifferenceStep::absolute`.
With unknown noise, adaptive differences comparing changing steps offer diagnostics ([Richardson extrapolation and Ridders' method](../richardson-and-ridders/)).
After 3 nonimproving levels, adaptive differences stop with `RoundoffLimit` and publish the best values and estimates so far.

The next cell differentiates $f(x) = e^x + \sigma \eta(x)$ centrally at $x_0 = 1$ and plots errors over steps $h = 10^{-12}$–$1$.
$\eta(x) \in [-1, 1)$ models noise through pseudorandom values from $x$ bits and seed, identical at identical points and independent at different points.
Since random-number crates follow this chapter, the cell uses splitmix64 mixing directly.
Since $\lvert \eta \rvert < 1$, noise error stays below $\sigma$ ($e^x$ rounding $u e \approx 3 \times 10^{-16}$ is negligible against $\sigma$).
Because $f'(1) = f'''(1) = e$, dashed estimates are $E(h) = e h^2/6 + \sigma/h$.
The table compares errors at defaults and $h^\ast$ with estimates $E(h)$ at the same steps.

```rust
use scioxide_differentiate::{DifferenceScheme, DifferenceStep, FiniteDifference};

    z = (z ^ (z >> 30)).wrapping_mul(0xBF58_476D_1CE4_E5B9);
    z = (z ^ (z >> 27)).wrapping_mul(0x94D0_49BB_1331_11EB);
    z ^= z >> 31;
    (z >> 11) as f64 / (1_u64 << 53) as f64 * 2.0 - 1.0
};

// Central error at step (None means default).

    if let Some(h) = step {
        method = method.with_step(DifferenceStep::absolute(h).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?);

    let result = method
        .derivative(
            |t: f64| Ok::<f64, SciOxideError>(RealScalar::exp(t) + sigma * noise(t)),

        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;

};

    let h = 10.0_f64.powf(-12.0 + 0.25 * k as f64);
    observed.push([h, error_at(Some(h))?]);
    theory.push([h, model(h)]);
}

let default_h = f64::EPSILON.cbrt();
let rows = vec![
    (
        "Default ε^(1/3)",
        default_h,
        error_at(None)?,
        model(default_h),
    ),
    (
        "Estimated h*",
        optimum,
        error_at(Some(optimum))?,
        model(optimum),
    ),
];

    { "key": "0", "label": "Step selection" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Step h [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Absolute derivative error [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Estimate E(h) = e h²/6 + σ/h [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);

    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "noisy" })
);

        "name": "Central error",
        "points": observed,
        "style": { "showSymbol": true }

    serde_json::json!({
        "name": "Estimate e h²/6 + σ/h",
```

At default $\sigma = 10^{-6}$, default step $6.06 \times 10^{-6}$ has error $6.42 \times 10^{-2}$, with estimate $0.165$ dominated by $\sigma/h$.

Estimates assume worst-case noise alignment; actual errors are often smaller.
At $\sigma = 10^{-3}$, the minimum shifts right to $h^\ast = 0.103$ and $E(h^\ast) = 1.45 \times 10^{-2}$.
At $\sigma = 10^{-12}$, it shifts left to $h^\ast = 1.03 \times 10^{-4}$ and $E(h^\ast) = 1.45 \times 10^{-8}$.
A factor $10^9$ change in $\sigma$ changes $E(h^\ast)$ by $10^6$, consistent with minimum error proportional to $\sigma^{2/3}$.
Even at $\sigma = 10^{-12}$, noise exceeds rounding ($u e \approx 3 \times 10^{-16}$), so default error $6.42 \times 10^{-8}$ is 11 times error $5.74 \times 10^{-9}$ at $h^\ast$.
Seed 7 changes individual errors ($1.14 \times 10^{-4}$ at $h^\ast$), leaving minimum position and depth unchanged.

## Differentiating table values

When functions are unavailable and only fixed-point tables exist, steps cannot be chosen freely.
The choice is which table points serve as nodes.
[`FiniteDifferenceStencil`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-differentiate/0.1.0/scioxide_differentiate/struct.FiniteDifferenceStencil.html)`::new(c, nodes, order)` constructs order-`order` derivative weights at `c` from selected nodes `nodes`; `apply(values)` returns physical-unit derivatives from node-ordered values.
[Difference-stencil weights and arbitrary nodes](oxref:differentiate-noise-amplification) explains weight construction and amplification through absolute sums.

When table values have errors within $\pm \sigma$, the value-error contribution to the error in an order-$m$ derivative is at most $\sigma A_w / s^m$.
Here $A_w$ sums absolute weights at normalized displacements $(x_j - c)/s$ and is returned by `weight_amplification()`; $s = \max_j \lvert x_j - c \rvert$ is the displacement scale from `scale()`.
Wider node spacing reduces weights but increases truncation.
Larger table errors therefore favor wider nodes.

For symmetric nodes of spacing $h$, first-derivative formulas and leading errors follow.

| Nodes | Formula | $A_w / s$ | Leading truncation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 nodes $c, c \pm h$ | $\dfrac{f(c + h) - f(c - h)}{2h}$ | $1/h$ | $\tfrac{1}{6} h^2 \lvert f''' \rvert$ |
| 5 nodes $c, c \pm h, c \pm 2h$ | $\dfrac{f(c - 2h) - 8 f(c - h) + 8 f(c + h) - f(c + 2h)}{12 h}$ | $3/(2h)$ | $\tfrac{1}{30} h^4 \lvert f^{(5)} \rvert$ |

The next cell tabulates $\sin x$ at spacing $\Delta = 0.001$, rounds to $d$ decimal places, and differentiates at $x_0 = 0.5$.
Nodes are three-point $x_0, x_0 \pm r \Delta$ and five-point $x_0, x_0 \pm r \Delta, x_0 \pm 2 r \Delta$; double $r$ from 1 through 64 to widen spacing $h = r\Delta$.
Rounding is at most $\sigma = 0.5 \times 10^{-d}$, and “Value-error bound” is $\sigma A_w / s$.
At $0 < x < \pi/2$, $\lvert f''' \rvert = \lvert f^{(5)} \rvert = \cos x$, so estimate leading terms at $x_0$.
Dashed curves add value-error bounds and leading truncation to estimate errors.
Exact $\cos 0.5$ and table values use f64 inherent methods.

```rust
let mut estimated = [Vec::new(), Vec::new()];
```

Rounded value errors are fixed per point, not independent random numbers, so spacing dependence is irregular and some spacings cancel accidentally.
For table derivatives, choose spacing from data-error magnitude and check stability under changed spacing.

## Summary

For function-value errors $\varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert$, difference rounding error grows as $1/h$ for first derivatives or $1/h^2$ for second derivatives, forming a U-shaped curve when added to truncation error.
Optimal steps scale as $\varepsilon_f^{1/(p + m)}$ and attainable errors as $\varepsilon_f^{p/(p + m)}$.
`FiniteDifference` defaults substitute $\varepsilon$ for $\varepsilon_f$ in that power rule; when $\lvert f \rvert / \lvert f^{(k)} \rvert$ is far from 1, estimating $h^\ast$ and specifying it through `DifferenceStep` reduces errors.
With noise $\sigma$, replace $\varepsilon_f \lvert f \rvert$ by $\sigma$ to choose steps; with unknown noise, examine adaptive estimates and termination.
For tables alone, select nodes through `FiniteDifferenceStencil`, estimate value-error amplification from absolute weights, and balance against truncation.
