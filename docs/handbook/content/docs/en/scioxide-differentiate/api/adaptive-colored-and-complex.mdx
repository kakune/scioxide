This table covers adaptive differences with shrinking steps and extrapolation, sparse-Jacobian coloring, and complex-step methods.
Only adaptive differences and coloring return error estimates, derived from extrapolation tables and rounding estimates; they are not upper bounds.
Complex-step methods return no error estimates; callers must ensure the assumed analytic extension.
The library checks only that the function value at the base point is real.
Every evaluation leaves borrowed output unchanged on errors or when output is not published; borrowed forms with sufficient workspace allocate no numerical storage.

| [`AdaptiveDifference`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-differentiate/0.1.0/scioxide_differentiate/struct.AdaptiveDifference.html) | Struct | Adaptive-difference method value with extrapolation error estimates | Holds settings only; defaults to `Ridders`, at most 16 levels, initial step $0.5 \max(1, \lvert x_i \rvert)$, tolerance $\tau_{\mathrm{abs}} = \tau_{\mathrm{rel}} = \sqrt{\varepsilon}$ (maximum norm), sequential execution, unlimited `SolveBudgets`, no workspace limit, and `MathematicalZero`. `with_method`, `with_tolerance`, `with_max_levels`, `with_step`, `with_execution`, `with_max_workspace_bytes`, `with_budgets`, `with_zero_direction`, `step()`. Element $D$ converges when estimated error is at most $\tau_{\mathrm{abs}} + \tau_{\mathrm{rel}} \lvert D \rvert$ for 2 consecutive levels and the selected best estimate also meets tolerance; uses at least 3 levels (absolute tolerance has derivative units). Settings are checked per operation; level counts outside 3–64 or norms other than maximum give `InvalidInput` (`self`, `OutOfRange`); componentwise absolute-tolerance length mismatching output gives `DimensionMismatch`. Owned forms `derivative(f, x)`, `second_derivative(f, x)`, `gradient(f, x, bounds)`, `hessian(f, x, bounds)`, `jacobian(f, x, m, bounds)`, and `jvp(f, x, v, m, bounds)`; borrowed forms `gradient_into(f, x, bounds, out, errors, workspace, scope)`, `hessian_into`, `jacobian_into`, and `jvp_into` also accept an error-estimate array. No `base`. Real and complex values. Each level uses second-order central differences; if the step does not fit both sides, uses same-order one-sided nodes in the direction with more room instead of shrinking to the bound. A change in node shape resets the extrapolation table. Directional derivatives select nodes by the same rule along a line. Evaluations comprise 1 base-point call plus 2 per element per level (3 for one-sided second derivatives; 4 for mixed Hessian terms with both axes symmetric, at most 8 with one-sided nodes). Initial directional step is $\min_i h_i / \lvert v_i \rvert$ ($h_i$ is coordinate $i$'s initial step). Termination reasons are `Converged`, `RoundoffLimit`, `RefinementLimit`, `StepSizeLimit`, limits, and external stops; if every element has an estimate and neither limits nor external stops intervene, values and estimates are published even without `Converged`. Estimates are not upper bounds. ## Complex steps

| Item | Kind | Role | Contract essentials | Explanation | Executable example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`ComplexStep`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-differentiate/0.1.0/scioxide_differentiate/struct.ComplexStep.html) | Struct | Method value for complex-step formula $\operatorname{Im} f(x + ih\mathbf{e}_i)/h$ | Function must be analytic extension $f: \mathbb{C}^n \to \mathbb{C}^m$ of a real function; only exact zero of the imaginary part at base point $x$ is checked (violation gives `InvalidInput` (`function`, `UnsupportedConfiguration`)). Conjugation, absolute value, extracting real parts, or branching on imaginary parts violates assumptions and may silently return wrong values. Defaults: step $h = \varepsilon$, sequential execution, unlimited `SolveBudgets`, no workspace limit, and `MathematicalZero`. `with_step`, `with_execution`, `with_budgets`, `with_max_workspace_bytes`, `with_zero_direction`. Steps are checked per operation; nonfinite steps give `NonFinite`; steps smaller than minimum normal `MIN_POSITIVE` give `InvalidInput` (`self`, `OutOfRange`). First derivatives only: owned forms `derivative(f, x)`, `gradient(f, x)`, `jacobian(f, x, m)`, and `jvp(f, x, v, m)`; borrowed forms `gradient_into`, `jacobian_into`, and `jvp_into`. No bounds. Real results without error estimates; ends with `Completed` unless limits or external stops intervene. Evaluation counts: first derivative 2; gradient and Jacobian $n + 1$; directional derivative $\operatorname{Im} f(x + ih\mathbf{v})/h$ takes 2. Overflow or rounding to 0 of $h v_i$ gives `InvalidInput` (`direction`, `UnsupportedConfiguration`); nonzero imaginary parts becoming subnormal or quotients after division by $h$ rounding to 0 give `NumericalInstability` (`Underflow`) without publishing. Exactly zero imaginary parts return derivative 0, being indistinguishable from true zero | [Imaginary perturbations](oxref:differentiate-complex-perturbation), [ComplexStep](oxref:differentiate-complex-step-api) | [Relative errors of real differences and complex steps versus step size](oxref:differentiate-complex-step-sweep), [Complex-step results for nonanalytic complex functions](oxref:differentiate-analytic-contract), [Apparent activation energy of the modified Arrhenius equation by four computations](oxref:differentiate-arrhenius-sensitivity) |
| [`ComplexStepPlan`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-differentiate/0.1.0/scioxide_differentiate/struct.ComplexStepPlan.html) | Struct | Plan computing fixed-point Hessians from real-node stencils and complex-step gradients | `new(point, nodes, method)` computes first-derivative Fornberg weights for each coordinate's nodes at construction ($O(k^2)$ for $k$ nodes). Step and `ZeroDirectionPolicy` errors match `ComplexStep` with argument `method`; mismatched node-sequence counts give `DimensionMismatch`; fewer than 2 nodes give `InvalidInput` (`nodes`, `OutOfRange`); duplicates give `InvalidInput` (`nodes`, `Inconsistent`). Element $(i, j)$ applies the axis-$j$ stencil to complex-step values of $\partial f / \partial x_i$ at axis-$j$ nodes; mixed terms average the two orders, giving exact symmetry. At most $1 + n\sum_j k_j$ evaluations. Accuracy follows real-stencil truncation and rounding, not `ComplexStep` accuracy. Second derivatives are the $n = 1$ case. `evaluate(f, bounds)` and `evaluate_into(f, bounds, out, workspace, scope)` take no evaluation point; out-of-bounds points or nodes give `InvalidInput` (`bounds`). `output_len()` is $n^2$ | [Real-node method values and Plans](oxref:differentiate-complex-node-plans), [Second derivatives and Hessians](oxref:differentiate-complex-second) | [Complex-step Hessian evaluation counts and symmetry](oxref:differentiate-complex-hessian-jvp) |
| [`RelativeComplexStep`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-differentiate/0.1.0/scioxide_differentiate/struct.RelativeComplexStep.html) | Struct | Complex-step Hessian node method relocating nodes from displacements at every evaluation | `new(offsets, method)` stores per-coordinate displacements, constructs nodes $x_i + \Delta$ per evaluation, and recomputes weights from rounded displacements without allocation. Fewer than 2 displacements give `InvalidInput` (`offsets`, `OutOfRange`); duplicates give `InvalidInput` (`offsets`, `Inconsistent`). Out-of-bounds $x$ gives `InvalidInput` (`x`, `OutOfRange`); nonfinite, out-of-bounds, or merged rounded nodes give `InvalidInput` (`x`, `UnsupportedConfiguration`). Accuracy, evaluation counts, and function assumptions match `ComplexStepPlan`; a univariate second derivative with $k$ nodes takes at most $1 + k$ calls. Provides `evaluate(f, x, bounds)` and `evaluate_into(f, x, bounds, out, workspace, scope)`; `output_len()` is $n^2$ | [Real-node method values and Plans](oxref:differentiate-complex-node-plans), [Second derivatives and Hessians](oxref:differentiate-complex-second) | [Errors of complex-step second derivatives with real nodes versus real second differences](oxref:differentiate-complex-second-derivative), [Complex-step Hessian evaluation counts and symmetry](oxref:differentiate-complex-hessian-jvp) |

The latter two borrow the Plan or method value. Contains `bytes()`. Exceeded limits give `ResourceLimit` | [Plans, requirements, and workspaces](oxref:differentiate-plans-requirements) | [Requirements and workspace reservation by family](oxref:differentiate-workspace-reuse) |
