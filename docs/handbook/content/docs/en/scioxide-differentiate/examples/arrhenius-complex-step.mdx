Temperature sensitivity of rate constant $k(T)$ compares experiments and models through apparent activation energy.
Rates span tens of orders with temperature, making logarithmic derivatives more meaningful than $k$ itself.
Treat the rate procedure as a black box and obtain derivatives by complex steps, central differences, and adaptive differences.
A temperature-dependent prefactor in modified Arrhenius makes apparent energy vary with temperature, with an independent analytic check.

## Modified Arrhenius and apparent activation energy

The modified equation includes temperature power $T^n$ in its prefactor.

$$
k(T) = A \, T^n \exp\!\left( -\frac{E_a}{R T} \right).
$$

Here $A$ is the prefactor, $n$ the temperature exponent, $E_a$ activation energy in J/mol, and $R$ the gas constant.
For a hypothetical first-order reaction, use $A = 10^{10}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}\,K^{-n}}$ and $R = 8.314462618\,\mathrm{J/(mol \cdot K)}$ (the leading 10 digits of SI-defined $N_A k_B$), varying $E_a$ and $n$ through inputs.
Over 250–1500 K, $k$ with $E_a = 200\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$ and $n = 0$ changes about 35 orders, from $1.6 \times 10^{-32}$ to $1.1 \times 10^{3}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.

Apparent energy is defined by the slope of an Arrhenius plot, $\ln k$ against $1/T$ (IUPAC Gold Book, activation energy).

$$
E_{a,\mathrm{app}}(T) = -R \frac{\mathrm{d} \ln k}{\mathrm{d}(1/T)} = R T^2 \frac{\mathrm{d} \ln k}{\mathrm{d}T} = R T^2 \frac{k'(T)}{k(T)}.
$$

Differentiating modified-Arrhenius $\ln k = \ln A + n \ln T - E_a/(R T)$ gives this analytic expression.

$$
E_{a,\mathrm{app}}(T) = E_a + n R T.
$$

For $n \ne 0$, apparent energy varies linearly with temperature and differs from input $E_a$.
Its independent derivation makes it a numerical-differentiation check.
If $k$ is exact, relative errors of $E_{a,\mathrm{app}}$ equal those of $k'$.

## Three methods and error estimates

For complex steps, write the $k$ procedure for complex inputs and compute $k'(T) = \operatorname{Im} k(T + ih)/h$ ([Complex-step differentiation and analytic extensions](../../algorithms/complex-step/)).
As a product of powers and exponentials, $k$ is holomorphic near the positive real axis and satisfies `ComplexStep`'s analytic-extension assumption.
`ComplexStep::default()` uses step $h = \varepsilon$ (double machine epsilon $2.2 \times 10^{-16}$), without cancellation because it avoids subtraction.
Relative truncation error $(h^2/6) \lvert k'''/k' \rvert$ is at most $10^{-30}$ here, leaving errors determined by rounding in the evaluation of $k$.
There are 2 evaluations, $k(T)$ and $k(T + ih)$.

Central differences use `derivative` of `FiniteDifference::default()` with default step $h = \varepsilon^{1/3} \max(1, T) = \varepsilon^{1/3} T$ and 3 calls.
Setting $g = \ln k$ gives $k''' / k' = g'^2 + 3 g'' + g'''/g'$ and the following leading relative central error.

$$
\frac{h^2}{6} \left\lvert \frac{k'''}{k'} \right\rvert = \frac{h^2}{6} \left\lvert g'^2 + 3 g'' + \frac{g'''}{g'} \right\rvert, \qquad g' = \frac{n}{T} + \frac{E_a}{R T^2} .
$$

For large $E_a/(RT)$, term $g'^2$ dominates and relative errors are approximately $\varepsilon^{2/3} (E_a/(RT) + n)^2 / 6$.
Larger $E_a$ and lower $T$ increase truncation.
Relative rounding is about $\varepsilon/(h g')$, over two orders below truncation for defaults.

Adaptive Ridders shrinks initial $0.5 \max(1, T)$ by 1.4 per level and extrapolates central differences ([Richardson extrapolation and Ridders' method](oxref:differentiate-stopping-rules)).
Two consecutive levels with estimated error at or below the threshold $\tau_{\mathrm{abs}} + \tau_{\mathrm{rel}} \lvert D \rvert$ ($D$ is the derivative estimate) give `Converged`, requiring at least 3 levels.
Calls total one $k(T)$ plus two per level, hence 7 at 3 levels.
`AdaptiveDifference::default()` tolerance is $\tau_{\mathrm{abs}} = \tau_{\mathrm{rel}} = \sqrt{\varepsilon} \approx 1.5 \times 10^{-8}$; absolute tolerance has derivative $k'$ units ($\mathrm{s^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$).
Low-temperature $k'$ is about $10^{-29}$, over 20 orders of magnitude below the absolute tolerance, so estimated errors far exceeding $k'$ still fall below the threshold.

The following block translates these formulas to shared Rust functions for two cells.
`rate` represents $k(T)$; `rate_complex` its analytic extension; `log_rate` represents $\ln k$; `apparent_energy` analytic $E_a + nRT$; `rate_slope` check $k'(T) = k \, E_{a,\mathrm{app}}/(R T^2)$; `central_truncation` leading central relative error.
Differentiated functions use SciOxide `RealScalar::exp`, `RealScalar::powf`, `RealScalar::ln`, and `Complex` operations; check `central_truncation` uses f64 operations.

```rust
//| id: differentiate-arrhenius-model
//| kind: definitions
//| title: "Modified Arrhenius rate and apparent activation energy"
//| crates: [scioxide-core]
use scioxide_core::{Complex, SciOxideError, RealScalar};

/// Prefactor A [s⁻¹ K⁻ⁿ].
pub(crate) const PREFACTOR: f64 = 1.0e10;
/// Gas constant R [J/(mol·K)].
pub(crate) const GAS_CONSTANT: f64 = 8.314462618;

/// Rate k(T) = A Tⁿ exp(−Ea/(RT)) [s⁻¹]; Ea in J/mol, T in K.
pub(crate) fn rate(ea: f64, n: f64, t: f64) -> f64 {
    PREFACTOR * RealScalar::powf(t, n) * RealScalar::exp(-ea / (GAS_CONSTANT * t))
}

/// Analytic k extension: principal zⁿ and exp(−Ea/(Rz)) are holomorphic near the positive real axis and real-valued on it.
pub(crate) fn rate_complex(ea: f64, n: f64, z: Complex<f64>) -> Result<Complex<f64>, SciOxideError> {
    let boltzmann = (Complex::new(-ea / GAS_CONSTANT, 0.0) / z).exp();
    Ok(z.powf(n) * boltzmann * PREFACTOR)
}

/// ln k(T) = ln A + n ln T − Ea/(RT), dimensionless.
pub(crate) fn log_rate(ea: f64, n: f64, t: f64) -> f64 {
    RealScalar::ln(PREFACTOR) + n * RealScalar::ln(t) - ea / (GAS_CONSTANT * t)
}

/// Analytic check Ea,app = Ea + nRT [J/mol].
pub(crate) fn apparent_energy(ea: f64, n: f64, t: f64) -> f64 {
    ea + n * GAS_CONSTANT * t
}

/// Check k′(T) = k Ea,app/(RT²) [s⁻¹ K⁻¹].
pub(crate) fn rate_slope(ea: f64, n: f64, t: f64) -> f64 {
    rate(ea, n, t) * apparent_energy(ea, n, t) / (GAS_CONSTANT * t * t)
}

/// Leading central relative error (h²/6)|g′² + 3g″ + g‴/g′|; h = ε^(1/3) T, g = ln k.
pub(crate) fn central_truncation(ea: f64, n: f64, t: f64) -> f64 {
    let h = f64::EPSILON.cbrt() * t;
    let g1 = n / t + ea / (GAS_CONSTANT * t * t);
    let g2 = -n / (t * t) - 2.0 * ea / (GAS_CONSTANT * t.powi(3));
    let g3 = 2.0 * n / t.powi(3) + 6.0 * ea / (GAS_CONSTANT * t.powi(4));
    h * h / 6.0 * (g1 * g1 + 3.0 * g2 + g3 / g1).abs()
}
```

## Relative errors over temperature

The next cell computes $E_{a,\mathrm{app}} = R T^2 k'/k$ four ways at 50 K increments from 250 to 1500 K, finding relative errors against analytic $E_a + nRT$.
The four are complex steps, central differences, default-tolerance adaptation, and adaptation with absolute tolerance 0 by passing `ErrorTolerance::scalar(0.0, √ε, NormSelection::Infinity)` to `with_tolerance`.
Compute $k$ of $k'/k$ with `rate`.
The table shows maximum relative errors, ranges of calls per point, termination, and exactly zero-error counts.
The final row gives the maximum leading central relative error $(h^2/6) \lvert k'''/k' \rvert$ computed above.
Pass temperaturewise errors and leading terms to the next cell, and $E_a$ and $n$ also to the following cell.

```rust
//| id: differentiate-arrhenius-sensitivity
//| ref: differentiate-arrhenius-sensitivity
//| title: "Apparent activation energy of the modified Arrhenius equation by four computations"
//| run: reactive
//| uses: [differentiate-arrhenius-model]
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-differentiate]
//| exports: [ea_kj, n, temperatures, errors, theory]
//| inputs:
//|   ea_kj: {"type":"range","label":"Activation energy Ea [kJ/mol]","min":50,"max":250,"step":25,"value":200}
//|   n: {"type":"range","label":"Temperature exponent n","min":-1.0,"max":2.0,"step":0.5,"value":1.5}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"low-barrier","label":"Ea = 50 kJ/mol, n = −1","description":"Low-temperature k′ grows to about 10⁻⁴ s⁻¹ K⁻¹, making default adaptive tolerance give correct values too.","inputs":{"ea_kj":50,"n":-1.0}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"max":1e-13}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/1/1","approx":{"value":5.484e-8,"rtol":0.02}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/2/1","range":{"min":1}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/2/4","equals":"Converged"}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/3/1","range":{"max":1e-11}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/4/1","approx":{"value":5.484e-8,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|   - case: low-barrier
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"max":1e-13}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/2/1","range":{"max":1e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/2/4","equals":"Converged"}
use scioxide_core::{ErrorTolerance, SciOxideError, NormSelection};
use scioxide_differentiate::{AdaptiveDifference, ComplexStep, FiniteDifference};

let ea = ea_kj * 1000.0;
// Set absolute tolerance to 0 and test only relative √ε.
let relative_only = ErrorTolerance::scalar(0.0, f64::EPSILON.sqrt(), NormSelection::Infinity)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let adaptive = AdaptiveDifference::default();
let relative = AdaptiveDifference::default().with_tolerance(relative_only);
let temperatures: Vec<f64> = (0..=25).map(|k| 250.0 + 50.0 * f64::from(k)).collect();
let mut errors: [Vec<f64>; 4] = Default::default();
let mut counts = [(usize::MAX, 0_usize); 4];
let mut terminations: [Vec<String>; 4] = Default::default();
for &t in &temperatures {
    let real = |s: f64| Ok::<_, SciOxideError>(rate(ea, n, s));
    let results = [
        ComplexStep::default().derivative(|z| rate_complex(ea, n, z), t),
        FiniteDifference::default().derivative(real, t),
        adaptive.derivative(real, t),
        relative.derivative(real, t),
    ];
    let exact = apparent_energy(ea, n, t);
    for (i, result) in results.into_iter().enumerate() {
        let result = result.map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
        let slope = result.values().ok_or("Derivative not published")?[0];
        // Relative error of Ea,app = R T² k′/k against Ea + nRT.
        let apparent = GAS_CONSTANT * t * t * slope / rate(ea, n, t);
        errors[i].push(((apparent - exact) / exact).abs());
        let evaluations = result.report().evaluations();
        counts[i] = (counts[i].0.min(evaluations), counts[i].1.max(evaluations));
        let name = result.report().termination().to_string();
        if !terminations[i].contains(&name) {
            terminations[i].push(name);
        }
    }
}
// Leading central relative error, theoretical.
let theory: Vec<f64> = temperatures
    .iter()
    .map(|&t| central_truncation(ea, n, t))
    .collect();
let largest = |values: &[f64]| values.iter().fold(0.0_f64, |m, v| m.max(*v));
let labels = [
    "Complex-step method",
    "Central difference",
    "Adaptive, default tolerance",
    "Adaptive, zero absolute tolerance",
];
// (Computation, maximum relative error, minimum calls, maximum calls, termination, zero-error points)
let mut rows: Vec<_> = (0..4)
    .map(|i| {
        (
            labels[i],
            largest(&errors[i]),
            Some(counts[i].0),
            Some(counts[i].1),
            Some(terminations[i].join(", ")),
            Some(errors[i].iter().filter(|e| **e == 0.0).count()),
        )
    })
    .collect();
rows.push((
    "Leading central truncation, theoretical",
    largest(&theory),
    None,
    None,
    None,
    None,
));
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Computation" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Maximum relative error [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Minimum calls per point [count]", "type": "integer", "nullText": "—" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Maximum calls per point [count]", "type": "integer", "nullText": "—" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Termination reason", "nullText": "—" },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Zero-relative-error points [count]", "type": "integer", "nullText": "—" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "summary" })
);
export!(ea_kj, &ea_kj);
export!(n, &n);
export!(temperatures, &temperatures);
export!(errors, &errors);
export!(theory, &theory);
```

At defaults $E_a = 200\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$ and $n = 1.5$, apparent energy varies from 203.118 kJ/mol at 250 K to 218.708 kJ/mol at 1500 K.
Complex-step relative errors are at most $1.5 \times 10^{-14}$ at all temperatures with 2 calls per point, and are exactly zero at 3 of the 26 points.
Central differences take 3 calls and have maximum relative error $5.5 \times 10^{-8}$ at 250 K, matching leading maximum $5.5 \times 10^{-8}$.
Default adaptation exceeds relative error 1 at five points at or below 450 K, reaching order $10^{9}$ at 250 K, yet returns `Converged` everywhere.
Adaptive differences with zero absolute tolerance take 19–31 calls and keep relative errors at or below $1.1 \times 10^{-13}$.

At $E_a = 50\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$ and $n = -1$, 250 K $k'$ grows to about $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$, and default adaptive relative errors stay around $10^{-8}$.
Default problems depend on derivative magnitude relative to absolute tolerance.

## Absolute tolerance and error estimates

Compare estimates and thresholds at one temperature to explain wrong-value `Converged` under defaults.
The next cell receives $E_a$ and $n$ from the temperature-sweep producer and compares three adaptive calculations at selected $T$.
Rows 1 and 2 differentiate $k(T)$ with default tolerances and zero absolute tolerance, respectively.
Row 3 differentiates dimensionless $\ln k(T)$ with defaults to obtain $E_{a,\mathrm{app}} = R T^2 \, \mathrm{d} \ln k / \mathrm{d}T$ directly.
$\mathrm{d} \ln k / \mathrm{d}T$ is 0.39 K⁻¹ at 250 K and 0.012 K⁻¹ at 1500 K, so absolute $\sqrt{\varepsilon}$ is small relative to derivatives throughout.
The table shows derivative estimate $D$, estimated error, threshold $\tau_{\mathrm{abs}} + \tau_{\mathrm{rel}} \lvert D \rvert$, actual error against the analytic value, $E_{a,\mathrm{app}}$ and its relative error, calls, and termination.
$D$ units are $\mathrm{s^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ for rows 1–2 and $\mathrm{K^{-1}}$ for row 3.

```rust
//| id: differentiate-arrhenius-tolerance
//| ref: differentiate-arrhenius-tolerance
//| title: "Adaptive absolute tolerances and error estimates"
//| run: reactive
//| uses: [differentiate-arrhenius-model]
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-differentiate]
//| dependsOn:
//|   model: differentiate-arrhenius-sensitivity
//| inputs:
//|   temperature: {"type":"range","label":"Temperature T [K]","description":"Derivative temperature; lower temperatures give smaller k′.","min":250,"max":1500,"step":50,"value":250}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"high-temperature","label":"T = 1500 K","description":"k′ exceeds 10⁵ s⁻¹ K⁻¹, giving correct values even with defaults.","inputs":{"temperature":1500}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/0/8","equals":"Converged"}
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"max":1.5e-8}}
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/0/6","range":{"min":1}}
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/1/6","range":{"max":1e-11}}
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/2/5","approx":{"value":203.118,"atol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/2/6","range":{"max":1e-8}}
//|   - case: high-temperature
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/0/5","approx":{"value":218.708,"atol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/0/6","range":{"max":1e-8}}

use scioxide_differentiate::AdaptiveDifference;

let ea = model["ea_kj"].as_f64().ok_or("No activation energy")? * 1000.0;
let n = model["n"].as_f64().ok_or("No temperature exponent")?;

let root_eps = f64::EPSILON.sqrt();
let relative_only = ErrorTolerance::scalar(0.0, root_eps, NormSelection::Infinity)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let exact = apparent_energy(ea, n, t);
let mut rows = Vec::new();
for (name, logarithm, atol) in [
    ("k(T), default tolerance", false, root_eps),
    ("k(T), zero absolute tolerance", false, 0.0),
    ("ln k(T), default tolerance", true, root_eps),

    let method = if atol > 0.0 {
        AdaptiveDifference::default()
    } else {
        AdaptiveDifference::default().with_tolerance(relative_only.clone())
    };

        method.derivative(|s: f64| Ok::<_, SciOxideError>(log_rate(ea, n, s)), t)
    } else {

    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;

    let estimate = result.errors().ok_or("Error estimate not published")?[0];

        (exact / (GAS_CONSTANT * t * t), GAS_CONSTANT * t * t * value)
    } else {
        (
            rate_slope(ea, n, t),
            GAS_CONSTANT * t * t * value / rate(ea, n, t),
        )
    };
    rows.push((
        name,
        value,
        estimate,
        atol + root_eps * value.abs(),
        (value - reference).abs(),

        ((apparent - exact) / exact).abs(),

        result.report().termination().to_string(),
```

Default adaptation returns $D = -5.9 \times 10^{-20}$ after 7 calls, reaching `Converged` with even the wrong sign.
Estimate $1.2 \times 10^{-19}$ is about twice actual error $5.9 \times 10^{-20}$ and correctly indicates its scale.
Yet it lies far below threshold $\sqrt{\varepsilon} \approx 1.5 \times 10^{-8}$, passing two consecutive levels.
The cause is mismatched absolute tolerance, not the estimate itself.
Zero absolute tolerance shrinks threshold to $\sqrt{\varepsilon} \lvert D \rvert$; 31 calls then give relative error $10^{-13}$.
Differentiating $\ln k$ obtains $E_{a,\mathrm{app}} = 203.118\,\mathrm{kJ/mol}$ in 17 calls with defaults.
At 1500 K, large $k'$ of $7.4 \times 10^{5}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ makes absolute tolerance negligible, so all three settings are correct.

## Summary

The apparent activation energy $R T^2 \, \mathrm{d}\ln k / \mathrm{d}T$ of the modified Arrhenius equation equals $E_a + nRT$ and varies with temperature, enabling an independent check of numerical derivatives.
Complex implementation of the rate lets complex steps return derivatives with relative error around $10^{-14}$ in 2 calls.
Real central relative errors follow leading $(h^2/6) \lvert k'''/k' \rvert$, increasing with $E_a/(RT)$.
Default absolute adaptive tolerance $\sqrt{\varepsilon}$ has derivative units; for tiny quantities such as $10^{-29}$ it fails as a useful threshold, permitting wrong `Converged` results.
For quantities spanning orders, differentiate order-one transformations such as logarithms, or match absolute tolerance to scale or set it to zero, and check results independently of estimates.
