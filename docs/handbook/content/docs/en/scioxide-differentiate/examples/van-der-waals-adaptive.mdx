Pressure volume derivative $(\partial p / \partial V)_T$ is used for isothermal compressibility $\kappa_T = -1/(V (\partial p / \partial V)_T)$ and sound speed, vanishing at criticality.
Analytic van der Waals derivatives independently check numerical values and estimates.
Differentiate CO2 with adaptive `AdaptiveDifference` to examine variable scales and defaults, near-critical relative errors, and reliability of estimates and termination.
Adaptive componentwise estimates are not upper bounds ([Richardson extrapolation and Ridders' method](../../algorithms/richardson-and-ridders/)).

Liquid–gas coexistence is primarily covered by [van der Waals gas and Maxwell's equal-area rule](../../../scioxide-rootfind/examples/van-der-waals-maxwell/); implicit root sensitivities by [Implicit sensitivity of the van der Waals equation](../../../scioxide-ad/examples/van-der-waals-implicit/).
CO2 critical values match those pages.

## Model and critical point

Pressure $p$ [Pa] at molar volume $V$ [m³/mol] and temperature $T$ [K] follows.

$$
p(V, T) = \frac{R T}{V - b} - \frac{a}{V^2}, \qquad V > b.
$$

Constants $a$ [Pa·m⁶/mol²] and $b$ [m³/mol] represent attraction and excluded volume; $R = 8.314462618\,\mathrm{J/(mol \cdot K)}$ is the gas constant.
First and second volume derivatives follow.

$$
\left( \frac{\partial p}{\partial V} \right)_T = -\frac{R T}{(V - b)^2} + \frac{2a}{V^3},
\qquad
\left( \frac{\partial^2 p}{\partial V^2} \right)_T = \frac{2 R T}{(V - b)^3} - \frac{6a}{V^4}.
$$

Criticality is a horizontal isotherm inflection where both vanish.
Taking the ratio of conditions $RT/(V - b)^2 = 2a/V^3$ and $2RT/(V - b)^3 = 6a/V^4$ gives $(V - b)/2 = V/3$ and critical values.

$$
V_c = 3b, \qquad T_c = \frac{8a}{27 R b}, \qquad p_c = \frac{a}{27 b^2}.
$$

Solving inversely gives $a = 27 R^2 T_c^2 / (64 p_c)$ and $b = R T_c / (8 p_c)$.
CO2 critical $T_c = 304.13\,\mathrm{K}$ and $p_c = 7.3773\,\mathrm{MPa}$ give $a = 0.36566\,\mathrm{Pa \cdot m^6/mol^2}$, $b = 4.2846 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m^3/mol}$, and $V_c = 1.2854 \times 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3/mol}$.

Reduced variables $V_r = V/V_c$, $T_r = T/T_c$, and $p_r = p/p_c$ remove substance dependence.

$$
p_r = \frac{8 T_r}{3 V_r - 1} - \frac{3}{V_r^2},
\qquad
\frac{\partial p_r}{\partial V_r} = -\frac{24 T_r}{(3 V_r - 1)^2} + \frac{6}{V_r^3}.
$$

Convert derivatives back through $\partial p / \partial V = (p_c / V_c)\, \partial p_r / \partial V_r$ and $\partial^2 p / \partial V^2 = (p_c / V_c^2)\, \partial^2 p_r / \partial V_r^2$.
At critical volume $V_r = 1$, using temperature offset $\tau = T_r - 1$ gives

$$
\frac{\partial p_r}{\partial V_r} = -6\tau,
\qquad
\frac{\partial^2 p_r}{\partial V_r^2} = 18\tau,
\qquad
\frac{\partial^3 p_r}{\partial V_r^3} = -9 - 81\tau
$$


The first derivative vanishes proportionally to $\tau$, while compressibility $\kappa_T = 1/(6 \tau p_c)$ diverges as $1/\tau$.

The following model is shared by three cells.
`pressure` represents $p(V, T)$; `reduced_pressure` represents $p_r(V_r, T_r)$; `exact_derivatives` gives analytic first and second derivatives used only for checks.

```rust
//| id: differentiate-vdw-model
//| kind: definitions
//| title: "CO2 van der Waals equation and analytic derivatives"
//| crates: [scioxide-core]
/// Gas R [J/(mol·K)], CO2 critical T_c [K] and p_c [Pa].
pub(crate) const GAS_CONSTANT: f64 = 8.314462618;
pub(crate) const CRITICAL_TEMPERATURE: f64 = 304.13;
pub(crate) const CRITICAL_PRESSURE: f64 = 7.3773e6;

/// Critical-value-derived (a [Pa·m⁶/mol²], b [m³/mol]).
pub(crate) fn coefficients() -> (f64, f64) {
    let (r, tc, pc) = (GAS_CONSTANT, CRITICAL_TEMPERATURE, CRITICAL_PRESSURE);
    (27.0 * r * r * tc * tc / (64.0 * pc), r * tc / (8.0 * pc))
}

/// Pressure p(V, T) = RT/(V − b) − a/V² [Pa].
pub(crate) fn pressure(temp: f64, volume: f64) -> f64 {
    let (a, b) = coefficients();
    GAS_CONSTANT * temp / (volume - b) - a / (volume * volume)
}

/// Reduced p_r = 8T_r/(3V_r − 1) − 3/V_r².
pub(crate) fn reduced_pressure(reduced_temp: f64, reduced_volume: f64) -> f64 {
    8.0 * reduced_temp / (3.0 * reduced_volume - 1.0) - 3.0 / (reduced_volume * reduced_volume)
}

/// Reference: analytic ∂p/∂V [Pa·mol/m³] and ∂²p/∂V² [Pa·mol²/m⁶].
pub(crate) fn exact_derivatives(temp: f64, volume: f64) -> [f64; 2] {
    let (a, b) = coefficients();
    let rt = GAS_CONSTANT * temp;
    [
        -rt / (volume - b).powi(2) + 2.0 * a / volume.powi(3),
        2.0 * rt / (volume - b).powi(3) - 6.0 * a / volume.powi(4),
    ]
}
```

## Default steps in physical units

`AdaptiveDifference` computes central differences at shrinking $h_0, h_0/1.4, h_0/1.4^2, \ldots$ and extrapolates to zero step with Ridders.
Each level adds two calls, with one initial $f(x)$.
Two consecutive levels with both current and best error estimates below $\mathrm{atol} + \mathrm{rtol} \lvert D \rvert$ give `Converged`; three levels without improvement in the best error estimate give `RoundoffLimit`; exhausting the level limit (default 16) gives `RefinementLimit`.
Initial default step is $h_0 = 0.5 \max(1, \lvert x \rvert)$ ([Step and stopping rules](oxref:differentiate-stopping-rules)).

For physical $V \approx 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3/mol}$, $h_0$ is $0.5\,\mathrm{m^3/mol}$.
This exceeds pole distance $V - b \approx 10^{-4}$ to $V = b$ by over three orders, crossing poles $V = b$ and $V = 0$.
Compare two remedies.
Specify an initial step below pole distance through `with_step(DifferenceStep::absolute(h0))`, here $h_0 = 0.1 (V - b)$.
Alternatively use reduced variables of order 1 with defaults.
In reduced variables, the pole is at $V_r = 1/3$; at $V_r = 1.4$, default $h_0 = 0.7$ is 0.66 times the pole distance $1.07$.

The next cell computes first and second derivatives at selected temperature and volume under three settings, comparing analytic values.
“$h_0$ ÷ pole distance” divides initial step by $V - b$, or reduced $V_r - 1/3$.
Both actual relative errors and estimates divided by analytic magnitudes are shown.

```rust
//| id: differentiate-vdw-isotherm
//| ref: differentiate-vdw-isotherm
//| title: "Adaptive volume differences of the van der Waals equation with three settings"
//| run: reactive
//| uses: [differentiate-vdw-model]
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-differentiate]
//| inputs:
//|   temp: {"type":"range","label":"Temperature T [K]","min":280,"max":340,"step":5,"value":310}
//|   volume_r: {"type":"range","label":"Reduced volume V/V_c [-]","min":0.6,"max":3.0,"step":0.2,"value":1.4}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"dilute","label":"V/V_c = 3.0","description":"Farther from poles, reduced first derivatives reach Converged, while physical default steps still fail.","inputs":{"volume_r":3.0}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":"RoundoffLimit"}
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/0/5","range":{"min":0.5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/0/6","range":{"max":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/2/2","equals":"Converged"}
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/2/5","range":{"max":1e-11}}
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/3/2","equals":"Converged"}
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/3/5","range":{"max":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/4/2","equals":"RoundoffLimit"}
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/4/5","range":{"min":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/5/2","equals":"Converged"}
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/5/5","range":{"max":1e-9}}
//|   - case: dilute
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":"RoundoffLimit"}
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/4/2","equals":"Converged"}
//|       - {"artifact":"isotherm","path":"/rows/4/5","range":{"max":1e-9}}
use scioxide_differentiate::{AdaptiveDifference, DifferenceStep};

let (_, b) = coefficients();
let vc = 3.0 * b;
let volume = volume_r * vc;
let reduced_temp = temp / CRITICAL_TEMPERATURE;
let exact = exact_derivatives(temp, volume);
let explicit = DifferenceStep::absolute(0.1 * (volume - b)).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
// (Setting name, method, reduced variables?, initial step ÷ pole distance)
let configs = [
    (
        "Physical units, defaults",
        AdaptiveDifference::default(),
        false,
        0.5 / (volume - b),
    ),
    (
        "Physical units, step 0.1 (V − b)",
        AdaptiveDifference::default().with_step(explicit),
        false,
        0.1,
    ),
    (
        "Reduced variables, defaults",
        AdaptiveDifference::default(),
        true,
        0.5 * volume_r.max(1.0) / (volume_r - 1.0 / 3.0),
    ),
];
let mut rows = Vec::new();
for (name, method, reduced, step_ratio) in configs {
    for (k, label) in ["∂p/∂V", "∂²p/∂V²"].into_iter().enumerate() {
        // Differentiate in V_r and multiply by p_c / V_c^(k+1) to restore physical units.
        let (point, scale) = if reduced {
            (volume_r, CRITICAL_PRESSURE / vc.powi(k as i32 + 1))
        } else {
            (volume, 1.0)
        };
        let f = |v: f64| {
            Ok(if reduced {
                reduced_pressure(reduced_temp, v)
            } else {
                pressure(temp, v)
            })
        };
        let result = if k == 0 {
            method.derivative(f, point)
        } else {
            method.second_derivative(f, point)
        }
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
        let value = scale * result.values().ok_or("Derivative not published")?[0];
        let estimate = scale * result.errors().ok_or("Error estimate not published")?[0];
        rows.push((
            name,
            label,
            result.report().termination().to_string(),
            result.report().evaluations(),
            step_ratio,
            ((value - exact[k]) / exact[k]).abs(),
            estimate / exact[k].abs(),
        ));
    }
}
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Setting" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Derivative" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Termination reason" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Evaluations [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "h₀ ÷ pole distance [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Relative error [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "6", "label": "Estimated error ÷ |analytic| [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "isotherm" })
);
```

At defaults $T = 310\,\mathrm{K}$ and $V = 1.4 V_c$, physical default results have relative error 1 and are unusable.
Error estimates are only $10^{-6}$ times the analytic derivative values, understating actual errors by six orders of magnitude.
Table differences produce estimates; when every entry is meaningless from crossing poles, estimates are meaningless too.
Only termination `RoundoffLimit` signals the problem, after 11 calls and 5 levels.

Explicit $0.1(V - b)$ gives `Converged` for both orders, with relative errors about $10^{-13}$ first and $10^{-11}$ second, and estimates above actual errors.
Reduced defaults converge at second order, but first derivatives stop with `RoundoffLimit` in 13 calls, 6 levels.
Initial $0.7$ is 0.66 times pole distance, giving large early truncation before the table enters asymptotic $h \to 0$ behavior.
The best early estimate fails to improve for three levels, triggering `RoundoffLimit` without rounding dominance.
Relative error $6.6 \times 10^{-2}$ then greatly exceeds estimate $1.8 \times 10^{-4}$.
This depends on the point: the same $V/V_c = 1.4$ at 280 or 340 K converges in 19 calls.
At $V/V_c = 3$, reduced first derivatives also converge in 15 calls ($h_0$ ÷ pole distance 0.56); physical defaults fail throughout the input range because $h_0 = 0.5\,\mathrm{m^3/mol}$ crosses poles.
Order-one scaling alone may be insufficient; choose initial steps well below nearest-singularity distance and confirm termination `Converged`.

## Approaching criticality

At criticality $\partial p / \partial V = 0$, so constant absolute errors cause relative growth $1/\tau$.
At $V = V_c$, the next cell approaches critical temperature through $T_c (1 + \tau)$ and $\tau = 10^{-1}$–$10^{-7}$, computing $\partial p_r / \partial V_r$ in reduced variables.
The chart plots actual relative errors and relative estimates; dashes show convergence threshold $\mathrm{atol} + \mathrm{rtol} \lvert D \rvert$ divided by $\lvert D \rvert = 6\tau$.
Choose default tolerances $\mathrm{atol} = \mathrm{rtol} = \sqrt{\varepsilon}$ or relative-only tolerances $\mathrm{atol} = 0$ and $\mathrm{rtol} = \sqrt{\varepsilon}$.
The table lists decade values and analytic compressibility $\kappa_T = 1/(6 \tau p_c)$.

```rust
//| id: differentiate-vdw-critical
//| ref: differentiate-vdw-critical
//| title: "Adaptive relative errors and estimates approaching criticality"
//| run: reactive
//| uses: [differentiate-vdw-model]
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-differentiate]
//| inputs:
//|   tolerance: {"type":"select","label":"Tolerance","options":["atol = rtol = √ε","atol = 0, rtol = √ε"],"value":"atol = rtol = √ε"}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"relative","label":"atol = 0","description":"Relative √ε demands prevent near-critical convergence and give RoundoffLimit.","inputs":{"tolerance":"atol = 0, rtol = √ε"}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"critical","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":"Converged"}
//|       - {"artifact":"critical","path":"/rows/0/3","range":{"max":1e-11}}
//|       - {"artifact":"critical","path":"/rows/6/1","equals":"Converged"}
//|       - {"artifact":"critical","path":"/rows/6/3","range":{"max":1e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"critical","path":"/rows/6/5","approx":{"value":2.25920e-1,"rtol":1e-4}}
//|   - case: relative
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"critical","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":"Converged"}
//|       - {"artifact":"critical","path":"/rows/4/1","equals":"Converged"}
//|       - {"artifact":"critical","path":"/rows/5/1","equals":"RoundoffLimit"}
//|       - {"artifact":"critical","path":"/rows/6/1","equals":"RoundoffLimit"}
//|       - {"artifact":"critical","path":"/rows/6/3","range":{"max":1e-6}}
use scioxide_core::{ErrorTolerance, NormSelection, RealScalar};
use scioxide_differentiate::AdaptiveDifference;

let root = <f64 as RealScalar>::SQRT_EPSILON;
let absolute = if tolerance.starts_with("atol = 0") {
    0.0
} else {
    root
};
let tolerance = ErrorTolerance::scalar(absolute, root, NormSelection::Infinity)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let method = AdaptiveDifference::default().with_tolerance(tolerance);
let (mut actual, mut estimated, mut threshold, mut rows) =
    (Vec::new(), Vec::new(), Vec::new(), Vec::new());
for k in 0..=12 {
    let tau = 10.0_f64.powf(-1.0 - 0.5 * f64::from(k));
    let result = method
        .derivative(|v: f64| Ok(reduced_pressure(1.0 + tau, v)), 1.0)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    // Reference at V_r = 1: ∂p_r/∂V_r = −6τ.
    let exact = -6.0 * tau;
    let value = result.values().ok_or("Derivative not published")?[0];
    let estimate = result.errors().ok_or("Error estimate not published")?[0] / exact.abs();
    let error = ((value - exact) / exact).abs();
    // Exclude zero errors from logarithmic axes.
    if error > 0.0 {
        actual.push([tau, error]);
    }
    estimated.push([tau, estimate]);
    threshold.push([tau, (absolute + root * exact.abs()) / exact.abs()]);
    if k % 2 == 0 {
        let termination = result.report().termination().to_string();
        let compressibility = 1.0 / (6.0 * tau * CRITICAL_PRESSURE);
        let evaluations = result.report().evaluations();
        rows.push((
            tau,
            termination,
            evaluations,
            error,
            estimate,
            compressibility,
        ));
    }
}
emit_chart!(
    serde_json::json!({
        "kind": "line",
        "dataZoom": true,
        "tooltip": true,
        "series": [
            { "name": "Actual relative error", "points": actual, "style": { "showSymbol": true } },
            { "name": "Estimate ÷ |analytic|", "points": estimated, "style": { "symbol": "triangle" } },
            { "name": "Threshold ÷ |analytic|", "points": threshold, "style": { "lineType": "dashed" } }
        ],
        "xLabel": "Critical-temperature offset τ [-]",
        "yLabel": "∂p/∂V relative error [-]",
        "xType": "log",
        "yType": "log",
        "yMin": 1e-16,
        "yMax": 1.0
    }),
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "approach" })
);
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "τ = T/T_c − 1 [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Termination reason" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Evaluations [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Relative error [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Estimated error ÷ |analytic| [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Analytic κ_T [1/Pa]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "critical" })
);
```

With defaults, all seven table points reach `Converged` in 21 calls.
Absolute errors remain below $8 \times 10^{-14}$ independently of $\tau$, while relative errors rise irregularly roughly as $1/\tau$ from $1.3 \times 10^{-13}$ at $\tau = 10^{-1}$ to $5.5 \times 10^{-8}$ at $\tau = 10^{-7}$.
Estimates exceed errors 54–5000 times throughout; relative estimates grow roughly as $1/\tau$ from $7.1 \times 10^{-12}$ to $6.2 \times 10^{-6}$.

Relative estimates above $\sqrt{\varepsilon} = 1.5 \times 10^{-8}$ still give `Converged` because absolute estimates remain below $\sqrt{\varepsilon}(1 + 6\tau)$.
The threshold approaches $\sqrt{\varepsilon}$ as $\tau \to 0$, making the relative dashed curve grow as $1/\tau$; default tolerances do not guarantee relative accuracy.

Selecting $\mathrm{atol} = 0$ gives threshold $\sqrt{\varepsilon} \cdot 6\tau$, a horizontal relative dashed line.
From $\tau = 10^{-2}$ through $10^{-5}$, 2–4 extra calls yield `Converged`; at $\tau \le 10^{-6}$ thresholds fall below rounding and cannot be met, stopping with `RoundoffLimit` in 29 calls.
The best estimates returned have relative errors $10^{-8}$–$10^{-7}$, comparable to or larger than errors from default tolerances with `Converged`.
Tighter tolerances cannot beat rounding; treat `RoundoffLimit` as failure to reach requested relative accuracy.
When relative accuracy matters, inspect relative estimates even under defaults.

## Noisy pressure

Measured or truncated-iteration pressure adds errors far above rounding.
The next cell adds noise $\sigma / p_c$ to reduced pressure, computes $\partial p_r / \partial V_r$ once at $V_r = 1$, and emits termination as its conclusion.
Noise uses pseudorandom $[-1, 1)$ from point bits and seed, identical at identical points and independent elsewhere ([Noisy functions](oxref:differentiate-noisy-functions)).
For noise magnitude $\delta$, central attainable errors are about $E^\ast = \tfrac{3}{2} (\lvert f''' \rvert / 3)^{1/3} \delta^{2/3}$.
Here $\delta = \sigma / p_c$ and $f''' = \partial^3 p_r / \partial V_r^3 = -9 - 81\tau$; the table also divides $E^\ast$ by analytic magnitude.
Outcome is satisfied when `is_success()` is true, otherwise partial with published best estimates.

```rust
//| id: differentiate-vdw-noise
//| ref: differentiate-vdw-noise
//| title: "Volume derivatives of noisy pressure and adaptive termination"
//| run: reactive
//| uses: [differentiate-vdw-model]
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-differentiate]
//| inputs:
//|   noise: {"type":"select","label":"Pressure noise σ [Pa]","options":["0","10","1000"],"value":"10"}
//|   tau: {"type":"select","label":"Critical-temperature offset τ [-]","options":["1e-1","1e-3"],"value":"1e-1"}
//|   seed: {"type":"range","label":"Noise seed","min":0,"max":15,"step":1,"value":7}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"smooth","label":"σ = 0","description":"Without noise, Converged estimates exceed actual errors.","inputs":{"noise":"0"}}
//|   - {"id":"loud","label":"σ = 1000 Pa","description":"Seed 7 reaches the level limit with estimates only 1/100 of actual errors.","inputs":{"noise":"1000"}}
//|   - {"id":"near-critical","label":"τ = 10⁻³","description":"A smaller derivative makes the same noise produce 100 times the relative error.","inputs":{"tau":"1e-3"}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"noise","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"RoundoffLimit"}
//|       - {"artifact":"noise","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"max":5.47e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"noise","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":5.46974e-4,"rtol":1e-5}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"partial"}
//|   - case: smooth
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"noise","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"Converged"}
//|       - {"artifact":"noise","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"max":1e-11}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: loud
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"noise","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"RefinementLimit"}
//|       - {"artifact":"noise","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":33}
//|       - {"artifact":"noise","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"min":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"noise","path":"/rows/0/3","range":{"max":1e-3}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"partial"}
//|   - case: near-critical
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"noise","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"min":1e-4,"max":4.43e-2}}
//|       - {"artifact":"noise","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":4.42941e-2,"rtol":1e-5}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"partial"}
use scioxide_differentiate::AdaptiveDifference;

let sigma: f64 = noise.parse().map_err(|_| "Cannot read noise magnitude")?;
let tau: f64 = tau.parse().map_err(|_| "Cannot read τ")?;
let delta = sigma / CRITICAL_PRESSURE;
// Noise model: mix point bits and seed with splitmix64 into [-1, 1).
let eta = |v: f64| {
    let mut z = v.to_bits() ^ (seed as u64).wrapping_mul(0x9E37_79B9_7F4A_7C15);
    z = (z ^ (z >> 30)).wrapping_mul(0xBF58_476D_1CE4_E5B9);
    z = (z ^ (z >> 27)).wrapping_mul(0x94D0_49BB_1331_11EB);
    z ^= z >> 31;
    (z >> 11) as f64 / (1_u64 << 53) as f64 * 2.0 - 1.0
};
let result = AdaptiveDifference::default()
    .derivative(
        |v: f64| Ok(reduced_pressure(1.0 + tau, v) + delta * eta(v)),
        1.0,
    )
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let report = *result.report();
// Reference at V_r = 1: ∂p_r/∂V_r = −6τ, and central guideline E* under noise δ.
let exact = -6.0 * tau;
let best = 1.5 * ((9.0 + 81.0 * tau) / 3.0).cbrt() * delta.powf(2.0 / 3.0);
let value = result.values().ok_or("Derivative not published")?[0];
let error = ((value - exact) / exact).abs();
let estimate = result.errors().ok_or("Error estimate not published")?[0] / exact.abs();
let termination = report.termination().to_string();
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Termination reason" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Evaluations [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Relative error [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Estimated error ÷ |analytic| [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "4", "label": "E* ÷ |analytic| [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);
let rows = vec![(
    termination.clone(),
    report.evaluations(),
    error,
    estimate,
    best / exact.abs(),
)];
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "noise" })
);
let outcome = if report.is_success() {
    Outcome::satisfied("Estimated error met tolerance")
} else {
    Outcome::partial("Extrapolation stopped before tolerance")
        .explanation("Values and estimates are published as best estimates; estimates are not bounds, so compare with noise-based error estimates.")
};
emit_outcome!(
    outcome
        .status(termination)
        .diagnostic("Evaluations", report.evaluations() as f64, None)
        .diagnostic("Relative error", error, None)
        .diagnostic("Estimate ÷ |analytic|", estimate, None)
);
```

At defaults $\sigma = 10\,\mathrm{Pa}$ and $\tau = 10^{-1}$, shrinking steps increases noise $\delta / h$ and stops improvement, giving `RoundoffLimit` in 21 calls.
The same noiseless point gives `Converged`, so this termination indicates noise.
Relative error $8.4 \times 10^{-6}$ and relative estimate $2.2 \times 10^{-5}$ are both below relative $E^\ast$ guideline $5.5 \times 10^{-4}$.
Extrapolation raises truncation order $p$, enlarging optimal steps and reducing attainable errors to order $\delta^{p/(p + 1)}$ ([Total error and optimal steps](oxref:differentiate-optimal-step)).
$E^\ast$ is a guideline for central differences at a single step size, not an upper bound on adaptive-difference error.

Estimate-to-error ratios depend on realized noise.
With the same $\sigma$ and $\tau$ and seeds 0, 3, 7, 11, 15, all stop with `RoundoffLimit`, but the ratio of estimated to actual error ranges from 0.06 to 2.6.
For noisy functions, estimates can understate actual errors by over an order.

At $\sigma = 1000\,\mathrm{Pa}$ with seed 7, 16 levels and 33 calls give `RefinementLimit`.
Relative error $1.9 \times 10^{-2}$ matches relative $E^\ast$ scale $1.2 \times 10^{-2}$, while relative estimate $1.9 \times 10^{-4}$ is only 1/100 of actual error.
At $\tau = 10^{-3}$, derivative $-6\tau$ shrinks hundredfold, so even $\sigma = 10\,\mathrm{Pa}$ yields relative errors about $10^{-3}$.
Near criticality, noisy-pressure derivatives lose relative accuracy and numerical differentiation cannot follow diverging $\kappa_T$.

## Summary

For physical volume $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3/mol}$, default initial $0.5\,\mathrm{m^3/mol}$ crosses poles, giving meaningless results and underestimated errors.
An explicit initial step below pole distance gives relative errors at most $10^{-11}$ and larger error estimates.
Even reduced order-one variables can stop prematurely with `RoundoffLimit` if initial steps approach singularity distances.
Near criticality, derivatives vanish and relative errors grow as $1/\tau$; default tolerances have an absolute floor of $\sqrt{\varepsilon}$ and do not guarantee relative accuracy.
Noisy pressure gives `RoundoffLimit` or `RefinementLimit`; estimates are not bounds, so compare non-`Converged` results with noise-based errors before use.
