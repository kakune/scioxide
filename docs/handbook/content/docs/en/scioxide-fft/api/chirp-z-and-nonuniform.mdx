## Chirp Z-transforms and frequency-band zooming

| Item | Kind | Role | Contract essentials | Explanation | Executable example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`czt`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.czt.html) | Function | Evaluate the $z$-transform at $z_k = a\,w^{-k}$ once | Takes real or complex sequences; packed bins give `InvalidInput` (`UnsupportedConfiguration`). Empty input or $m = 0$ gives `InvalidInput` (`Empty`), $w = 0$ or $a = 0$ gives `InvalidInput` (`OutOfRange`), and non-finite values `NonFinite`. Unrepresentable results give `NumericalInstability` (`Overflow`). `Unscaled` and `Inverse` apply no scaling; `Forward` divides by input length $n$ and `Orthonormal` by $\sqrt{n}$ | [Plans, workspaces, and resource limits](oxref:fft-czt-plan), [Constructing a contour](oxref:fft-czt-contour) | [The czt function, plans, axis execution, and byte limits](oxref:fft-czt-function-axis-limits) |
| [`zoom_fft`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.zoom_fft.html) | Function | Evaluate a unit-circle frequency band once | Takes real or complex sequences. Includes the endpoint only when `endpoint` is true. Packed bins give `InvalidInput` (`UnsupportedConfiguration`), empty input or $m = 0$ gives `InvalidInput` (`Empty`), nonpositive sampling frequency `InvalidInput` (`OutOfRange`), non-finite values `NonFinite`, and overflow of normalized start frequency, step, or result `NumericalInstability` (`Overflow`) | [zoom FFT](oxref:fft-zoom-fft), [Plans, workspaces, and resource limits](oxref:fft-czt-plan) | [Narrowband spectra with varying observation time and evaluation-point count](oxref:fft-zoom-tone-resolution) |
| [`CztContour`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/struct.CztContour.html) | Struct | Contour in logarithmic radius and turns | $z_k = \exp(\rho_0 + k\rho + 2\pi i(t_0 + kt))$. `new` (logarithmic radius and increment, initial phase and phase increment [turns]), `dft(n)`, `zoom(start, end, m, fs, endpoint)`, `from_wa(w, a)`, `point(k)`. Non-finite values give `NonFinite`; point count 0 or nonpositive sampling frequency gives `InvalidInput`. `from_wa` zeroes logarithmic radii with absolute value at most $2\varepsilon$ | [Constructing a contour](oxref:fft-czt-contour), [Contours in the z-plane and the CZT definition](oxref:fft-chirp-z), [zoom FFT](oxref:fft-zoom-fft) | [The czt function, plans, axis execution, and byte limits](oxref:fft-czt-function-axis-limits), [Measure damped-oscillation peak widths by changing the contour radius](oxref:fft-czt-damped-mode), [Algorithm selection and error by contour and size](oxref:fft-czt-algorithm-choice) |
| [`CztPlanSpec`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/struct.CztPlanSpec.html) | Struct | Chirp Z-transform plan specification | `new(n, m, contour, normalization)`, `with_limits`, `with_execution_policy`, `with_backend_strategy`. $n$ and $m$ are validated during preparation | [Plans, workspaces, and resource limits](oxref:fft-czt-plan) | [The czt function, plans, axis execution, and byte limits](oxref:fft-czt-function-axis-limits), [Measure damped-oscillation peak widths by changing the contour radius](oxref:fft-czt-damped-mode), [Algorithm selection and error by contour and size](oxref:fft-czt-algorithm-choice) |
| [`CztPlan`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/struct.CztPlan.html) | Struct | Chirp Z-transform plan | Unit-circle contours use Bluestein; otherwise $nm \le 4096$ uses direct summation, while larger problems use Bluestein if $\lvert\rho\rvert\max(n, m)^2 \le 0.5$ and direct summation otherwise. $n = 0$ or $m = 0$ gives `InvalidInput` (`Empty`) at preparation. `execute_into(input, output, scratch, context)` takes real/complex input; noncomplex output gives `InvalidInput` (`UnsupportedConfiguration`). `execute_axis_into` does not count evaluations. `spec().contour().point(k)`, `algorithm`, `plan_bytes` | [Plans, workspaces, and resource limits](oxref:fft-czt-plan), [Algorithm selection](oxref:fft-czt-algorithm) | [The czt function, plans, axis execution, and byte limits](oxref:fft-czt-function-axis-limits), [Measure damped-oscillation peak widths by changing the contour radius](oxref:fft-czt-damped-mode), [Algorithm selection and error by contour and size](oxref:fft-czt-algorithm-choice) |
| [`CztAlgorithm`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/enum.CztAlgorithm.html) | Enum | Selected algorithm | `Bluestein` (2 FFTs of length $L = $ `next_fast_len(n+m-1)` plus $O(n+m)$) and `ScaledDirect` (extended-exponent direct summation, $O(nm)$) | [Plans, workspaces, and resource limits](oxref:fft-czt-plan), [Algorithm selection](oxref:fft-czt-algorithm) | [Algorithm selection and error by contour and size](oxref:fft-czt-algorithm-choice), [The czt function, plans, axis execution, and byte limits](oxref:fft-czt-function-axis-limits) |

Preparation limit excludes child FFT preparation. Exceeding it gives `ResourceLimit` | [Plans, workspaces, and resource limits](oxref:fft-czt-plan) | [The czt function, plans, axis execution, and byte limits](oxref:fft-czt-function-axis-limits) |

## Nonuniform Fourier transforms

| Item | Kind | Role | Contract essentials | Explanation | Executable example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`nufft_type1`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.nufft_type1.html) | Function | Sum from nonuniform points to integer modes once | Arguments `(points, input, modes, direction, normalization, accuracy)`; real/complex input. Points are a shape-`[point count, dimension]` view; modes use row-major order increasing from $-\lfloor N/2\rfloor$ per axis. `Forward` uses exponent sign $-1$. Oversampling factor 2, no resource limits. Packed input gives `InvalidInput` (`UnsupportedConfiguration`), out-of-range `accuracy` gives `InvalidInput` (`OutOfRange`), mode count 0 gives `InvalidInput`, shape mismatch `DimensionMismatch`, non-finite values `NonFinite`, and `accuracy` below the rounding estimate gives `NumericalInstability` | [The 3 types, signs, and scales](oxref:fft-nufft-types), [Plans, diagnostics, and resource limits](oxref:fft-nufft-plan), [Checking type 3 and resource limits](oxref:fft-nufft-type3-check) | [Type-3 direct-sum comparison, function–plan agreement, diagnostics, and limits](oxref:fft-nufft-type3-limits), [Type-1 mode order and direct sums, and the adjoint identity with type 2](oxref:fft-nufft-type1-type2-adjoint) |
| [`nufft_type2`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.nufft_type2.html) | Function | Sum from integer modes to nonuniform points once | Argument order and errors match `nufft_type1`; input is mode coefficients. Evaluates a Fourier sum, without solving the type-1 inverse problem | [The 3 types, signs, and scales](oxref:fft-nufft-types), [Checks using direct sums and the adjoint identity](oxref:fft-nufft-adjoint-check) | [Type-1 mode order and direct sums, and the adjoint identity with type 2](oxref:fft-nufft-type1-type2-adjoint) |
| [`nufft_type3`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.nufft_type3.html) | Function | Sum from nonuniform points to arbitrary angular frequencies once | Arguments `(points, input, frequencies, direction, normalization, accuracy)`; real/complex input. Points and frequencies are rank-2 views of the same dimension, with frequencies in radians per coordinate unit. Errors match `nufft_type1`, without mode-count validation | [Checking type 3 and resource limits](oxref:fft-nufft-type3-check), [The 3 types, signs, and scales](oxref:fft-nufft-types) | [Type-3 direct-sum comparison, function–plan agreement, diagnostics, and limits](oxref:fft-nufft-type3-limits) |

Borrows views and mode counts | [The 3 types, signs, and scales](oxref:fft-nufft-types) | [Type-1 mode order and direct sums, and the adjoint identity with type 2](oxref:fft-nufft-type1-type2-adjoint), [Type-3 direct-sum comparison, function–plan agreement, diagnostics, and limits](oxref:fft-nufft-type3-limits), [Compute Fourier sums at irregular observation times with type 3 and compare with direct summation](oxref:fft-nufft-direct-sum), [Compare type-1 NUFFT Fourier sums of irregular observations against direct summation](oxref:fft-nonuniform-periodogram) |
| [`NufftPlanSpec`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/struct.NufftPlanSpec.html) | Struct | Nonuniform Fourier transform specification | `new(direction, normalization, accuracy)`, `with_limits`, `with_oversampling` (default 2), `with_execution_policy`, `with_backend_strategy`. `accuracy` requests maximum output error relative to input 1-norm; preparation checks $0 < \epsilon < 1$ and oversampling at least 2 (non-finite gives `NonFinite`, out-of-range `InvalidInput`) | [The 3 types, signs, and scales](oxref:fft-nufft-types), [Plans, diagnostics, and resource limits](oxref:fft-nufft-plan), [Kaiser–Bessel kernels and oversampled grids](oxref:fft-nufft-kernel) | [Type-1 mode order and direct sums, and the adjoint identity with type 2](oxref:fft-nufft-type1-type2-adjoint), [Type-3 direct-sum comparison, function–plan agreement, diagnostics, and limits](oxref:fft-nufft-type3-limits), [Compute Fourier sums at irregular observation times with type 3 and compare with direct summation](oxref:fft-nufft-direct-sum), [Compare type-1 NUFFT Fourier sums of irregular observations against direct summation](oxref:fft-nonuniform-periodogram) |
| [`NufftPlan`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/struct.NufftPlan.html) | Struct | Nonuniform Fourier transform plan | `new(transform, spec)`. Requests below rounding estimate $E_r$ give `NumericalInstability`; exceeding `NufftLimits` gives `ResourceLimit`. `execute_into(input, output, &mut workspace, scope)` counts one operator evaluation; noncomplex output gives `InvalidInput` (`UnsupportedConfiguration`). `execute_axis_into` counts once when at least one sequence exists. Preserves output on failure. Apply the adjoint with `AdjointOperator` | [Plans, diagnostics, and resource limits](oxref:fft-nufft-plan), [The 3 types, signs, and scales](oxref:fft-nufft-types) | [Type-1 mode order and direct sums, and the adjoint identity with type 2](oxref:fft-nufft-type1-type2-adjoint), [Type-3 direct-sum comparison, function–plan agreement, diagnostics, and limits](oxref:fft-nufft-type3-limits), [Compute Fourier sums at irregular observation times with type 3 and compare with direct summation](oxref:fft-nufft-direct-sum), [Compare type-1 NUFFT Fourier sums of irregular observations against direct summation](oxref:fft-nonuniform-periodogram) |
