|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`dct`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.dct.html) | Function | One-dimensional DCT | Output has input length; $O(n\log n)$. Errors match `dct` | [Definitions and scales of the 4 types](oxref:fft-dct-types), [Algorithms and APIs](oxref:fft-dct-api) | [Check defining sums, round-trip factors, and orthogonality for all 8 types](oxref:fft-dct-dst-conventions) |

I and IV invert to scaled copies of themselves; II and III are scaled inverses of each other | [Definitions and scales of the 4 types](oxref:fft-dct-types), [Symmetric extensions and boundary conditions](oxref:fft-dct-boundary-symmetry), [Algorithms and APIs](oxref:fft-dct-api) | [Check defining sums, round-trip factors, and orthogonality for all 8 types](oxref:fft-dct-dst-conventions), [Measure whether the 8 kernels are eigenvectors of boundary-closed second differences](oxref:fft-dct-dst-difference-eigenvectors), [Measure discrete-Laplacian eigenvalues with DST-I and DCT-II](oxref:fft-boundary-bases), [Plans mixing DCT and DST by axis and their workspace requirements](oxref:fft-nd-mixed-real-transforms) |
