## Complex Fourier transforms

| Item | Kind | Role | Contract essentials | Explanation | Executable example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`fft`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.fft.html) | Function | Forward transform of a complex sequence | Output has input length and `fftfreq` order. Internally prepares mixed-radix Cooley–Tukey when prime factors are only 2, 3, 5, 7, otherwise Rader or Bluestein; every length costs $O(n\log n)$. Empty input gives `InvalidInput` (`Empty`), non-finite values `NonFinite`, unrepresentable output `NumericalInstability` (`Overflow`), and allocation failure `AllocationFailure` | [DFT definition and inverse transform](oxref:fft-dft-definition), [Algorithm-selection rules](oxref:fft-algorithm-selection), [Plan preparation and ownership](oxref:fft-plan-preparation) | [Compare FFT cross-correlation with signal's correlate](oxref:fft-correlation-lag-order), [Measure the scalings and Parseval ratios of all 4 normalizations](oxref:fft-normalization-parseval), [Compare a two-dimensional FFT with per-axis one-dimensional FFTs](oxref:fft-2d-separable), [Agreement of packed transforms with full-length complex transforms](oxref:fft-hermitian-duality) |
| [`ifft`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.ifft.html) | Function | Inverse transform of a complex sequence | Computes the positive-exponent sum and applies the inverse factor from `Normalization`. With matching normalization other than `Unscaled`, `ifft(fft(x))` restores `x` within rounding error. Errors match `fft` | [DFT definition and inverse transform](oxref:fft-dft-definition), [Normalization and Parseval's identity](oxref:fft-normalization-conventions), [Weights for the discrete analytic signal](oxref:fft-analytic-signal-discrete) | [Compare an analytic signal constructed with rfft and ifft against signal's hilbert](oxref:fft-analytic-signal-weights), [Compare FFT cross-correlation with signal's correlate](oxref:fft-correlation-lag-order), [Measure the scalings and Parseval ratios of all 4 normalizations](oxref:fft-normalization-parseval), [Agreement of packed transforms with full-length complex transforms](oxref:fft-hermitian-duality) |

| [`rfft`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.rfft.html) | Function | Real sequence to packed nonnegative-frequency bins | Returns $\lfloor n/2\rfloor + 1$ bins and logical length $n$ in `HermitianSpectrum`. Packing leaves normalization unchanged. | [`hfft`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.hfft.html) | Function | Packed half of Hermitian-symmetric samples to a real spectrum | Reads logical length from `HermitianSpectrum` and computes the negative-exponent sum. Symmetry checks and errors match `irfft`. `ihfft` is the inverse | [hfft and ihfft](oxref:fft-hfft-ihfft), [Hermitian-symmetry validation](oxref:fft-hermitian-check), [Storage layouts in plans](oxref:fft-real-plan-layout) | [Agreement of packed transforms with full-length complex transforms](oxref:fft-hermitian-duality) |

Empty input gives `InvalidInput` (`Empty`), non-finite values `NonFinite`, and unrepresentable results `NumericalInstability` (`Overflow`) | [hfft and ihfft](oxref:fft-hfft-ihfft), [Storage layouts in plans](oxref:fft-real-plan-layout), [HermitianSpectrumNd and inverse transforms](oxref:fft-real-nd-spectrum) | [Agreement of packed transforms with full-length complex transforms](oxref:fft-hermitian-duality) |

## Frequency axes and transform lengths

| Item | Kind | Role | Contract essentials | Explanation | Executable example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`fftfreq`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.fftfreq.html) | Function | Full-spectrum bin frequencies | Computes $[0, \dots, \lceil n/2\rceil - 1, -\lfloor n/2\rfloor, \dots, -1]/(nd)$ directly from integer $k$, each term as $(k/n)/d$. $n = 0$ or $d = 0$ gives `InvalidInput`, non-finite $d$ gives `NonFinite`, inexactly representable length/index or indistinguishable adjacent frequencies gives `ScalarConversion`, and overflow or rounding to 0 of $1/(nd)$ or frequencies gives `NumericalInstability` | [Frequency axis and bin order](oxref:fft-frequency-axis), [Notation for the discrete Fourier transform](oxref:fft-notation) | [Compare fftfreq, rfftfreq, fftshift, and ifftshift order for even and odd lengths](oxref:fft-frequency-order), [Periodic Poisson solves with single- and multiple-mode manufactured solutions](oxref:fft-spectral-poisson) |
| [`rfftfreq`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.rfftfreq.html) | Function | Frequencies of `rfft` bins | $[0, \dots, \lfloor n/2\rfloor]/(nd)$. The last even-length bin is positive Nyquist frequency $1/(2d)$. Errors match `fftfreq` | [Frequency axis and bin order](oxref:fft-frequency-axis), [Real-sequence spectra and Hermitian symmetry](oxref:fft-hermitian-symmetry), [Observing aliasing and leakage](oxref:fft-aliasing-experiment) | [Compare fftfreq, rfftfreq, fftshift, and ifftshift order for even and odd lengths](oxref:fft-frequency-order), [Move signal frequency across the Nyquist frequency](oxref:fft-sampling-alias), [Measure spectral leakage by varying periods and windows](oxref:fft-leakage-windows), [Periodic Poisson solves with single- and multiple-mode manufactured solutions](oxref:fft-spectral-poisson) |
| [`fftshift`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.fftshift.html) | Function | Reorder zero frequency to the center | Rotates left by $\lceil n/2\rceil$, placing zero frequency at $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$. Empty input gives empty output. Only allocation failure (`AllocationFailure`) is an error | [Frequency axis and bin order](oxref:fft-frequency-axis), [fftshift and ifftshift](oxref:fft-frequency-shift), [Cross-correlation and lag order](oxref:fft-correlation) | [Compare fftfreq, rfftfreq, fftshift, and ifftshift order for even and odd lengths](oxref:fft-frequency-order) |
| [`ifftshift`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.ifftshift.html) | Function | Inverse reordering of `fftshift` | Rotates left by $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$, differing from `fftshift` for odd lengths. Only allocation failure is an error | [Frequency axis and bin order](oxref:fft-frequency-axis), [fftshift and ifftshift](oxref:fft-frequency-shift) | [Compare fftfreq, rfftfreq, fftshift, and ifftshift order for even and odd lengths](oxref:fft-frequency-order) |
| [`next_fast_len`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.next_fast_len.html) | Function | Smallest product of 2, 3, 5, 7 at least $n$ | Zero-padding length helper; other lengths also transform. No allocation or floating-point computation. $n = 0$ gives `InvalidInput` (`OutOfRange`); no qualifying length fitting `usize` gives `SizeOverflow`. | [Choosing fast lengths](oxref:fft-fast-lengths), [Zero-padding length](oxref:fft-zero-padding), [Bluestein's identity](oxref:fft-bluestein-czt) | [Compare FFT convolution with direct summation with and without zero-padding](oxref:fft-convolution-delay), [Compare FFT cross-correlation with signal's correlate](oxref:fft-correlation-lag-order), [Check length-dependent algorithm selection and f32 error with plan diagnostics](oxref:fft-planner-algorithm-choice), [FFT-multiplication coefficient error versus base and digit count](oxref:fft-integer-multiplication) |
| [`prev_fast_len`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.prev_fast_len.html) | Function | Largest product of 2, 3, 5, 7 at most $n$ | $n = 0$ gives `InvalidInput` (`OutOfRange`). No allocation | [Choosing fast lengths](oxref:fft-fast-lengths) | [Check length-dependent algorithm selection and f32 error with plan diagnostics](oxref:fft-planner-algorithm-choice) |
