## Discrete Hankel transforms and FFTLog

| Item | Kind | Role | Contract essentials | Explanation | Executable example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`dht`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.dht.html) | Function | Discrete Hankel transform on a Bessel-zero grid once | Takes real samples, zeros, order `order`, and range `extent`, approximating $\int_0^R f(r)J_\nu(kr)\,r\,dr$ with zero-grid quadrature. Fewer than 2 zeros give `InvalidInput` (`Empty`), sample count other than zero count − 1 gives `DimensionMismatch`, non-finite values `NonFinite`, and $\nu \le -1/2$, $R \le 0$, or zeros outside default tolerances give `InvalidInput` (`OutOfRange`). Preparation and application both cost $O(n^2)$ | [Plans and convenience functions](oxref:fft-hankel-api) | [Agreement of dht/idht with plans and the fields of HankelLimits](oxref:fft-hankel-function-limits) |
| [`idht`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.idht.html) | Function | Inverse-direction discrete Hankel quadrature once | Takes the same zeros, order, and range as `dht`, mapping samples at frequency nodes $k_m = j_m/R$ to radial nodes $r_m = R\,j_m/j_{n+1}$. Uses factor $1/R^2$ instead of $(R/j_{n+1})^2$ and is not the exact inverse of `dht`, so round trips contain quadrature error. Errors match `dht` | [Plans and convenience functions](oxref:fft-hankel-api) | [Agreement of dht/idht with plans and the fields of HankelLimits](oxref:fft-hankel-function-limits) |
| [`DiscreteHankelPlanSpec`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/struct.DiscreteHankelPlanSpec.html) | Struct | Discrete Hankel transform specification | `new(order, extent, direction)` (default zero tolerance `Tolerance::from_sqrt_epsilon()`), `with_tolerance`, `with_limits`, `with_iteration_budget`, `with_execution_policy`. Order $\nu > -1/2$ and positive range are validated during preparation | [Validating zeros](oxref:fft-hankel-zero-check), [Plans and convenience functions](oxref:fft-hankel-api) | [Agreement of dht/idht with plans and the fields of HankelLimits](oxref:fft-hankel-function-limits), [Compute a three-dimensional Gaussian radial transform on a Bessel-zero grid](oxref:fft-hankel-gaussian) |
| [`DiscreteHankelPlan`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/struct.DiscreteHankelPlan.html) | Struct | Discrete Hankel transform plan | `new(zeros, spec)` rejects fewer than 2 zeros or zeros failing positivity, increasing order, tolerance, or consecutiveness with `InvalidInput` (argument `zeros`). Preparation and execution cost $O(n^2)$. Componentwise rounding range $8\varepsilon(n+2)\sum_j\lvert m_{ij}\rvert\lvert x_j\rvert$. `execute_into` takes complex slices, counting one operator evaluation and $n$ iterations. `nodes`, `frequencies`, `diagnostics`, `execute_axis_into`, `LinearOperator`, and `AdjointOperator` | [Plans and convenience functions](oxref:fft-hankel-api) | [Agreement of dht/idht with plans and the fields of HankelLimits](oxref:fft-hankel-function-limits), [Compute a three-dimensional Gaussian radial transform on a Bessel-zero grid](oxref:fft-hankel-gaussian) |
| [`DiscreteHankelDiagnostics`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/struct.DiscreteHankelDiagnostics.html) | Struct | Zero validation and preparation work | `maximum_zero_residual` (maximum $\lvert J_\nu(z_m)\rvert$), `maximum_zero_correction` (maximum Newton correction), `preparation_work` | [Plans and convenience functions](oxref:fft-hankel-api) | [Agreement of dht/idht with plans and the fields of HankelLimits](oxref:fft-hankel-function-limits) |
| [`HankelLimits`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/struct.HankelLimits.html) | Struct | Discrete Hankel transform limits | `preparation_bytes` and `workspace_bytes` (excess gives `ResourceLimit`), and `preparation_work` (exhaustion gives `ConvergenceFailure`). Unlimited by default | [Plans and convenience functions](oxref:fft-hankel-api) | [Agreement of dht/idht with plans and the fields of HankelLimits](oxref:fft-hankel-function-limits) |
| [`FftLogLimits`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/struct.FftLogLimits.html) | Struct | FFTLog limits | `preparation_bytes` and `workspace_bytes` (excess gives `ResourceLimit`), and `preparation_work` (exhaustion gives `ConvergenceFailure`). Unlimited by default | [Plans and convenience functions](oxref:fft-fftlog-api) | [Agreement of fht/ifht with plans and the fields of FftLogLimits](oxref:fft-fftlog-function-limits) |
| [`fht`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.fht.html) | Function | FFTLog for real input | Central input radius is 1. Treats input as periodic in $\ln r$ without zero-padding. Empty input or nonpositive `dln` gives `InvalidInput`, non-finite values `NonFinite`, forward multiplier poles `Singular`, and nonzero multipliers rounding to 0 or multiplier/weight/result overflow `NumericalInstability` | [Plans and convenience functions](oxref:fft-fftlog-api) | [Agreement of fht/ifht with plans and the fields of FftLogLimits](oxref:fft-fftlog-function-limits) |
| [`ifht`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.ifht.html) | Function | Inverse of `fht` | Applies the inverse prepared discrete map and returns its real part. Zero multipliers give `Singular`. Other errors match `fht` | [Plans and convenience functions](oxref:fft-fftlog-api) | [Agreement of fht/ifht with plans and the fields of FftLogLimits](oxref:fft-fftlog-function-limits), [Compute a Gaussian function's Hankel transform on a logarithmic grid with FFTLog](oxref:fft-fftlog-gaussian) |
| [`FftLogPlanSpec`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/struct.FftLogPlanSpec.html) | Struct | FFTLog specification | `new(length, log_spacing, order, offset, bias, direction)` (bias is complex), `with_log_radius_center`, `with_limits`, `with_iteration_budget`, `with_execution_policy`, `with_backend_strategy`. Validated during preparation | [Plans and convenience functions](oxref:fft-fftlog-api) | [Agreement of fht/ifht with plans and the fields of FftLogLimits](oxref:fft-fftlog-function-limits), [Compute a Gaussian function's Hankel transform on a logarithmic grid with FFTLog](oxref:fft-fftlog-gaussian), [Compare an acoustic-scale-like peak from FFTLog with a closed form](oxref:fft-fftlog-correlation-function), [Check bias and conversion factors with a power-law closed form](oxref:fft-fftlog-power-law) |
| [`FftLogPlan`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/struct.FftLogPlan.html) | Struct | FFTLog plan | Preparation computes $n$ multipliers. Length 0 or nonpositive spacing gives `InvalidInput`, non-finite values `NonFinite`, nonzero multipliers rounding to 0 gives `NumericalInstability` (`Underflow`), and out-of-range weights/binary exponents `NumericalInstability` (`Overflow`). Execution uses 2 FFTs plus $O(n)$. Periodically extends biased input in $\ln r$ without implicit zero-padding. Preparation succeeds with multiplier zeros/poles; execution returns `Singular` depending on direction. `execute_into` takes complex slices, counting one operator evaluation and $n$ iterations. `log_radius`, `log_frequency`, `diagnostics`, `execute_axis_into`, `LinearOperator`, and `AdjointOperator` | [Plans and convenience functions](oxref:fft-fftlog-api) | [Agreement of fht/ifht with plans and the fields of FftLogLimits](oxref:fft-fftlog-function-limits), [Compute a Gaussian function's Hankel transform on a logarithmic grid with FFTLog](oxref:fft-fftlog-gaussian), [Compare an acoustic-scale-like peak from FFTLog with a closed form](oxref:fft-fftlog-correlation-function), [Check bias and conversion factors with a power-law closed form](oxref:fft-fftlog-power-law) |
| [`FftLogDiagnostics`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/struct.FftLogDiagnostics.html) | Struct | FFTLog preparation diagnostics | `log_multiplier_condition` (natural log of the maximum/minimum multiplier-magnitude ratio; infinite for zeros/poles) and `preparation_work`. Not an error bound | [Plans and convenience functions](oxref:fft-fftlog-api), [Cost and accuracy](oxref:fft-fftlog-accuracy) | [Agreement of fht/ifht with plans and the fields of FftLogLimits](oxref:fft-fftlog-function-limits) |
| [`fftlog_low_ringing_offset`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.fftlog_low_ringing_offset.html) | Function | Select a low-ringing offset | Returns a value within $\Delta/2$ of `initial`. Non-finite values give `NonFinite`, nonpositive spacing `InvalidInput`; log-gamma poles or exhausted work propagate their respective errors | [Low-ringing offset](oxref:fft-fftlog-low-ringing), [Converting the spherical Bessel transform to FFTLog](oxref:fft-fftlog-correlation-mapping) | [Compute a Gaussian function's Hankel transform on a logarithmic grid with FFTLog](oxref:fft-fftlog-gaussian), [Compare an acoustic-scale-like peak from FFTLog with a closed form](oxref:fft-fftlog-correlation-function), [Check bias and conversion factors with a power-law closed form](oxref:fft-fftlog-power-law) |
