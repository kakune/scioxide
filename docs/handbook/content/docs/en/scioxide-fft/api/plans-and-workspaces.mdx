## Multidimensional FFTs and shapes

| Item | Kind | Role | Contract essentials | Explanation | Executable example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`fftn`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.fftn.html) | Function | Complex forward transform on selected axes | Takes a packed row-major slice, shape, and axes, returning `Vec` with the same shape and order. Unselected axes are independent batches. Shape element-count overflow gives `SizeOverflow`, slice-length mismatch `DimensionMismatch`, out-of-range axis `OutOfBounds`, duplicates `InvalidInput` (`Inconsistent`), and selected length-0 axis `InvalidInput` (`Empty`). Transform strided arrays with `FftNdPlan` | [Row-major storage and batch axes](oxref:fft-row-major-batches), [Packing real arrays](oxref:fft-real-nd-layout) | [Compare a two-dimensional FFT with per-axis one-dimensional FFTs](oxref:fft-2d-separable), [Packed shapes of real arrays and frequency reordering](oxref:fft-rfftn-packing) |
| [`ifftn`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.ifftn.html) | Function | Complex inverse transform on selected axes | Arguments and errors match `fftn`. `Inverse` normalization divides by the selected axis-length product | [Row-major storage and batch axes](oxref:fft-row-major-batches) | [Compare a two-dimensional FFT with per-axis one-dimensional FFTs](oxref:fft-2d-separable) |
| [`rfftn`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.rfftn.html) | Function | Real array to packed bins | Takes a row-major real slice and shape, packs the last selected axis to $\lfloor n/2\rfloor + 1$, and returns `HermitianSpectrumNd` carrying original shape and axes. Empty axes give `InvalidInput` (`Empty`). Other errors match `fftn` | [Packing real arrays](oxref:fft-real-nd-layout), [Row-major storage and batch axes](oxref:fft-row-major-batches), [Fast Poisson solvers](oxref:fft-fast-poisson) | [Packed shapes of real arrays and frequency reordering](oxref:fft-rfftn-packing), [Periodic Poisson solves with single- and multiple-mode manufactured solutions](oxref:fft-spectral-poisson) |
| [`irfftn`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.irfftn.html) | Function | Packed bins to a real array | Takes `&HermitianSpectrumNd`. Checks conjugate pairs on packed-axis DC/Nyquist planes against per-spectrum tolerances, returning `InvalidInput` beyond tolerance. Imaginary parts remaining in real results beyond the rounding bound give `NumericalInstability` (`Breakdown`) | [Hermitian-symmetry validation](oxref:fft-hermitian-check), [HermitianSpectrumNd and inverse transforms](oxref:fft-real-nd-spectrum), [Hermitian-symmetry validation](oxref:fft-hermitian-tolerance) | [Packed shapes of real arrays and frequency reordering](oxref:fft-rfftn-packing), [Periodic Poisson solves with single- and multiple-mode manufactured solutions](oxref:fft-spectral-poisson) |

Symmetry checks and errors match `irfftn` | [HermitianSpectrumNd and inverse transforms](oxref:fft-real-nd-spectrum), [Hermitian-symmetry validation](oxref:fft-hermitian-check), [Hermitian-symmetry validation](oxref:fft-hermitian-tolerance) | [Packed shapes of real arrays and frequency reordering](oxref:fft-rfftn-packing) |
| [`ihfftn`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.ihfftn.html) | Function | Real spectrum to packed Hermitian-symmetric samples | Takes a row-major real slice and shape, returning `HermitianSpectrumNd`. Errors match `rfftn` | [Row-major storage and batch axes](oxref:fft-row-major-batches), [HermitianSpectrumNd and inverse transforms](oxref:fft-real-nd-spectrum) | [Packed shapes of real arrays and frequency reordering](oxref:fft-rfftn-packing) |
| [`fftshift_nd`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-fft/0.1.0/scioxide_fft/fn.fftshift_nd.html) | Function | Move zero frequency to the center on selected axes | Takes a packed row-major slice, shape, and axes, returning `Vec` with the same shape and order. Shape element-count overflow gives `SizeOverflow`, slice-length mismatch `DimensionMismatch`, out-of-range axis `OutOfBounds`, duplicates `InvalidInput` (`Inconsistent`), and result-allocation failure `AllocationFailure` | [fftshift and ifftshift](oxref:fft-frequency-shift), [Frequency axis and bin order](oxref:fft-frequency-axis) | [Packed shapes of real arrays and frequency reordering](oxref:fft-rfftn-packing) |

Errors match `fftshift_nd` | [fftshift and ifftshift](oxref:fft-frequency-shift), [Frequency axis and bin order](oxref:fft-frequency-axis) | [Packed shapes of real arrays and frequency reordering](oxref:fft-rfftn-packing) |
