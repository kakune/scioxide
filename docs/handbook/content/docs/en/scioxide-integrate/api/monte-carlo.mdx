These tables summarize integration types that select samples randomly.
Standard errors and confidence intervals describe statistical sampling uncertainty; they neither bound bias nor detect contributions from unsampled regions.
Intervals use the Student-t distribution by default.
Intervals are computed separately for real components and do not provide simultaneous coverage across components.
Samples from `RandomStreams` are determined by the root and stream number; unless cancellation or a deadline stops execution, estimates, statistics, and evaluation counts are identical across execution policies and worker counts.
Samples used to train VEGAS and MISER do not enter the evaluation estimator.
Shared result types are listed on the [API guide's first page](../).

## VEGAS and MISER training

Trainers construct maps or strata; evaluation passes these to `MonteCarloIntegrator::importance` or `MonteCarloIntegrator::stratified` and uses a separate random stream.

| Item | Kind | Role | Key specifications | Explanation | Runnable examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`VegasTrainer`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/struct.VegasTrainer.html) | Struct | Training a separable importance map `VegasMap` | `new(&bounds, VegasOptions)` gives `InvalidInput` for fewer than 2 bins or a nonpositive damping exponent. `train(f, streams)` returns `(VegasMap, TrainingReport)`; `train_into(f, streams, &mut map, ws, scope)` writes to a `uniform_map()` map. Each iteration adds $(\lVert f\rVert/q)^2$ to bins ($\lVert f\rVert$ is the Euclidean component norm), then redraws boundaries using compression and averaging. Adapts to axis-aligned structure but cannot track coordinate correlations. A stop after at least one iteration returns the last completed map with `Ok`. Training results are independent of execution policy | [VEGAS training of separable importance maps](oxref:integrate-vegas-map), [Separating training and evaluation](oxref:integrate-training-and-evaluation) | [Vary heat-source width and random seed to compare uniform MC and VEGAS standard errors](oxref:integrate-adaptive-monte-carlo-source) |
| [`VegasOptions`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/struct.VegasOptions.html) | Struct | VEGAS bins, iterations, pilot samples, and damping | Defaults: 50 bins per axis, 5 iterations × 2048 samples, damping exponent 1.5, sequential execution. `with_bins` (at least 2), `with_iterations`, `with_samples_per_iteration`, `with_damping` (finite and positive; smaller values change the map more slowly), `with_execution_policy`.
