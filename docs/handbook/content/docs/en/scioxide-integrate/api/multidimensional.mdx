These tables summarize multidimensional quadrature without random sample selection.
Use tensor-product rules, Genz–Malik adaptive cubature, or sparse grids on boxes; iterated integration and coordinate maps for bounds depending on outer coordinates; and fixed rules for triangles, tetrahedra, and spheres.
Integrands accept points `&[S]` and return real, complex, or fixed-size vector values in `Result`.
Adaptive errors are estimates and may miss local features hidden between nodes.
Workspace corresponds to one dimension and component count; `integrate_into` with sufficiently sized workspace allocates nothing.
Shared result types and `SubdivisionOptions` are listed on the [API guide's first page](../).

## Adaptive cubature

| Item | Kind | Role | Key specifications | Explanation | Runnable examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`CubaturePlan`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/struct.CubaturePlan.html) | Struct | Global adaptive cubature over boxes | `new(&bounds, SubdivisionOptions)`. $d=1$ uses G7K15 (without breakpoints, same value, error, and evaluations as `AdaptiveIntegrator`) and gives `ToleranceReached`. $d\ge2$ uses Genz–Malik degree-7 and embedded degree-5 rules, with $2^d+2d^2+2d+1$ evaluations per region (17 and 33 for $d=2,3$); bisects the axis of largest fourth difference for $2n(d)$ evaluations. The $d\ge2$ estimate is $4\lvert Q_7-Q_5\rvert$ (at least the roundoff floor), carries `ErrorReliability::Heuristic`, and converges with `HeuristicToleranceReached`. A roundoff floor above tolerance or a region too narrow for bisection with distinct nodes gives `Stagnation`. `with_breakpoints(axis, fractions)` splits axes at strictly increasing ratios in $(0,1)$; initial regions are their Cartesian product. Limits, cancellation, or deadlines after measuring all initial regions return the estimate with `BudgetLimit` or `ExecutionStopped`; earlier stops give errors `ConvergenceFailure` or `ExecutionStopped`. A zero-width axis returns exact zero and `DegenerateDomain` without evaluation | [Genz–Malik adaptive cubature](oxref:integrate-genz-malik-cubature), [Error estimation and termination](oxref:integrate-cubature-termination), [Initial splits at feature locations inside boxes](oxref:integrate-box-breakpoints) | [Vary dimension and peak shape to compare Genz–Malik cubature error estimates with actual errors](oxref:integrate-genz-malik-gaussian-peak), [Compute transmission through a rotated masking band with product rules and adaptive quadrature](oxref:integrate-aperture-cubature) |
| [`CubatureWorkspace`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/struct.CubatureWorkspace.html) | Struct | Cubature region list and per-component estimates | Created with `CubaturePlan::workspace(components)`; holds up to $r=k+m$ regions from $k$ initial regions and bisection limit $m$, using $O(r(d+c))$ memory for $c$ components. Reusable with plans of the same dimension and component count while regions fit; grow for added breakpoints with `check` and `try_reserve`. Workspace dimension or region-count mismatch in `integrate_into` gives `DimensionMismatch`. `component_errors()` is `None` after a failed call or a call without estimates (degenerate boxes or $d=0$) | [Error estimation and termination](oxref:integrate-cubature-termination), [Initial splits at feature locations inside boxes](oxref:integrate-box-breakpoints) | [Vary dimension and peak shape to compare Genz–Malik cubature error estimates with actual errors](oxref:integrate-genz-malik-gaussian-peak) |
| [`integrate_box`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/fn.integrate_box.html) | Function | One-shot box integration with defaults | `integrate_box(f, &lower, &upper)` constructs a box from corner coordinates and integrates sequentially with `CubaturePlan` using default `SubdivisionOptions` ($\sqrt\varepsilon$, 50 bisections), returning value and error estimate in an `Integral`. Unequal corner lengths give `DimensionMismatch`; dimension at least `usize::BITS` gives `InvalidInput`; endpoint errors come from `Interval` construction. Dimension zero returns the value from one evaluation; a zero-width axis gives zero without evaluation. High-dimensional smooth functions may reach default limits. Unsuccessful termination returns no value: limits and stagnation give `ConvergenceFailure` with reasons such as `EvaluationLimit`, `ResourceLimit`, or `Stagnation`; cancellation or deadline gives `ExecutionStopped` | [One-shot integrate_box](oxref:integrate-one-shot-box), [One-shot functions and configured plans](oxref:integrate-choosing-one-shot) | [Vary peak sharpness and compare one-shot integrate_box with a plan](oxref:integrate-box-one-shot) |

## Iterated integration with variable bounds and coordinate maps

| Item | Kind | Role | Key specifications | Explanation | Runnable examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`IteratedDomainPlan`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/struct.IteratedDomainPlan.html) | Struct | Iterated adaptive quadrature with bounds depending on outer coordinates | `new(dimension, IteratedOptions)` accepts dimensions 0–32; above 32 gives `InvalidInput` (`OutOfRange`). Axis 0 is outermost; `bounds(outer)` returns a finite interval for axis $k$ ($k$ is slice length) from a slice of $k$ outer coordinates (infinite bounds give `InvalidInput`). Uses G7K15 per axis; outer nodes are themselves inner integrals, requiring at least $15^d$ evaluations. `integrate(f, bounds)` uses the identity map; `integrate_into(f, bounds, &map, out, ws, scope)` uses `CoordinateMap`. Takes integrand and `bounds` by value. Inner errors propagate as the `inner` component. Convergence gives `ToleranceReached`; no refinable regions gives `Stagnation`; zero outermost width gives zero and `DegenerateDomain`. Inner integrals ending with `Stagnation` or `RefinementLimit` pass estimates and errors to the outer rule. Other inner stops (limits, cancellation, deadlines) fail the entire call. `with_breakpoints(axis, fractions)` gives ratios within the axis's current interval | [Iterated integration with variable bounds](oxref:integrate-iterated-domains), [Interpreting iterated integration and cubature termination](oxref:integrate-iterated-termination) | [Vary density gradients and tolerance to compare Cartesian and polar-map evaluation counts](oxref:integrate-iterated-disk-inertia) |
| [`IteratedOptions`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/struct.IteratedOptions.html) | Struct | Iterated integration tolerance and limits | Tolerance in `new(SubdivisionOptions)` applies to the outermost integral; bisection and evaluation limits count across all axes. `with_max_iterations` limits G7K15 applications including outer axes, with no default limit. `default()` uses default `SubdivisionOptions` ($\sqrt\varepsilon$, 50 bisections across all axes). Outer regions start allocating half their tolerance to inner integrals; if inner error dominates, the allocation is halved and recomputed | [Allocating tolerance to inner integration](oxref:integrate-iterated-inner-tolerance) | [Vary density gradients and tolerance to compare Cartesian and polar-map evaluation counts](oxref:integrate-iterated-disk-inertia) |
| [`IteratedWorkspace`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/struct.IteratedWorkspace.html) | Struct | Region lists per axis | Created with `IteratedDomainPlan::workspace(components)`; holds $O(drc)$ scalars ($r$ is regions per axis, the segment count plus bisection limit). Reusable with plans of the same dimension and component count while regions fit. Dimension or region-count mismatch gives `InvalidInput` (`Inconsistent`). `component_errors()` is `None` after a failed latest call | [Allocating tolerance to inner integration](oxref:integrate-iterated-inner-tolerance) | [Vary density gradients and tolerance to compare Cartesian and polar-map evaluation counts](oxref:integrate-iterated-disk-inertia) |
| [`CoordinateMap`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/trait.CoordinateMap.html) | Trait | Variable transformation $x=\varphi(t)$ for iterated integration | `map(coordinates, physical)` writes $\varphi(t)$ and returns $J(t)$. For the change-of-variables formula, return $J=\lvert\det D\varphi\rvert$. The plan multiplies $J$ directly without taking its absolute value; a signed determinant changes the integral sign. Coordinates and $J$ must be finite; map and $J$ errors are excluded from error estimates. | [`IdentityMap`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/struct.IdentityMap.html) | Struct | Identity map $\varphi(t)=t$, $J=1$ | Default map used by `IteratedDomainPlan::integrate`. May be passed explicitly to `integrate_into` | [Coordinate maps and Jacobians](oxref:integrate-coordinate-maps) | - |
| [`integrate_iterated`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/fn.integrate_iterated.html) | Function | One-shot integration with bounds depending on outer coordinates and defaults | `integrate_iterated(f, bounds, dimension)` integrates with `IteratedDomainPlan` using default `IteratedOptions` (absolute and relative tolerance $\sqrt\varepsilon$, 50 bisections across all axes) and the identity map, returning an `Integral` with an error estimate including inner error. `bounds` takes a slice of outer coordinates like `IteratedDomainPlan`. Evaluations grow exponentially with dimension. Dimension above 32 gives `InvalidInput`. Unsuccessful termination returns no value: limits and stagnation give `ConvergenceFailure`, cancellation and deadlines `ExecutionStopped` | [One-shot integrate_iterated](oxref:integrate-one-shot-iterated), [One-shot functions and configured plans](oxref:integrate-choosing-one-shot) | [Use one-shot integrate_iterated in Cartesian and polar coordinates and compare with the default plan](oxref:integrate-iterated-one-shot) |

## Dimension-adaptive sparse grids

| Item | Kind | Role | Key specifications | Explanation | Runnable examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`SparseGridPlan`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/struct.SparseGridPlan.html) | Struct | Dimension-adaptive Smolyak sparse grids with nested Clenshaw–Curtis rules | `new(dimension, SparseGridOptions)` prepares difference rules through the maximum level; levels above 52 or a maximum-level one-dimensional rule exceeding the evaluation limit give `InvalidInput`. `integrate(f, bounds)`, `integrate_into(f, bounds, out, ws, scope)`. Accepts the candidate index with largest surplus and never evaluates the same node twice within a call. Error estimate is four times the sum of absolute surpluses of candidate indices and accepted indices with unexplored forward neighbors, has no separate roundoff floor, and carries `ErrorReliability::Heuristic`. Tolerance met with level 1 accepted on every axis gives `HeuristicToleranceReached`. After the initial estimate, evaluation limits give `BudgetLimit`, index limits or exhausted candidates give `RefinementLimit`, and nodes merged by rounding in later refinement give `GeometryFailure`; all return the last complete estimate. An initial midpoint unrepresentable inside an axis gives `NumericalInstability` (`Breakdown`); stopping before the initial estimate gives errors `ConvergenceFailure` or `ExecutionStopped`. Insufficient index or evaluation capacity gives `InvalidInput`; a zero-width axis gives zero and `DegenerateDomain` | [Nested Clenshaw–Curtis rules](oxref:integrate-nested-clenshaw-curtis), [Dimension-adaptive refinement](oxref:integrate-dimension-adaptive-refinement), [Error estimation and termination](oxref:integrate-sparse-grid-termination) | [Vary layer count and optical thickness to inspect sparse-grid error estimates and directional indicators](oxref:integrate-sparse-grid-qmc-transmission), [Vary family, dimension, and difficulty to inspect sparse-grid and Genz–Malik errors and estimates](oxref:integrate-genz-deterministic) |
| [`SparseGridOptions`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/struct.SparseGridOptions.html) | Struct | Sparse-grid tolerance and level, index, and evaluation limits | Modify `SparseGridOptions::default()` with `with_tolerance`, `with_max_level`, `with_max_indices`, `with_max_evaluations`, `with_execution_policy`. Defaults: absolute and relative tolerance $\sqrt{\varepsilon}$ with root-mean-square norm, maximum level 4, 64 indices, 4096 evaluations, sequential execution. Limits also determine workspace size. `SparseGridPlan::new` validates values | [Error estimation and termination](oxref:integrate-sparse-grid-termination) | [Vary layer count and optical thickness to inspect sparse-grid error estimates and directional indicators](oxref:integrate-sparse-grid-qmc-transmission), [Vary family, dimension, and difficulty to inspect sparse-grid and Genz–Malik errors and estimates](oxref:integrate-genz-deterministic) |
| [`SparseGridWorkspace`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/struct.SparseGridWorkspace.html) | Struct | Storage for index sets and evaluated node values | Created with `SparseGridPlan::workspace(components)`; holds $O(I(d+c)+E(d+c))$ values ($I$ and $E$ are index and evaluation limits). Reusable for plans with equal or lower limits; node values are not carried into the next call. `directional_indicators()` indicates axes with remaining estimated error; zero does not imply zero error on that axis. `cached_nodes()` counts distinct nodes evaluated in the latest call; `component_errors()` is `None` after a failed call, degenerate box, or $d=0$ | [Plans and workspace](oxref:integrate-sparse-grid-plan) | [Vary layer count and optical thickness to inspect sparse-grid error estimates and directional indicators](oxref:integrate-sparse-grid-qmc-transmission) |

## Fixed simplex and sphere rules

| Item | Kind | Role | Key specifications | Explanation | Runnable examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`SimplexRule`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-integrate/0.1.0/scioxide_integrate/struct.SimplexRule.html) | Struct | Fixed cubature over a $d$ simplex | `positive(dimension, minimum_degree, options)` provides positive weights and interior nodes for dimensions 1–3 and requested degrees 0–7 (Gauss–Legendre on segments, Xiao–Gimbutas tables on triangles and tetrahedra). `new(dimension, s, options)` gives arbitrary-dimensional Grundmann–Möller rules with total degree $2s+1$, $\binom{d+s+1}{s}$ nodes, and alternating-sign weights. Out-of-range values or $(d+1)!$ above `predicate_terms` give `InvalidInput`. `integrate(f, vertices)`, `integrate_into(f, vertices, out, ws, scope)`, `integrate_values_into(vertices, values, out, ws, scope)` (user-computed node values, no evaluations), `integrate_mesh_cell_into(f, &mesh, cell, out, ws, scope)` (elements of scioxide-spatial `SimplexMesh`). Overflow in edge vectors, nodes, volumes, or weights, or nonzero volume or weight underflowing to zero, gives `NumericalInstability`. Terminates with `FixedRuleCompleted`; exactly flat simplices return zero and `DegenerateDomain` without evaluation. No error estimate | [Quadrature on simplices](oxref:integrate-simplex-rules), [Mapping, orientation, and mesh elements](oxref:integrate-simplex-orientation-mesh) | [Vary mesh refinement and compare simplex-rule degree and mass errors](oxref:integrate-simplex-mesh-mass), [Reorder vertices to compare simplex orientation, measure, mapped weights, and mass](oxref:integrate-simplex-orientation) |

Integration uses $\lvert\det J\rvert$ and is orientation-independent | [Mapping, orientation, and mesh elements](oxref:integrate-simplex-orientation-mesh) | [Vary mesh refinement and compare simplex-rule degree and mass errors](oxref:integrate-simplex-mesh-mass), [Reorder vertices to compare simplex orientation, measure, mapped weights, and mass](oxref:integrate-simplex-orientation) |

Either count zero gives `InvalidInput`. Nodes cluster near poles.
