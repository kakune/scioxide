These tables cover two computations closely related to integration.
The first is infinite-series summation: Levin u and Wynn ε use heuristic stopping estimates, while Euler–Maclaurin bounds are conditional on the correctness of user-supplied tail information.
The second is parameter differentiation of finite-interval one-dimensional integrals: combine the integral of the integrand derivative at fixed $x$ with Leibniz terms for moving endpoints, computing derivatives of the discretized integral at the same nodes.
Adaptive partition selection is not differentiated.

## Infinite-series summation

Non-finite terms give `NonFinite`; errors from `term` propagate unchanged. Cost per component is $O(8N)$ ($N$ is term count).
