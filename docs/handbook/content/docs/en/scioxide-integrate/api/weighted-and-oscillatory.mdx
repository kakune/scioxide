These tables summarize integration types whose plans incorporate known difficult factors (endpoint singularities, poles, and oscillatory factors) as weights, leaving callbacks to supply only smooth amplitudes.
Do not multiply the same weight into the callback again.
Prepared plans are immutable; `integrate_into` with supplied workspace allocates nothing.
Reported error separates discretization, roundoff floor, moments, extrapolation, and tail components; the total adds other components to $\max(\text{discretization},\text{roundoff})$ and is an estimate, not an upper bound.
Shared result types and `SubdivisionOptions` are listed on the [API guide's first page](../).

## Weighted quadrature

`WeightedQuadraturePlan` has four constructors for breakpoints, endpoint singularities, Cauchy principal values, and Fourier weights.
Where weights are singular or strongly oscillatory, the amplitude is interpolated at 25 Chebyshev points and combined with modified Chebyshev moments (modified Clenshaw–Curtis quadrature); the difference from a nested 13-point rule estimates error.
Other regions use G7K15 on the product $f\,w$.
