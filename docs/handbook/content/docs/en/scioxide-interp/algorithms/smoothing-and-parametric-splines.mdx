Curves through every noisy measurement can strongly amplify noise under differentiation.
Smoothing relaxes sample agreement and constrains roughness.
Allowed discrepancies depend on measurement errors and downstream uses.
This page covers fixed-basis penalized least squares, adaptive target-driven knots/penalties, and parameters/curvature.

## Coefficient-difference penalties

Let $B_{ij}=B_j(x_i)$ be basis matrix for B-splines $B_j$ ([B-splines, tensor products, and NURBS](../b-splines-tensor-and-nurbs/)) and $W$ diagonal weights.
Choose coefficients $c$ minimizing

$$
J(c)=\|W^{1/2}(Bc-y)\|_2^2+\lambda\|D_qc\|_2^2
$$

this objective.
The first term is data fit and the second penalty.
$D_q$ is the $(n-q)\times n$ matrix of order-$q$ forward coefficient differences, with $n$ controls.
Rows contain $(-1)^{q-k}\binom qk$ ($k=0,\dots,q$); `difference_coefficients(q)` returns these coefficients.
For example, order $q=2$ gives $[1,-2,1]$.
This is Eilers–Marx P-splines.

`SplinePenalty::new(lambda, q)` checks finite nonnegative $\lambda$ and positive $q$, otherwise `InvalidInput`.
Order $q$ must be below control count, checked during Plan preparation.

Stationarity gives

$$
(B^TWB+\lambda D_q^TD_q)c=B^TWy
$$


Local degree-$p$ support makes the left side symmetric banded with half-bandwidth $\max(p,q)$, or $p$ for $\lambda=0$.
SciOxide assembles directly from local basis rows and factors band Cholesky once.
For samples $m$, controls $n$, effective half-bandwidth $k$, assembly/factorization cost $O(m(p^2+\log n)+nk^2)$ and storage $O(m+n(k+1))$ scalars.
$\log n$ is binary span search per sample; $nk^2$ band Cholesky work.
Fitting prepared Plans costs $O(m(p^2+\log n))$ for right sides and $O(nk)$ per component for substitutions.

Write left matrix $A$ and right side $b$ below.
Explicit normal equations square design $W^{1/2}B$ conditioning in $A$ when $\lambda=0$.
Non-positive-definite $A$ means nonunique minimizers.
At $\lambda=0$ this occurs for dependent $W^{1/2}B$ columns, such as bases supported only in unsampled regions.
At $\lambda>0$ it occurs exactly when nullspaces of $W^{1/2}B$ and $D_q$ share nonzero vectors.
Positive penalties link unsampled coefficients to neighbors, but sparse samples can still fail if coefficient sequences in $D_q$ nullspace, described below, vanish at all samples.
SciOxide does not add diagonal jitter and stops preparation with `DomainError`.

Coefficient differences generally differ from $\int(s^{(q)})^2dx$ on nonuniform knots.
Uniform knots give rough proportionality, but knot density, units, or common weight scaling change the meaning of equal $\lambda$.
As $\lambda\to\infty$, coefficient sequences approach the nullspace of $D_q$: polynomials of degree at most $q-1$ in index $j$.
These correspond to physical degree-at-most-$q-1$ polynomials only when Greville $\xi_j=(t_{j+1}+\dots+t_{j+p})/p$ is uniform in index, such as nonrepeated-end uniform knots.
Repeated endpoints or nonuniform knots generally have nonpolynomial limits.

## Fixed-Plan reuse and diagnostics

`PenalizedSplinePlan::new(basis, samples, weights, penalty)` fixes bases, positions, weights, and penalties and factors normal matrices.
Positions may be unsorted/duplicated but outside basis domains give `DomainError`.
Weight `None` assigns every sample unit weight.
Explicit weights must be finite nonnegative with at least one positive.
`build` performs preparation within caller `SolveScope`.
Band Cholesky charges one child iteration per column, allowing parent limits to stop factoring with `ConvergenceFailure`, or `ExecutionStopped` for cancellation/deadlines.
`preparation_bytes` returns a preparation heap bound without allocation.

`fit(&values)` returns `PenalizedSplineFit` whose `spline()` is fitted `BSpline`.
Real, complex, or array components solve separately with shared factors.
Prepare `fit_into` workspace `PenalizedSplineWorkspace` for component counts.
Requirements `PenalizedSplineRequirements` from `workspace_requirements(m)` passed to `check` return sufficiency `Capacity`; `try_reserve` grows in place.
After successful `fit_into`, workspace `diagnostics()` returns per-component diagnostics.
With `fit_into`, callers also provide coefficient output and reuse Plans with changed values.
Failed `fit_into` preserves output.

`SplineFitReport` aggregates components, maximizing backward errors; `SplineFitDiagnostics` stores component diagnostics.
`data_fidelity` is weighted squared residual $\sum_iw_i(s(x_i)-y_i)^2$; `penalty` weighted penalty $\lambda\|D_qc\|^2$.
`normal_residual` is normal-equation componentwise backward error

$$
\max_r\frac{|(Ac-b)_r|}{|b_r|+\sum_j|A_{rj}||c_j|}
$$

of machine-epsilon scale for backward-stable solves.
This diagnoses solves, not true-function errors or condition-amplified coefficient errors.
`PenalizedSplinePlan::diagonal_ratio` is minimum/maximum absolute normal diagonals, a cheap warning rather than condition estimate.

## Fit and roughness

The next cell smooths 41 samples $y_i=\sin(2\pi x_i)\pm0.08$ with cubic bases, 31 uniform internal knots and 35 controls, and second-difference penalties.
P-splines use many controls near sample count, controlling smoothness by penalties alone.
Perturbation signs alternate by sample.
This is a fixed verification signal prone to differentiation amplification, not stochastic measurement noise.
Input base strength $\lambda_0$ produces seven fits from $10^{-3}$ to $10^3$ times $\lambda_0$.
Outputs show data fidelity, the sum of squared coefficient differences recovered by dividing the reported penalty term by $\lambda$, normal-equation backward error, and maximum error against the true function $\sin(2\pi x)$.
The final independent check is available only for artificial data, measured on 401 points.

For exact minimizers on fixed bases/data, increasing strengths cannot increase roughness or decrease residuals.
Columns 2 and 3 follow these monotone trends.
Compare with perturbation squared sum $41\times0.08^2=0.2624$.
At default $\lambda_0=0.1$, weakest $\lambda=10^{-4}$ gives fit about 0.19 below that sum, following some perturbation.
Consequently roughness is large and true error reaches about 0.39.

At $\lambda=100$, data fidelity of 0.62 greatly exceeds the sum of squared perturbations; the curve suppresses the peaks of $\sin(2\pi x)$, increasing error again.
With $\lambda_0 = 1$ reaching $\lambda = 1000$, fit is 4.74 and true error 0.64; coefficients approach $D_2$ nullspace, linear index sequences.
Weakest $\lambda = 10^{-5}$ under $\lambda_0 = 0.01$ gives 0.41, nearly unchanged from $\lambda = 10^{-4}$, already following all perturbations.
Real data cannot directly determine true-error-minimizing penalties without truth.
Normal-equation backward errors remain about $10^{-16}$ regardless of strength, so the solve's backward error is sufficiently small.

## Adaptive residual targets and termination

Fit selection can use allowed residuals rather than fixed bases/penalties.
`AdaptiveSplineOptions` has two `SplineFitTarget` targets.
`Penalty(λ)` solves specified penalties once on initial bases without insertion.
`Residual(s)` inserts knots until the weighted residual sum of squares $R$ is at most $s + \tau$ (where $\tau$ is the residual tolerance), then seeks the largest penalty preserving this bound.
Default `Residual(0)` with absolute/relative machine epsilon requests nearly exact interpolation.
Absolute tolerance has squared-value units; match targets/tolerance to scales far from 1.
Detailed curves `fit_parametric_spline_with_options(points, parameterization, weights, options)` and spheres `fit_spherical_spline_with_options(samples, values, weights, options)` return models plus reports.
One-shot `fit_parametric_spline(points, residual, tolerance)` and `fit_spherical_spline(points, values, residual, tolerance)` use chord-length curves or scattered spheres, unit weights, default limits, and target `Residual(residual)`, returning target-achieving models only.
Early stopping gives `ConvergenceFailure` with `TruncationLimit` for knot/insertion limits, `Stagnation` for no insertable intervals, or `IterationLimit` for penalty limits.
Tolerance $(\tau_{\mathrm{abs}}, \tau_{\mathrm{rel}})$ is used as $\tau = \tau_{\mathrm{abs}} + \tau_{\mathrm{rel}} s$.
Near-$\varepsilon$ tolerances may exhaust 32 trials without finding $|R - s| \le \tau$ windows; smoothing $s > 0$ should use about $\tau_{\mathrm{rel}} = 10^{-5}$.
Curve penalties are fixed to second differences.

Residuals sum weighted squares over all real components, complex magnitudes squared, and samples, not maximum absolute errors.
Known independent noise variance $\sigma^2$ suggests target $s\approx m\sigma^2$ for $m$ samples, following Reinsch.
Automatic cross-validation/GCV penalty selection is unavailable.
If needed, construct `PenalizedSplinePlan` per penalty and compare held-out errors yourself.

Curves start with one cubic interval, endpoint parameter breaks, and four controls.
Thus target `Penalty(λ)` fits a single cubic to all samples by penalized least squares, or ordinary least squares at $\lambda=0$.
Residual targets sum weighted sample errors per break interval and split the largest with interior samples at their minimum/maximum midpoint.
Stop insertion without sampled interior intervals or at insertion/knot limits.

Penalty search starts at $\lambda=1$ and multiplies $\lambda$ by 16 while bounds hold.
Once a violating value appears, bisect against the accepted value.
Keep the last bound-satisfying fit; stop within target tolerance or when bisection cannot change floating-point values.
Monotone residuals in penalties let this bracket the acceptance boundary.
Target $s = 0$ skips searches and finishes at fit $\lambda = 0$.

`SplineFitLimits` sets knots default 128, insertions 64, penalty trials 32, and memory 64 MiB.
Excess-memory trials give `ResourceLimit` errors; knot/insertion/penalty limits stop through termination below.
`AdaptiveSplineOptions::with_budgets` iteration limits count one fit trial per iteration.
A rejected first trial has no model and gives `ConvergenceFailure`.
Intermediate non-positive-definite normal matrices, curve `DomainError`, or deficient designs, spherical `NumericalInstability`, stop with errors rather than termination reports.
Targets far below squared noise drive continued insertion toward such failures.

`AdaptiveSplineReport::termination` is the sole status; detailed forms return last accepted models at every termination.
`FixedPenalty` completes specified penalties; `ResidualSatisfied` meets residual bounds and finishes searches; only these satisfy `is_success()`.
`PenaltySearchLimit` meets bounds but exhausts penalty trials before approaching targets.
`RefinementLimit` and `NoAdmissibleKnot` stop before bounds through knot limits or missing insertable intervals.
`BudgetExhausted` reaches iteration limits: bounds unmet during insertion, met during penalty search.
Callers decide use from termination and `residual()`, `penalty()`, `refinements()`, `penalty_iterations()`, and `fit_trials()`.
`reciprocal_condition()` cheaply indicates final-system conditioning; small values warn but do not bound condition numbers.
`fit_parametric_spline_scoped` performs the detailed fit as nested `SolveScope`, obeying caller stops and charging trials against `options` limits.

## Parameters and curvature

Planar/spatial curves assign ordered parameter $u$ and fit coordinates as functions of $u$ with common knots.
Detailed `ParametricSplineFit` holds common-knot `spline()`, actual `parameters()`, and report `report()`.
`CurveParameterization::ChordLength` accumulates Euclidean sample distances over real components and normalizes to $[0,1]$.
Adjacent identical points have zero distance and nonincreasing parameters, rejected with `InvalidInput`.
`Supplied` uses caller finite, strictly increasing parameters in original units.
Uniform or centripetal cumulative square-root distances are not built-in; calculate them and pass to `Supplied`.
At least four samples are required.

Parameter choices change curve shapes.
Uniform parameters with unequal sample gaps can twist short intervals into overshoot or loops.
Chord lengths reflect gaps and often stabilize shape.

Planar $r(u)=(x(u),y(u))$ signed curvature is

$$
\kappa(u)=\frac{x'(u)y''(u)-y'(u)x''(u)}
{(x'(u)^2+y'(u)^2)^{3/2}}
$$


in parameter derivatives, invariant under orientation-preserving regular reparameterization.
It is undefined at zero speed $(x',y')$.
Derivatives of the fitted curve are returned with respect to the parameter, so the second derivative $d^2r/du^2$ alone must not be read as curvature.

## Arc smoothing and curvature checks

The next cell perturbs 41 angle-uniform points on radius-$r = 2$ m semicircles radially by alternating $\pm\delta$ ($\delta = 0.02$ m) and smooths them.
True curvature is everywhere $1/r = 0.5$ m⁻¹, independently checking positions and curvature.
Pass chord-length parameterization and target `Residual(s)` to `fit_parametric_spline_with_options`, setting $s = c\,m\delta^2$ to $c$ times the sum of squared perturbations $m\delta^2$ ($m = 41$).
Residual tolerances are $\tau_{\mathrm{abs}} = 0$ and $\tau_{\mathrm{rel}} = 10^{-5}$.
Also nearly interpolate the same points with default `Residual(0)`.

Inputs are multiplier $c$ and smoothing insertion limit `SplineFitLimits::with_max_refinements`.
Small limits stop before bounds with `RefinementLimit`.
Tables compare both terminations, residual $R$ [m²], penalty $\lambda$, inserted knots, penalty/fit trials, and 201-point maximum radius $\max|\,|r(u)| - r\,|$ and curvature $\max|\kappa(u) - 1/r|$ errors.
Charts plot error $|\kappa(u) - 1/r|$ against parameter $u$ logarithmically.
Outcome assesses smoothing termination with `is_success()`.

```rust
//| id: interp-parametric-arc-smoothing
//| ref: interp-parametric-arc-smoothing
//| title: "Smooth perturbed arcs to residual targets and compare curvature"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-interp]
//| inputs:
//|   residual_factor: {"type":"range","label":"Target multiplier c, s = c·mδ²","description":"Target/squared-noise mδ² ratio.","min":0.25,"max":4,"step":0.25,"value":1}
//|   max_refinements: {"type":"range","label":"Maximum knot insertions [count]","description":"Passed to smoothing SplineFitLimits::with_max_refinements.","min":1,"max":64,"step":1,"value":64}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"loose-target","label":"c = 4","description":"Fourfold targets strengthen penalties and move curves away from circles.","inputs":{"residual_factor":4}}
//|   - {"id":"tight-target","label":"c = 0.25","description":"Targets below perturbations add knots, follow noise, and give interpolation-sized curvature errors.","inputs":{"residual_factor":0.25}}
//|   - {"id":"refinement-limit","label":"One insertion maximum","description":"Stops before residual bounds with RefinementLimit.","inputs":{"max_refinements":1}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"fits","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":"ResidualSatisfied"}
//|       - {"artifact":"fits","path":"/rows/0/2","approx":{"value":0.0164,"rtol":1e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"fits","path":"/rows/0/7","range":{"max":0.01}}
//|       - {"artifact":"fits","path":"/rows/0/8","range":{"max":0.05}}
//|       - {"artifact":"fits","path":"/rows/1/2","range":{"max":1e-20}}
//|       - {"artifact":"fits","path":"/rows/1/8","range":{"min":10}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/2/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: loose-target
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"fits","path":"/rows/0/2","approx":{"value":0.0656,"rtol":1e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"fits","path":"/rows/0/7","range":{"min":0.03}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/2/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: tight-target
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"fits","path":"/rows/0/4","range":{"min":20}}
//|       - {"artifact":"fits","path":"/rows/0/8","range":{"min":10}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/2/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: refinement-limit
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"fits","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":"RefinementLimit"}
//|       - {"artifact":"fits","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"min":0.0165}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/2/state","equals":"partial"}
use scioxide_core::{RealScalar, Tolerance};
use scioxide_interp::{
    AdaptiveSplineOptions, CurveParameterization, Differentiable, ExtrapolationPolicy,
    Interpolant, ParametricSplineFit, SplineFitLimits, SplineFitTarget, TwiceDifferentiable,
    fit_parametric_spline_with_options,
};

let (radius, perturbation, count) = (2.0_f64, 0.02_f64, 41_u32);
// 41 points on radius-2 m semicircles with alternating radial ±δ
let points: Vec<[f64; 2]> = (0..count)
    .map(|i| {
        let angle = std::f64::consts::PI * f64::from(i) / f64::from(count - 1);
        let r = radius + if i % 2 == 0 { perturbation } else { -perturbation };
        [r * RealScalar::cos(angle), r * RealScalar::sin(angle)]
    })
    .collect();
let target = residual_factor * f64::from(count) * perturbation * perturbation;
let smoothing = AdaptiveSplineOptions::new()
    .with_target(SplineFitTarget::Residual(target))
    .with_residual_tolerance(Tolerance::new(0.0, 1e-5).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?)
    .with_limits(SplineFitLimits::new().with_max_refinements(max_refinements as usize));
let chord = CurveParameterization::ChordLength;
let smooth = fit_parametric_spline_with_options(&points, chord, None, smoothing)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
// Compare nearly interpolating default Residual(0)
let interpolating =
    fit_parametric_spline_with_options(&points, chord, None, AdaptiveSplineOptions::new())
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;

// Independent 201-point radii and signed κ = (x'y'' − y'x'')/|r'|³ against circles
let policy = ExtrapolationPolicy::Reject;
let profile = |fit: &ParametricSplineFit<f64, [f64; 2]>| -> Result<_, String> {
    let (mut curve, mut radial, mut curvature) = (Vec::new(), 0.0_f64, 0.0_f64);
    for j in 0..=200 {
        let u = f64::from(j) / 200.0;
        let [x, y] = fit.spline().evaluate([u], policy).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
        let [[dx, dy]] = fit.spline().gradient([u], policy).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
        let [[[ddx, ddy]]] = fit.spline().hessian([u], policy).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
        let speed = RealScalar::hypot(dx, dy);
        let kappa = (dx * ddy - dy * ddx) / (speed * speed * speed);
        radial = radial.max((RealScalar::hypot(x, y) - radius).abs());
        let error = (kappa - 1.0 / radius).abs();
        curvature = curvature.max(error);
        if error > 0.0 {
            curve.push([u, error]);
        }
    }
    Ok((curve, radial, curvature))
};
let fits = [("Smoothing", &smooth), ("Interpolation, Residual(0)", &interpolating)];
let mut rows = Vec::new();
let mut series = Vec::new();
for (name, fit) in fits {
    let (curve, radial, curvature) = profile(fit)?;
    let report = fit.report();
    rows.push((
        name,
        format!("{:?}", report.termination()),
        report.residual(),
        report.penalty(),
        report.refinements(),
        report.penalty_iterations(),
        report.fit_trials(),
        radial,
        curvature,
    ));
    series.push(serde_json::json!({ "name": name, "points": curve }));
}
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Fit" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Termination" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Residual R [m²]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Penalty λ [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Inserted knots [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Penalty trials [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "6", "label": "Fitting trials [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "7", "label": "Maximum radius error [m]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "8", "label": "Maximum curvature error [1/m]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(columns, rows, metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "fits" }));
// Use logarithmic curvature-error axes, omitting zeros
emit_line_chart!(
    &series,
    options = &serde_json::json!({
        "xLabel": "Chord-length u [-]",
        "yLabel": "Curvature error |κ(u) − 1/r| [1/m]",
        "yType": "log",
        "yMin": 1e-5,
        "yMax": 1e2
    })
);

let report = smooth.report();
let termination = report.termination();
let outcome = if termination.is_success() {
    Outcome::satisfied("Smoothing met targets and completed penalty searches")
} else {
    Outcome::partial("Smoothing stopped before targets").explanation(
        "The curve is the last accepted fit and may exceed targets; reconsider limits or targets.",
    )
};
emit_outcome!(
    outcome
        .status(format!("{termination:?}"))
        .diagnostic("Residual R", report.residual(), Some("m²"))
        .diagnostic("Target s", target, Some("m²"))
        .diagnostic("Fit trials", report.fit_trials() as f64, None)
);
```

At default $c = 1$, the target is $s = 41 \times 0.02^2 = 0.0164$ m²; smoothing meets the residual upper bound after inserting three knots.
Eighteen penalty trials select $\lambda = 0.0041$, reaching `ResidualSatisfied` after 22 total trials.
Residual $R = 0.01640$ m² differs from target within tolerance $10^{-5} s$.
Maximum radius error 0.0074 m is below half perturbation $\delta = 0.02$ m; curvature error 0.031 m⁻¹ is about 6% of truth.
Nearly interpolating curves use 37 knots to reach residual $10^{-29}$ m² but follow the perturbations and oscillate between samples, with radius error 0.054 m and curvature error 38 m⁻¹.

Residual targets near the noise sum of squares $m\sigma^2$ can yield curves that avoid following noise or excessively suppressing the true shape.

## Summary

Penalty strength $\lambda$ balances data fidelity and coefficient roughness; stronger penalties increase the residual sum of squares and reduce roughness.
Knots, weights, or units change equal-$\lambda$ effects; select strengths from validation or known noise.
Known noise variance also suggests $m\sigma^2$ in `SplineFitTarget::Residual` for automatic knots/penalties.
Only `FixedPenalty` and `ResidualSatisfied` succeed; otherwise inspect residuals and trials before using curves.
Parameterization changes shapes; curvature combines first and second derivatives.
Near-interpolation of perturbations can magnify curvature by orders; match smoothing targets to noise sums for curvature uses.
