Treating global fields as longitude/latitude rectangles creates seam and polar-coordinate degeneracy problems.
Every longitude at the north pole describes the same place.
Spherical bases must enforce that identity and longitude periodicity.
This page explains SciOxide coordinates, bases, fitting, derivatives, and integration.

## Angles and spherical area

`SphericalSamples` and `SphericalSpline` coordinates are ordered `[colatitude θ, longitude φ]` in rad.
Colatitude is north-pole angle $0\le\theta\le\pi$; geographic latitude is $\pi/2-\theta$.
Every finite longitude wraps to $[0,2\pi)$.
Specify scattered `Scattered` or gridded `Rectangular { theta, longitude }` samples.
Grid values are longitude-fastest, rowwise in colatitude, and axes finite and strictly increasing.
Rows at colatitude 0 or $\pi$, repeating poles across longitudes, and seam columns containing both $0$ and $2\pi$ are allowed.
Polar and seam duplicates merge into one sample with summed weights.

The unit-sphere area element is $dA=\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi$.
Uniform map averages overweight small-area polar regions.
Compute global means from $\overline T=(4\pi)^{-1}\int T\,dA$.
A radius-$R$ sphere scales integrals by $R^2$ without changing means.

## Bases with poles and periodic boundaries

Reproducing kernels and spherical-harmonic expansions are other established spherical interpolation methods.
Wahba's (1981) reproducing-kernel spherical splines use spherical Sobolev kernel $K$ in $s(\xi) = d + \sum_i c_i K(\xi_i \cdot \xi)$, one basis per sample, solving a sample-sized dense system.
scioxide-interp provides neither that reproducing-kernel method nor spherical-harmonic expansions.
SciOxide uses regression splines with basis counts fixed by breaks rather than samples.
Interior tensors combine cubic colatitude B-splines with periodic cubic longitude B-splines, using six polar modes nearby.
With normalized colatitude $u=\theta/\pi$ and cubic Hermite bases $h_{00},h_{01},h_{10},h_{11}$, one each for endpoint values and slopes, polar modes are

$$
h_{00}(u),\quad h_{01}(u),\quad
\pi h_{10}(u)\cos\phi,\quad \pi h_{10}(u)\sin\phi,\quad
\pi h_{11}(u)\cos\phi,\quad \pi h_{11}(u)\sin\phi
$$


$h_{00}$ equals 1 at north and $h_{01}$ at south, both longitude-independent, ensuring unique pole values.
$h_{10}$ and $h_{11}$ vanish at both poles with slopes at north and south respectively.
Near poles $\theta\cos\phi$ and $\theta\sin\phi$ are tangent-plane Cartesian coordinates; the four remaining modes represent tangent affine functions.

Remove interior tensor bases corresponding to two endpoint controls at each colatitude end.
Only removed bases have polar values or slopes, leaving zero polar values and colatitude slopes for interior bases.
Interior terms therefore do not alter pole values or tangent gradients, giving $C^1$ surfaces at poles.
Polar $C^1$ is distinct from arbitrary-order global smoothness.
Interior cubics are generally $C^2$.

`SphericalSplinePlan` takes colatitude breaks from $0$ to $\pi$ and longitude breaks from $0$ to $2\pi$.
Longitude requires at least four intervals.
For $N_\theta+1$ colatitude breaks and $N_\phi$ longitude intervals, mode count is $6+(N_\theta-1)N_\phi$, exposed by `mode_count()`.
Positive penalties add squared second differences of interior coefficients in periodic longitude and colatitude, leaving polar modes unpenalized.

Design matrices combine merged-sample and penalty rows.
Tall or square designs use column-pivoted QR least squares; wide designs use full-row-rank LQ minimum-norm interpolation.
Numerically deficient designs give `NumericalInstability` without added jitter.
For $r$ total merged/penalty rows and $c$ modes, design/factor storage is $O(rc)$ and factorization $O(rc\min(r,c))$.
Arbitrary scattered samples and global polar modes prevent thin per-axis band matrices.
`SplineFitLimits` memory limits are checked before allocation, with excess `ResourceLimit`.
Start with few breaks in memory-limited environments such as Wasm.

## Fitting and reuse

For fixed breaks and penalties, prepare factorization with `SphericalSplinePlan::new(theta_breaks, longitude_breaks, samples, weights, penalty, limits)` and obtain `SphericalSplineFit` through `fit`.
Its `spline()` is immutable `SphericalSpline`; `report()` is `AdaptiveSplineReport`; fixed-Plan termination is `FixedPenalty`.
Real, complex, or array values fit componentwise bitwise-identically to separate scalar fits.
`SphericalSplineFitRequirements` expresses coefficient and solve requirements by component count.
Reserve `SphericalSplineFitWorkspace` and `fit_into` coefficient output to reuse Plans with changed values.
After successful `fit_into`, workspace `residuals()` returns weighted squared sample residuals.
Queries instead use separate `SphericalSplineWorkspace` from interpolant `workspace()`.

`fit_spherical_spline_with_options(samples, values, weights, options)` selects breaks from targets, returning surfaces and reports in `SphericalSplineFit`.
One-shot `fit_spherical_spline(points, values, residual, tolerance)` solves target `Residual(residual)` for scattered unit-weight samples, returning target-achieving `SphericalSpline` only, otherwise `ConvergenceFailure`.
Start with polar-only bases, endpoint colatitude breaks, and four longitude intervals; add breaks one at a time in high-residual intervals.
Alternate colatitude and longitude, prioritizing colatitude until an interior row exists.
Targets, limits, and termination match [Smoothing and parametric splines](oxref:interp-adaptive-termination).
`fit_spherical_spline_scoped` performs the same detailed fit in nested caller scopes, obeying cancellation/deadlines and charging trial iterations against `options` work limits.
Inspect termination to check targets after adaptive refinement or penalty searches.

## Reproduction versus approximation

Constants belong to the basis as $h_{00}+h_{01}=1$, so sufficient rank reproduces them within rounding.
$\cos\theta$ has pole values $\pm1$ and zero slopes; $h_{00}-h_{01}$ plus interior terms approximates it, but finitely many cubics generally cannot represent it exactly.
The next cell simultaneously fits constants and $\cos\theta$ as two components, comparing nonsample errors.
Samples form 25 colatitude rows including poles and 12 longitudes, merging into 278 points.
Fix four longitude intervals and zero penalty.
Input $N_\theta$ controls colatitude intervals.

Fit twice.
First construct surfaces with `fit` for error measurements.
Second grow one-component workspace to two-component requirements with `try_reserve` and write coefficients alone through `fit_into`.
Check `fit_into` and `fit` coefficients bitwise and read maximum sample weighted squared residual from `residuals()`.
The table gives mode count, constant reproduction error, maximum $\cos\theta$ error, sample residual sum of squares, maximum weighted squared residual over samples, north-pole error of the $\cos\theta$ component, and the coefficient difference between two fits.
Measure maxima at 41 inter-row points on longitude-0.37 rad meridian.

```rust
//| id: interp-spherical-harmonic-recovery
//| ref: interp-spherical-harmonic-recovery
//| title: "Distinguish spherical constant reproduction and harmonic approximation"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-interp]
//| inputs:
//|   divisions: {"type":"range","label":"Colatitude intervals N_θ","description":"Place N_θ + 1 uniform breaks on [0, π].","min":2.0,"max":8.0,"step":1.0,"value":4.0}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"coarse","label":"N_θ = 2","description":"One interior row gives cos θ errors reaching 10⁻².","inputs":{"divisions":2}}
//|   - {"id":"fine","label":"N_θ = 8","description":"Halving widths reduces cos θ errors to order 10⁻⁵.","inputs":{"divisions":8}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"recovery","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":18}
//|       - {"artifact":"recovery","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"max":1e-13}}
//|       - {"artifact":"recovery","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":5.5728e-4,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"recovery","path":"/rows/0/7","equals":0}
//|   - case: coarse
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"recovery","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":10}
//|       - {"artifact":"recovery","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":1.3210e-2,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|   - case: fine
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"recovery","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":34}
//|       - {"artifact":"recovery","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":3.5016e-5,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"recovery","path":"/rows/0/7","equals":0}
use scioxide_core::{ExecutionContext, RealScalar};
use scioxide_interp::{
    ExtrapolationPolicy, Interpolant, SphericalSamples, SphericalSplinePlan, SplineFitLimits,
};

let divisions = divisions as usize;
let pi = std::f64::consts::PI;
// 25 colatitude rows including poles, 12 longitudes; longitude-fastest values
let theta: Vec<_> = (0..=24).map(|i| pi * f64::from(i) / 24.0).collect();
let longitude: Vec<_> = (0..12).map(|j| pi * f64::from(j) / 6.0).collect();
let values: Vec<_> = theta
    .iter()
    .flat_map(|&t| longitude.iter().map(move |_| [1.0, RealScalar::cos(t)]))
    .collect();
let breaks: Vec<_> = (0..=divisions)
    .map(|i| pi * i as f64 / divisions as f64)
    .collect();
let plan = SphericalSplinePlan::new(
    &breaks,
    &[0.0, pi / 2.0, pi, 1.5 * pi, 2.0 * pi],
    SphericalSamples::Rectangular {
        theta: &theta,
        longitude: &longitude,
    },
    None,
    0.0,
    SplineFitLimits::new(),
)
.map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let fit = plan.fit(&values).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;

// Second same-Plan fit: grow one-component workspace to two-component requirements
let requirements = plan.workspace_requirements(2).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mut workspace = plan.workspace(1).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
workspace
    .try_reserve(&requirements)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mut coefficients = vec![0.0; requirements.coefficients()];
let report = plan
    .fit_into(
        &values,
        coefficients.as_mut_slice(),
        &mut workspace,
        ExecutionContext::sequential_native(),
    )
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let coefficient_change = coefficients
    .iter()
    .zip(fit.spline().coefficients())
    .map(|(a, b)| (a - b).abs())
    .fold(0.0_f64, f64::max);
let largest_residual = workspace.residuals().iter().copied().fold(0.0_f64, f64::max);

// Measure constant and cos θ errors at 41 inter-row points
let policy = ExtrapolationPolicy::Reject;
let mut errors = [0.0_f64; 2];
for i in 0..41 {
    let t = pi * (f64::from(i) + 0.5) / 41.0;
    let [constant, harmonic] = fit
        .spline()
        .evaluate([t, 0.37], policy)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    errors[0] = errors[0].max((constant - 1.0).abs());
    errors[1] = errors[1].max((harmonic - RealScalar::cos(t)).abs());
}
// Query north pole at longitude 2 rad to check longitude independence
let [_, north] = fit
    .spline()
    .evaluate([0.0, 2.0], policy)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "N_θ", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Modes", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Maximum constant error [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Maximum cos θ error [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Residual sum, fit_into report [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Maximum sample residual [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "6", "label": "North-pole cos θ error [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "7", "label": "fit/fit_into coefficient difference [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    vec![(
        divisions,
        plan.mode_count(),
        errors[0],
        errors[1],
        report.residual(),
        largest_residual,
        (north - 1.0).abs(),
        coefficient_change
    )],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "recovery" })
);
```

Constant errors remain about $10^{-14}$ regardless of interval count, reproducing functions in the basis's span within rounding.
Maximum $\cos\theta$ errors are $1.32\times10^{-2}$ at $N_\theta=2$, $5.57\times10^{-4}$ at $N_\theta=4$, and $3.50\times10^{-5}$ at $N_\theta=8$.
Halving widths reduces errors 16–24 times, consistent with smooth cubic approximation $O(h^4)$.
Residual sums cover all 278 points and two components, distinct from nonsample maxima.
The maximum weighted squared residual over samples at default $N_\theta = 4$ is $3.1\times10^{-7}$, so residuals are small at every sample.
Pole values are unique in longitude but fitted by least squares, so polar errors remain approximation-sized.
`fit_into` and `fit` coefficients differ by zero, confirming identical workspace fitting arithmetic.

## Derivatives and area integrals

`gradient` and `hessian` return coordinate derivatives with respect to angles $(\theta,\phi)$, rather than physical gradients on the tangent plane.
`derivative(point, orders, policy)` supports through second order per axis; order at least 3 gives `InvalidInput`.
On a radius-$R$ sphere away from the poles, the physical gradient components are $R^{-1}\partial_\theta T$ and $(R\sin\theta)^{-1}\partial_\phi T$.
At poles, $\sin\theta$ vanishes; do not divide by it directly.
Read tangent gradients from affine functions encoded by polar-mode coefficients.
Colatitude follows policies; `Clamp` gives zero colatitude derivatives outside $[0,\pi]$.
No policy extends colatitude beyond $[0,\pi]$.

`integral(lower, upper, policy)` integrates angular box $[\theta_0,\theta_1]\times[\phi_0,\phi_1]$ including area $\sin\theta$.
Check finite endpoints and lower ≤ upper per axis first.
Colatitude limits follow policy, clamped to the sphere under `Clamp`.
Longitude limits do not wrap; widths above $2\pi$ integrate more than one revolution.
Global integration uses `integral([0.0, 0.0], [PI, 2.0 * PI], policy)`, returning $4\pi$ for constant 1.
Integration costs scale with modes and components.

## Adaptive fitting and termination

The next cell perturbs samples of this smooth unit-sphere field:

$$
f(\theta, \phi) = \cos\theta + \tfrac{1}{2}\sin^2\theta\cos 2\phi
$$

and smooths with residual targets supplied to `fit_spherical_spline_with_options`.
$f$ equals Cartesian $z + (x^2 - y^2)/2$, smooth even at poles, with zero global integral.
Samples are 192 points, 12 nonpolar colatitudes $\theta_i = (i + 1/2)\pi/12$ and 16 longitudes, with checkerboard perturbations $\pm\delta$ ($\delta = 0.02$).
Target $s = c\,m\delta^2$ is $c$ times squared perturbations $m\delta^2$ ($m = 192$); residual tolerances are $\tau_{\mathrm{abs}} = 0$ and $\tau_{\mathrm{rel}} = 10^{-5}$.
Inputs are multiplier $c$ and insertion limit.

The table shows termination, residual $R$, selected penalty $\lambda$, count of inserted breaks, penalty-attempt and fit-attempt counts, each axis's knot-vector length, and order of continuity at the poles.
The second independently checks maximum $f$ error at 400 nonsample Fibonacci points and global `integral` integral against exact zero.
Outcome tests termination with `is_success()`.

```rust
//| id: interp-spherical-adaptive-fit
//| ref: interp-spherical-adaptive-fit
//| title: "Smooth perturbed spherical samples to residual targets"
//| run: button
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-interp]
//| inputs:
//|   residual_factor: {"type":"range","label":"Target multiplier c, s = c·mδ²","description":"Target/squared-perturbation ratio; below 1 causes numerical design rank deficiency.","min":1,"max":4,"step":0.25,"value":1}
//|   max_refinements: {"type":"range","label":"Break-insertion limit [count]","description":"Passed to SplineFitLimits::with_max_refinements.","min":1,"max":64,"step":1,"value":64}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"loose-target","label":"c = 2","description":"Twice-noise targets meet bounds with one break and stronger smoothing, making f errors exceed δ.","inputs":{"residual_factor":2}}
//|   - {"id":"refinement-limit","label":"One insertion maximum","description":"Stops before residual bounds with RefinementLimit.","inputs":{"max_refinements":1}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"report","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"ResidualSatisfied"}
//|       - {"artifact":"report","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":0.0768,"rtol":1e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"report","path":"/rows/0/8","equals":1}
//|       - {"artifact":"check","path":"/rows/0/0","range":{"max":0.01}}
//|       - {"artifact":"check","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"min":-1e-4,"max":1e-4}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/2/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: loose-target
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"report","path":"/rows/0/3","equals":1}
//|       - {"artifact":"check","path":"/rows/0/0","range":{"min":0.02}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/2/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: refinement-limit
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"report","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"RefinementLimit"}
//|       - {"artifact":"report","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"min":0.0769}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/2/state","equals":"partial"}
use scioxide_core::{RealScalar, Tolerance};
use scioxide_interp::{
    AdaptiveSplineOptions, ExtrapolationPolicy, Integrable, Interpolant, SphericalSamples,
    SplineFitLimits, SplineFitTarget, fit_spherical_spline_with_options,
};

let pi = std::f64::consts::PI;
let field = |t: f64, p: f64| {
    let s = RealScalar::sin(t);
    RealScalar::cos(t) + 0.5 * s * s * RealScalar::cos(2.0 * p)
};
let delta = 0.02_f64;
// Nonpolar 12 × 16 grid, longitude-fastest values, checkerboard perturbations
let theta: Vec<f64> = (0..12).map(|i| (f64::from(i) + 0.5) * pi / 12.0).collect();
let longitude: Vec<f64> = (0..16).map(|j| f64::from(j) * pi / 8.0).collect();
let mut values = Vec::new();
for (i, &t) in theta.iter().enumerate() {
    for (j, &p) in longitude.iter().enumerate() {
        let sign = if (i + j) % 2 == 0 { 1.0 } else { -1.0 };
        values.push(field(t, p) + sign * delta);
    }
}
let target = residual_factor * values.len() as f64 * delta * delta;
let options = AdaptiveSplineOptions::new()
    .with_target(SplineFitTarget::Residual(target))
    .with_residual_tolerance(Tolerance::new(0.0, 1e-5).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?)
    .with_limits(SplineFitLimits::new().with_max_refinements(max_refinements as usize));
let grid = SphericalSamples::Rectangular {
    theta: &theta,
    longitude: &longitude,
};
let fit = fit_spherical_spline_with_options(grid, &values, None, options)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let report = fit.report();
let termination = report.termination();
let [theta_knots, longitude_knots] = report.knot_counts();
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Termination" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Residual R [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Penalty λ [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Inserted breaks [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Penalty trials [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Fitting trials [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "6", "label": "Colatitude knots", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "7", "label": "Longitude knots", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "8", "label": "Polar continuity order", "type": "integer", "nullText": "None" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    vec![(
        format!("{termination:?}"),
        report.residual(),
        report.penalty(),
        report.refinements(),
        report.penalty_iterations(),
        report.fit_trials(),
        theta_knots,
        longitude_knots,
        report.pole_continuity()
    )],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "report" })
);

// Independent check: 400 nonsample Fibonacci errors and zero global integral
let policy = ExtrapolationPolicy::Reject;
let golden = pi * (3.0 - RealScalar::sqrt(5.0_f64));
let mut largest = 0.0_f64;
for k in 0..400 {
    let z = 1.0 - (2.0 * f64::from(k) + 1.0) / 400.0;
    let (t, p) = (RealScalar::acos(z), golden * f64::from(k));
    let value = fit.spline().evaluate([t, p], policy).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    largest = largest.max((value - field(t, p)).abs());
}
let integral = fit
    .spline()
    .integral([0.0, 0.0], [pi, 2.0 * pi], policy)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let check_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Maximum f error at 400 unused points [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Perturbation δ [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Global ∫ s dA [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    check_columns,
    vec![(largest, delta, integral)],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "check" })
);

let outcome = if termination.is_success() {
    Outcome::satisfied("Spherical fit met residual targets and completed penalty search")
} else {
    Outcome::partial("Spherical fit stopped before residual targets").explanation(
        "The surface is the last accepted fit and may exceed targets; reconsider limits or targets.",
    )
};
emit_outcome!(
    outcome
        .status(format!("{termination:?}"))
        .diagnostic("Residual R", report.residual(), None)
        .diagnostic("Target s", target, None)
        .diagnostic("Fit trials", report.fit_trials() as f64, None)
);
```

At default $c = 1$, the target is $s = 192 \times 0.02^2 = 0.0768$; fitting meets the residual upper bound after inserting 17 breaks, then 14 penalty trials select $\lambda = 0.0013$ and termination `ResidualSatisfied`.
Final knot-vector lengths are 17 for colatitude and 19 for longitude, with order of continuity 1 at the poles ($C^1$).
Maximum nonsample $f$ error 0.0075 is below perturbation $\delta = 0.02$, showing substantial noise removal.
Global integral $-1.1\times10^{-5}$ differs from zero by little compared with error 0.0075.

At $c = 2$, one break meets residual bounds and penalty searches raise $\lambda$, flattening surfaces and giving $f$ error 0.044 above $\delta$.
Insertion limit 1 stops at residual 0.0943 above bound 0.0768 with `RefinementLimit` and partial outcome.
The input minimum $c = 1$ avoids smaller targets on this grid, which keep inserting until numerical design rank deficiency gives `NumericalInstability` (`IllConditioned`).
Adaptive searches do not backtrack from rank-deficient trials; do not set targets below the estimated noise sum of squares $m\sigma^2$.
Default 32 repeated dense QR trials take several seconds in wasm.

## Summary

Spherical coordinates are `[colatitude θ, longitude φ]` in rad; convert geographic latitudes with $\pi/2-\theta$.
Six polar modes enforce unique pole values and tangent-plane $C^1$ joins; periodic interior tensors do not alter pole values or gradients.
Constants in the basis are reproduced within rounding, while general functions such as $\cos\theta$ are approximated with errors roughly proportional to the fourth power of interval width; check errors at unused points.
Fit and query workspaces have distinct types; fixed Plans reuse factors with changed values.
Gradients are angular derivatives; convert them to physical gradients using $R$ and $\sin\theta$, and read them from the polar modes at the poles.
Match adaptive residual targets to estimated noise sums; non-`ResidualSatisfied` surfaces remain unmet-target estimates.
