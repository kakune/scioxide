This table covers multivariate B-splines, fitting without exact sample agreement (smoothing), rational splines, and spherical splines.
The shared component is checked univariate basis `BSplineBasis`, one per tensor axis.
B-spline coefficients follow control-point order, row-major multiindices with the last axis fastest, followed by each control value's real components.
Spherical coefficients follow mode order, with six polar modes first.

Entry points depend on the goal.
Use one-shot `BSpline::interpolate` for one tensor-grid interpolation with not-a-knot bases; use `TensorSplineInterpolationPlan` to choose bases or repeatedly solve new values on the same grid.
Use `PenalizedSplinePlan` for noisy univariate smoothing with fixed knots and penalties.
For automatic knot and penalty selection from residual targets, use `fit_parametric_spline` for curves or `fit_spherical_spline` for spheres, receiving only target-achieving models.
Detailed `fit_parametric_spline_with_options` and `fit_spherical_spline_with_options` let callers choose parameters, geometry, weights, and limits and read reports.
Fixed spherical breaks and penalties use `SphericalSplinePlan`; positive-weight rational curves and surfaces use `Nurbs`.

Plans (`*Plan`) factor once, returning models with `fit` (penalized and spherical results pair reports in `PenalizedSplineFit` or `SphericalSplineFit`), or writing caller coefficients with `fit_into` and reserved workspace.
Check `workspace_requirements(dimension)` requirements with `check` and grow with `try_reserve`.
Reserved `fit_into` allocates nothing and preserves output on errors.
Fitted models are `BSpline` or `SphericalSpline` and evaluate through shared traits.

Read fits through reports.
`SplineFitReport` and `SplineFitDiagnostics` are observations of the original objective, not forward-error guarantees.
Detailed and scoped adaptive forms return models in `Ok` even with unmet targets; assess success with `is_success()` of `AdaptiveSplineReport::termination()`.
Only limits reached before the first trial give `ConvergenceFailure` there; one-shot functions give `ConvergenceFailure` for unmet targets.
Non-positive-definite normal matrices give `DomainError`, spherical rank deficiency `NumericalInstability`, and memory-limit excess `ResourceLimit`.

## Summary

Use `TensorSplineInterpolationPlan` to interpolate grids and `PenalizedSplinePlan` to smooth noise, preparing per-axis `BSplineBasis` first.
With `PenalizedSplinePlan`, callers choose knots and $\lambda$ by comparing data-fit and penalty terms in `SplineFitReport`.
For automatic curve or sphere knots and penalties, one-shot functions return target-achieving models only; detailed forms set limits with `SplineFitLimits` and require successful `AdaptiveSplineReport::termination()` before use.
For repeated fits with the same design and changed values, prepare Plan and workspace once and repeat `fit_into`.
