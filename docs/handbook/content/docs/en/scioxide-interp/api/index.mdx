`scioxide-interp` exposes univariate polynomial, rational, and piecewise interpolation; tensor, smoothing, NURBS, and spherical splines; planar grids; scattered RBF/Delaunay methods; local polynomials/RBFs; and Gaussian processes.
This guide identifies task-based entry points and groups 115 public items into five tables.
Item-name links point to the version 0.1.0 API reference on docs.rs.

## Task-based entry points

| Goal | Entry points | Conventions and cautions | Table |
|---|---|---|---|
| Polynomial interpolation through samples | `BarycentricInterpolator`, `NewtonInterpolator` | Geometry determines errors; Chebyshev barycentric construction costs $O(n)$ | [One-dimensional interpolation](one-dimensional/) |
| Match values and derivatives | `HermiteInterpolator` | Degree at most $2n-1$ at $n$ nodes | [One-dimensional interpolation](one-dimensional/) |
| One value and error estimate | `NevilleInterpolator::evaluate_with_error` | Final corrections estimate errors, without bounds | [One-dimensional interpolation](one-dimensional/) |
| Preserve monotonicity | `PchipInterpolator` | $C^1$ curves monotone on ranges where the corresponding component's samples are monotone | [One-dimensional interpolation](one-dimensional/) |
| Continuous-curvature curves | `CubicSpline::new`, `CubicSpline::with_boundary`, `SplineBoundary` | `new` uses not-a-knot; pass other boundaries through `with_boundary`. Periodic endpoints are checked for agreement | [One-dimensional interpolation](one-dimensional/) |
| Control continuity with knots | `BSpline`, `KnotSide` | Explicit one-sided knot derivatives | [One-dimensional interpolation](one-dimensional/) |
| Repeated definite integrals | `prepare_antiderivative`, `PreparedSplineAntiderivative`, `PreparedPolynomialAntiderivative` | Prepared `integral_into` allocates nothing | [One-dimensional interpolation](one-dimensional/) |
| Rational approximation/interpolation | `aaa`, `aaa_with_options`, `FloaterHormannInterpolator`, `PadeApproximant` | `aaa` errors on nonconvergence; inspect detailed termination and poles in either form | [Rational interpolation](rational/) |
| Smooth noisy samples | `PenalizedSplinePlan`, `fit_parametric_spline` | Callers choose knots/penalties for `PenalizedSplinePlan`; `fit_parametric_spline` uses residual targets | [Tensor, smoothing, and spherical splines](fitted-splines/) |
| Tensor-grid or spherical data | `BSpline::interpolate`, `TensorSplineInterpolationPlan`, `SphericalSplinePlan` | Tensor band factors per axis versus dense spherical designs have different costs | [Tensor, smoothing, and spherical splines](fitted-splines/) |
| Rectangular planar grids | `BilinearInterpolator`, `BicubicInterpolator` | Values row-major `ny × nx` | [Two-dimensional, scattered, and local models](multidimensional/) |
| Scattered interpolation | `RbfInterpolator::new`, `interpolate_scattered`, `SimplexLinearInterpolator`, `CloughTocherInterpolator` | RBF kernels are arguments, with conditioning reports and bounding-rectangle domains; Delaunay domains are hulls | [Two-dimensional, scattered, and local models](multidimensional/) |
| Local models and kriging | `LocalModelPlan`, `GaussianProcessModel` | Predictive variance is statistical uncertainty | [Two-dimensional, scattered, and local models](multidimensional/) |
| Evaluate univariate points | `Univariate` (`value_at`, `values_at`, `derivative_at`, `integrate`) | Outside gives `DomainError`; use policy forms to extend | [Shared contracts and batch evaluation](contracts-and-batches/) |
| Evaluate many points | `Interpolant::evaluate_many`, `BatchEvaluator::evaluate_into`, `BatchWorkspace` | Failed batches preserve output | [Shared contracts and batch evaluation](contracts-and-batches/) |

## Type relationships

- Capabilities: `Interpolant<S, D>` values, `Differentiable` gradients, `TwiceDifferentiable` Hessians, and `Integrable` box integrals.
  Supply coordinates as `[S; D]`; interpolants implement only supported traits.
  Barycentric, Newton, and Hermite integrals use antiderivatives prepared with `prepare_antiderivative` rather than `Integrable`.
- Construction: `new(data)` takes samples alone with fixed defaults.
  `with_options(data, options)` takes settings implementing `Default` by value; univariate/grid `SampleOptions` stores node separation.
  Named constructors such as `CubicSpline::with_boundary` select other preparation kinds.
  `aaa`, `fit_parametric_spline`, `fit_spherical_spline`, and `CloughTocherInterpolator::new` give `ConvergenceFailure` for unmet iterative targets; detailed forms return models with reports.
- Extrapolation: `ExtrapolationPolicy` is mandatory per policy-taking evaluation, without defaults.
  Univariate scalar `Univariate` accepts no policy and rejects outside domains like `Reject`.
  `report(point, policy, workspace)` creates coordinate reports only when requested.
- Preparation/workspace: construct model-matched `workspace()` and pass to `evaluate_into` or similar.
  Reserved forms allocate nothing and preserve output on errors.
  Owned `evaluate`, `gradient`, `evaluate_many` and scalar `Univariate` may allocate output or workspace.
  Fit Plans and AAA workspace sized by `RationalRequest` use `workspace_requirements` requirements checked by `check` and grown by `try_reserve`.
- Value types: componentwise scalar methods directly accept affine real, complex, array, and vector values.
  AAA, Padé, and `BarycentricRational` require field arithmetic and use value type `NumericScalar`.

## Minimal executable example

The unique degree-at-most-two polynomial through $(0, 0)$, $(1, 1)$, and $(2, 4)$ is $p(x) = x^2$.
Analytic checks are therefore $p(0.5) = 0.25$ and $\int_0^2 p(x)\,dx = 8/3$.
The next cell constructs `NewtonInterpolator::new(x, y)`, evaluates inside $[0, 2]$ with `value_at` and integrates with antiderivatives prepared by `prepare_antiderivative`, comparing analytic values in table `checks`.
Then evaluate outside $x = 2.5$ through scalar `value_at` and policy-taking `Interpolant::evaluate` in table `outside`.
Empty value columns indicate errors, with kind `e.kind()` in the last column.

```rust
//| id: interp-api-quadratic-calculus
//| ref: interp-api-quadratic-calculus
//| title: "Check three-point Newton interpolation with values and areas"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-interp]
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"checks","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":0.25,"atol":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"checks","path":"/rows/1/1","approx":{"value":2.6666666666666665,"rtol":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"checks","path":"/rows/1/3","range":{"max":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"outside","path":"/rows/0/3","equals":"DomainError"}
//|       - {"artifact":"outside","path":"/rows/1/3","equals":"DomainError"}
//|       - {"artifact":"outside","path":"/rows/2/2","approx":{"value":4,"atol":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"outside","path":"/rows/3/3","equals":"DomainError"}
//|       - {"artifact":"outside","path":"/rows/4/2","approx":{"value":6.25,"rtol":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"outside","path":"/rows/5/2","approx":{"value":6.25,"rtol":1e-14}}
use scioxide_core::SciOxideError;
use scioxide_interp::{ExtrapolationPolicy, Interpolant, NewtonInterpolator, Univariate};

// Newton quadratic through (0, 0), (1, 1), (2, 4), domain [0, 2]
let curve = NewtonInterpolator::new(vec![0.0_f64, 1.0, 2.0], vec![0.0, 1.0, 4.0])
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let value = curve.value_at(0.5).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let primitive = curve.prepare_antiderivative().map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let integral = primitive
    .integral(0.0, 2.0, ExtrapolationPolicy::Reject)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;

// Independent checks: errors against p(0.5) = 0.25 and ∫₀² x² dx = 8/3
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let digits = serde_json::json!({ "significantDigits": 17 });
let check_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Quantity" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "SciOxide value [-]", "type": "number", "format": digits },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Analytic value [-]", "type": "number", "format": digits },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Absolute error [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    check_columns,
    vec![
        ("p(0.5)", value, 0.25, (value - 0.25).abs()),
        (
            "∫₀² p(x) dx",
            integral,
            8.0 / 3.0,
            (integral - 8.0 / 3.0).abs()
        ),
    ],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "checks" })
);

// Outside x = 2.5; put either value or error kind in the table
let split = |result: Result<f64, SciOxideError>| match result {
    Ok(v) => (Some(v), None),
    Err(e) => (None, Some(format!("{:?}", e.kind()))),
};
let x = 2.5;
let attempts = [
    ("value_at(x)", "None, same as Reject", curve.value_at(x)),
    (
        "evaluate",
        "Reject",
        curve.evaluate([x], ExtrapolationPolicy::Reject),
    ),
    (
        "evaluate",
        "Clamp",
        curve.evaluate([x], ExtrapolationPolicy::Clamp),
    ),
    (
        "evaluate",
        "BoundedContinue（0.25）",
        curve.evaluate(
            [x],
            ExtrapolationPolicy::BoundedContinue { max_distance: 0.25 },
        ),
    ),
    (
        "evaluate",
        "BoundedContinue（1）",
        curve.evaluate(
            [x],
            ExtrapolationPolicy::BoundedContinue { max_distance: 1.0 },
        ),
    ),
    (
        "evaluate",
        "MethodDefined",
        curve.evaluate([x], ExtrapolationPolicy::MethodDefined),
    ),
];
let rows: Vec<_> = attempts
    .into_iter()
    .map(|(call, policy, result)| {
        let (v, kind) = split(result);
        (call, policy, v, kind)
    })
    .collect();
let outside_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Call" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Extrapolation policy" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Returned value [-]", "type": "number", "format": digits, "nullText": "—" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Error kind", "nullText": "—" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    outside_columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "outside" })
);
```

Values and integrals match analytic results within rounding, 0 for values and $4.4 \times 10^{-16}$ for integrals.
Outside $x = 2.5$ gives `DomainError` under `value_at` and `Reject`, without extension.
`Clamp` projects to boundary $x = 2$ and returns $p(2) = 4$.
`BoundedContinue` extends only when distance $0.5$ meets the limit; limit $0.25$ gives `DomainError` and limit $1$ gives $2.5^2 = 6.25$.
`MethodDefined` extends without distance limits, returning the same $6.25$.
Specify policies per call when extended values are required.

## Reading results, errors, and diagnostics

Failures return `SciOxideError`; kinds through `e.kind()` (`SciOxideErrorKind`), details through `e.view()`.
Common kinds follow.

| Kind | Main causes |
|---|---|
| `InvalidInput` | Empty or insufficient samples, nonincreasing nodes, node/scattered duplicates under `NodeSeparation`, invalid parameters (negative penalties, invalid degrees, nonpositive weights/scales, negative/nonfinite `max_distance`), reversed integration limits, or AAA samples below support limits |
| `DimensionMismatch` | Node/value lengths or shapes mismatch; insufficient output or workspace |
| `DomainError` | Outside queries under `Reject` or scalar forms; non-`Clamp` natural-neighbor policies outside hulls; undefined knot/projection-switch derivatives (`Undefined`); Padé poles; non-positive-definite penalized/parametric normal matrices or GP covariance |
| `ResourceLimit` | Query, preparation, or fitting memory exceeds limits |
| `Singular` | RBF/local polynomial/local RBF/GP rank or condition failures; singular cubic/tensor collocation systems; penalized solve failures; degenerate Clough–Tocher vertex systems; nonpositive GP leave-one-out precision |
| `NonFinite` | Nonfinite input (`Input`), intermediate (`Intermediate`), or unrepresentable result (`Output`) |
| `NumericalInstability` | Rational poles (Bulirsch–Stoer and `Breakdown` of `BarycentricRational`), excess Padé coefficient residuals, spherical numerical rank deficiency, or nearly degenerate simplex queries |
| `ConvergenceFailure`, `ExecutionStopped` | Unmet iterations in `aaa`, `fit_parametric_spline`, `fit_spherical_spline`, or `CloughTocherInterpolator::new`; work-rejected trials before accepted states; cancellation or deadlines |

Input-validation kinds are shared throughout.
Empty required collections give `InvalidInput` (`Empty`), insufficient counts `OutOfRange`, and corresponding-length mismatches `DimensionMismatch`.
Nonfinite inputs give `NonFinite` (`Input`); decreasing or duplicate nodes/points `InvalidInput` (`Inconsistent`); negative weights `OutOfRange`.
All-zero weights give `Inconsistent`.

Even detailed `Ok` results require fit-report inspection.
`termination()` of `RationalFit::report()`, `AdaptiveSplineReport::termination()`, and `CloughTocherReport::termination()` are sole termination authorities; detailed forms return accepted models even below targets.
`RbfConditioning`, `LocalFitReport`, `SplineFitDiagnostics`, and `RationalDiagnostics` are estimates or observations, not error guarantees.

## Summary

Choose task-appropriate interpolants from the entry table, then check supported value, gradient, Hessian, and integral traits.
Use scalar forms such as `value_at` inside univariate domains; outside, specify policies and read extended values with coordinate reports and distances.
Prepare primitives or workspace once for repeated integrals or evaluations, and process many points with `evaluate_many` or `BatchEvaluator`.
Classify errors with `kind()` and inspect fit termination and reports before use.
