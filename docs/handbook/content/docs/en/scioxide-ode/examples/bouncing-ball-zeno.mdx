A bouncing ball combines smooth motion equations with discontinuous velocity changes at floor impacts.
Each bounce reduces speed by a fixed factor, making intervals geometric and infinitely many impacts accumulate at finite time.
This page repeats terminal-event stops, velocity reversal, and restart through `DormandPrince5`, comparing impact times with closed forms.
Quadratic-in-time solutions have no integration error, leaving event-time tolerance to determine timing error.
Once bounce intervals fall below tolerance or floating-point spacing, impacts can be missed or time can stop advancing.

## Free fall and restitution

With height $y$ [m], upward velocity $v$ [m/s], and gravitational acceleration $g = 9.81$ m/s², airborne motion is

$$
y' = v, \qquad v' = -g
$$


Release from rest at height $h_0$ gives first-impact time and speed

$$
t_1 = \sqrt{\frac{2 h_0}{g}}, \qquad v_1 = g t_1 = \sqrt{2 g h_0}
$$


For pre-impact velocity $v^-$, post-impact velocity is $v^+ = -e v^-$; $0 < e < 1$ is the coefficient of restitution.
After impact $n$, upward speed is $e^n v_1$ and time to the next impact $2 e^n v_1 / g$.
Impact times therefore satisfy

$$
t_{n+1} = t_n + \frac{2 e^n v_1}{g}
$$

and summing the geometric series gives

$$
t_n = t_1 + \frac{2 e v_1}{g} \cdot \frac{1 - e^{n-1}}{1 - e}, \qquad
t_\infty = \lim_{n \to \infty} t_n = t_1 + \frac{2 e v_1}{g (1 - e)}
$$


Infinitely many impacts occur before $t_\infty$.
Infinitely many events accumulating at finite time is the Zeno phenomenon.
A real ball rests on the floor at $t_\infty$, but this model defines no motion after $t_\infty$.

## Stopping and restarting at impact

Impact is where event function $g_{\mathrm{ev}}(t, y, v) = y$ changes from positive to nonpositive.
Adding `EventDirection::Falling` and `with_terminal(true)` to `EventFn::new(|_, s| Ok(s[0]))` stops integration at impact.
Stopped time and state belong to `OdeState`, while `OdeSolveReport::termination` returns `TerminalEvent`.
Replace state $v$ with $-e v$ and restart at the same time through `OdeState::reset`.
Initial-time $g_{\mathrm{ev}} = 0$ is not counted as an event, preventing immediate repeated stops ([Dense output and event detection](../../algorithms/dense-output-and-events/)).
Reuse the same workspace (`OdeWorkspace`) for every restart.

Locate event times on accepted-step dense output with Alefeld–Potra–Shi enclosure (TOMS 748).
The settings `OdeEventOptions::with_localization` supply time tolerance (`StepTolerance`) and iteration limits.
Default absolute and relative tolerances are both $4\varepsilon$ ($\varepsilon$ is machine epsilon of `f64`).

One `DormandPrince5` step is exact for quadratic solutions apart from rounding; fourth-order dense output also reproduces quadratics exactly.
Timing error therefore comes from root-bracket width, controlled by time tolerance.
A time offset $\delta t$ shifts impact velocity by $g\,\delta t$ and next flight time $2e\lvert v \rvert/g$ by $2e\,\delta t$.
Impact height $y$ is also not exactly zero, retaining a value of order $\lvert v \rvert \delta t$.
The next cell restarts with this height unchanged, without resetting it to zero.

## Zeno behavior and computational breakdown

As $n$ grows, flight time $2 e^n v_1/g$ approaches time tolerance or floating-point spacing near $t$ (about $8.9 \times 10^{-16}$ s at $t \approx 4$ s).
Either of two failures follows.

- A negative residual impact height makes the restarted ball rise and fall from below the floor.
  If upward and downward crossings share one step, endpoint signs agree, hiding both events and letting the ball pass through the floor.
- Tolerance larger than flight time can prevent impact times advancing, repeating impacts at the same time.

Both reflect computation limits rather than the model.
In practice, switch to a resting-state model once rebound speed falls below a threshold.

“Default (4ε)” uses values from `OdeEventOptions::new()`.

Each interval ends at $2 t_\infty$; stop if that endpoint is reached without impact.
Also stop if an impact time does not advance beyond the preceding impact.

```rust
//| id: ode-bouncing-ball
//| ref: ode-bouncing-ball
//| title: "Stop at impact, reverse velocity, and compare impact times with closed form"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-linalg, scioxide-ode]
//| exports: [height, errors, flights, limit_time, localization_time]
//| inputs:
//|   restitution: {"type":"range","label":"Restitution coefficient e [-]","description":"Factor shrinking bounce intervals.","min":0.5,"max":0.95,"step":0.05,"value":0.8}
//|   drop_height: {"type":"range","label":"Initial height h₀ [m]","min":0.5,"max":10,"step":0.5,"value":1}
//|   localization: {"type":"select","label":"Event-time tolerance [s]","description":"Defaults: absolute and relative both 4ε.","options":["Default (4ε)","1e-12","1e-9","1e-6","1e-3"],"value":"Default (4ε)"}
//|   max_bounces: {"type":"range","label":"Maximum impacts","min":10,"max":200,"step":10,"value":60}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"loose-localization","label":"Tolerance 10⁻⁶ s","description":"Timing differences grow to order 10⁻⁶ s; shrinking intervals eventually stop time advancing.","inputs":{"localization":"1e-6","max_bounces":200}}
//|   - {"id":"many-bounces","label":"200 impacts","description":"Even default tolerances miss impacts once flight times fall below about 10⁻¹¹ s.","inputs":{"max_bounces":200}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":60}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"max":1e-13}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: loose-localization
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"min":1e-7,"max":1e-4}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"partial"}
//|   - case: many-bounces
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":"ReachedEnd"}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"partial"}
use scioxide_core::{Budget, EvaluationRole, StepTolerance};
use scioxide_linalg::Vector;
use scioxide_ode::{
    DormandPrince5, EventDirection, EventFn, EventFunction, OdeEventOptions, OdeFn, OdeMethod,
    OdeOptions, OdeOutputSchedule, OdeProblem, OdeState, OdeTermination, OdeTrajectory,
};

let sci2 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 2 });
let digits12 = serde_json::json!({ "significantDigits": 12 });
let (e, gravity) = (restitution, 9.81);
let falling = OdeFn::new(2, move |_: f64, s: &[f64], f: &mut [f64]| {
    f[0] = s[1];
    f[1] = -gravity;
    Ok(())
})
.map_err(|err| err.to_string())?;
let ground = EventFn::new(|_: f64, s: &[f64]| Ok(s[0]))
    .with_direction(EventDirection::Falling)
    .with_terminal(true);
let events: [&dyn EventFunction<f64>; 1] = [&ground];
// Closed forms: t₁ = √(2h₀/g), v₁ = g t₁, t∞ = t₁ + 2e v₁ / (g(1 − e)).
let t1 = (2.0 * drop_height / gravity).sqrt();
let v1 = gravity * t1;
let t_inf = t1 + 2.0 * e * v1 / (gravity * (1.0 - e));
let (event_options, absolute) = match localization.as_str() {
    "Default (4ε)" => (OdeEventOptions::new(), 4.0 * f64::EPSILON),
    text => {
        let tol: f64 = text.parse().map_err(|_| "Cannot read tolerance")?;
        let step = StepTolerance::new(tol, 0.0).map_err(|err| err.to_string())?;
        let limit = Budget::try_from(64).map_err(|err| err.to_string())?;
        (OdeEventOptions::new().with_localization(step, limit), tol)
    }
};
let problem = OdeProblem::new(&falling, 2.0 * t_inf).with_events(&events);
let method = DormandPrince5::new();
let base = OdeOptions::new().with_event_options(event_options);
let mut workspace = method
    .workspace(&problem, &base)
    .map_err(|err| err.to_string())?;
let mut state = OdeState::new(0.0, Vector::from_vec(vec![drop_height, 0.0]));

let (mut height, mut errors, mut flights) = (Vec::new(), Vec::new(), Vec::new());
let (mut exact, mut previous, mut bounces, mut exact_hits) = (t1, 0.0, 0_usize, 0_usize);
let (mut max_error, mut rhs, mut event_calls) = (0.0_f64, 0, 0);
let mut last = OdeTermination::ReachedEnd;
for n in 1..=max_bounces as usize {
    // Plot the first 30 flights at 16 subdivisions each (times beyond terminal events are not emitted).
    let flight = if n == 1 {
        t1
    } else {
        2.0 * state.values()[1].abs() / gravity
    };
    let schedule = if n <= 30 {
        let start = state.time();
        let times = (1..=16).map(|k| start + flight * f64::from(k) / 16.0);
        OdeOutputSchedule::times(times.filter(|&t| t < 2.0 * t_inf).collect())
    } else {
        OdeOutputSchedule::FinalOnly
    };
    let options = base.clone().with_output_schedule(schedule);
    let mut path = OdeTrajectory::new();
    let report = method
        .advance(
            &problem,
            &options,
            &mut state,
            &mut path,
            &mut workspace,
            scioxide_core::ExecutionContext::sequential(),
        )
        .map_err(|err| err.to_string())?;
    rhs += report.counts().provider(EvaluationRole::RightHandSide);
    event_calls += report.event_search().map_or(0, |s| s.evaluations());
    last = report.termination().ok_or("No termination status")?;
    if n <= 30 {
        let points = path.times().iter().zip(path.states());
        height.extend(points.map(|(&t, s)| [t, s.as_slice()[0]]));
    }
    if last != OdeTermination::TerminalEvent || state.time() <= previous {
        break;
    }
    bounces = n;
    previous = state.time();
    // Independent check: difference from closed-form t_n.
    let gap = (state.time() - exact).abs();
    max_error = max_error.max(gap);
    if gap > 0.0 {
        errors.push([n as f64, gap]);
    } else {
        exact_hits += 1;
    }
    flights.push([n as f64, 2.0 * e.powi(n as i32) * v1 / gravity]);
    exact += 2.0 * e.powi(n as i32) * v1 / gravity;
    // Reverse velocity and restart from the event state, without zeroing height.
    let (t, y, v) = (state.time(), state.values()[0], state.values()[1]);
    state
        .reset(t, &[y, -e * v])
        .map_err(|err| err.to_string())?;
}
export!(height, height);
export!(errors, errors);
export!(flights, flights);
export!(limit_time, t_inf);
export!(localization_time, absolute);
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Detected impacts", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Final interval termination status" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Maximum time difference [s]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Zero-difference impacts", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Last impact time [s]", "type": "number", "format": digits12 },
    { "key": "5", "label": "t∞ [s]", "type": "number", "format": digits12 },
    { "key": "6", "label": "Right-hand-side evaluations", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "7", "label": "Event-function evaluations", "type": "integer" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    vec![(
        bounces,
        last.to_string(),
        max_error,
        exact_hits,
        previous,
        t_inf,
        rhs,
        event_calls,
    )],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "summary" })
);

let outcome = if bounces == max_bounces as usize {
    Outcome::satisfied(format!("Found all {bounces} impacts"))
} else if last == OdeTermination::TerminalEvent {
    Outcome::partial(format!("Time stopped advancing at impact {}", bounces + 1))
        .explanation("Flight time became shorter than time tolerance.")
} else {
    Outcome::partial(format!(
        "Reached the interval endpoint without finding impact {}",
        bounces + 1
    ))
    .explanation("The ball passed through the floor. Final-interval values do not solve the model.")
};
emit_outcome!(
    outcome
        .status(last.to_string())
        .diagnostic("Maximum time difference", max_error, Some("s"))
        .diagnostic("Detected impacts", bounces as f64, None)
);
```

With defaults ($e = 0.8$, $h_0 = 1$ m, tolerance $4\varepsilon$, limit 60), $t_1 = 0.4515$ s and $t_\infty = 4.0637128$ s; all 60 impacts are found.
Maximum closed-form time difference is $9.8 \times 10^{-15}$ s, about 11 floating-point spacings at $t \approx 4$ s.
Six impacts have exactly zero difference; the remainder are of accumulated-rounding magnitude.
For 60 impacts, 1074 right-hand-side and 973 event evaluations suffice, with two to seven steps per flight.

Time tolerances $10^{-12}$, $10^{-9}$, $10^{-6}$, and $10^{-3}$ s give maximum differences $5.4 \times 10^{-13}$, $1.0 \times 10^{-9}$, $2.1 \times 10^{-6}$, and $3.4 \times 10^{-3}$ s, within 0.5–3.4 times tolerance.
Without integration error, differences come from event localization and carry into subsequent flights on restart.
At tolerance $10^{-6}$ s and limit 200, impacts through 62 are found, but impact 63 does not advance time.
Flight time after impact 62 is about $9 \times 10^{-7}$ s, below tolerance $10^{-6}$ s.

At default tolerance and limit 200, differences through impact 124 stay below $1.3 \times 10^{-14}$ s, but impact 125 is missed; the ball passes through the floor and reaches $2 t_\infty$ (`ReachedEnd`).
Flight time after impact 124 is about $9 \times 10^{-13}$ s; upward and downward crossings share one step, hiding the event as described above.
Final status `ReachedEnd` is successful, but this interval does not solve the model.
At $e = 0.5$, faster interval shrinkage causes the same failure at impact 40.

## Summary

For state-determined times such as impacts, stop on terminal events, modify state with `OdeState::reset`, and restart with the same workspace, independently of step grids.
For quadratic solutions, `DormandPrince5` integration and dense output are exact; impact timing error follows `OdeEventOptions::with_localization` time tolerance, around $10^{-14}$ s at default $4\varepsilon$.
Near tolerance or floating-point spacing, impact times can cease advancing or events can be missed, ending with `ReachedEnd`.
Even a successful `ReachedEnd` requires comparing detected counts and final states with the model.
For Zeno models, provide a model-level cutoff, such as switching to rest below a rebound-speed threshold.
