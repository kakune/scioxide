Differential evolution (DE) searches a population for global candidates without derivatives (Storn and Price, 1997).
It handles functions without gradients, with noise, or with discontinuities, deriving search step sizes from population differences rather than externally supplied steps.
This page derives one DE generation and explains SciOxide's `DifferentialEvolution` settings, stopping, and per-generation diagnostics.
DE does not claim global optimality; none of its terminations is successful.
General limitations are in [Global-search limits and basin-hit probabilities](../global-search/).

## Differential-evolution algorithm

Maintain population $\{\mathbf{x}_1, \dots, \mathbf{x}_N\}$ of $N$ individuals in finite box $[\mathbf{l}, \mathbf{u}] \subset \mathbb{R}^d$.
SciOxide stores the population in normalized coordinates $z_j = (x_j - l_j)/(u_j - l_j)$ mapping the box affinely to unit box $[0, 1]^d$, performing mutation, crossover, and bound handling there.
Evaluate objectives after conversion back to original coordinates.
The initial population uses user-supplied $\mathbf{x}_0$ as its first individual and uniform in-box samples for the rest.

Each generation applies mutation, crossover, and selection to each individual $\mathbf{x}_i$.

### Mutation through vector differences

Mutation forms vector $\mathbf{v}_i$ by adding randomly selected individuals' differences multiplied by $F > 0$.
Standard DE/rand/1 uniformly chooses mutually distinct indices $r_1, r_2, r_3$, also different from $i$, and sets

$$
\mathbf{v}_i = \mathbf{x}_{r_1} + F (\mathbf{x}_{r_2} - \mathbf{x}_{r_3})
$$


Initially, widely scattered individuals give large difference vectors and broad exploration.
As individuals gather in one valley, differences shrink and search focuses within it.
This automatic step adaptation to population spread is characteristic of DE.

`DeStrategy` selects four strategies.
$\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{best}}$ is the population's best individual; parentheses give the strategy's minimum population size.

| Strategy | Mutation vector | Minimum population |
|---|---|---|
| `Rand1` (default) | $\mathbf{x}_{r_1} + F(\mathbf{x}_{r_2} - \mathbf{x}_{r_3})$ | 4 |
| `Best1` | $\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{best}} + F(\mathbf{x}_{r_1} - \mathbf{x}_{r_2})$ | 4 |
| `Rand2` | $\mathbf{x}_{r_1} + F(\mathbf{x}_{r_2} - \mathbf{x}_{r_3}) + F(\mathbf{x}_{r_4} - \mathbf{x}_{r_5})$ | 6 |
| `CurrentToBest1` | $\mathbf{x}_i + F(\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{best}} - \mathbf{x}_i) + F(\mathbf{x}_{r_1} - \mathbf{x}_{r_2})$ | 4 |

Best-individual strategies concentrate the population sooner, refining valleys faster while more easily missing other valleys.

### Binomial crossover and bound handling

Binomial crossover mixes components of parent $\mathbf{x}_i$ and mutation vector $\mathbf{v}_i$ to form trial $\mathbf{u}_i$.

$$
u_{ij} = \begin{cases} v_{ij} & U_{ij} < C_R \text{ or } j = j_{\mathrm{rand}} \\ x_{ij} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}
$$

$C_R \in [0, 1]$ is crossover probability, $U_{ij}$ uniform on $[0, 1)$, and $j_{\mathrm{rand}}$ a uniformly chosen index per individual.
Component $j_{\mathrm{rand}}$ always comes from the mutation vector, so even $C_R = 0$ takes at least one mutation coordinate.
Small $C_R$ changes few coordinates, suiting coordinate-separable functions.
$C_R$ near 1 changes all coordinates together, suiting correlated variables.

Mutation components may leave the unit interval.
`DifferentialEvolutionBoundary` chooses clipping to an endpoint (`Clip`, default) or repeated boundary reflection as needed (`Reflect`).
`Clip` tends to accumulate points at endpoints and can bias searches when global minima lie inside the box.

### One-to-one greedy selection

Replace the parent if the trial objective is no greater.

$$
\mathbf{x}_i \leftarrow \begin{cases} \mathbf{u}_i & f(\mathbf{u}_i) \le f(\mathbf{x}_i) \\ \mathbf{x}_i & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}
$$

Individual objectives never increase, so neither does the population best across generations.
The population best equals the best point evaluated so far.
Replacement on ties allows movement across flat regions.

`UpdatingMode` determines replacement timing.
With default `Immediate`, accepted trials affect later individuals' mutation in the same generation; `Deferred` replaces together at generation end, so every trial uses the initial population.

A generation costs $N$ objective evaluations and $O(Nd)$ arithmetic, with $O(Nd)$ workspace.
DE has no general convergence guarantee to global minima; it returns the best observed point.

## Stopping and results

At each generation's start, compare maximum $f_{\max}$ minus minimum $f_{\min}$ population objectives with tolerance;

$$
f_{\max} - f_{\min} \le \epsilon_{\mathrm{abs}} + \epsilon_{\mathrm{rel}} \max(|f_{\min}|, |f_{\max}|)
$$

the condition gives termination `PopulationStagnation`.
Tolerance is `ResidualTolerance` in `with_fitness_tolerance`, default $\epsilon_{\mathrm{abs}} = \epsilon_{\mathrm{rel}} = \sqrt{\varepsilon}$.
This only says population objectives have become similar; it also holds when the population collapses to a nonminimum point.
Reaching the generation limit gives `SearchBudgetCompleted`.
Neither is successful; results hold the best finite observed point.

Evaluation limits, cancellation, or deadlines after the first evaluation also return the best point and stopping reason.
Stops before the first evaluation give error `ConvergenceFailure` or `ExecutionStopped`.

## DifferentialEvolution settings and calls

`DifferentialEvolutionOptions::default()` uses `Rand1`, $F = 0.8$, $C_R = 0.9$, `Clip`, `Immediate`, generation limit 1000, population size $\max(15d, 4)$, seed 42, and sequential execution.
Change with `with_strategy`, `with_mutation_factor`, `with_crossover_rate`, `with_boundary`, `with_updating`, `with_max_generations`, `with_population_size`, `with_seed`, `with_fitness_tolerance`, `with_time_limit`, `with_execution`.
Setting functions do not validate values.

`DifferentialEvolution::new(options)` checks all settings together.
NaN/infinite $F$ or $C_R$ gives `NonFinite`; $F$ outside $(0, 2]$, $C_R$ outside $[0, 1]$, or population below the strategy minimum gives `InvalidInput`.
Nonsequential policy combined with `Immediate` is also rejected with `InvalidInput`.

`workspace(dimension)` prepares population and generation-report workspace `DifferentialEvolutionWorkspace`; `minimize_into(problem, x0, output, workspace, scope)` solves.
Query requirements through `dimension()`, `population()`, and `generations()` of `DifferentialEvolutionWorkspaceRequirements` returned by `workspace_requirements(dimension)`.
`minimize(problem, x0)` prepares workspace internally and returns results.
Wrong `x0` length gives `DimensionMismatch`, NaN/infinite entries `NonFinite`, and out-of-box entries `InvalidInput`.
NaN or infinite objective values also stop with `NonFinite`.

`DifferentialEvolutionWorkspace::generations()` returns `DifferentialEvolutionGeneration` for each generation completed in the last call.
Generation reports hold index `generation()`, best/worst objectives `best_fitness()` and `worst_fitness()`, counts of replacing/nonreplacing trials `accepted()` and `rejected()`, boundary-corrected coordinates `repaired_coordinates()`, and cumulative evaluations `counts()`.

Randomness comes from one sequence seeded by `with_seed`, giving identical results for identical settings and seed.
To evaluate trials in parallel, choose nonsequential policy and `UpdatingMode::Deferred` with `with_execution`, and construct the problem with `BoundedObjective::new_sync`.
Trials are generated and selected in individual order, so results do not depend on worker count.

One-shot `minimize_global(f, &bounds, seed)` constructs default DE from bound pairs `&[(lo, hi)]` and a seed, using the box midpoint for `x0`.
`minimize_global` returns results for `PopulationStagnation` and `SearchBudgetCompleted` too, turning other unsuccessful terminations into errors.
Returned termination is unsuccessful; treat the best point as a candidate for local refinement.

## Generation acceptance and spread on Himmelblau

Himmelblau $f(x, y) = (x^2 + y - 11)^2 + (x + y^2 - 7)^2$ has 4 local minima (all $f = 0$) in box $[-5, 5]^2$.
This cell uses 20 individuals, 60 generations, DE/rand/1, and binomial `DifferentialEvolution`, varying difference multiplier $F$, crossover probability $C_R$, and seed.
Outputs include best/worst generation objectives on logarithmic axes, a table of termination and acceptance rates, and an outcome.
Acceptance rate is replacing trials divided by all trials; “Initial” and “Final” in the table use the first and last 5 generations.
Independently check the final point by distances to the 4 known minima.

```rust
//| id: optimize-de-generation-diagnostics
//| ref: optimize-de-generation-diagnostics
//| title: "Observe generation acceptance and spread with varying difference multiplier and crossover"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-optimize]
//| inputs:
//|   differential_weight: {"type":"range","label":"Difference multiplier F [-]","description":"Change the population difference-vector multiplier.","min":0.1,"max":1.8,"step":0.1,"value":0.8}
//|   crossover_rate: {"type":"range","label":"Crossover probability CR [-]","description":"Probability of taking a mutation-vector coordinate.","min":0,"max":1,"step":0.05,"value":0.7}
//|   seed: {"type":"range","label":"Random seed [-]","description":"Change random sequences for initialization and mutation.","min":1,"max":100,"step":1,"value":42}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"small-weight","label":"F = 0.1","description":"Small steps give high acceptance 0.68, but contraction causes PopulationStagnation at generation 41 with objective only 1.4×10⁻⁴.","inputs":{"differential_weight":0.1}}
//|   - {"id":"large-weight","label":"F = 1.8","description":"Mutation often leaves the box; corrected coordinates rise to 877, final-5-generation acceptance is 0.09, and objective 4.3×10⁻².","inputs":{"differential_weight":1.8}}
//|   - {"id":"full-crossover","label":"CR = 1","description":"All coordinates come from mutation; objective falls to 2.9×10⁻¹⁰ and distance to minimum (3, 2) to 4.3×10⁻⁶.","inputs":{"crossover_rate":1}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"generations","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"SearchBudgetCompleted"}
//|       - {"artifact":"generations","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":60}
//|       - {"artifact":"generations","path":"/rows/0/3","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"generations","path":"/rows/0/7","equals":1220}
//|       - {"artifact":"generations","path":"/rows/0/8","equals":0}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/2/state","equals":"partial"}
//|   - case: small-weight
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"generations","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"PopulationStagnation"}
//|       - {"artifact":"generations","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":0.68,"atol":0.005}}
//|   - case: large-weight
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"generations","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"SearchBudgetCompleted"}
//|       - {"artifact":"generations","path":"/rows/0/6","equals":877}
//|   - case: full-crossover
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"generations","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"SearchBudgetCompleted"}
//|       - {"artifact":"generations","path":"/rows/0/3","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-4}}
use scioxide_core::{Bounds, Budget, ExecutionContext};
use scioxide_optimize::{
    BoundedObjective, DifferentialEvolution, DifferentialEvolutionGeneration,
    DifferentialEvolutionOptions, ObjectiveFn, OptimizeResult, OptimizeTermination,
};

let himmelblau = ObjectiveFn::new(2, |p: &[f64]| {
    let first = p[0] * p[0] + p[1] - 11.0;
    let second = p[0] + p[1] * p[1] - 7.0;
    Ok(first * first + second * second)
});
let bounds = Bounds::new(vec![(Some(-5.0), Some(5.0)); 2]).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let problem = BoundedObjective::new(&himmelblau, &bounds).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let options = DifferentialEvolutionOptions::default()
    .with_population_size(Budget::try_from(20).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?)
    .with_mutation_factor(differential_weight)
    .with_crossover_rate(crossover_rate)
    .with_max_generations(Budget::try_from(60).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?)
    .with_seed(seed.round() as u64);
let solver = DifferentialEvolution::new(options).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mut work = solver.workspace(2).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mut out = OptimizeResult::empty();
let scope = ExecutionContext::sequential();
solver
    .minimize_into(&problem, &[0.0, 0.0], &mut out, &mut work, scope)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;

// Best/worst generation objectives. Omit nonpositive values from logarithmic axes and show their count in the table.
let gens = work.generations();
let series = |value: fn(&DifferentialEvolutionGeneration<f64>) -> f64| -> Vec<[f64; 2]> {
    gens.iter()
        .filter(|g| value(g) > 0.0)
        .map(|g| [g.generation() as f64, value(g)])
        .collect()
};
let best = series(|g| g.best_fitness());
let worst = series(|g| g.worst_fitness());
let dropped = 2 * gens.len() - best.len() - worst.len();
emit_line_chart!(
    &vec![
        serde_json::json!({ "name": "Population best", "points": best, "style": { "showSymbol": true } }),
        serde_json::json!({ "name": "Population worst", "points": worst, "style": { "showSymbol": true } })
    ],
    options = &serde_json::json!({
        "xLabel": "Generation [-]",
        "yLabel": "Objective [-]",
        "yType": "log",
        "xMin": 0.0,
        "xMax": 60.0
    })
);
// Acceptance = replacing trials / all trials. None without generations.
let rate = |gs: &[DifferentialEvolutionGeneration<f64>]| -> Option<f64> {
    let accepted: usize = gs.iter().map(|g| g.accepted()).sum();
    let trials: usize = gs.iter().map(|g| g.accepted() + g.rejected()).sum();
    (trials > 0).then(|| accepted as f64 / trials as f64)
};
let head = &gens[..gens.len().min(5)];
let tail = &gens[gens.len().saturating_sub(5)..];
let repaired: usize = gens.iter().map(|g| g.repaired_coordinates()).sum();
// Independent check: minimum distance to the 4 known minima.
let minima = [
    (3.0, 2.0),
    (-2.805118, 3.131312),
    (-3.779310, -3.283186),
    (3.584428, -1.848126),
];
let x = out.x();
let distance = minima
    .iter()
    .map(|&(a, b)| (x[0] - a).hypot(x[1] - b))
    .fold(f64::INFINITY, f64::min);
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let fixed2 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 2 });
let na = "No generations";
emit_table_with_columns!(
    serde_json::json!([
        { "key": "0", "label": "Termination reason" },
        { "key": "1", "label": "Completed generations [-]", "type": "integer" },
        { "key": "2", "label": "Final objective [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
        { "key": "3", "label": "Minimum distance to a local minimum [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
        { "key": "4", "label": "Initial acceptance rate [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed2, "nullText": na },
        { "key": "5", "label": "Final acceptance rate [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed2, "nullText": na },
        { "key": "6", "label": "Boundary-corrected coordinates [count]", "type": "integer" },
        { "key": "7", "label": "Objective evaluations [count]", "type": "integer" },
        { "key": "8", "label": "Points omitted from logarithmic axes [count]", "type": "integer" }
    ]),
    vec![(
        out.termination().to_string(),
        gens.len(),
        out.fun(),
        distance,
        rate(head),
        rate(tail),
        repaired,
        out.fun_evals(),
        dropped
    )],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "generations" })
);
// Both terminations are unsuccessful; use partial and put the stopping reason in the heading.
let headline = match out.termination() {
    OptimizeTermination::PopulationStagnation => "Stopped with similar population objectives",
    _ => "Generation limit reached",
};
emit_outcome!(
    Outcome::partial(format!("{headline}. The final point is a global-minimum candidate"))
        .status(out.termination().to_string())
        .diagnostic("Final objective", out.fun(), None)
        .diagnostic("Minimum distance to a local minimum", distance, None)
        .diagnostic("Completed generations", gens.len() as f64, None)
);
```

At default $F = 0.8$, $C_R = 0.7$, and seed 42, all 60 generations are used and termination is `SearchBudgetCompleted`.
There are 1220 objective evaluations: 20 initial individuals plus 1200 across 60 generations.
Final objective is $2.8 \times 10^{-7}$ and distance to nearest local minimum $(3, 2)$ is $1.1 \times 10^{-4}$.
Acceptance is 0.23 in the first 5 generations and 0.29 in the last 5.
The best value descends in nonincreasing steps; the worst falls from 311 at generation 1 to $3.6 \times 10^{-5}$ at generation 60, reducing objective spread.
All 4 Himmelblau minima have $f = 0$, so reduced objective spread alone does not reveal whether the population occupies one valley.

At $F = 0.1$, small steps give first-5-generation acceptance 0.68.
But the population contracts early, reaching `PopulationStagnation` at generation 41 with objective only $1.4 \times 10^{-4}$.
At $F = 1.8$ mutation often leaves the box; the number of coordinates corrected at the boundary rises from the default 219 to 877, acceptance over the last 5 generations is 0.09, and the objective is $4.3 \times 10^{-2}$.
At $C_R = 0$ each trial changes only 1 coordinate and objective remains $2.7 \times 10^{-2}$.
At $C_R = 1$ it falls to $2.9 \times 10^{-10}$; for this seed and coordinate-coupled Himmelblau, simultaneous all-coordinate trials reduce the objective more.
Seed 1 gives objective $9.1 \times 10^{-3}$ and seed 100 $4.2 \times 10^{-6}$; even identical settings depend on randomness.
Every termination is unsuccessful; treat final points as candidates for local refinement.

## Summary

DE repeats population-difference mutation, binomial crossover, and one-to-one greedy selection, narrowing search without increasing its best objective.
$F$ controls exploration breadth and boundary corrections; $C_R$ controls the fraction of coordinates changed together; both affect acceptance and final objectives.
Neither `PopulationStagnation` nor `SearchBudgetCompleted` is successful; check final points through known properties, local refinement, and replication with other seeds.
Read generation acceptance, population spread, and boundary corrections from `DifferentialEvolutionWorkspace::generations()`.
For one default solve, pass a box and seed to `minimize_global`; create `DifferentialEvolution` to choose strategy or population.
