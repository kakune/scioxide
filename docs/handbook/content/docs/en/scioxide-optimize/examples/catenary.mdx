Suspend a uniform chain of length $L$ between two supports at the same height.
Assume the chain neither stretches nor resists bending, and find the shape minimizing potential energy.
Let support separation be $D$, linear density $\rho$ and gravitational acceleration $g$.
Dividing length by $D$, energy by $\rho gD^2$ and tension by $\rho gD$ gives dimensionless quantities with support separation, linear density and gravitational acceleration all equal to 1.
All coordinates, lengths and tensions below use these dimensionless units; the only remaining input is ratio $L/D$.
We require $L>D$; $L=D$ is the singular limit of a fully taut chain.

## Continuum equilibrium

For a section cut from the chain, endpoint tensions balance its weight.
Resolve tension into horizontal component $H$ and vertical component $V$; with no horizontal external force, $H$ is constant throughout the chain.
The vertical component increases by weight 1 per arc length $s$, giving $dV/ds=1$.
Using $V=Hy'$ and $ds=\sqrt{1+y'^2}\,dx$ gives

$$
y''=\frac1H\sqrt{1+y'^2}
$$


Set $a=H$; the symmetric solution with supports at $(0,0)$ and $(1,0)$ is $y=a\cosh((x-1/2)/a)-a\cosh(1/(2a))$.
This translates catenary $y=a\cosh(x/a)+c$ to place its lowest point at $x=1/2$ and chooses constant $c$ so both endpoints have height 0.
Arc-length condition $L=2a\sinh(1/(2a))$ uniquely determines $a$; tension at each position is $T=H\sqrt{1+y'^2}=a\cosh((x-1/2)/a)$.
For $L\to1$, $a\to\infty$ and the chain becomes fully taut; increasing $L$ decreases $a$, the horizontal tension.

## Discrete model and tension

Divide the chain into $n$ straight segments of length $\ell=L/n$, fixing endpoints.
Place each segment's mass $\ell$ at its midpoint; interior node $i$ receives half from each adjacent segment, giving potential energy $U=\ell\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}y_i$.
Use the $2(n-1)$ interior-node coordinates $x_i,y_i$ as variables and impose $n$ equality constraints $c_i=\|r_{i+1}-r_i\|-\ell=0$ on the segments.
Here $r_i=(x_i,y_i)$ is the position of node $i$.
Having at least as many variables as constraints requires $n\ge2$; here we use $n\ge4$.

Define Lagrangian $\mathcal L=U+\sum_i\lambda_i c_i$.
The segment-length gradient is tangent unit vector $t_i=(r_{i+1}-r_i)/\|r_{i+1}-r_i\|$, so stationarity condition $\nabla_{r_k}\mathcal L=0$ at interior node $k$ becomes

$$
\lambda_k t_k-\lambda_{k-1}t_{k-1}=(0,\ell)
$$


This has the same form as $T_kt_k-T_{k-1}t_{k-1}=(0,\ell)$, balancing weight $\ell$ with segment tensions $T_k$ acting tangentially on node $k$.
Thus multiplier $\lambda_i$ equals segment tension $T_i$ in these dimensionless units.
`SqpState::multipliers` returns multipliers with convention $\nabla\mathcal L=\nabla U+\sum_i\lambda_i\nabla c_i$, so tensions are read as positive values.
Using squared lengths as constraints multiplies $\nabla c_i$ by $2\|r_{i+1}-r_i\|$, so multipliers can no longer be read directly as tensions.

The horizontal component of this equation makes $\lambda_kt_{k,x}$ constant across segments in the discrete model too.
Discrete-chain horizontal tension approximates continuous-solution $a$ but does not equal it, because the model joins straight segments and concentrates weight at nodes.

## SQP iterations and computational cost

Each SQP iteration solves a QP subproblem consisting of a quadratic model with a damped-BFGS approximation to the Lagrangian Hessian and linearized constraints, updating the step and multipliers.
An Armijo-type backtracking search on $\ell_1$ merit function $U+\nu\sum_i|c_i|$ accepts steps, keeping penalty $\nu$ at least 1 larger than multiplier magnitudes (this symbol differs from linear density $\rho$).
All constraints are equalities, so the subproblem solves a KKT system.
The dense system has $2(n-1)$ variables and $n$ constraints, so linear algebra costs $O(n^3)$ per iteration.
[Sequential quadratic programming](oxref:optimize-sqp-method) explains SQP acceptance conditions and multipliers in detail.

## Comparison with the continuous solution

Independent checks use $a$ obtained by bisecting the arc-length condition, together with the continuous tension and shape.
Differences depend on both discretization and solving errors; when constraint violation and stationarity are sufficiently small, the remaining difference is mainly discretization.

Inputs are chain length $L/D$ and segment count $n$.
Outputs are a shape plot; a per-segment table of length violations, tensions and horizontal tensions; a table of termination, diagnostics and differences from the continuous solution; and the solve outcome.
SQP starts from equally spaced horizontal positions on the continuous solution.
Distances between these nodes are unequal, so SQP must satisfy the discrete constraints afresh.

```rust
//| id: optimize-catenary-sqp
//| ref: optimize-catenary-sqp
//| title: "Check chain shape and SQP multipliers against a catenary"
//| run: button
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-optimize]
//| inputs:
//|   length: {"type":"range","label":"Chain length L/D [-]","min":1.1,"max":2,"step":0.1,"value":1.5}
//|   segments: {"type":"range","label":"Segment count n [segments]","min":4,"max":16,"step":4,"value":8}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"refined","label":"16 segments","description":"Doubling the segment count reduces the horizontal-tension difference from a from −8.9×10⁻⁴ to −2.3×10⁻⁴ and maximum height difference from 5.3×10⁻³ to 1.3×10⁻³, each by about a factor of 1/4.","inputs":{"segments":16}}
//|   - {"id":"taut","label":"L/D = 1.1 (taut chain)","description":"Horizontal tension increases to a = 0.655; with 8 segments the difference between horizontal tension and a is −3.8×10⁻³.","inputs":{"length":1.1}}
//|   - {"id":"slack","label":"L/D = 2 (slack chain)","description":"Horizontal tension decreases to a = 0.230; tension in the end segment is 0.905.","inputs":{"length":2}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"segments","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"segments","path":"/rows/0/2","approx":{"value":0.724655,"atol":1e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"segments","path":"/rows/3/2","approx":{"value":0.321326,"atol":1e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"segments","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":0.307345,"atol":1e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"segments","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":-8.91e-4,"atol":1e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"Converged"}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":0.308236,"atol":1e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/5","approx":{"value":5.25e-3,"atol":5e-5}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/3/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: refined
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"segments","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":-2.27e-4,"atol":5e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/5","approx":{"value":1.27e-3,"atol":2e-5}}
//|   - case: taut
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"segments","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":-3.77e-3,"atol":5e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":0.65496,"atol":1e-4}}
//|   - case: slack
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"segments","path":"/rows/0/2","approx":{"value":0.90467,"atol":1e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":0.22964,"atol":1e-4}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/3/state","equals":"satisfied"}
use scioxide_core::{Budget, ExecutionContext, RealScalar};
use scioxide_optimize::{
    ConstrainedProblem, ConstraintFn, Constraints, ObjectiveFn, OptimizeResult, Sqp, SqpOptions,
};
// Coordinates of node i. Fix endpoints (0, 0) and (1, 0); only interior nodes are variables.
fn point(q: &[f64], i: usize, n: usize) -> [f64; 2] {
    if i == 0 {
        [0.0, 0.0]
    } else if i == n {
        [1.0, 0.0]
    } else {
        [q[2 * (i - 1)], q[2 * (i - 1) + 1]]
    }
}
let n = segments as usize;
let ell = length / n as f64;
let dim = 2 * (n - 1);
// U = ℓ Σ yᵢ (each segment's mass ℓ lies at its midpoint, with half assigned to each interior node).
let objective = ObjectiveFn::new(dim, move |q: &[f64]| {
    Ok(ell * q.chunks_exact(2).map(|p| p[1]).sum::<f64>())
})
.with_gradient(move |_: &[f64], g: &mut [f64]| {
    for (i, gi) in g.iter_mut().enumerate() {
        *gi = if i % 2 == 1 { ell } else { 0.0 };
    }
    Ok(())
});
// cᵢ = ‖rᵢ₊₁ − rᵢ‖ − ℓ. Its gradient is the tangent unit vector, making multipliers tensions.
let mut constraints = Constraints::new(dim).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
for edge in 0..n {
    let constraint = ConstraintFn::new(dim, move |q: &[f64]| {
        let (a, b) = (point(q, edge, n), point(q, edge + 1, n));
        Ok(RealScalar::hypot(b[0] - a[0], b[1] - a[1]) - ell)
    })
    .with_jacobian(move |q: &[f64], row: &mut [f64]| {
        let (a, b) = (point(q, edge, n), point(q, edge + 1, n));
        let d = RealScalar::hypot(b[0] - a[0], b[1] - a[1]);
        row.fill(0.0);
        for k in 0..2 {
            if edge > 0 {
                row[2 * (edge - 1) + k] = (a[k] - b[k]) / d;
            }
            if edge + 1 < n {
                row[2 * edge + k] = (b[k] - a[k]) / d;
            }
        }
        Ok(())
    });
    constraints = constraints
        .equality(constraint)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
}
// Independent check: solve continuous arc-length condition L = 2a sinh(1/(2a)) by bisection.
let (mut lo, mut hi) = (0.01_f64, 10.0_f64);
for _ in 0..80 {
    let a = (lo + hi) / 2.0;
    if 2.0 * a * RealScalar::sinh(0.5 / a) > length {
        lo = a;
    } else {
        hi = a;
    }
}
let a = (lo + hi) / 2.0;
let exact = |x: f64| a * (RealScalar::cosh((x - 0.5) / a) - RealScalar::cosh(0.5 / a));
// Start at equally spaced horizontal positions on the continuous solution. Node distances are unequal.
let start: Vec<_> = (1..n)
    .flat_map(|i| {
        let x = i as f64 / n as f64;
        [x, exact(x)]
    })
    .collect();
let problem = ConstrainedProblem::new(&objective, &constraints).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let solver = Sqp::new(
    SqpOptions::default()
        .with_max_iterations(Budget::try_from(500_usize).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?),
);
let mut work = solver.workspace(&problem).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mut result = OptimizeResult::empty();
solver
    .minimize_into(
        &problem,
        &start,
        &mut result,
        &mut work,
        ExecutionContext::sequential(),
    )
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let points: Vec<_> = (0..=n).map(|i| point(result.x(), i, n)).collect();
let reference: Vec<_> = (0..=100)
    .map(|i| {
        let x = i as f64 / 100.0;
        [x, exact(x)]
    })
    .collect();
emit_line_chart!(
    &vec![
        serde_json::json!({"name": "Discrete chain (SQP)", "points": points}),
        serde_json::json!({
            "name": "Catenary (continuous solution)",
            "points": reference,
            "style": {"lineType": "dashed"}
        })
    ],
    options = &serde_json::json!({"xLabel": "Horizontal position x/D [-]", "yLabel": "Height y/D [-]"})
);
// Multipliers follow equality-constraint order (segments 0, 1, …). Horizontal tension is λᵢ times the tangent's x component.
let rows: Vec<_> = points
    .windows(2)
    .enumerate()
    .map(|(i, p)| {
        let distance = RealScalar::hypot(p[1][0] - p[0][0], p[1][1] - p[0][1]);
        let tension = work.state().multipliers()[i];
        let horizontal = tension * (p[1][0] - p[0][0]) / distance;
        (i, (distance - ell).abs(), tension, horizontal, horizontal - a)
    })
    .collect();
let fixed6 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 6 });
let sci2 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 2 });
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let segment_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Segment i", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Length violation |cᵢ| [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Tension λᵢ = T/(ρgD) [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed6 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Horizontal tension H/(ρgD) [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed6 },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Horizontal tension minus a [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    segment_columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "segments" })
);
let shape_error = points
    .iter()
    .map(|p| (p[1] - exact(p[0])).abs())
    .fold(0.0_f64, f64::max);
let summary_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Termination reason" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Iterations [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Primal feasibility [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2, "nullText": "Not computed" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Stationarity [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2, "nullText": "Not computed" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Continuous-solution a [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed6 },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Maximum height difference [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    summary_columns,
    vec![(
        result.termination().to_string(),
        result.iterations(),
        result.primal_feasibility(),
        result.stationarity(),
        a,
        shape_error
    )],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "summary" })
);
let outcome = if result.is_success() {
    Outcome::satisfied("SQP satisfies its stopping test; multipliers can be read as tensions")
} else {
    Outcome::partial("SQP stopped before satisfying its stopping test")
        .explanation("Do not read multipliers as tensions unless constraint violation and stationarity are small.")
};
let mut outcome = outcome
    .status(result.termination().to_string())
    .diagnostic("Iteration count", result.iterations() as f64, None)
    .diagnostic("Maximum height difference", shape_error, None);
if let Some(stationarity) = result.stationarity() {
    outcome = outcome.diagnostic("Stationarity", stationarity, None);
}
emit_outcome!(outcome);
```

For default $L/D=1.5$ with 8 segments, termination reason is `Converged`, segment-length violations are at most $10^{-13}$, and stationarity is $1.2\times10^{-8}$.
Multiplier-derived tensions are 0.7247 in end segments and 0.3213 in central segments, decreasing toward the chain's lowest point.
Horizontal tension is 0.30735 in every segment, differing from continuous-solution $a=0.30824$ by $-8.9\times10^{-4}$.
With 16 segments this difference is $-2.3\times10^{-4}$, and maximum height difference decreases from $5.3\times10^{-3}$ to $1.3\times10^{-3}$.
Doubling segments reduces differences by about 1/4, indicating discretization differences decreasing as $O(n^{-2})$.
However, the 4-segment horizontal-tension difference $-8.1\times10^{-4}$ is nearly the same as for 8 segments; this decrease is not yet visible at small segment counts.

For $L/D=1.1$, horizontal tension is large at $a=0.655$; for $L/D=2$ it falls to $a=0.230$, with end-segment tension 0.905.
If height differences persist despite small length violations, increase segments to examine discretization effects.

## Summary

Potential energy and length constraints determine chain shape; the continuous solution is catenary $y=a\cosh((x-1/2)/a)+\text{const}$.
When constraints use lengths themselves, SQP multipliers equal segment tensions; using squared lengths changes the factor.
Dimensionless tension uses unit $\rho gD$; recover physical chain tension by multiplying these values by linear density, gravitational acceleration and support separation.
Read constraint violation, stationarity, constancy of horizontal tension and continuous-solution differences separately, and estimate discretization errors from difference reductions when doubling segments.
