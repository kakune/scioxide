Consider where to open warehouses or distribution centers among candidate sites.
More facilities reduce customer transport costs, but each incurs an opening cost.
Uncapacitated facility location simultaneously decides which facilities to open and where to assign each customer.

## Variables and constraints

Let $y_i\in\{0,1\}$ indicate opening facility $i$, and $x_{ij}\ge0$ the fraction of customer $j$ demand assigned to facility $i$.
For opening costs $f_i$ and transport costs $c_{ij}$, solve

$$
\min\sum_i f_i y_i+\sum_{i,j}c_{ij}x_{ij},\qquad
\sum_i x_{ij}=1,\quad x_{ij}\le y_i
$$


The first constraint assigns all customer demand; the second prevents assignment to closed facilities.
With integer opening variables, each customer is assigned to the cheapest open facility.
Equal transport costs can give split assignments with the same optimum, so assignment variables need not themselves be integer.
For $p$ candidate facilities and $q$ customers, there are $p+pq$ variables and $q+pq$ constraints.

## Strong and weak formulations

Aggregating the second constraint per facility as $\sum_jx_{ij}\le q\,y_i$ gives the same feasible set for integer $y$.
Constraints decrease to $p$, but in the LP relaxation removing integrality of $y$, assigning one entire customer to facility $i$ needs only $y_i=1/q$, charging only $1/q$ of its opening cost.
Individual constraints $x_{ij}\le y_i$ require $y_i=1$ to assign customer $j$ entirely to facility $i$.
Summing $x_{ij}\le y_i$ over $j$ gives $\sum_jx_{ij}\le q\,y_i$, so the strong relaxation's feasible region is contained in the weak relaxation's region and its lower bound is at least as large.
Integer programming prunes branches using relaxation lower bounds, so stronger bounds tend to shrink search trees.
The following cell solves the strong MILP formulation and also reports the weak LP relaxation for comparison.

## Independent exhaustive search

For small facility count $p$, enumerate all $2^p-1$ nonempty opening sets.
For a fixed set, cost is the sum of opening costs plus each customer's minimum transport cost among open facilities.
Evaluating a set costs $O(pq)$, so exhaustive search costs $O(2^ppq)$.
This independent check does not use the MILP search tree.
LP relaxation removes only integrality of $y$, giving a lower bound on minimum cost.
If all relaxed $y$ are 0 or 1, the solution also solves the integer program and no branching is needed.

Inputs are candidate-facility count, customer count, opening cost and random seed.
Place candidate facilities and customers uniformly in a 10 km square; transport cost $c_{ij}$ is Euclidean distance times 0.1 × 10⁴ yen/km, the monthly cost of transporting one customer's demand.
Opening cost $f$ is common to every facility and prorated to the same one-month period [10⁴ yen].
Outputs are a layout plot; a table of opening variables and strong-relaxation $y$; comparisons of integer cost, exhaustive search and both LP relaxations; branch-and-bound statistics; and a search outcome.
The `BranchAndBound` test checks whether incumbent minus lower bound is within gap tolerance; the lower bound is a floating-point estimate, not a proof.

```rust
//| inputs:
//|   facilities: {"type":"range","label":"Candidate facilities p [facilities]","min":2,"max":6,"step":1,"value":4}
//|   customers: {"type":"range","label":"Customers q [customers]","min":3,"max":8,"step":1,"value":5}
//|   opening: {"type":"range","label":"Opening cost per facility f [10⁴ yen]","min":0.1,"max":2,"step":0.1,"value":0.5}
//|   seed: {"type":"range","label":"Random seed [-]","min":1,"max":100,"step":1,"value":11}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"cheap","label":"Opening cost 0.1 × 10⁴ yen","description":"Cheap opening costs open 3 facilities and reduce total cost to 2.106 × 10⁴ yen. The strong LP lower bound equals integer cost; the weak bound is only 1.906 × 10⁴ yen.","inputs":{"opening":0.1}}
//|   - {"id":"expensive","label":"Opening cost 2 × 10⁴ yen","description":"Only 1 facility opens, giving total cost 4.433 × 10⁴ yen. The weak lower bound is 3.806 × 10⁴ yen; its 0.627 × 10⁴ yen gap to integer cost matches the default.","inputs":{"opening":2}}
//|   - {"id":"fractional","label":"Seed 81 (fractional relaxation)","description":"The strong relaxation is fractional, y = (0.5, 0, 0.5, 0.5); its lower bound 2.6525 × 10⁴ yen is below integer cost 2.6762 × 10⁴ yen. Branch and bound processes 3 nodes.","inputs":{"seed":81}}
//|   - {"id":"large","label":"6 facilities, 8 customers, seed 3","description":"There are 63 opening sets; 2 facilities open and integer cost matches exhaustive-search 3.212 × 10⁴ yen. The strong LP bound is also 3.212 × 10⁴ yen, while the weak bound is 2.174 × 10⁴ yen.","inputs":{"facilities":6,"customers":8,"seed":3}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"costs","path":"/rows/0/0","approx":{"value":2.9328521003,"atol":1e-9}}

//|       - {"artifact":"costs","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":2.9328521003,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"costs","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":2.3058789228,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"costs","path":"/rows/0/5","equals":1}
//|       - {"artifact":"costs","path":"/rows/0/6","equals":"Converged"}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/4/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: cheap
//|     expect:

//|       - {"artifact":"costs","path":"/rows/0/0","approx":{"value":2.1058789228,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"costs","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":1.9058789228,"atol":1e-9}}

//|       - {"artifact":"costs","path":"/rows/0/0","approx":{"value":4.4328521003,"atol":1e-9}}

//|       - {"artifact":"costs","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"min":-1e-9,"max":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"costs","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":2.6525449842,"atol":1e-8}}
//|       - {"artifact":"search","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":3}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/4/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: large
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"costs","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"min":-1e-9,"max":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"costs","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":3.2115027183,"atol":1e-8}}

//|       - {"artifact":"costs","path":"/rows/0/5","equals":2}
use scioxide_core::Bounds;
use scioxide_linalg::Matrix;
use scioxide_optimize::{
    BranchAndBound, LinearConstraintSense, LinearConstraints, LpProblem, MipProblem,
    SimplexSolver,
};
use scioxide_random::{BitGenerator, SeedableRng, SplitMix64, decode_u64};
let (p, q) = (facilities as usize, customers as usize);
// Place candidate facilities and customers uniformly in a 10 km square; transport cost cᵢⱼ [10⁴ yen] is distance [km] × 0.1 × 10⁴ yen/km.
let (side, rate) = (10.0_f64, 0.1_f64);
let mut rng = SplitMix64::from_seed(seed as u64);
let mut points = Vec::new();
for _ in 0..p + q {
    let x: f64 = decode_u64(rng.next_uniform_u64().map_err(|e| e.to_string())?);
    let y: f64 = decode_u64(rng.next_uniform_u64().map_err(|e| e.to_string())?);
    points.push([side * x, side * y]);
}
let mut costs = vec![0.0; p * q];
for i in 0..p {
    for j in 0..q {
        costs[i * q + j] = rate
            * scioxide_core::RealScalar::hypot(
                points[i][0] - points[p + j][0],
                points[i][1] - points[p + j][1],
            );
    }
}
// Variable order is (y₀, …, y_{p−1}, x₀₀, …, x_{p−1,q−1}); xᵢⱼ occupies column p + iq + j.
let n = p + p * q;
let mut objective = vec![opening; p];
objective.extend_from_slice(&costs);
// Strong formulation: Σᵢ xᵢⱼ = 1 (q rows) and xᵢⱼ ≤ yᵢ (pq rows).
let rows = q + p * q;
let mut matrix = vec![0.0; rows * n];
let mut rhs = vec![0.0; rows];
let mut senses = vec![LinearConstraintSense::LessEqual; rows];
for j in 0..q {
    rhs[j] = 1.0;
    senses[j] = LinearConstraintSense::Equal;
    for i in 0..p {
        matrix[j * n + p + i * q + j] = 1.0;
    }
}
for i in 0..p {
    for j in 0..q {
        let row = q + i * q + j;
        matrix[row * n + p + i * q + j] = 1.0;
        matrix[row * n + i] = -1.0;
    }
}
let lp = LpProblem::new(objective.clone())
    .and_then(|v| {
        v.with_constraints(LinearConstraints::new(
            Matrix::from_vec(rows, n, matrix)?,
            rhs,
            senses,
        )?)
    })
    .and_then(|v| v.with_bounds(Bounds::new(vec![(Some(0.0), Some(1.0)); n])?))
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
// Weak comparison formulation: aggregate xᵢⱼ ≤ yᵢ per facility into Σⱼ xᵢⱼ ≤ q yᵢ (p rows).
let weak_rows = q + p;
let mut weak = vec![0.0; weak_rows * n];
let mut weak_rhs = vec![0.0; weak_rows];
let mut weak_senses = vec![LinearConstraintSense::LessEqual; weak_rows];
for j in 0..q {
    weak_rhs[j] = 1.0;
    weak_senses[j] = LinearConstraintSense::Equal;
    for i in 0..p {
        weak[j * n + p + i * q + j] = 1.0;
    }
}
for i in 0..p {
    weak[(q + i) * n + i] = -(q as f64);
    for j in 0..q {
        weak[(q + i) * n + p + i * q + j] = 1.0;
    }
}
let weak_lp = LpProblem::new(objective)
    .and_then(|v| {
        v.with_constraints(LinearConstraints::new(
            Matrix::from_vec(weak_rows, n, weak)?,
            weak_rhs,
            weak_senses,
        )?)
    })
    .and_then(|v| v.with_bounds(Bounds::new(vec![(Some(0.0), Some(1.0)); n])?))
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let relaxation = SimplexSolver::default()
    .solve(&lp)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let weak_relaxation = SimplexSolver::default()
    .solve(&weak_lp)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mip = MipProblem::new(lp, (0..p).collect()).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let result = BranchAndBound::default()
    .solve(&mip)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
// Independent check: enumerate opening sets and assign each customer to the cheapest open facility.
let exact = (1_usize..1 << p)
    .map(|mask| {
        opening * mask.count_ones() as f64
            + (0..q)
                .map(|j| {
                    (0..p)
                        .filter(|&i| mask & (1 << i) != 0)
                        .map(|i| costs[i * q + j])
                        .fold(f64::INFINITY, f64::min)
                })
                .sum::<f64>()
    })
    .fold(f64::INFINITY, f64::min);
if !result.has_incumbent() {
    return Err(format!("No integer candidate: {}", result.termination()));
}
let open: Vec<_> = (0..p).map(|i| result.x()[i] > 0.5).collect();
let opened: Vec<_> = (0..p).filter(|&i| open[i]).map(|i| points[i]).collect();
let closed: Vec<_> = (0..p).filter(|&i| !open[i]).map(|i| points[i]).collect();
emit_scatter_chart!(
    &vec![
        serde_json::json!({"name":"Open facilities", "points":opened}),
        serde_json::json!({"name":"Closed candidate facilities", "points":closed}),
        serde_json::json!({"name":"Customers", "points":&points[p..]})
    ],
    options = &serde_json::json!({
        "xLabel": "East–west position [km]",
        "yLabel": "North–south position [km]",
        "xMin": 0,
        "xMax": 10,
        "yMin": 0,
        "yMax": 10
    })
);
let fixed4 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 4 });
let sci2 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 2 });
let open_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Facility i", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Opening variable yᵢ [-]", "type": "number", "format": { "fractionDigits": 0 } },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Strong LP relaxation yᵢ [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 }
]);
let open_rows: Vec<_> = (0..p)
    .map(|i| (i, result.x()[i], relaxation.x()[i]))
    .collect();
emit_table_with_columns!(
    open_columns,
    open_rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "opening" })
);
let cost_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Integer cost [10⁴ yen]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Exhaustive search [10⁴ yen]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Difference [10⁴ yen]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Strong LP lower bound [10⁴ yen]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Weak LP lower bound [10⁴ yen]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Open facilities [facilities]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "6", "label": "Termination reason" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    cost_columns,
    vec![(
        result.fun(),
        exact,
        result.fun() - exact,
        relaxation.fun(),
        weak_relaxation.fun(),
        open.iter().filter(|&&o| o).count(),
        result.termination().to_string()
    )],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "costs" })
);
let stats = result.statistics();
let search_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Processed nodes [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "LP solves [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Cutting planes [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Basis reuses [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Absolute gap [10⁴ yen]", "type": "number", "format": sci2, "nullText": "Not computed" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    search_columns,
    vec![(
        stats.nodes(),
        stats.relaxations(),
        stats.cuts(),
        stats.basis_reuses(),
        result.absolute_gap()
    )],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "search" })
);
let outcome = if result.is_success() {
    Outcome::satisfied("Branch and bound satisfies its gap test")
        .explanation("The lower bound is a floating-point estimate, not a proof.")
} else {
    Outcome::partial("Branch and bound stopped before satisfying its gap test")
        .explanation("Integer cost is the incumbent value; optimality is unestablished.")
};
let mut outcome = outcome
    .status(result.termination().to_string())
    .diagnostic("Processed nodes", stats.nodes() as f64, None)
    .diagnostic("Difference from exhaustive search", result.fun() - exact, Some("10⁴ yen"));
if let Some(gap) = result.absolute_gap() {
    outcome = outcome.diagnostic("Absolute gap", gap, Some("10⁴ yen"));
}
emit_outcome!(outcome);
```

Default inputs (4 facilities, 5 customers, opening cost 0.5 × 10⁴ yen, seed 11) open only facility 1; integer cost 2.9329 × 10⁴ yen matches exhaustive search.
The strong LP bound is also 2.9329 × 10⁴ yen and its solution already integer, so branch and bound finishes at the single root node with absolute gap 0.
The weak lower bound is 2.3059 × 10⁴ yen, leaving 0.627 × 10⁴ yen to integer cost.

Reducing opening cost to 0.1 × 10⁴ yen opens 3 facilities and lowers total cost to 2.106 × 10⁴ yen; increasing it to 2 × 10⁴ yen returns to 1 facility at 4.433 × 10⁴ yen.
Larger opening costs favor consolidation, but optimal opening counts change discretely.
With 6 facilities, 8 customers and seed 3, integer cost also matches exhaustive-search 3.212 × 10⁴ yen; the strong bound equals integer cost and the weak bound remains 2.174 × 10⁴ yen.
So far strong relaxations already return integer solutions, but seed 81 gives fractional relaxation $y=(0.5,0,0.5,0.5)$.
The strong lower bound 2.6525 × 10⁴ yen is then below integer cost 2.6762 × 10⁴ yen; branch and bound processes 3 nodes and solves 10 LPs including trials and repairs.
Of these, 9 start from previously solved LP bases.
Integer cost matches exhaustive search; weak bound 1.9438 × 10⁴ yen is lower still than the strong bound.
Read the LP-bound/integer-cost difference and searcher's reported gap separately because their comparison targets differ.

## Summary

Binary opening variables and continuous assignments express uncapacitated facility location as a MILP.
Even when formulations represent the same integer solutions, the strong $x_{ij}\le y_i$ LP bound is at least the weak $\sum_jx_{ij}\le qy_i$ bound and can reduce search.
Strong relaxations can still be fractional; read search size through branching nodes and LP solve counts.
Small-scale set enumeration independently checks integer-program outputs; at larger scales inspect candidate availability, relaxation lower bounds, reported gaps and search limits.
