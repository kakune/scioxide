In continuous linear programming, a shadow price (dual variable) gives the optimal-value increase from one extra resource unit.
With integer production lots, one extra resource unit does not raise profit until another lot becomes feasible.
Using the same plan as [Choose finished-product lots by mixed-integer programming](../integer-production/), this page varies the battery-cell limit one at a time and compares relaxed and integer optima as resource functions.

## LP value functions and shadow prices

Maximize profit $190x+p_yy$ [thousand yen] for $x$ standard-set lots and $y$ premium-set lots under battery-cell $4x+7y\le C$, assembly $4x+3y\le H$ and demand constraints $0\le x\le18$ and $0\le y\le12$.
The optimal value $V_{\mathrm{LP}}(C)$ as a function of battery-cell limit $C$ is called the value function.
The LP optimum is concave and piecewise linear in the right-hand side; each interval's slope equals the battery-cell constraint's shadow price there.

Derive the value function at defaults $H=52$ and $p_y=310$.
For $C\le52$, only cells constrain production; per-cell profits are $190/4=47.5$ for standard and $310/7\approx44.3$ for premium, so produce only $x=C/4$ standard lots.
For $52\le C\le100$, both resources are active equalities; $y=(C-52)/4$ and $x=(52-3y)/4$ give

$$
V_{\mathrm{LP}}(C)=2470+41.875\,(C-52)
$$


For $C\ge100$, $y$ reaches demand limit 12 and the solution remains at $(x,y)=(4,12)$, leaving unused cells.
The slopes (shadow prices) decrease through $47.5$, $41.875$ and $0$ thousand yen/cell.

## Integer-optimum staircases

Integer optimum $V_{\mathrm{MIP}}(C)$ is nondecreasing in $C$ but discontinuous.
Adding one battery cell leaves the optimum unchanged if the integer-point set is unchanged (a horizontal interval).
The optimum steps upward only when a new integer point becomes feasible.

Step heights need not equal one lot's profit.
At default inputs, $C=79$ changes the optimum from $(7,7)$ to $(4,9)$, increasing profit by $-3\times190+2\times310=50$ thousand yen.
At $C=80$, it changes from $(4,9)$ to $(6,8)$, increasing profit by $2\times190-310=70$ thousand yen.
Steps arise from changing lot combinations; their heights reflect the difference between combinations.

The value functions coincide at values of $C$ for which relaxed solutions are integer points.
For $52\le C\le100$, when $C-52$ is a multiple of 16, namely $C=52,68,84,100$, relaxed solutions are $(13,0)$, $(10,4)$, $(7,8)$ and $(4,12)$.
The average increase of $V_{\mathrm{MIP}}$ from $C=52$ to $C=100$ equals LP slope 41.875 thousand yen/cell, but each single-cell increase is either 0 or a step height.
LP shadow prices cannot serve as the value of one battery cell in integer-lot plans.

## Vary the cell limit one at a time

Fix assembly time and premium profit, solving LP relaxations and MIPs for 76 cell limits $C=45,\dots,120$.
The first plot shows LP optima (dashed) and MIP optima (stairs); the second shows their difference $V_{\mathrm{LP}}-V_{\mathrm{MIP}}$, linked on the shared $C$ axis.
The table gives $C$ at the largest difference, LP slope measured by centered differences around $C=80$, and maximum difference between enumerated integer optima and MIP values.
Enumeration independently substitutes 247 integer points into the original inequalities.
If LP or MIP is unsuccessful for any $C$, return an error containing that $C$ and termination status.

```rust
//| id: optimize-integer-capacity-sweep
//| ref: optimize-integer-capacity-sweep
//| title: "LP upper bounds and integer-profit steps versus battery-cell resources"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-linalg, scioxide-optimize]
//| inputs:
//|   sweep_labor_capacity: {"type":"range","label":"Fixed assembly time H [h]","description":"Change constraints active besides battery cells.","min":35,"max":80,"step":1,"value":52}
//|   sweep_premium_profit: {"type":"range","label":"Premium-set profit pᵧ [thousand yen/lot]","description":"Change the selected integer combination.","min":240,"max":420,"step":10,"value":310}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"premium-heavy","label":"Premium-set profit pᵧ = 420","description":"Producing only premium sets becomes favorable; the largest difference is 180 thousand yen at scan endpoint C = 45.","inputs":{"sweep_premium_profit":420}}
//|   - {"id":"tight-labor","label":"Assembly time H = 35 h","description":"Assembly time becomes active first; the largest difference grows to 206.7 thousand yen at C = 82.","inputs":{"sweep_labor_capacity":35}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":76}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/1/1","equals":99}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/2/1","approx":{"value":148.125,"atol":1e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/3/1","approx":{"value":41.875,"atol":1e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/4/1","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-6}}
//|   - case: premium-heavy
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/1/1","equals":45}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/2/1","approx":{"value":180,"atol":1e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/4/1","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-6}}
//|   - case: tight-labor
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/1/1","equals":82}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/2/1","approx":{"value":206.6667,"atol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/4/1","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-6}}
use scioxide_core::{Bounds, SciOxideError};
use scioxide_linalg::Matrix;
use scioxide_optimize::{
    BranchAndBound, LinearConstraintSense, LinearConstraints, LpProblem, MipProblem,
    SimplexSolver,
};
use serde_json::json;

// Build LP relaxations varying only cell limit C (negate profit for minimization).
let relaxation = |cell: f64| -> Result<LpProblem<f64>, SciOxideError> {
    let rows = LinearConstraints::new(
        Matrix::from_vec(2, 2, vec![4.0_f64, 7.0, 4.0, 3.0])?,
        vec![cell, sweep_labor_capacity],
        vec![LinearConstraintSense::LessEqual; 2],
    )?;
    LpProblem::new(vec![-190.0_f64, -sweep_premium_profit])?
        .with_constraints(rows)?
        .with_bounds(Bounds::new(vec![
            (Some(0.0), Some(18.0)),
            (Some(0.0), Some(12.0)),
        ])?)
};
// Independent check: substitute all integer points into original inequalities to find the integer optimum.
let enumerate = |cell: f64| {
    let mut best = 0.0_f64;
    for x in 0..=18 {
        for y in 0..=12 {
            let (x, y) = (f64::from(x), f64::from(y));
            if 4.0 * x + 7.0 * y <= cell && 4.0 * x + 3.0 * y <= sweep_labor_capacity {
                best = best.max(190.0 * x + sweep_premium_profit * y);
            }
        }
    }
    best
};
let (mut lp_curve, mut mip_curve, mut gap_curve) = (Vec::new(), Vec::new(), Vec::new());
let (mut widest, mut enumeration_error) = ((0.0_f64, 0.0_f64), 0.0_f64);
for cell in 45..=120 {
    let c = f64::from(cell);
    let problem = relaxation(c).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let lp = SimplexSolver::<f64>::default()
        .solve(&problem)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let mip = BranchAndBound::<f64>::default()
        .solve(&MipProblem::new(problem, vec![0, 1]).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    if !lp.is_success() || !mip.is_success() {
        return Err(format!(
            "Unsuccessful at C = {c}: LP {}, MIP {}",
            lp.termination(),
            mip.termination()
        ));
    }
    let (lp_value, mip_value) = (-lp.fun(), -mip.fun());
    let gap = lp_value - mip_value;
    if gap > widest.1 {
        widest = (c, gap);
    }
    enumeration_error = enumeration_error.max((mip_value - enumerate(c)).abs());
    lp_curve.push([c, lp_value]);
    mip_curve.push([c, mip_value]);
    gap_curve.push([c, gap]);
}
emit_line_chart!(
    &vec![
        json!({
            "name": "LP-relaxation optimum (upper bound)",
            "points": lp_curve,
            "style": {"lineType": "dashed"}
        }),
        json!({"name": "MIP optimum", "points": mip_curve, "style": {"step": "end"}}),
    ],
    options = &json!({
        "link": {"group": "integer-capacity"},
        "xLabel": "Battery-cell limit C [cells]",
        "yLabel": "Optimal profit [thousand yen]"
    })
);
emit_line_chart!(
    &vec![json!({"name": "V_LP − V_MIP", "points": gap_curve, "style": {"step": "end"}})],
    options = &json!({
        "link": {"group": "integer-capacity"},
        "xLabel": "Battery-cell limit C [cells]",
        "yLabel": "Difference from LP upper bound V_LP − V_MIP [thousand yen]",
        "annotations": [{"kind": "line", "axis": "x", "value": widest.0, "label": "C with largest difference"}]
    })
);
// Centered difference around C = 80. C = 79 and 81 are scan indices 35 and 37.
let lp_slope = (lp_curve[36][1] - lp_curve[34][1]) / 2.0;
let columns = json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Quantity" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Value", "type": "number", "format": { "significantDigits": 6 } }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    vec![
        ("Cell limits C solved [count]", lp_curve.len() as f64),
        ("C with largest difference [cells]", widest.0),
        ("Corresponding difference V_LP − V_MIP [thousand yen]", widest.1),
        ("LP slope at C = 80 (centered difference) [thousand yen/cell]", lp_slope),
        ("Maximum MIP/enumeration difference [thousand yen]", enumeration_error),
    ],
    metadata = json!({ "id": "summary" })
);
```

For defaults ($H=52$, $p_y=310$), LP and MIP succeed at all 76 values of $C$, with MIP matching enumeration.
The dashed LP curve bends at $C=52$ and $C=100$; the centered-difference slope between them is also 41.875 thousand yen/cell, matching the derived shadow price.
For $C\le100$, the MIP staircase has horizontal intervals 1–4 cells wide and steps 50–190 thousand yen high, touching the dashed curve at $C=48,52,68,84$ and $C\ge100$.
The largest difference is 148.1 thousand yen at $C=99$.
The LP bound there is 4438.1 thousand yen, but integer optimum $(3,12)$ yields 4290 thousand yen; one extra cell jumps to $(4,12)$ with 4480 thousand yen.

At premium profit 420 thousand yen, producing only premium sets is favorable; maximum difference 180 thousand yen occurs at left scan endpoint $C=45$.
A maximum at a scan endpoint may increase further if the scan range expands.
Reducing assembly time to 35 h activates assembly first, increasing the largest difference to 206.7 thousand yen at $C=82$.

Difference $V_{\mathrm{LP}}-V_{\mathrm{MIP}}$ is absolute profit difference; comparing different business scales also requires relative differences.
Relative differences become unstable near zero profit, however, so combine them with decision-relevant absolute differences.

## Summary

The LP-relaxation value function is concave and piecewise linear in cell limits; the default plan's slopes (shadow prices) are 47.5, 41.875 and 0 thousand yen/cell.
Integer optima form a nondecreasing staircase: one extra cell changes profit by 0 or a 50–190 thousand yen step, while LP shadow prices correspond only to average slopes.
When deciding resource additions for integer-lot plans, resolve MIPs at candidate resource quantities and compare them instead of using shadow prices.
LP/MIP differences vary from 0 to 148.1 thousand yen with resources; do not transfer a gap measured at one resource level to another.
