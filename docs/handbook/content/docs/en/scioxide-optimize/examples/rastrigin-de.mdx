When many periodic irregularities overlay a smooth global trend, gradient methods stop in local depressions near their starts.
The Rastrigin function is typical; its analytically known global minimizer at the origin with value 0 makes it a common global-search test problem.
This page examines its local-minimum structure, comparing quasi-Newton BFGS stopping at local minima with differential evolution (DE).
Start BFGS from DE's best point to check stationarity and compare the known global minimum.

## Small waves and local minima

Consider the two-dimensional Rastrigin function

$$
f(x,y)=2A+x^2+y^2
-A\{\cos(2\pi x)+\cos(2\pi y)\},
\qquad (x,y)\in[-5.12,5.12]^2
$$

with dimensionless coordinates and $f$.
$A>0$ controls periodic irregularity strength.
Rewriting as $f=\{x^2+A(1-\cos2\pi x)\}+\{y^2+A(1-\cos2\pi y)\}$ shows every term nonnegative, vanishing only for $x=y=0$.
The origin is therefore the unique global minimizer, with $f(0,0)=0$.
Use this known answer as an independent algorithm-comparison check.

Partial derivatives are

$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}
=2x+2\pi A\sin(2\pi x),\qquad
\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}
=2y+2\pi A\sin(2\pi y)
$$


Large $A$ makes sinusoidal terms dominate gradients, creating stationary points near integers along each coordinate.
Local minima along $x$ lie slightly toward the origin from integers $n$, with $f$ increasing roughly as $n^2$ (0.995 at $x=\pm0.995$ and 3.98 at $\pm1.990$ for $A=10$).
In two dimensions, combinations of coordinate minima are local minima, giving $11^2=121$ within the box for $A=10$.

This is not an actual layout model, but represents objectives with grid interference, periodic penalties or multiple resonance conditions.
Properties checked on known-answer tests do not guarantee performance on real problems with unknown answers.

## Limits of local methods

Local methods such as BFGS reduce objectives through gradients and line search, converging to near-zero-gradient points.
`GradientSmall` means the gradient norm is within tolerance; which local minimum is reached depends on starts and intervening steps.
A small final gradient does not imply the lowest minimum.
See [First-order methods, nonlinear conjugate gradients and quasi-Newton methods](../../algorithms/first-order-and-quasi-newton/) for BFGS updates and line search.

## Three differential-evolution operations

Differential evolution keeps population $\{x_i\}_{i=1}^{N_p}$ rather than one point.
Default DE/rand/1 chooses mutually distinct individuals $r_1,r_2,r_3$, all distinct from $i$, and forms mutant

$$
v_i=x_{r_1}+F(x_{r_2}-x_{r_3})
$$

with mutation factor $F\in(0,2]$.
Difference vectors directly reflect current population spread and orientation.
Binomial crossover then takes each coordinate from the mutant with probability $C_R$, otherwise from parent $x_i$, forcing at least one mutant coordinate.
Out-of-box coordinates default to the nearest boundary.
If trial point $u_i$ satisfies

$$
f(u_i)\le f(x_i)
$$

replace $x_i$ with it.

Small $F$ concentrates search near the population; large values increase out-of-bound trials.
Small $C_R$ changes few coordinates conservatively; large values try more mutant coordinates together.
Small populations reduce work but lose diversity of difference directions.
No parameter value is best for all problems.

A generation evaluates the objective once per individual.
Including initial population evaluation, the evaluation bound is

$$
N_{\mathrm{eval}}=N_p(G+1)
$$

where $G$ is the generation limit.
Assess work through population times generations, not generations alone.
For objective cost $O(d)$ per evaluation, generation mutation, crossover and selection require $O(N_pd)$ arithmetic, with objective evaluations dominating.

`DifferentialEvolution` stops with `PopulationStagnation` when population objective spread $f_{\max}-f_{\min}$ is within tolerance (relative to $\max(|f_{\min}|,|f_{\max}|)$ plus absolute), or `SearchBudgetCompleted` at the generation limit.
Neither is successful or establishes global-minimum attainment.
A whole population collapsing into the wrong basin can also give `PopulationStagnation`.
See [Differential evolution](../../algorithms/differential-evolution/) for mutation strategies and boundaries.

## Local and population search

Vary Rastrigin amplitude, BFGS start and DE population, generations, mutation factor, crossover probability and seed.
Include the BFGS start in DE's initial population.
Set both absolute and relative tolerances for population objective spread to $10^{-10}$.

Outputs are:

- Results table for BFGS, DE and BFGS started from DE's best point (DE → BFGS)
- Objective-landscape and diagonal $x=y$ cross-section plots
- Outcome for DE → BFGS

Compute objective/gradient cosine and sine through `RealScalar::cos` and `RealScalar::sin`.
Use known global value 0 and distance to the origin to check local stationarity and global location separately.
The outcome is satisfied only when DE → BFGS terminates with `GradientSmall` and $f\le10^{-8}$.

```rust
//| id: optimize-rastrigin-de
//| ref: optimize-rastrigin-de
//| title: "Compare BFGS and differential evolution on a multimodal landscape"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-optimize]
//| inputs:
//|   rastrigin_amplitude: {"type":"range","label":"Irregularity amplitude A [-]","description":"Change cosine strength creating local minima.","min":2,"max":18,"step":1,"value":10}
//|   local_initial_coordinate: {"type":"range","label":"Initial coordinate s [-]","description":"Start BFGS at (s, −s) and include it in the DE population.","min":0.4,"max":4.8,"step":0.2,"value":3.4}
//|   de_population_size: {"type":"range","label":"Population Nₚ [points]","description":"Change per-generation work and search-direction diversity.","min":8,"max":80,"step":4,"value":36}
//|   de_generation_budget: {"type":"range","label":"Generation limit G [generations]","description":"Change search duration.","min":20,"max":300,"step":10,"value":140}
//|   de_mutation_factor: {"type":"range","label":"Mutation factor F [-]","description":"Change difference-vector jump magnitude.","min":0.2,"max":1.8,"step":0.1,"value":0.8}
//|   de_crossover_rate: {"type":"range","label":"Crossover probability CR [-]","description":"Change the fraction of mutant coordinates.","min":0.1,"max":1,"step":0.05,"value":0.9}
//|   de_seed: {"type":"range","label":"DE seed [-]","description":"Choose reproducible initial populations and random sequences.","min":1,"max":1000,"step":1,"value":202}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"short-budget","label":"Generation limit G = 20","description":"DE stops at f = 1.08 with SearchBudgetCompleted; subsequent BFGS converges with GradientSmall to neighboring minimum (−0.995, 0), f = 0.995, instead of the origin.","inputs":{"de_generation_budget":20}}
//|   - {"id":"small-mutation","label":"Mutation factor F = 0.2","description":"The population stagnates at f = 0.112. Subsequent BFGS reaches within 6×10⁻¹⁰ of the origin, but rounding f to 0 makes line search fail with LineSearchFailed.","inputs":{"de_mutation_factor":0.2}}
//|   - {"id":"near-start","label":"Initial coordinate s = 0.4","description":"The start lies in the origin's basin, so BFGS alone reaches it (f = 0) in 4 iterations.","inputs":{"local_initial_coordinate":0.4}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":7.95966,"atol":1e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":2.81416,"atol":1e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/0/8","equals":"GradientSmall"}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/1/3","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-8}}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/1/4","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/1/6","range":{"min":60,"max":140}}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/2/3","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/2/8","equals":"GradientSmall"}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/3/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: short-budget
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/1/8","equals":"SearchBudgetCompleted"}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/1/6","equals":20}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/2/3","approx":{"value":0.994959,"atol":1e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/2/4","approx":{"value":0.994959,"atol":1e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/2/8","equals":"GradientSmall"}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/3/state","equals":"partial"}
//|   - case: small-mutation
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/1/3","approx":{"value":0.1124,"atol":5e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/2/4","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/2/3","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-12}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/3/state","equals":"failed"}
//|   - case: near-start
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/0/3","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/0/4","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"methods","path":"/rows/0/8","equals":"GradientSmall"}
use scioxide_core::{Bounds, Budget, RealScalar, ResidualTolerance};
use scioxide_optimize::{
    Bfgs, BoundedObjective, DifferentialEvolution, DifferentialEvolutionOptions, ObjectiveFn,
    OptimizeTermination,
};
use std::f64::consts::PI;

let sig4 = serde_json::json!({ "significantDigits": 4 });
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
// f(x) = 2A + Σ (xᵢ² − A cos 2πxᵢ). Known global minimum f = 0 at the origin.
let a = rastrigin_amplitude;
let f = move |p: &[f64]| {
    2.0 * a
        + p.iter()
            .map(|&v| v * v - a * RealScalar::cos(2.0 * PI * v))
            .sum::<f64>()
};
// Gradient ∂f/∂xᵢ = 2xᵢ + 2πA sin 2πxᵢ.
let objective = ObjectiveFn::new(2, move |p: &[f64]| Ok(f(p))).with_gradient(
    move |p: &[f64], g: &mut [f64]| {
        for (gi, &v) in g.iter_mut().zip(p) {
            *gi = 2.0 * v + 2.0 * PI * a * RealScalar::sin(2.0 * PI * v);
        }
        Ok(())
    },
);
// Local method: BFGS from (s, −s).
let initial = [local_initial_coordinate, -local_initial_coordinate];
let local = Bfgs::<f64>::default()
    .minimize(&objective, &initial)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
// Population method: DE inside [−5.12, 5.12]² up to the generation limit.
let bounds = Bounds::new(vec![(Some(-5.12_f64), Some(5.12)); 2]).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let bounded = BoundedObjective::new(&objective, &bounds).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let options = DifferentialEvolutionOptions::default()
    .with_fitness_tolerance(
        ResidualTolerance::new(1.0e-10_f64, 1.0e-10).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?,
    )
    .with_max_generations(
        Budget::try_from(de_generation_budget.round() as usize).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?,
    )
    .with_population_size(
        Budget::try_from(de_population_size.round() as usize).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?,
    )
    .with_mutation_factor(de_mutation_factor)
    .with_crossover_rate(de_crossover_rate)
    .with_seed(de_seed.round() as u64);
let global = DifferentialEvolution::new(options)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?
    .minimize(&bounded, &initial)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
// Two stages: BFGS from DE's best point checks local stationarity.
let polished = Bfgs::<f64>::default()
    .minimize(&objective, global.x())
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let norm = |v: &[f64]| v.iter().map(|x| x * x).sum::<f64>().sqrt();
let rows: Vec<_> = [
    ("BFGS (local)", &local),
    ("Differential evolution", &global),
    ("DE → BFGS", &polished),
]
.into_iter()
.map(|(name, r)| {
    (
        name,
        r.x()[0],
        r.x()[1],
        r.fun(),
        norm(r.x()),
        r.grad().map(norm),
        r.iterations(),
        r.fun_evals(),
        r.termination().to_string(),
    )
})
.collect();
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Method" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Final x [-]", "type": "number", "format": sig4 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Final y [-]", "type": "number", "format": sig4 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Objective f [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Distance from origin [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Gradient norm [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3, "nullText": "None" },
    { "key": "6", "label": "Iterations / generations [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "7", "label": "Objective evaluations [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "8", "label": "Termination reason" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "methods" })
);
// Plot the landscape (61×61 grid) and diagonal section f(x, x).
let grid = |i: usize| -5.12 + 10.24 * i as f64 / 60.0;
let landscape: Vec<[f64; 3]> = (0..=60)
    .flat_map(|r| (0..=60).map(move |c| [grid(c), grid(r), f(&[grid(c), grid(r)])]))
    .collect();
emit_chart!(serde_json::json!({
    "kind": "heatmap",
    "title": "Rastrigin f(x, y) [-] (color)",
    "data": landscape,
    "xLabel": "Design coordinate x [-]",
    "yLabel": "Design coordinate y [-]",
    "tooltip": true
}));
let diagonal: Vec<[f64; 2]> = (0..=400)
    .map(|i| {
        let v = -5.12 + 10.24 * i as f64 / 400.0;
        [v, f(&[v, v])]
    })
    .collect();
emit_line_chart!(
    &vec![serde_json::json!({ "name": "f(x, x)", "points": diagonal })],
    options = &serde_json::json!({
        "xLabel": "Diagonal coordinate x = y [-]",
        "yLabel": "Objective f(x, x) [-]",
        "annotations": [{ "kind": "line", "axis": "x", "value": 0.0, "label": "Global minimum x = 0" }]
    })
);
// Outcome: are both stationarity (GradientSmall) and known global minimum f = 0 satisfied?
let termination = polished.termination();
let outcome = if polished.is_success() && polished.fun() <= 1.0e-8 {
    Outcome::satisfied("DE → BFGS reached a stationary point with known global minimum f = 0")
} else if polished.is_success() {
    Outcome::partial("BFGS reached stationarity but not known global minimum f = 0")
        .explanation("Vary DE generations, population or seed to search again for the origin's basin.")
} else if matches!(termination, OptimizeTermination::LineSearchFailed(_)) {
    Outcome::failed("BFGS line search found no acceptable step").explanation(
        "The returned point has no successful status; check objective and origin distance separately.",
    )
} else {
    Outcome::partial("BFGS stopped before passing its gradient test")
};
let outcome = outcome
    .status(termination.to_string())
    .diagnostic("Objective f", polished.fun(), None)
    .diagnostic("Distance from origin", norm(polished.x()), None);
emit_outcome!(outcome);
```

At defaults BFGS moves from $(3.4,-3.4)$ to $(-1.990,1.990)$ in 4 iterations, terminating with `GradientSmall` at gradient norm $1.9\times10^{-10}$.
Yet objective 7.96 and origin distance 2.81 show that it accurately found a local minimum.
This minimum is not even the nearest one $(2.985,-2.985)$ to the start, but lies across the origin.
Starts alone do not readily predict which minimum local methods reach.

DE uses 121 generations and 4392 evaluations to return objective $1.6\times10^{-13}$ and origin distance $2.9\times10^{-8}$, but termination `PopulationStagnation` reflects contracted objective spread and is not classified as success.
Evaluation count $36\times(121+1)=4392$ matches formula $N_p(G+1)$.
One subsequent BFGS iteration gives objective 0 and `GradientSmall` with gradient norm $2.8\times10^{-9}$, yielding satisfied.
Search selects the basin; local optimization checks stationarity.

The two-stage approach succeeds only when search selects the correct basin.
At generation limit 20, DE stops with `SearchBudgetCompleted` at $f=1.08$.
BFGS then converges with `GradientSmall` to neighboring minimum $(-0.995,0)$, $f=0.995$, giving partial.
Gradient tests alone cannot distinguish this from the origin.

At mutation factor 0.2, the population stagnates at $f=0.112$.
BFGS then approaches within $5.9\times10^{-10}$ of the origin, where $f$ rounds to zero.
Both $x^2$ and $A(1-\cos2\pi x)$ are around $10^{-16}$ or smaller and disappear in roundoff when adding/subtracting $A\cos2\pi x\approx10$ and $2A=20$ in $f$ (1 ulp at 10 is $1.8\times10^{-15}$).
Gradient norm $2.3\times10^{-7}$ still exceeds tolerance; line search cannot verify sufficient decrease and stops with `LineSearchFailed(iteration limit reached)`.
Even at the origin this is unsuccessful, giving failed.
Initial coordinate $s=0.4$ lies in the origin's basin; BFGS alone reaches it in 4 iterations.

The landscape shows grid-like ripples on a paraboloid centered at the origin.
The diagonal section shows near-integer valleys rising outward, with intervening peak heights controlled by $A$.
Amplitude 2 lowers peaks and BFGS stops at minimum $(0.975,-0.975)$, $f=1.95$.

## Summary

BFGS `GradientSmall` establishes only stationarity; default start $(3.4,-3.4)$ reaches local minimum $f=7.96$.
DE `SearchBudgetCompleted` and `PopulationStagnation` are not global-optimality successes; start BFGS from the best point to check stationarity.
Then compare objectives with known answers or other methods to distinguish neighboring local minima.
Near the origin, rounding may make objectives zero and stop line search with `LineSearchFailed`; read location separately from termination.
Compare DE work using evaluation limit $N_p(G+1)$ rather than generations.
