For a Poisson problem with Neumann conditions on every boundary and no reaction, adding a constant to the solution leaves the equation and boundary conditions unchanged ([pure Neumann problems](oxref:pde-pure-neumann-compatibility)).
A load must satisfy a condition for a solution to exist (the compatibility condition), and an additional condition (a gauge) is needed to select a unique solution.
Removing the singularity by adding a small diagonal term solves a different problem.
`scioxide-pde` requires the user to specify a gauge instead of applying this regularization.
This page explains the mechanism through linear algebra and describes how to read the solve report `PdeDiagnostics`.

## Left and right nullspaces

When the matrix $A$ of an $n$-unknown discrete system $A\mathbf{u}=\mathbf{b}$ is singular, the space of vectors satisfying $A\mathbf{r}=0$ is the right nullspace, and the space of vectors satisfying $\mathbf{l}^HA=0$ is the left nullspace ($^H$ denotes the conjugate transpose).
Let $R$ and $L$ be matrices whose columns are orthonormal bases of these spaces, respectively.
The condition that $\mathbf{b}$ lies in the range of $A$, and hence that a solution exists, is $L^H\mathbf{b}=0$; this is the discrete compatibility condition.
The solution is undetermined up to a linear combination of the columns of $R$, so the gauge $R^H\mathbf{u}=\mathbf{c}$ selects one solution.

`BorderedSystem` in `scioxide-linalg` constructs an augmented system by bordering the matrix with the bases.

$$
\begin{bmatrix}A&L\\R^H&0\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{u}\\\boldsymbol{\lambda}\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}\hat{\mathbf{b}}\\\mathbf{c}\end{bmatrix}
$$

$\hat{\mathbf{b}}$ is a load prepared to satisfy the compatibility condition.
With correct bases, this augmented system is nonsingular; its construction checks that the bases are orthonormal and that $\|A\mathbf{r}_j\|$ and $\|A^H\mathbf{l}_j\|$ are within tolerance.
When $A$ is symmetric and $L=R$, the augmented system is symmetric but indefinite, so conjugate gradients, which requires positive definiteness, cannot be used.

`EllipticProblem` obtains the nullspace in two ways.

- **Structurally known constant modes** (`PdeNullspaceSource::StructuralConstant`): when the plan's `has_constant_modes()` is true, normalized constant vectors for each component form the bases $L=R$.
  The solve fixes the first unknown of each component to 0, solves the remaining system with the default direct method (tridiagonal elimination for a one-dimensional tridiagonal system, sparse LU factorization otherwise), and then imposes the gauge.
- **User-supplied bases** (`PdeNullspaceSource::Supplied`): when the nullspace cannot be inferred structurally, as with degenerate diffusion or multiple nullspaces, supply orthonormal bases of the eliminated matrix's left and right nullspaces through `EllipticProblem::with_nullspace(left, right)`.
  In this case, LU factorization solves the sparse augmented system itself, so memory use also depends on the nullspace dimension.

When an iterative method is injected through `EllipticWorkspace::with_linear_solver`, iteration for problems with a nullspace acts on the augmented system.

## Types of gauge

Select a gauge from `EllipticGauge` through `EllipticOptions::with_gauge`.

| `EllipticGauge` | Imposed condition | Applicable cases |
|---|---|---|
| `Unspecified` (default) | None. Problems with a known nullspace are rejected with `InvalidInput` (`UnsupportedConfiguration`) | Problems without a nullspace |
| `Orthogonal` | Sets the coordinates in the right nullspace basis to 0 ($R^H\mathbf{u}=0$). For constant modes, sets each component's arithmetic mean to 0 | Structurally known constant modes and user-supplied bases |
| `WeightedMean(c)` | Sets each component's lumped-mass-weighted mean $\bar u=\sum_i m_iu_i/\sum_i m_i$ to $c$ | Structurally known constant modes only |

$m_i$ is the value from `lumped_mass()`: the nodal control width in FDM (halved at the ends), the cell volume in FVM, and the diagonal of the scaled mass matrix in FEM.
The weighted mean is a quadrature approximation to the continuous field's mean $\frac{1}{|\Omega|}\int_\Omega u\,dx$, so it is suitable for specifying a physical reference such as mean temperature.
Specifying `Orthogonal` or `WeightedMean` for a problem without a nullspace is rejected with `InvalidInput`.
Specifying `WeightedMean` for a problem with user-supplied bases is also rejected.
Because `solve()` accepts no gauge, it cannot be used for a problem with a known nullspace; use `solve_with_options` or `solve_into`.

## The right-hand side changed by projection

`EllipticOptions::with_compatibility(policy, tolerance)` selects how to handle compatibility and the tolerance used to check it.
This tolerance is independent of the solution acceptance tolerance; both default to $\sqrt{\varepsilon}$.

- `CompatibilityPolicy::RequireCompatible` (the default) rejects loads satisfying $\|L^H\mathbf{b}\|_2>\tau_a+\tau_r\|\mathbf{b}\|_2$ with `DomainError` ($\tau_a$ and $\tau_r$ are the absolute and relative compatibility tolerances).
- `CompatibilityPolicy::Project` constructs the projected load $\hat{\mathbf{b}}=\mathbf{b}-L(L^H\mathbf{b})$ and solves for it.

This is a Euclidean projection of the discrete load vector; it does not prescribe how to correct the continuous source term or boundary data.
For a constant mode, projection subtracts the same value from every entry of the load.
FDM and FVM plans also provide `check_constant_mode_compatibility(&rhs, tolerance)` to check load compatibility alone before solving.
It checks whether each component's load sum is within tolerance relative to the sum of absolute values.

## Reading diagnostics

`diagnostics()` on the solve report `PdeSolveReport` returns `PdeDiagnostics`.
For a steady solve, read the following values.

- `termination()` and `method()`: the termination reason and the name of the selected linear solver.
- `linear_residual_report()`: the residual of the original equation and a backward error measure ([solution acceptance checks](oxref:pde-elliptic-acceptance)).
  `residual_norm()` is its raw value.
- `nullspace()`: augmented-system diagnostics `BorderedSystemDiagnostics`.
  These independently evaluate, using the original system, the number of basis columns `nullity()`, the original load's left nullspace component $\|L^H\mathbf{b}\|_2$ (`compatibility_defect()`), the actual modification $\|\mathbf{b}-\hat{\mathbf{b}}\|_2$ (`projection_norm()`), the residual for the original load $\|A\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{b}\|_2$ (`original_residual_norm()`), and the projected residual $\|A\mathbf{u}-\hat{\mathbf{b}}\|_2$ (`projected_residual_norm()`).
- `nullspace_gauge_defect()`: the defect in the requested gauge, which is $|\bar u-c|$ for a weighted mean.
  `EllipticProblem` rejects solutions for which this value exceeds the solution acceptance tolerance with `NumericalInstability`.
- `nullspace_source()`: the source of the nullspace (`PdeNullspaceSource`), or `None` if there is no nullspace.

`gauge_defect()` in `nullspace()` is the defect in the orthogonal gauge $R^H\mathbf{u}=0$ used internally by the augmented system.
With `WeightedMean`, a constant is added to the solution to match the mean, so this value does not measure the error in the requested gauge and becomes large when $c\neq0$.
Check the requested gauge with `nullspace_gauge_defect()`.

For time-dependent solves, the same type also records time step counts (`num_time_steps()`, `num_accepted_steps()`, `num_rejected_steps()`, `num_failed_steps()`), the last step's error estimate, right-hand side and Jacobian evaluation counts, and assembly and factorization counts.

## A Neumann problem with a specified physical mean

Solve $-u''=0$ on $[0,1]$ with outward conormal fluxes (equal to normal derivatives because $k=1$) of $-1$ at the left end and $1+\delta$ at the right end.
With a specified weighted mean $c$, the analytical solution for $\delta=0$ is $u(x)=x+c-\tfrac12$.
The trapezoidal weights integrate the mean of a linear function exactly, so the discrete solution should also match this expression.
When $\delta\neq0$, the load sum becomes $\delta$ and compatibility fails: `RequireCompatible` rejects the solve, whereas `Project` projects the load and solves.
The cell below constructs a 17-node `StructuredFdmPlan` once, solves the same `EllipticProblem` under both policies, and lists the two types of defect and the residuals.
The final row checks that the default settings, which do not specify a gauge, are rejected.
The outcome shows the termination reason for the result solved with `Project`.

```rust
//| id: pde-neumann-physical-mean
//| ref: pde-neumann-physical-mean
//| title: "Specify a physical mean for a Neumann problem and compare policies for incompatible loads"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-spatial, scioxide-pde, scioxide-linalg]
//| inputs:
//|   mean_target: {"type":"range","label":"Specified weighted mean c [-]","min":-2,"max":4,"step":0.5,"value":2}
//|   flux_imbalance: {"type":"range","label":"Excess right-end conormal flux δ [-]","description":"Nonzero values violate compatibility.","min":0,"max":0.2,"step":0.05,"value":0}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"incompatible","label":"Incompatible load δ = 0.2","description":"RequireCompatible rejects it with DomainError; Project solves with an original residual equal to the compatibility defect, 4.85×10⁻².","inputs":{"flux_imbalance":0.2}}
//|   - {"id":"negative-mean","label":"Mean c = −2","description":"The solution shifts to the specified mean, and its difference from the analytical solution u = x − 2.5 is at roundoff level.","inputs":{"mean_target":-2}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":"Completed"}
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/1/1","equals":"Completed"}
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/1/2","equals":"StructuralConstant"}
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/0/6","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/0/7","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-13}}
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/2/1","equals":"Rejected InvalidInput"}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: incompatible
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":"Rejected DomainError"}
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/0/3","equals":null}
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/1/1","equals":"Completed"}
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/1/3","approx":{"value":0.0485071,"rtol":1e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/1/4","approx":{"value":0.0485071,"rtol":1e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/1/5","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/1/7","equals":null}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: negative-mean
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/1/6","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"policies","path":"/rows/1/7","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-13}}
use scioxide_core::ResidualTolerance;
use scioxide_linalg::CompatibilityPolicy;
use scioxide_pde::{
    BoundaryCondition, BoundaryData, ConstantDiffusion, EllipticGauge, EllipticOptions,
    EllipticProblem, FieldData, SpatialDiscretization, StructuredBoundaries, StructuredFdmPlan,
};
use scioxide_spatial::{
    AxisSide, AxisTopology, BoundarySet, CoordinateAxis, Grid1d, SegmentMesh,
};

let axis = CoordinateAxis::uniform(0.0_f64, 1.0, 17).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let grid = Grid1d::new([axis], [AxisTopology::Bounded]).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mesh = SegmentMesh::new(grid).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
// Outward conormal flux k du/dn (k = 1): −1 at the left end, 1 + δ at the right end.
let side = |side| BoundarySet::axis_side(&mesh, 0, side).map_err(|e| e.to_string());
let conditions = vec![
    BoundaryCondition::natural(side(AxisSide::Start)?, BoundaryData::constant(-1.0)),
    BoundaryCondition::natural(
        side(AxisSide::End)?,
        BoundaryData::constant(1.0 + flux_imbalance),
    ),
];
let boundaries = StructuredBoundaries::new(&mesh, 1, conditions).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let k = ConstantDiffusion::new(1.0);
let plan = StructuredFdmPlan::new(mesh, &k, &boundaries).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let source = FieldData::zero();
let problem = EllipticProblem::new(&plan, &source);
let tolerance = ResidualTolerance::new(1e-10, 1e-10).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mut rows = Vec::new();
let mut outcome = None;
for (label, policy) in [
    ("RequireCompatible", CompatibilityPolicy::RequireCompatible),
    ("Project", CompatibilityPolicy::Project),
] {
    let options = EllipticOptions::new()
        .with_gauge(EllipticGauge::WeightedMean(mean_target))
        .with_compatibility(policy, tolerance);
    let solution = match problem.solve_with_options(&options) {
        Ok(solution) => solution,
        Err(e) => {
            let status = format!("Rejected {:?}", e.kind());
            rows.push((label, status, "-".to_owned(), None, None, None, None, None));
            continue;
        }
    };
    let report = solution.report();
    let d = report.diagnostics();
    let b = d.nullspace().ok_or("Missing nullspace diagnostics")?;
    let gauge = d.nullspace_gauge_defect().ok_or("Missing gauge defect")?;
    let origin = d
        .nullspace_source()
        .map_or("-".to_owned(), |s| format!("{s:?}"));
    // Independent check: compare with the analytical solution u = x + c − 1/2 only when δ = 0.
    let mut error = 0.0_f64;
    for (node, u) in solution.values().iter().enumerate() {
        let x = plan.dof_point(node).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?.point[0];
        error = error.max((u - (x + mean_target - 0.5)).abs());
    }
    let error = (flux_imbalance == 0.0).then_some(error);
    let (defect, original) = (b.compatibility_defect(), b.original_residual_norm());
    let projected = b.projected_residual_norm();
    let status = report.termination().to_string();
    rows.push((
        label,
        status,
        origin,
        Some(defect),
        Some(original),
        Some(projected),
        Some(gauge),
        error,
    ));
    if label == "Project" {
        outcome = Some((report.termination(), b.projection_norm(), gauge));
    }
}
// The default settings without a gauge reject problems with constant modes.
let unspecified = problem.solve_with_options(&EllipticOptions::new());
let status = unspecified
    .err()
    .map_or("Accepted".to_owned(), |e| format!("Rejected {:?}", e.kind()));
let modes = format!("Constant modes {}", plan.has_constant_modes());
rows.push(("Unspecified", status, modes, None, None, None, None, None));
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Policy" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Result" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Nullspace" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Compatibility defect ‖Lᴴb‖ [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3, "nullText": "-" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Residual ‖Au−b‖ [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3, "nullText": "-" },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Residual ‖Au−b̂‖ [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3, "nullText": "-" },
    { "key": "6", "label": "Gauge defect |ū−c| [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3, "nullText": "-" },
    { "key": "7", "label": "Maximum error against analytical solution [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3, "nullText": "-" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "policies" })
);
let (termination, projection, gauge) = outcome.ok_or("Missing Project result")?;
let result = if termination.is_success() {
    Outcome::satisfied("Project solve completed")
        .explanation("When δ ≠ 0, this is a solution for the projected load.")
} else {
    Outcome::partial("Project solve did not complete")
};
emit_outcome!(
    result
        .status(termination.to_string())
        .diagnostic("Load modification ‖b − b̂‖", projection, None)
        .diagnostic("Requested gauge defect", gauge, None)
);
```

At the defaults ($c=2$, $\delta=0$), both policies returned `Completed`, and the nullspace source was `StructuralConstant`.
Both the maximum error against the analytical solution and the requested gauge defect were 0, consistent with the trapezoidal weights integrating the mean of a linear function exactly.
When $\delta=0.2$, the load sum is 0.2, and the compatibility defect is its component $\delta/\sqrt{17}\approx4.85\times10^{-2}$ along the normalized constant vector $(1,\dots,1)/\sqrt{17}$.
`RequireCompatible` rejected it with `DomainError`, and `Project` returned a solution.
That solution's residual for the original load is $4.85\times10^{-2}$, equal to the projection norm, and the projected residual is $1.8\times10^{-14}$.
This residual records that a different load was solved; it is not a solver failure.
`gauge_defect()` in `nullspace()` is omitted from the table.
It refers to the orthogonal gauge before matching the mean, so it becomes large when $c\neq0$.

## Nullspaces by discretization and multidimensional plans

A plan receives reaction coefficients only at sample points and does not determine their sign as a continuous function.
A nonzero reaction, however small, is not treated as 0, and no constant mode is detected.
The structural conditions for detecting constant modes are as follows for each discretization.

| Plan | Conditions for reporting constant modes |
|---|---|
| `StructuredFdmPlan` | All reaction and advection are 0, with no Dirichlet conditions or nonzero Robin coefficients. Periodic axes have no boundary conditions and therefore satisfy this condition |
| `StructuredFvmPlan`, `SimplexFvmPlan` | All reaction is 0, with no Dirichlet conditions or nonzero Robin coefficients. Periodic meshes are not accepted |
| `SimplexFemPlan` | All reaction is 0, with no fixed unknowns or nonzero Robin coefficients |

A plan with multiple components has a constant mode for each component.
For problems with degenerate diffusion or nonconstant nullspaces, supply problem-specific bases through `EllipticProblem::with_nullspace` and solve with the `Orthogonal` gauge.

`EllipticProblem` solves with the same procedure regardless of dimension, for any of `StructuredFdmPlan`, `SimplexFemPlan`, `StructuredFvmPlan`, and `SimplexFvmPlan` ([multidimensional structured-grid differences](../multidimensional-spatial-plans/), [multidimensional finite volumes](../multidimensional-finite-volumes/), [simplex-mesh finite elements](../simplex-finite-elements/)).
FVM uses opposite signs for the same face flux in neighboring cells, so interior contributions cancel, and the oriented face fluxes returned by `face_fluxes` allow balance checks.
Matching a mean and conserving flux are separate properties.
`IncompressibleOptions` manages pressure gauges for incompressible flow: `new()` automatically places a zero weighted-mean gauge on each closed connected component, and `with_gauges` explicitly specifies a sequence of `EllipticGauge` values by connected component ([pressure gauges and flux compatibility](oxref:pde-pressure-gauge)).

## Summary

Elliptic problems with a known nullspace are rejected unless a gauge is explicitly specified through `EllipticOptions::with_gauge`.
Use `WeightedMean(c)` to specify a physical mean, and check its error with `nullspace_gauge_defect()`.
Incompatible loads produce `DomainError` by default; selecting `Project` solves for a projected load, and the compatibility defect, projection norm, original residual, and projected residual in `nullspace()` show the size of the change.
For a nullspace that cannot be inferred structurally, supply orthonormal bases through `with_nullspace` and solve with `Orthogonal`.
