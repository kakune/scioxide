The residual $L(u)$ constructed in [Conservation laws and numerical fluxes](../conservation-laws/) and [Conservation-law reconstruction and boundaries](../conservation-reconstruction/) is the right-hand side of the cell-average ordinary differential equation $du/dt = L(u)$.
This page explains step limits for explicit methods, admissibility checks when advancing with `scioxide-ode` methods, and the spatial-plan APIs bringing them together.

## CFL condition and maximum step

Consider one forward Euler stage with the Rusanov flux for a one-dimensional scalar problem.

$$
\bar u_i^{n+1} = \bar u_i^n - \frac{\Delta t}{h}\Bigl(\hat F(\bar u_i, \bar u_{i+1}) - \hat F(\bar u_{i-1}, \bar u_i)\Bigr)
$$

If the face value $a$ bounds $|f'|$ on both sides, the partial derivatives $\partial_1 \hat F = \tfrac12(f'(u^-) + a)$ and $\partial_2 \hat F = \tfrac12(f'(u^+) - a)$ of $\hat F$ satisfy $0 \le \partial_1 \hat F \le a$ and $-a \le \partial_2 \hat F \le 0$, so the derivative of $\bar u_i^{n+1}$ with respect to $\bar u_i$ is at least $1 - \frac{\Delta t}{h}(a_{i+1/2} + a_{i-1/2})$.
Therefore, under the condition

$$
\frac{\Delta t}{h}\,\bigl(a_{i-1/2} + a_{i+1/2}\bigr) \le 1
$$

the update is a monotone function of the three values, and a maximum principle holds.
For a multidimensional cell $K$, this condition becomes $\Delta t \sum_{f\subset\partial K} |f|\,s_f \le |K|$.
$s_f$ is the maximum signal speed returned by the numerical flux at face $f$.

For second-order reconstruction, split the cell average into an average of face values and write the update as a convex combination of first-order face updates.
In one-dimensional linear reconstruction, the decomposition is $\bar u_i = \tfrac12(u_{i-1/2}^+ + u_{i+1/2}^-)$; each update has twice the step size, so the condition is halved to $\Delta t \sum_f |f|\,s_f \le \tfrac12 |K|$ (Zhang–Shu, 2010).

Using the Courant number $\nu$, `scioxide-pde` sets the maximum step at the state where the residual is evaluated to

$$
\Delta t_{\max} = \min_K \frac{\nu\,|K|}{\sum_{f\subset\partial K} |f|\,s_f}
$$


Select $\nu$ through `ConservationOptions::with_cfl` in the range greater than 0 and at most 1/2; the default is 0.45.
On a uniform one-dimensional grid with constant wave speed $s$, $\Delta t_{\max} = \nu h/(2s)$, and the usual Courant number $s\Delta t/h$ is $\nu/2 \le 1/4$.
`ConservationReport::maximum_step()` returns this value, `limiting_cell()` the cell giving the minimum, and `maximum_speed()` the maximum signal speed over all faces; when all wave speeds are 0, `maximum_step()` returns `None`.

This limit is local, computed from wave speeds at the evaluated state; it does not guarantee stability if wave speeds increase during the step.
The next section's checks handle system admissibility (positive density and pressure).

## SSP time integration

A spatial plan's `bind(equation, flux, workspace)` returns a `ConservationSystem` borrowing the equation, numerical flux, and workspace.
`ConservationSystem` implements `scioxide_ode::OdeSystem`.
The state contains cell averages with $N$ components per cell; the right-hand side is the residual normalized by mass (cell measure $|K|$).
No Jacobian is provided, so advance with an explicit method.

The intended methods are the SSP methods `scioxide_ode::ForwardEuler` and `scioxide_ode::Heun`.
`Heun` is the two-stage SSPRK2 method

$$
u^{(1)} = u^n + \Delta t\,L(u^n),\qquad
u^{n+1} = \tfrac12 u^n + \tfrac12\bigl(u^{(1)} + \Delta t\,L(u^{(1)})\bigr)
$$

where $L$ is the spatial residual.
Each stage consists of a forward Euler stage and convex combinations, so it preserves the properties maintained by forward Euler below the limit $\Delta t_{\mathrm{FE}}$ (maximum principle, TVD, and the admissible set) under the same step limit (SSP coefficient 1; Gottlieb–Shu, 1998).

Admissibility is checked through `OdeSystem::admits`.
If any cell average in a stage input ($u^{(1)}$ in `Heun`) or candidate $u^{n+1}$ is inadmissible, the trial is rejected without changing the accepted state and retried with half the step size.
Values are never clipped.
If a retry step falls below the minimum from `OdeOptions::with_minimum_step` or floating-point time cannot advance, integration stops with `StepSizeLimit` and returns the last accepted state (a partial result).
Calling `rhs` directly with an inadmissible state produces an `InvalidInput` error (argument `y`).

Explicit Runge–Kutta methods receive the first residual at the accepted state and the CFL limit together through `OdeSystem::rhs_with_step_limit`, avoiding an extra residual evaluation for the limit.
Each step is the minimum of the method's proposed step, $\Delta t_{\max}$, the maximum step in `OdeOptions`, and the remaining interval.
Because `ForwardEuler` and `Heun` are fixed-step methods, passing a large value to `with_initial_step` makes every step equal to $\Delta t_{\max}$.
Other methods receive the same limit through `step_limit`, which evaluates the residual once more.
`ConservationSystem::report(time, state, execution)` returns a `ConservationReport` for one residual evaluation without retaining a cache.

## Spatial plans and workspaces

A spatial plan prepares and stores mesh geometry, face pairs, reconstruction stencils, and boundary conditions.

- `StructuredConservationPlan::new(mesh, conditions)` and `with_options(mesh, conditions, options)`: structured grids of segments, quadrilaterals, or hexahedra (`CellMesh`).
  `conditions` is a sequence of `(BoundarySet, ConservationBoundary)` pairs that must cover each bounded exterior face exactly once.
  Do not place conditions at periodic seams.
- `SimplexConservationPlan::new(mesh, tags, conditions)` and `with_options(mesh, tags, conditions, options)`: triangles and tetrahedra (`SimplexMesh`, $D = 2, 3$).
  `SimplexBoundaryTags` supplies a tag per exterior face, and `conditions` contains one `(u32, ConservationBoundary)` per tag.
  Untagged exterior faces, tags without conditions, duplicate conditions, and unused conditions are rejected.

`ConservationOptions::new()` selects constant reconstruction, $\nu = 0.45$, sequential execution, and no resource limits.
Change these through `with_reconstruction`, `with_cfl`, `with_execution_policy`, and `with_limits`, and read them with the corresponding accessors.
The execution policy deterministically divides independent cell and face operations among at most 32 workers.
`ConservationLimits::new(max_unknowns, max_faces, max_storage_bytes)` limits cell unknowns across all components, face counts including duplicated periodic seams, and bytes for preparation and one workspace; exceeding a limit makes preparation fail with `InvalidInput`.
The byte estimate excludes the mesh, boundary callbacks, ODE storage, and output fields; read it through the plan's `storage_bound()`.

The main plan methods are as follows.

- `mesh()`, `centers()`, `volumes()`, `unknowns()`: the mesh, cell centroids, cell measures, and ODE component count (cell count × $N$).
- `workspace()`: allocates a `ConservationWorkspace`.
  It can be reused with plans of the same dimensions, and successful evaluations allocate no library storage, including on the first evaluation.
- `residual_into(equation, flux, time, state, output, workspace, execution)`: writes the mass-normalized residual to `output` and returns `ConservationReport`.
  Its cost is $O((\text{cells} + \text{faces})\,N D)$, and failure leaves `output` unchanged.
- `face_fluxes_into(...)`: writes oriented fluxes multiplied by face measure in canonical face order.
  Duplicated faces at periodic seams receive the same flux.
- `bind`, `initial_state(equation, field)`, `field(equation, state)`: construct an ODE system and convert between typed fields and ODE state vectors.
  Both conversions reject inadmissible states.

## When the CFL limit is not exposed

The next cell advances Toro's double-rarefaction problem ($\rho = 1$, $p = 0.4$, $u = -2$ for $x < 0.5$, $u = 2$ for $x > 0.5$, $\gamma = 1.4$) to time 0.15 on 200 cells over $[0, 1]$, using `Hllc`, MC reconstruction, and transmissive boundaries.
A region with density and pressure near 0 forms in the center, making admissibility checks likely to act.
With the same initial step $\Delta t_0$, it compares passing `ConservationSystem` directly with passing a system forwarding only `rhs` and `admits`, hiding the CFL limit.
The latter tries steps of size $\Delta t_0$; inadmissible stage inputs or candidates are rejected and the step is halved.
The table shows termination reasons, accepted and rejected counts, minimum density and pressure over accepted states, and `ConservationReport` values for the final state.

```rust

//| ref: pde-conservation-admissibility-retry
//| title: "Compare double-rarefaction steps and rejections with and without a CFL limit"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-ode, scioxide-pde, scioxide-spatial]
//| inputs:
//|   method: {"type":"select","label":"Time integration method","options":["Heun","ForwardEuler"],"value":"Heun"}
//|   cfl: {"type":"range","label":"Courant number ν [-]","description":"Coefficient in the CFL limit Δt_max = ν|K|/Σ|f|s_f.","min":0.05,"max":0.5,"step":0.05,"value":0.45}
//|   initial_step: {"type":"range","label":"Initial-step exponent p (Δt₀ = 10^p)","min":-4,"max":-1,"step":0.5,"value":-2}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"forward-euler","label":"ForwardEuler","description":"Forward Euler also proceeds at a constant step after three rejections when CFL is hidden.","inputs":{"method":"ForwardEuler"}}
//|   - {"id":"large-step","label":"Δt₀ = 0.1","description":"A larger initial step increases rejections to six, then advances to the end at the admissible step 0.1/64.","inputs":{"initial_step":-1}}
//|   - {"id":"half-cfl","label":"ν = 0.25","description":"Reducing the Courant number decreases steps and increases the accepted count for the run exposing CFL.","inputs":{"cfl":0.25}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"runs","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":"ReachedEnd"}
//|       - {"artifact":"runs","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":367}
//|       - {"artifact":"runs","path":"/rows/0/3","equals":0}
//|       - {"artifact":"runs","path":"/rows/1/2","equals":120}
//|       - {"artifact":"runs","path":"/rows/1/3","equals":3}
//|       - {"artifact":"runs","path":"/rows/0/5","approx":{"value":0.0067097975,"rtol":1e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"runs","path":"/rows/0/8","approx":{"value":0.00040933879,"rtol":1e-5}}
//|   - case: forward-euler
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"runs","path":"/rows/1/3","equals":3}
//|       - {"artifact":"runs","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":"ReachedEnd"}
//|   - case: large-step
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"runs","path":"/rows/1/3","equals":6}
//|       - {"artifact":"runs","path":"/rows/1/2","equals":96}
//|   - case: half-cfl
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"runs","path":"/rows/0/3","equals":0}
use scioxide_core::{ExecutionContext, SciOxideError, RealScalar, SolveContext};
use scioxide_ode::{ForwardEuler, Heun, OdeMethod, OdeOptions, OdeOutputSchedule, OdeProblem};
use scioxide_ode::{OdeState, OdeSystem};
use scioxide_pde::{ConservationBoundary, ConservationOptions, Hllc, IdealGasEuler};
use scioxide_pde::{ConservationReconstruction, StructuredConservationPlan};
use scioxide_spatial::SegmentMesh;
use scioxide_spatial::{AxisSide, AxisTopology, BoundarySet, CellField, CoordinateAxis, Grid1d};

// A system forwarding only rhs and admits, without exposing a CFL limit (step_limit, etc.).
struct WithoutCfl<'a, S>(&'a S);
impl<S: OdeSystem<f64>> OdeSystem<f64> for WithoutCfl<'_, S> {
    fn dimension(&self) -> usize {
        self.0.dimension()
    }
    fn rhs(
        &self,
        t: f64,
        y: &[f64],
        out: &mut [f64],
        cx: &mut SolveContext<'_, '_>,
    ) -> Result<(), SciOxideError> {
        self.0.rhs(t, y, out, cx)
    }
    fn admits(
        &self,
        t: f64,
        y: &[f64],
        cx: &mut SolveContext<'_, '_>,
    ) -> Result<bool, SciOxideError> {
        self.0.admits(t, y, cx)
    }
}

let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let gas = IdealGasEuler::new(1.4).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let execution = ExecutionContext::sequential();
let mut rows = Vec::new();
for advertised in [true, false] {
    let axis = CoordinateAxis::uniform(0.0, 1.0, 201).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let grid = Grid1d::new([axis], [AxisTopology::Bounded]).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let mesh = SegmentMesh::new(grid).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let start = BoundarySet::axis_side(&mesh, 0, AxisSide::Start).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let finish = BoundarySet::axis_side(&mesh, 0, AxisSide::End).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let conditions = vec![
        (start, ConservationBoundary::Transmissive),
        (finish, ConservationBoundary::Transmissive),
    ];
    let options = ConservationOptions::new()
        .with_reconstruction(ConservationReconstruction::MonotonizedCentral)
        .with_cfl(cfl);
    let plan = StructuredConservationPlan::with_options(mesh, conditions, options)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let values = plan
        .centers()
        .iter()
        .map(|x| gas.conserved(1.0, [if x[0] < 0.5 { -2.0 } else { 2.0 }], 0.4))
        .collect::<Result<Vec<[f64; 3]>, _>>()
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let field = CellField::new(plan.mesh(), values).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let mut workspace = plan.workspace().map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let bound = plan
        .bind(&gas, &Hllc, &mut workspace)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let hidden = WithoutCfl(&bound);
    let system: &dyn OdeSystem<f64> = if advertised { &bound } else { &hidden };
    let problem = OdeProblem::new(system, 0.15);
    let options = OdeOptions::new()
        .with_initial_step(RealScalar::powf(10.0, initial_step))
        .with_output_schedule(OdeOutputSchedule::AcceptedSteps);
    let vector = plan
        .initial_state(&gas, field.view())
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let mut state = OdeState::new(0.0, vector);
    // Minimum density and pressure over accepted states. Every accepted state is admissible.
    let (mut density, mut pressure) = (f64::INFINITY, f64::INFINITY);
    let mut sink = |_: f64, y: &[f64]| {
        for u in y.as_chunks::<3>().0 {
            density = RealScalar::min(density, u[0]);
            pressure = RealScalar::min(pressure, gas.pressure::<1, 3>(u)?);
        }
        Ok(())
    };
    let solve = if method == "Heun" {
        let ode = Heun::new();
        let mut work = ode
            .workspace(&problem, &options)
            .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
        ode.advance(
            &problem, &options, &mut state, &mut sink, &mut work, execution,
        )
    } else {
        let ode = ForwardEuler::new();
        let mut work = ode
            .workspace(&problem, &options)
            .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
        ode.advance(
            &problem, &options, &mut state, &mut sink, &mut work, execution,
        )
    }
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let report = bound
        .report(state.time(), state.values(), execution)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    rows.push((
        if advertised {
            "CFL exposed"
        } else {
            "CFL hidden"
        },
        solve.termination().map_or("None".into(), |t| t.to_string()),
        solve.accepted_steps(),
        solve.rejected_steps(),
        solve.final_time(),
        density,
        pressure,
        report.hlle_fallback_faces(),
        report.maximum_step(),
    ));
}
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "System" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Termination reason" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Accepted [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Rejected [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Final time [-]", "type": "number", "format": { "significantDigits": 6 } },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Minimum density [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "6", "label": "Minimum pressure [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "7", "label": "Faces using HLLE fallback [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "8", "label": "Last Δt_max [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3, "nullText": "None" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "runs" })
);
```

At the defaults (`Heun`, $\nu = 0.45$, $\Delta t_0 = 10^{-2}$), the run exposing the CFL limit accepted 367 steps with no rejections.
The final state's $\Delta t_{\max}$ is $4.09 \times 10^{-4}$, and the maximum signal speed is about 2.75, so the usual Courant number $s\Delta t/h$ is 0.225.
With the limit hidden, trials at $\Delta t_0 = 10^{-2}$ were rejected three times, reducing the step to $1.25 \times 10^{-3}$; 120 steps of that size reached the end.
For fixed-step methods, the step halved by rejection becomes the step for subsequent advances.
This step corresponds to a Courant number of about 0.69, exceeding the theoretical range in the preceding section, but every accepted state was admissible and the run ended with `ReachedEnd`.

In both runs, the minimum density over accepted states ($6.7 \times 10^{-3}$ and $7.5 \times 10^{-3}$) is below the exact central density $\rho^* = 0.0219$.
This difference is spatial discretization error; in the double-rarefaction computation in [Sod's shock tube](../../examples/sod-shock-tube/), density error decreases with more cells.
Admissibility checks catch violations of positivity and retry trials, but do not guarantee step stability or solution accuracy.
Let steps follow the CFL limit and use admissibility checks as protection against its being a local estimate.

## Summary

Choose explicit steps so that each cell satisfies $\Delta t \sum_f |f| s_f \le \nu |K|$.
`ConservationReport::maximum_step` returns this limit at every residual evaluation, and `ConservationSystem` passes it to explicit Runge–Kutta methods together with the first residual, so step control needs no additional evaluation.
The SSP methods `ForwardEuler` and `Heun` preserve forward Euler's maximum principle and TVD properties under this limit.
Positive density and pressure are checked stage by stage through `admits`; inadmissible trials are rejected and the step halved, and reaching the minimum step stops with a `StepSizeLimit` partial result.
Because admissibility checks do not replace stability, pass the system exposing its limit directly to `OdeMethod`.
