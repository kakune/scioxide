This page collects items for finite-volume discretization of homogeneous hyperbolic conservation laws $\partial_t u+\nabla\cdot F(u)=0$.
States are cell averages; each face evaluates a numerical flux, using an exact or approximate Riemann solver, from left and right reconstructed states.
A spatial Plan constructs the mass-normalized residual $du_K/dt=-\frac{1}{|K|}\sum_{F\subset\partial K} |F|\,\hat F_{K,F}$.
Here $K$ is a cell, $|K|$ its volume, $F$ a face of $K$, $|F|$ the face measure, and $\hat F_{K,F}$ the numerical flux in the outward unit-normal direction from $K$.
`OdeMethod` in `scioxide-ode` performs time integration.
Through `OdeSystem::admits`, `ConservationSystem` exposes density, pressure, and other admissibility conditions for stage inputs and candidates; inadmissible trials are rejected without changing the accepted state and retried at half the step size.

## Time integration

`ConservationSystem` is intended for integration with the built-in strong-stability-preserving (SSP) methods `scioxide_ode::ForwardEuler` and `scioxide_ode::Heun` (SSPRK2) through `scioxide_ode::OdeMethod`.
Heun's first forward-Euler substep is checked for admissibility as the second stage's input, and its second substep as the candidate convex combination.
If retries reach the minimum step size, integration stops with `StepSizeLimit` without clipping values.
Explicit Runge–Kutta Plans receive the CFL limit together with the first-stage residual, avoiding extra residual evaluations for the limit.

## Summary

Conservation-law Plans construct cell-average residuals and face fluxes, passing CFL step limits and admissibility to `OdeMethod` as an ODE system.
Numerical flux implementations depend on the equation: `Godunov` supports the two scalar equations, `Hlle` / `Hllc` support only Euler equations, and `Rusanov` supports every equation.
Choose reconstruction by grid family: use `Minmod` / `MonotonizedCentral` on structured grids and `BarthJespersen` on simplex meshes.
Density and pressure positivity is checked at every stage and candidate through `admits` of `ConservationSystem`, without clipping values.
Read CFL, boundary-balance, and limiting diagnostics for one residual evaluation with `ConservationReport`.
