This page collects items for passing semidiscrete systems to `scioxide-ode` and `scioxide-rootfind` solvers.
For spatial-discretization plans, `SemidiscreteSystem` constructs physical residuals for free unknowns; custom systems describe physical residuals with `scioxide_ode::OdeSystem`.
The only method-of-lines adapter is `MethodOfLinesPlan`, which normalizes diagonal masses and coordinate scales for time integration; `ParabolicProblem` and `HyperbolicProblem` use it internally too.
`NonlinearPdeProblem` passes the same right-hand-side form to Newton–Krylov as a steady residual $f(t,u)=0$; dense and sparse derivative representations reuse the assembled Jacobian for products at the same state.
No plan holds iteration or acceptance state; convergence is determined from the injected solver's report.

## Method-of-lines normalization

Treat $M$ of $My'=f(t,y)$ (or undamped $Mu''=f(t,u)$) and scales $D$ of physical state $y=Dz$ as positive real diagonal matrices, passing the system in normalized coordinates $z$ to an ODE solver.
General, singular, or state-dependent masses are not inferred; parameter-dependent masses use residual DAEs.
The `CflPolicy::Automatic` limit assumes real nonpositive eigenvalues for first-order dynamics (symmetric diffusion with diagonal mass) and purely imaginary eigenvalues for second-order dynamics.

## Summary

`SemidiscreteSystem` constructs a plan's semidiscrete system; bind it to `MethodOfLinesPlan` with masses and structure set through `method_of_lines` and integrate with any ODE solver.
Describe custom systems as physical residuals with `OdeSystem`, leaving diagonal-mass and scale normalization to the same adapter.
Classify integrated ODE reports into PDE termination reasons with `PdeSolveReport::try_from` and reconstruct complete physical fields with `MethodOfLinesSystem::reconstruct_into`.
Pass providers of the same form to Newton–Krylov as steady residuals through `NonlinearPdeProblem`, explicitly selecting dense, sparse, or operator derivatives with `PdeJacobianMode`.
Borrow fixed-state tangents through `PdeTangent` and combine fieldwise preconditioners with `PdeFieldPreconditioner`.
