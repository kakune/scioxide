This page collects contracts shared by all spatial discretizations, adapters for constant and isotropic coefficients, boundary-condition and space-time-data vocabulary, and time-integration settings.
One-dimensional problems use the same plans and solves as two- and three-dimensional problems.
A one-dimensional structured grid is `SegmentMesh` (`CellMesh<_, 1>`) from `scioxide-spatial`; a one-dimensional simplex mesh is `IntervalMesh` (`SimplexMesh<_, 1>`).
Plans themselves are covered in [Spatial discretization plans](../multidimensional-discretizations/), and solve lifecycles and results in [Solves, outputs, and diagnostics](../solves-outputs-and-diagnostics/).

## Spatial discretization contracts

The four plans expose linear spatial discretizations $Au=b(t)$ in the same form, with a time-independent eliminated operator $A$ and a load $b(t)$ evaluated at any time.
Unknowns are ordered with components grouped on the outside (component-major); Dirichlet unknowns have unit rows and columns, with their values entering through the load.
Steady solves evaluate data at time 0.

## Boundary conditions

One vocabulary applies to every family.
Natural-boundary data are outward conormal fluxes $n\cdot K\nabla u=g$; Robin data are $n\cdot K\nabla u+\alpha u=g$.
Periodicity belongs to mesh topology (`AxisTopology::Periodic`), not boundary conditions; no conditions are placed on periodic-seam faces.

## Time-integration settings

Settings for time integration of parabolic $Mu'=b(t)-Au$ and hyperbolic $Mu''=b(t)-Au$ problems.
The final time belongs to `ParabolicProblem`, not the settings.
Every boundary-condition kind and time-dependent sources and boundary data are accepted, but coefficients are time-independent.

## Summary

Custom plans and the four built-in plans all pass the eliminated operator $A$, lumped mass, and load $b(t)$ in the same form through `SpatialDiscretization`.
Supply constant or position-dependent isotropic coefficients with `ConstantDiffusion` and `IsotropicDiffusion` without implementing a coefficient trait.
Write boundary conditions with one vocabulary declaring kind, region, component, and time dependence, and bind them to one mesh revision through `StructuredBoundaries` or `TaggedBoundaries`.
Natural-boundary data are outward conormal fluxes in every family; periodicity is represented by mesh topology.
Time-integration settings hold the method, step size, tolerances, output, evaluation limits, and stability restrictions on steps; final time belongs to the problem.
