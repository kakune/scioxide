This page applies `MethodOfLinesPlan` and `MethodOfLinesStagePreconditioner` from [Method of lines and semidiscrete systems](../../algorithms/method-of-lines-and-nonlinear/) to a stiff two-field system coupled by heat exchange.
The two heat capacities differ by two orders of magnitude and become diagonal semidiscrete mass.
Normalize physical-unit residuals for integration, compare time-integration errors with tolerances, and examine different implicit-stage linear solvers.

## Thin-film two-temperature model

In a metal film heated by a femtosecond laser, absorbing electrons heat first and transfer heat to the lattice through electron–lattice coupling.
The two-temperature model (S. I. Anisimov, B. L. Kapeliovich, T. L. Perel'man, *Sov. Phys. JETP* **39**, 375 (1974)) represents electron temperature $T_e$ and lattice temperature $T_l$ as separate fields:

$$
C_e\,\frac{\partial T_e}{\partial t}=k_e\,\frac{\partial^2 T_e}{\partial x^2}-G\,(T_e-T_l),\qquad
C_l\,\frac{\partial T_l}{\partial t}=k_l\,\frac{\partial^2 T_l}{\partial x^2}+G\,(T_e-T_l)
$$


$C_e,C_l$ [J/(m³·K)] are volumetric heat capacities, $k_e,k_l$ [W/(m·K)] conductivities, and $G$ [W/(m³·K)] the electron–lattice coupling constant.
Hold both faces of a film of thickness $L=1$ µm at ambient temperature; interpret $T_e,T_l$ as temperature rises above ambient and impose $T_e=T_l=0$ as Dirichlet data at both ends.
Choose parameters of gold-like magnitude: $C_e=2.0\times10^4$ and $C_l=2.5\times10^6$ J/(m³·K), $k_e=300$ and $k_l=2$ W/(m·K), and $G=2\times10^{16}$ W/(m³·K).
Actual electronic heat capacity is proportional to temperature, but here it is constant to give a linear system.
Initial conditions are $T_e(x,0)=100\sin(\pi x/L)$ K and $T_l(x,0)=0$.

There are three time scales.
Electron-to-lattice heat transfer takes $C_e/G=1$ ps, electron diffusion across film thickness takes $C_e/(k_e\pi^2/L^2)\approx6.8$ ps, and joint cooling is estimated at $(C_e+C_l)/((k_e+k_l)\pi^2/L^2)\approx850$ ps.
The slow eigenvalue of the two-variable system derived below gives cooling time about 970 ps (the estimate differs because $k_e\pi^2/L^2$ is about 15% of $G$: even in the slow mode, electrons remain cooler than the lattice and electronic conduction loses less heat than estimated).
The highest-wavenumber electron mode on spacing $h$ decays over $C_e h^2/(4k_e)$, giving $4\times10^{-3}$ ps for $n=63$ points.
The ratio of slowest to fastest time scales exceeds $10^5$, making the semidiscrete system stiff.

Divide the interval into $n+1$ equal parts, use three-point differences at interior nodes $x_i=ih$, and multiply each nodal equation by $h$ to obtain

$$
(C_e h)\,\dot T_{e,i}=\frac{k_e}{h}\left(T_{e,i-1}-2T_{e,i}+T_{e,i+1}\right)-hG\,(T_{e,i}-T_{l,i})
$$

and an equation of the same form for $T_l$.
Left-hand factors $C_e h$ and $C_l h$ form diagonal mass $\mathbf{M}$; the right-hand side is physical residual $\mathbf{f}$ (units W/m²).
Order the state as $(T_{e,1},\dots,T_{e,n},T_{l,1},\dots,T_{l,n})$.

Initial vector $\sin(\pi x_i/L)$ is an eigenvector of the three-point Dirichlet matrix, with eigenvalue $-\mu_h$, $\mu_h=(4/h^2)\sin^2(\pi h/(2L))$.
Since both initial fields are proportional to this vector, semidiscrete solutions retain forms $\mathbf{T}_e(t)=A_e(t)\,\mathbf{s}$ and $\mathbf{T}_l(t)=A_l(t)\,\mathbf{s}$ ($s_i=\sin(\pi x_i/L)$), with amplitudes following the two-variable linear system

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\begin{pmatrix}A_e\\A_l\end{pmatrix}=K\begin{pmatrix}A_e\\A_l\end{pmatrix},\qquad
K=\begin{pmatrix}-(k_e\mu_h+G)/C_e & G/C_e\\ G/C_l & -(k_l\mu_h+G)/C_l\end{pmatrix}
$$


Using eigenvalues $r_1\ne r_2$ of $K$ gives $e^{Kt}=\left[e^{r_1t}(K-r_2I)-e^{r_2t}(K-r_1I)\right]/(r_1-r_2)$, from which the exact semidiscrete solution is obtained in closed form.
Replacing $\mu_h$ with continuous $\mu=(\pi/L)^2$ gives the exact PDE solution.
Numerical-minus-exact-semidiscrete differences measure only time-integration error; differences between the two exact solutions measure only spatial discretization error.

The following block defines the model shared by two cells.
In `TwoTemperature`, `rhs` and `jacobian_product` return residual $\mathbf{f}$ and its derivative; `sparse_jacobian` returns the Jacobian on sparse structure; `mass` returns diagonal mass $C h$.
`amplitudes` is the closed form for two amplitudes; pass $\mu_h$ or $\mu$ as argument `mu`.
`FieldStage` and `StageInverse` are stage preconditioners used later ([Stage Krylov methods and fieldwise preconditioning](#stage-krylov-methods-and-fieldwise-preconditioning)).

```rust
//| id: pde-two-temperature-model
//| kind: definitions
//| title: "Thin-film two-temperature model and stage preconditioning"
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-ode, scioxide-pde]
use core::cell::{Cell, RefCell};
use scioxide_core::{
    ErrorTolerance, SciOxideError, NormSelection, Preconditioner, SolveContext, StructuralPattern,
    StructuralPatternView,
};
use scioxide_ode::OdeSystem;
use scioxide_pde::{MethodOfLinesStagePreconditioner, PdeFieldPreconditioner};

/// Interior nodes of a film of thickness L = 1 µm. Component order [T_e,1..T_e,n, T_l,1..T_l,n]; field 0 is electronic.
pub(crate) struct TwoTemperature {
    pub(crate) nodes: usize,
    pub(crate) spacing: f64,
    pub(crate) conductivity: [f64; 2],
    pub(crate) capacity: [f64; 2],
    pub(crate) coupling: f64,
}

impl TwoTemperature {
    pub(crate) const LENGTH: f64 = 1.0e-6;

    pub(crate) fn new(nodes: usize, coupling: f64) -> Self {
        Self {
            nodes,
            spacing: Self::LENGTH / (nodes + 1) as f64,
            conductivity: [300.0, 2.0],
            capacity: [2.0e4, 2.5e6],
            coupling,
        }
    }

    /// Characteristic field magnitudes [K] (electron, lattice), used for coordinate scales D and tolerances.
    pub(crate) const TYPICAL: [f64; 2] = [100.0, 1.0];

    /// Coordinate scales D (in state order).
    pub(crate) fn scales(&self) -> Vec<f64> {
        (0..2 * self.nodes)
            .map(|row| Self::TYPICAL[row / self.nodes])
            .collect()
    }

    /// Initial data: T_e = 100 sin(πx/L) K, T_l = 0.
    pub(crate) fn initial(&self) -> Vec<f64> {
        (0..2 * self.nodes)
            .map(|row| {
                if row < self.nodes {
                    Self::TYPICAL[0] * self.mode(row)
                } else {
                    0.0
                }
            })
            .collect()
    }

    /// Physical tolerances: relative 10^p, absolute 10^p × (characteristic field magnitude) [K].
    pub(crate) fn tolerance(&self, exponent: f64) -> Result<ErrorTolerance<f64>, SciOxideError> {
        let epsilon = 10.0_f64.powf(exponent);
        let absolute: Vec<f64> = self.scales().iter().map(|d| epsilon * d).collect();
        ErrorTolerance::try_vector(&absolute, epsilon, NormSelection::Infinity)
    }

    /// Diagonal mass C h [J/(m²·K)] (in state order).
    pub(crate) fn mass(&self) -> Vec<f64> {
        (0..2 * self.nodes)
            .map(|row| self.capacity[row / self.nodes] * self.spacing)
            .collect()
    }

    /// sin(π x_i / L) at node i. Shape of initial and exact solutions.
    pub(crate) fn mode(&self, i: usize) -> f64 {
        let s = (i + 1) as f64 / (self.nodes + 1) as f64;
        scioxide_core::RealScalar::sin(core::f64::consts::PI * s)
    }

    /// Magnitude of three-point eigenvalue μ_h = (4/h²) sin²(πh/(2L)).
    pub(crate) fn discrete_mu(&self) -> f64 {
        let h = self.spacing;
        let s = scioxide_core::RealScalar::sin(core::f64::consts::PI * h / (2.0 * Self::LENGTH));
        4.0 / (h * h) * s * s
    }

    /// Closed-form amplitudes (A_e, A_l)(t) = e^{Kt} (A_e(0), 0) (for checking, standard f64 functions).
    pub(crate) fn amplitudes(&self, mu: f64, initial: f64, time: f64) -> [f64; 2] {
        let ([ce, cl], [ke, kl], g) = (self.capacity, self.conductivity, self.coupling);
        let k = [[-(ke * mu + g) / ce, g / ce], [g / cl, -(kl * mu + g) / cl]];
        let (trace, det) = (k[0][0] + k[1][1], k[0][0] * k[1][1] - k[0][1] * k[1][0]);
        let root = (trace * trace / 4.0 - det).sqrt();
        let (r1, r2) = (trace / 2.0 + root, trace / 2.0 - root);
        let (e1, e2) = ((r1 * time).exp(), (r2 * time).exp());
        let column = |row: usize, identity: f64| {
            (e1 * (k[row][0] - r2 * identity) - e2 * (k[row][0] - r1 * identity)) / (r1 - r2)
        };
        [initial * column(0, 1.0), initial * column(1, 0.0)]
    }

    /// Structure of tridiagonal blocks per field and electron–lattice coupling at the same node.
    pub(crate) fn pattern(&self) -> Result<StructuralPattern, SciOxideError> {
        let n = self.nodes;
        let (mut rows, mut columns) = (vec![0], Vec::new());
        for row in 0..2 * n {
            let (field, i) = (row / n, row % n);
            let mut entries: Vec<usize> = (i.saturating_sub(1)..=(i + 1).min(n - 1))
                .map(|j| field * n + j)
                .collect();
            entries.push((1 - field) * n + i);
            entries.sort_unstable();
            columns.extend(entries);
            rows.push(columns.len());
        }
        StructuralPattern::new(2 * n, 2 * n, rows, columns)
    }

    /// f(T) = (k/h) δ² T ∓ h G (T_e − T_l). Linearity makes JVP identical.
    fn apply(&self, x: &[f64], out: &mut [f64]) {
        let n = self.nodes;
        for row in 0..2 * n {
            let i = row % n;
            let below = if i > 0 { x[row - 1] } else { 0.0 };
            let above = if i + 1 < n { x[row + 1] } else { 0.0 };
            let diffusion =
                self.conductivity[row / n] / self.spacing * (below - 2.0 * x[row] + above);
            let exchange = self.spacing * self.coupling * (x[i] - x[n + i]);
            out[row] = if row < n {
                diffusion - exchange
            } else {
                diffusion + exchange
            };
        }
    }
}

impl OdeSystem<f64> for TwoTemperature {
    fn dimension(&self) -> usize {
        2 * self.nodes
    }

    fn jacobian_is_constant(&self) -> bool {
        true
    }

    fn rhs(
        &self,
        _: f64,
        x: &[f64],
        out: &mut [f64],
        _: &mut SolveContext<'_, '_>,
    ) -> Result<(), SciOxideError> {
        self.apply(x, out);
        Ok(())
    }

    fn jacobian_product(
        &self,
        _: f64,
        _: &[f64],
        v: &[f64],
        out: &mut [f64],
        _: &mut [f64],
        _: &mut SolveContext<'_, '_>,
    ) -> Result<Option<()>, SciOxideError> {
        self.apply(v, out);
        Ok(Some(()))
    }

    fn sparse_jacobian(
        &self,
        _: f64,
        _: &[f64],
        p: StructuralPatternView<'_>,
        out: &mut [f64],
        _: &mut SolveContext<'_, '_>,
    ) -> Result<Option<()>, SciOxideError> {
        let (n, g) = (self.nodes, self.spacing * self.coupling);
        for row in 0..2 * n {
            let d = self.conductivity[row / n] / self.spacing;
            for k in p.row_ptr()[row]..p.row_ptr()[row + 1] {
                let col = p.col_indices()[k];
                out[k] = match (col == row, col / n == row / n) {
                    (true, _) => -2.0 * d - g,
                    (false, true) => d,
                    (false, false) => g,
                };
            }
        }
        Ok(Some(()))
    }
}

/// Inverse field-diagonal block I − γ J_zz. Constant-coefficient tridiagonal matrix, solved with Thomas.
pub(crate) struct FieldStage {
    pub(crate) calls: Cell<usize>,
    lower: Cell<f64>,
    pivots: RefCell<Vec<f64>>,
    upper: RefCell<Vec<f64>>,
}

impl FieldStage {
    pub(crate) fn new(nodes: usize) -> Self {
        Self {
            calls: Cell::new(0),
            lower: Cell::new(0.0),
            pivots: RefCell::new(vec![1.0; nodes]),
            upper: RefCell::new(vec![0.0; nodes]),
        }
    }

    /// LU-factor the tridiagonal matrix with diagonal and upper/lower off entries.
    fn prepare(&self, diagonal: f64, off: f64) {
        let (mut m, mut c) = (self.pivots.borrow_mut(), self.upper.borrow_mut());
        for i in 0..m.len() {
            m[i] = diagonal - if i > 0 { off * c[i - 1] } else { 0.0 };
            c[i] = off / m[i];
        }
        self.lower.set(off);
    }
}

impl Preconditioner<f64> for FieldStage {
    fn dimension(&self) -> usize {
        self.pivots.borrow().len()
    }

    fn precondition_workspace_len(&self) -> Result<usize, SciOxideError> {
        Ok(0)
    }

    fn precondition_into(
        &self,
        r: &[f64],
        z: &mut [f64],
        _: &mut [f64],
    ) -> Result<(), SciOxideError> {
        self.calls.set(self.calls.get() + 1);
        let (m, c, off) = (self.pivots.borrow(), self.upper.borrow(), self.lower.get());
        for i in 0..m.len() {
            z[i] = (r[i] - if i > 0 { off * z[i - 1] } else { 0.0 }) / m[i];
        }
        for i in (0..m.len().saturating_sub(1)).rev() {
            z[i] -= c[i] * z[i + 1];
        }
        Ok(())
    }
}

/// Stage preconditioner: rebuild field blocks for each supplied shift γ.
pub(crate) struct StageInverse<'a> {
    pub(crate) model: &'a TwoTemperature,
    pub(crate) blocks: [&'a FieldStage; 2],
    pub(crate) inverse: &'a PdeFieldPreconditioner<'a, f64>,
}

impl MethodOfLinesStagePreconditioner<f64> for StageInverse<'_> {
    fn preconditioner(
        &self,
        _: f64,
        _: &[f64],
        shift: f64,
    ) -> Result<Option<&dyn Preconditioner<f64>>, SciOxideError> {
        let m = self.model;
        for (field, block) in self.blocks.iter().enumerate() {
            // Normalized-coordinate J_zz = (k/(C h²)) tridiag(1, −2, 1) − (G/C) I.
            let a = m.conductivity[field] / (m.capacity[field] * m.spacing * m.spacing);
            let g = m.coupling / m.capacity[field];
            block.prepare(1.0 + shift * (2.0 * a + g), -shift * a);
        }
        Ok(Some(self.inverse))
    }
}
```

## Integrating with heat capacity as mass

The next cell selects coupling $G$, interior-node count $n$, and tolerance exponent $p$, integrating the model to $t=1$ ns.
`MethodOfLinesPlan::new(2n, Some(&mass), Some(&scales))` receives diagonal mass $C h$ and characteristic field magnitudes (100 K electrons, 1 K lattice) as coordinate scales $D$.
Set physical absolute tolerances to $10^{p}\times$ (characteristic magnitude) K per field and relative tolerance $10^{p}$, converting to normalized coordinates with `error_tolerance`.
Integration uses Rosenbrock-W (`RosenbrockW`, `Ros34Pw2`), solving stage systems with sparse LU.

The integrator output function records film-center $x=L/2$ temperatures after every accepted step.
The table gives the integration record converted to `PdeSolveReport`; pass temperature histories and final state to the next cell.

```rust
//| id: pde-two-temperature-method-of-lines
//| ref: pde-two-temperature-method-of-lines
//| title: "Integrate a two-temperature model with heat capacities as mass"
//| run: reactive
//| uses: [pde-two-temperature-model]
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-linalg, scioxide-ode, scioxide-pde, scioxide-sparse]
//| exports: [nodes, coupling, tolerance_exponent, final_state, accepted_steps, electron_history, lattice_history]
//| inputs:
//|   coupling_scaled: {"type":"range","label":"Coupling constant G [10¹⁶ W/(m³·K)]","description":"Changes electron-to-lattice heat-transfer rate.","min":0.5,"max":5,"step":0.5,"value":2}
//|   tt_nodes: {"type":"range","label":"Interior-node count n [points]","description":"Unknowns per field. Odd so the central node lies at x = L/2.","min":15,"max":255,"step":16,"value":63}
//|   tolerance_exponent: {"type":"range","label":"Tolerance exponent p","description":"Relative 10^p, absolute 10^p × (characteristic field magnitude).","min":-9,"max":-3,"step":1,"value":-6}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"loose","label":"p = −3","description":"Fewer steps increase time-integration error to order 2×10⁻⁵ K.","inputs":{"tolerance_exponent":-3}}
//|   - {"id":"fine-grid","label":"n = 255","description":"Step count stays unchanged while spatial discretization error falls to about 1/16.","inputs":{"tt_nodes":255}}
//|   - {"id":"weak-coupling","label":"G = 0.5 × 10¹⁶","description":"Less heat reaches the lattice, lowering its final temperature.","inputs":{"coupling_scaled":0.5}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"record","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"Completed"}
//|       - {"artifact":"record","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"min":150,"max":250}}
//|       - {"artifact":"record","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":0}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: loose
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"record","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"max":60}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: fine-grid
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"record","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"min":150,"max":250}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: weak-coupling
//|     expect:
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"satisfied"}
use scioxide_core::ExecutionContext;
use scioxide_linalg::Vector;
use scioxide_ode::{
    OdeMethod, OdeOptions, OdeOutputSchedule, OdeProblem, OdeStageBackend, OdeState,
    RosenbrockMethod, RosenbrockW,
};
use scioxide_pde::{MethodOfLinesPlan, PdeSolveReport};
use scioxide_sparse::SparseLuLimits;

let n = tt_nodes as usize;
let model = TwoTemperature::new(n, coupling_scaled * 1.0e16);
let scales = model.scales();
let pattern = model.pattern().map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let plan = MethodOfLinesPlan::new(2 * n, Some(&model.mass()), Some(&scales))
    .and_then(|plan| plan.with_jacobian_pattern(pattern.clone()))
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mut workspace = plan.workspace().map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let system = plan
    .bind(&model, &mut workspace)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;

// Convert physical tolerances [K] to tolerances in normalized coordinates z = y / D.
let physical = model
    .tolerance(tolerance_exponent)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let options = OdeOptions::new()
    .with_error_tolerance(plan.error_tolerance(&physical).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?)
    .with_initial_step(1.0e-16)
    .with_output_schedule(OdeOutputSchedule::AcceptedSteps);
let limits = SparseLuLimits::new(20 * n, usize::MAX);
let solver = RosenbrockW::new(RosenbrockMethod::Ros34Pw2)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?
    .with_backend(OdeStageBackend::Sparse { pattern, limits });
let mut normalized = vec![0.0; 2 * n];
plan.encode_into(&model.initial(), &mut normalized)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let problem = OdeProblem::new(&system, 1.0e-9);
let mut state = OdeState::new(0.0, Vector::from_vec(normalized));
let center = (n - 1) / 2;
let mut history: [Vec<[f64; 2]>; 2] = [Vec::new(), Vec::new()];
let mut ode_workspace = solver
    .workspace(&problem, &options)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let raw = solver
    .advance(
        &problem,
        &options,
        &mut state,
        &mut |t: f64, z: &[f64]| {
            // Convert normalized coordinates back to physical temperatures y = D z (central node only).
            history[0].push([t * 1.0e12, z[center] * scales[center]]);
            history[1].push([t * 1.0e12, z[n + center] * scales[n + center]]);
            Ok(())
        },
        &mut ode_workspace,
        ExecutionContext::sequential(),
    )
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let report = PdeSolveReport::try_from(&raw).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mut physical_state = vec![0.0; 2 * n];
plan.decode_into(state.values(), &mut physical_state)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;

let diagnostics = report.diagnostics();
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Termination reason" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Accepted steps [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Rejected steps [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Factorizations [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Right-hand-side evaluations [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Jacobian evaluations [count]", "type": "integer" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    vec![(
        report.termination().to_string(),
        diagnostics.num_accepted_steps(),
        diagnostics.num_rejected_steps(),
        diagnostics.num_factorizations(),
        diagnostics.num_rhs_evaluations(),
        diagnostics.num_jacobian_evaluations(),
    )],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "record" })
);
export!(nodes, n);
export!(coupling, model.coupling);
export!(tolerance_exponent, tolerance_exponent);
export!(final_state, physical_state.clone());
export!(electron_history, history[0].clone());
export!(lattice_history, history[1].clone());
export!(accepted_steps, diagnostics.num_accepted_steps());

let outcome = if report.is_success() {
    Outcome::satisfied("Integrated to t = 1 ns")
} else {
    Outcome::partial("Stopped before the final time")
        .explanation("Tabulated temperatures are from the last accepted step.")
};
emit_outcome!(
    outcome
        .status(report.termination().to_string())
        .diagnostic(
            "Accepted steps",
            diagnostics.num_accepted_steps() as f64,
            None
        )
        .diagnostic("Time reached", report.final_time() * 1.0e12, Some("ps"))
);
```

With defaults $G=2\times10^{16}$ W/(m³·K), $n=63$, and $p=-6$, integration reaches $t=1$ ns in 195 steps (`Completed`), with no rejected steps.
The constant Jacobian (`jacobian_is_constant`) needs only 2 evaluations; factorization occurs once per accepted step, 195 times.
Tolerance determines step count: 40 steps for $p=-3$ and 1713 for $p=-9$.
Varying node count from 15 to 255 leaves step count at 195.
The fastest electron mode has time $C_e h^2/(4k_e)$ of $4\times10^{-3}$ ps at $n=63$, but implicit steps are governed by solution variation rather than this time scale.
Integrating the same system with explicit Dormand–Prince 5(4), the step limit at `CflPolicy::Automatic` is $0.9\times3.3065/\rho\approx1.2\times10^{-14}$ s ($\rho\approx2.47\times10^{14}$ s⁻¹ is the maximum row sum of $J_z$), requiring about $8.3\times10^4$ steps to reach 1 ns.

## Comparison with the exact semidiscrete solution

The next cell receives the preceding results and compares both exact solutions.

## Stage Krylov methods and fieldwise preconditioning

Implicit-stage system $(\mathbf{I}-\gamma J_z)\mathbf{k}=\mathbf{r}$ can also be solved with matrix-free GMRES (`OdeStageBackend::Krylov`) instead of sparse LU.
In normalized coordinates, the diagonal block for field $i$ is

$$
(\mathbf{I}-\gamma J_z)_{ii}=\mathbf{I}-\gamma\left[\frac{k_i}{C_ih^2}\,\mathrm{tridiag}(1,-2,1)-\frac{G}{C_i}\,\mathbf{I}\right]
$$

This is a constant-coefficient tridiagonal matrix (within-field scale $d_i$ cancels).
`StageInverse` LU-factors both blocks whenever shift $\gamma$ is supplied, returning their block-diagonal inverse arranged through `PdeFieldPreconditioner`.
Only off-diagonal electron–lattice coupling blocks are ignored (diagonal matrices with entry magnitude $\gamma\,(G/C_i)(d_j/d_i)$, where $d_i,d_j$ are the two field scales).

The next cell selects interior-node count $n$ and integrates to $t=1$ ns with $G=2\times10^{16}$ W/(m³·K), $p=-6$, both with and without stage preconditioning.
The table shows accepted/rejected steps, total GMRES iterations, electronic-block preconditioner applications, and maximum difference from the exact semidiscrete solution.
Unpreconditioned integration is expensive, so run by button.

```rust
//| id: pde-two-temperature-stage-krylov
//| ref: pde-two-temperature-stage-krylov
//| title: "Give stage Krylov methods fieldwise preconditioning"
//| run: button
//| timeoutMs: 120000
//| uses: [pde-two-temperature-model]
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-linalg, scioxide-ode, scioxide-pde]
//| inputs:
//|   stage_nodes: {"type":"range","label":"Interior-node count n [points]","description":"Unpreconditioned GMRES iterations grow with n.","min":15,"max":63,"step":16,"value":31}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"fine","label":"n = 63","description":"Without preconditioning, GMRES iterations rise to about 22,000 and rejected steps appear.","inputs":{"stage_nodes":63}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"krylov","path":"/rows/0/4","range":{"min":2000}}
//|       - {"artifact":"krylov","path":"/rows/1/4","range":{"max":2000}}
//|       - {"artifact":"krylov","path":"/rows/1/3","equals":0}
//|       - {"artifact":"krylov","path":"/rows/1/6","range":{"max":1e-5}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: fine
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"krylov","path":"/rows/0/3","range":{"min":20}}
//|       - {"artifact":"krylov","path":"/rows/0/4","range":{"min":10000}}
//|       - {"artifact":"krylov","path":"/rows/1/3","equals":0}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"satisfied"}
use scioxide_core::{Budget, ExecutionContext, Preconditioner};
use scioxide_linalg::Vector;
use scioxide_ode::{
    OdeMethod, OdeOptions, OdeProblem, OdeStageBackend, OdeState, RosenbrockMethod, RosenbrockW,
};
use scioxide_pde::{MethodOfLinesPlan, PdeFieldLayout, PdeFieldPreconditioner, PdeSolveReport};

let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let n = stage_nodes as usize;
let (coupling, exponent, end) = (2.0e16, -6.0, 1.0e-9);
let model = TwoTemperature::new(n, coupling);
let plan = MethodOfLinesPlan::new(2 * n, Some(&model.mass()), Some(&model.scales()))
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let physical = model.tolerance(exponent).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let options = OdeOptions::new()
    .with_error_tolerance(plan.error_tolerance(&physical).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?)
    .with_initial_step(1.0e-16);

// Arrange field blocks (electron, lattice) with PdeFieldPreconditioner.
let layout = PdeFieldLayout::new(&[n, n]).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let (electron, lattice) = (FieldStage::new(n), FieldStage::new(n));
let blocks: [&dyn Preconditioner<f64>; 2] = [&electron, &lattice];
let inverse = PdeFieldPreconditioner::new(&layout, &blocks).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let stage = StageInverse {
    model: &model,
    blocks: [&electron, &lattice],
    inverse: &inverse,
};
let backend = OdeStageBackend::Krylov {
    iterations: Budget::try_from(200).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?,
    restart: Budget::try_from(30).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?,
    operator_scratch: 0,
    preconditioner_scratch: inverse
        .precondition_workspace_len()
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?,
};
let solver = RosenbrockW::new(RosenbrockMethod::Ros34Pw2)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?
    .with_backend(backend);
let semi = model.amplitudes(model.discrete_mu(), TwoTemperature::TYPICAL[0], end);
let mut rows = Vec::new();
let mut all_reached = true;
for preconditioned in [false, true] {
    let mut workspace = plan.workspace().map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let mut system = plan
        .bind(&model, &mut workspace)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    if preconditioned {
        system = system.with_stage_preconditioner(&stage);
    }
    let mut normalized = vec![0.0; 2 * n];
    plan.encode_into(&model.initial(), &mut normalized)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let problem = OdeProblem::new(&system, end);
    let mut state = OdeState::new(0.0, Vector::from_vec(normalized));
    let calls_before = electron.calls.get();
    let mut ode_workspace = solver
        .workspace(&problem, &options)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let raw = solver
        .advance(
            &problem,
            &options,
            &mut state,
            &mut (),
            &mut ode_workspace,
            ExecutionContext::sequential(),
        )
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let report = PdeSolveReport::try_from(&raw).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    all_reached &= report.is_success();
    let mut values = vec![0.0; 2 * n];
    plan.decode_into(state.values(), &mut values)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    // Maximum difference from exact semidiscrete solution (closed form).
    let gap = (0..2 * n)
        .map(|row| (values[row] - semi[row / n] * model.mode(row % n)).abs())
        .fold(0.0_f64, f64::max);
    rows.push((
        ["No preconditioning", "Fieldwise preconditioning"][usize::from(preconditioned)],
        report.termination().to_string(),
        raw.accepted_steps(),
        raw.rejected_steps(),
        raw.accounting().nested().iterations(),
        electron.calls.get() - calls_before,
        gap,
    ));
}
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Stage preconditioning" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Termination reason" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Accepted steps [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Rejected steps [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "GMRES iterations [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Preconditioner applications [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "6", "label": "Exact-solution difference [K]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "krylov" })
);
let outcome = if all_reached {
    Outcome::satisfied("Both stage solvers integrated to t = 1 ns")
} else {
    Outcome::partial("Some integrations stopped before the final time")
        .explanation("Differences in those rows do not compare final-time values.")
};
emit_outcome!(
    outcome
        .status(rows[1].1.clone())
        .diagnostic("Unpreconditioned GMRES iterations", rows[0].4 as f64, None)
        .diagnostic("Preconditioned GMRES iterations", rows[1].4 as f64, None)
);
```

With default $n=31$, both integrations finish in 195 steps; total GMRES iterations are 3619 unpreconditioned and 1556 preconditioned.
Four stages over 195 steps give 780 linear solves, so preconditioned solves take 2 iterations each.
Preconditioner application count equals GMRES iteration count, consistent with one application of `PdeFieldPreconditioner` calling the electronic block once.
Increasing to $n=63$ raises unpreconditioned GMRES iterations to 22055, gives 101 rejections, and increases accepted steps to 396.
Preconditioned count 1556 stays unchanged from $n=15$ through 63.
Blocks exactly invert diffusion and exchange diagonals, leaving GMRES only interfield coupling, independent of grid resolution.
Every case differs from the exact solution by $1.3\times10^{-7}$–$1.6\times10^{-7}$ K, at the same level as time-integration error of the preceding sparse-LU-stage cell.

## Summary

In the two-temperature model, use electron and lattice heat capacities $C_e h$ and $C_l h$ as diagonal mass of `MethodOfLinesPlan`, while providers express only physical-unit residuals.
Using characteristic field magnitudes as coordinate scales and converting physical tolerances through `error_tolerance` allows fieldwise tolerances in K. Implicit-method step counts are determined by tolerance and remain unchanged as nodes increase from 15 to 255.
Choosing an eigenvector as initial data gives a two-variable closed-form exact semidiscrete solution, separating time-integration and spatial errors.
For Krylov stage solves, supplying inverse field-diagonal blocks in normalized coordinates as stage preconditioning greatly reduces GMRES iterations and rejections.
