This table collects public items for classical orthogonal polynomial families and expansions, sample matrices, and least-squares approximation.
The family enum `OrthogonalFamily` (with variants such as `ChebyshevT`) returns values, derivatives, zeros, weight functions, and three-term recurrence coefficients.
For expansion coefficients, specify the family, normalization, and coordinate map with `OrthogonalBasisSpec` and reuse an `OrthogonalBasisPlan` prepared to the maximum degree.

`ApproxPolynomial::orthogonal(coefficients, spec)` determines degree from the coefficient count, prepares a plan with default execution settings, and constructs a polynomial with the default policy.
`ApproxPolynomial::orthogonal_with_plan(coefficients, plan, policy)` takes ownership of a policy and an `OrthogonalBasisPlan` constructed with `OrthogonalBasisSpec::prepare`.
The coefficient count must be at least 1 and at most the prepared maximum degree plus 1; shorter expansions retain the prepared maximum degree and execution settings.
An orthogonal-basis `PolynomialFitPlan` also retains a prepared plan and passes that preparation to fitted polynomials.
`to_orthogonal(spec)` inherits the original execution settings when converting from orthogonal bases, and prepares with defaults when converting from monomial or Bernstein bases.

`evaluate` of `OrthogonalFamily::Legendre` delegates to `legendre_p` in `scioxide-special` and follows its accuracy, degree, and work contracts.
The recurrence in `legendre_p` uses $n-1$ steps for degree $n$, with a default work limit of 65,536 steps.
Consequently, away from $x=\pm1$, degrees exceeding the limit yield `ConvergenceFailure` (`IterationLimit`), while $n>2^{52}$ yields `InvalidInput` (`OutOfRange`).
Derivative `evaluate_derivative` uses the same $O(n)$ recurrence as other families.
Expansions, differentiation, and basis conversion use prepared recurrences including normalization and the coordinate map.

## Summary

Obtain classical-family values, zeros, and weight functions through the relevant `OrthogonalFamily` variant; Chebyshev zeros use closed forms and other zeros symmetric tridiagonal eigenvalue problems.
Quadrature weights are not returned; if needed, compute eigenvectors of the `JacobiRecurrence` matrix with `scioxide-linalg` and combine with `log_weight_mass`, or use [Gaussian quadrature and Clenshaw–Curtis rules in scioxide-integrate](../../../scioxide-integrate/algorithms/gaussian-and-clenshaw-curtis/).
For repeated expansion-coefficient operations, select family, normalization, and coordinates through `OrthogonalBasisSpec` and prepare conversion, differentiation, and antiderivative maps through `OrthogonalBasisPlan`.
For noisy samples, prepare `PolynomialFitPlan` and inspect rank and residuals in `PolynomialFitReport` before using results.
