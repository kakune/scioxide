This table collects public items for floating-point division, rational functions, common factors, elimination, and root reliability.
Numerical ranks, condition numbers, disks, and clusters here are estimates, not proofs of common factors or root counts.
For proofs, use exact-coefficient operations ([Exact arithmetic, fast operations, and multivariate polynomial APIs](../exact-and-fast/)) or interval arithmetic (scioxide-validated).
Prepared Plan execution takes `ExecutionContext` last.
The `_scoped` forms of `SylvesterElimination` and `SylvesterDiscriminant` take caller scopes (`ExecutionContext`, `SolveScope`, or `&mut SolveContext` creating a child epoch).
Neither iteration limits nor caller identities are passed (the approximate-GCD refinement iteration limit is fixed internally).

## Summary

Use `PolynomialDivision` of `div_rem` for quotient, remainder, and reconstruction residual, interpreting degree with coefficients left untrimmed.
Represent rational functions with `ApproxRationalFunction` and accept cancellation after reading `simplify` diagnostics.
Approximate GCDs, resultants, and discriminants include numerical rank and reciprocal conditioning of equilibrated matrices.
These diagnose matrices and do not prove common factors (zero inputs lack rank).
Prepare partial fractions from denominator factors and check reported reconstruction error and reciprocal condition number (`None` for constant denominators).
Use `ApproxPolynomial::root_condition` and `polynomial_root_diagnostics` to estimate candidate-root sensitivities and disk overlap.
