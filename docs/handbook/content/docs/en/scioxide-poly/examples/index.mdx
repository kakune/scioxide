Each example compares results computed with SciOxide APIs against a closed form, a cited reference value, another algorithm, or an algebraic identity.
Assess accuracy using the table's errors and diagnostics as well as the charts.
The thermocouple example uses temperature [°C] and electromotive force [µV]; the rational-saturation example uses dimensionless concentration divided by the dissociation constant.
The other examples are dimensionless.

| Subject | APIs used | Independent check |
|---|---|---|
| [Runge phenomenon and node placement](runge-nodes/): interpolation of Runge functions with varying pole locations | `ApproxPolynomial::interpolate_barycentric`, `backend::barycentric::chebyshev_points` | Differences from the analytic function, decay rates from Bernstein ellipse $\rho$, and divergence rates for equally spaced nodes derived from logarithmic potentials |

## Summary

For interpolation and approximation examples, compare errors between sample points against analytic functions or closed-form coefficients and compare decay rates against theoretical $\rho$.
For root and pole examples, distinguish residuals, backward errors, condition numbers, and candidate counts, checking against known roots or algebraic identities such as Vieta's formulas.
Before using cited coefficients such as calibration formulas for differentiation, integration, or inversion, first verify agreement with reference tables within their rounding precision.
Count properties requiring exact decisions (numbers of intersections and tangencies) using exact rational arithmetic separately from floating-point candidates.
