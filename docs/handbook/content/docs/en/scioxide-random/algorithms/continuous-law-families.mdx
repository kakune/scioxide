Besides basic laws such as normal, gamma, and Weibull, `scioxide-random` includes 20 classical families with CDFs or quantiles expressible through elementary or special functions.
The later section tabulates 13 other families mentioned only by name on other pages, including uniform, Irwin–Hall, generalized normal, and inverse Gaussian, from the same perspective.
This page defines each family using the constructor's parameter names and tabulates supports, parameter domains, and conditions for means and variances to exist.
Quantile and sample construction, tail calculations, and sample inspection are explained in [Quantiles, sampling, and tail computation for classical families](../continuous-law-computation/).

The conventions for capability traits (`Cdf`, `Quantile`, `Mean`, and others) and meaning of `LawOptions` are in [Capability traits](oxref:random-laws-capability-traits) and [LawOptions and numerical inversion](oxref:random-laws-options-and-inversion).

## Shared conventions for standard forms and capabilities

Except for `Uniform` and `DoubleParetoLogNormal`, the laws on this page are standard forms with location 0 and scale 1; add location and scale using `Affine::new(law, μ, σ)`.
`Affine` inherits only the capabilities of its underlying law, and a negative $\sigma$ reverses left and right.

The implemented capabilities depend on the family.
When a mean or variance does not exist for the parameters, `mean()` or `variance()` returns `DomainError` with reason `Undefined`.
`Alpha` (whose mean and variance do not exist), and `ExponentiatedWeibull`, `GeneralizedExponential`, `GeneralizedHalfLogistic`, and `PowerNormal` (for which no closed forms are provided), implement neither `Mean` nor `Variance`.
When these moments are needed, users compute $\mathrm{E}[X^r] = \int_0^1 Q(u)^r\,du$ using `quantile` and quadrature.
Among the families on this page, only `Uniform` implements `Entropy`.

Families using `LawOptions` apply its tolerances (default relative and absolute $\sqrt{\varepsilon} \approx 1.5 \times 10^{-8}$) and work limit (default 65,536) to special-function iterations, quadrature, and numerical inversion.
However, some Newton methods use fixed stopping criteria, taking only the work limit from `LawOptions` ([Quantile and sampling methods](oxref:random-families-quantiles-and-sampling)).
For these families, `new` constructs the law with default `LawOptions`, while `with_options` takes settings after the parameters.
These are the families marked with `with_options` in the tables below.
Families without `LawOptions` have only `new`, answering all queries through closed forms or bounded computations.

Constructors validate parameters.
Finite parameters outside their domains return `DomainError` with reason `OutsideDomain`; NaN or infinite parameters return `NonFinite`.
Even within the domain, parameters causing a construction quantity such as $1/c$ or $\chi^2/2$ to overflow give `NumericalInstability` (`Overflow`).

## Family definitions and supports

$\Phi$ and $\varphi$ are the standard normal CDF and density; $\bar\Phi = 1 - \Phi$, and $P(a, x)$ is the regularized lower incomplete gamma function.
The table's definition is a CDF $F$, upper-tail probability $S = 1 - F$, or density $f$ expressed in constructor parameters; this expression determines the law.

The families with bounded support are as follows.

| Constructor | Parameter domain | Support | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Anglit::new()` | None | $[-\pi/4, \pi/4]$ | $f(x) = \cos 2x$, $F(x) = \sin^2(x + \pi/4)$ |
| `Cosine::new()` (`with_options`) | None | $[-\pi, \pi]$ | $f(x) = (1 + \cos x)/(2\pi)$, $F(x) = (\pi + x + \sin x)/(2\pi)$ |
| `Argus::new(chi)` (`with_options`) | $\chi > 0$ and $\chi^2/2$ finite | $[0, 1]$ | $S(x) = P(3/2, \chi^2(1 - x^2)/2)\,/\,P(3/2, \chi^2/2)$ |
| `GeneralizedHalfLogistic::new(c)` | $c > 0$ and $1/c$ finite | $[0, 1/c]$ | $F(x) = (1 - t)/(1 + t)$ with $t = (1 - cx)^{1/c}$ |
| `TukeyLambda::new(lambda)` ($\lambda > 0$) | $\lambda$ and $1/\lambda$ finite | $[-1/\lambda, 1/\lambda]$ | Quantile $Q(p) = (p^\lambda - (1 - p)^\lambda)/\lambda$ |

The families supported on the positive half-line are as follows.

| Constructor | Parameter domain | Support | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Alpha::new(shape)` (`with_options`) | $a > 0$ | $(0, \infty)$ | $F(x) = \Phi(a - 1/x)/\Phi(a)$. $1/(a - Z)$ with standard normal $Z$ conditioned on $Z < a$ |
| `Burr12::new(c, d)` | $c, d > 0$ | $[0, \infty)$ | $F(x) = 1 - (1 + x^c)^{-d}$ |
| `ExponentiatedWeibull::new(a, c)` | $a, c > 0$ | $[0, \infty)$ | $F(x) = (1 - e^{-x^c})^a$ |
| `FatigueLife::new(shape)` | $c > 0$ | $(0, \infty)$ | $F(x) = \Phi\bigl((\sqrt{x} - 1/\sqrt{x})/c\bigr)$. Median 1 |
| `GeneralizedExponential::new(a, b, c)` (`with_options`) | $a, b, c > 0$ | $[0, \infty)$ | Hazard $a + b(1 - e^{-cx})$, $S(x) = \exp\bigl(-(a + b)x + b(1 - e^{-cx})/c\bigr)$ |
| `InverseWeibull::new(c)` | $c > 0$ | $(0, \infty)$ | $F(x) = \exp(-x^{-c})$ (Fréchet type) |
| `Mielke::new(k, s)` | $k, s > 0$ | $[0, \infty)$ | $F(x) = \bigl(x^s/(1 + x^s)\bigr)^{k/s}$. $k = s$ is log-logistic |
| `ReciprocalInverseGaussian::new(mu)` (`with_options`) | $\mu > 0$ | $(0, \infty)$ | $1/Y$, where $Y$ is inverse Gaussian with mean $\mu$ and shape 1 |

The families supported on the entire real line (a half-line for `Pearson3` with nonzero skewness) are as follows.

| Constructor | Parameter domain | Support | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DoubleGamma::new(shape)` (`with_options`) | $k > 0$ | $\mathbb{R}$ | $f(x) = \lvert x \rvert^{k-1} e^{-\lvert x \rvert}/(2\Gamma(k))$. $k = 1$ is Laplace |
| `DoubleWeibull::new(shape)` | $c > 0$ | $\mathbb{R}$ | $f(x) = (c/2)\lvert x \rvert^{c-1} e^{-\lvert x \rvert^c}$ |
| `GeneralizedLogistic::new(shape)` | $c > 0$ | $\mathbb{R}$ | $F(x) = (1 + e^{-x})^{-c}$ |
| `HyperbolicSecant::new()` | None | $\mathbb{R}$ | $F(x) = (2/\pi)\arctan e^{\pi x/2}$, $f(x) = \tfrac{1}{2}\operatorname{sech}(\pi x/2)$ |
| `Moyal::new()` | None | $\mathbb{R}$ | $f(x) = e^{-(x + e^{-x})/2}/\sqrt{2\pi}$, $F(x) = \operatorname{erfc}(e^{-x/2}/\sqrt{2})$ |
| `Pearson3::new(skew)` (`with_options`) | Skewness $\gamma$ finite | $[-2/\gamma, \infty)$ for $\gamma > 0$, $(-\infty, -2/\gamma]$ for $\gamma < 0$, $\mathbb{R}$ for $\gamma = 0$ | $(G - \beta^2)/\beta$ with $\beta = 2/\gamma$ and $G \sim \mathrm{Gamma}(\beta^2, 1)$. $\gamma = 0$ is standard normal |
| `PowerNormal::new(c)`（`with_options`） | $c > 0$ | $\mathbb{R}$ | $S(x) = \bar\Phi(x)^c$ |
| `TukeyLambda::new(lambda)` ($\lambda \le 0$) | $\lambda$ finite | $\mathbb{R}$ | $Q(p) = (p^\lambda - (1 - p)^\lambda)/\lambda$; $\lambda = 0$ gives $Q(p) = \ln(p/(1 - p))$ (logistic) |

`Pearson3` is a one-parameter (skewness) law standardized to mean 0 and variance 1; construct a three-parameter Pearson III distribution by adding location and scale with `Affine`.
`TukeyLambda` has no closed-form CDF and is defined by its quantile function.
Both $k$ in `DoubleGamma` and $c$ in `GeneralizedLogistic` correspond to constructor argument `shape`.

## Mean and variance

Means and variances are returned in closed form, evaluating gamma functions, digamma $\psi$, confluent hypergeometric ${}_1F_1$, and related functions with `scioxide-special`.

| Family | Mean | Variance | Existence condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Anglit` | 0 | $\pi^2/16 - 1/2$ | Always |
| `Cosine` | 0 | $\pi^2/3 - 2$ | Always |
| `Argus` | In terms of ${}_1F_1$ and $P(3/2, \cdot)$ | From the second raw moment | Always |
| `Burr12` | $\Gamma(1 + 1/c)\Gamma(d - 1/c)/\Gamma(d)$ | From the second raw moment | Order $r$ requires $r < cd$ |
| `FatigueLife` | $1 + c^2/2$ | $c^2(1 + 5c^2/4)$ | Always |
| `InverseWeibull` | $\Gamma(1 - 1/c)$ | $\Gamma(1 - 2/c) - \Gamma(1 - 1/c)^2$ | Order $r$ requires $r < c$ |
| `Mielke` | $\Gamma(k/s + 1/s)\Gamma(1 - 1/s)/\Gamma(k/s)$ | From the second raw moment | Order $r$ requires $r < s$ |
| `ReciprocalInverseGaussian` | $1/\mu + 1$ | $1/\mu + 2$ | Always |
| `DoubleGamma` | 0 | $k(k + 1)$ | Always |
| `DoubleWeibull` | 0 | $\Gamma(1 + 2/c)$ | Always |
| `GeneralizedLogistic` | $\psi(c) - \psi(1)$ | $\psi'(c) + \psi'(1)$ | Always |
| `HyperbolicSecant` | 0 | 1 | Always |
| `Moyal` | $\gamma_{\mathrm{E}} + \ln 2$ | $\pi^2/2$ | Always |
| `Pearson3` | 0 | 1 | Always |
| `TukeyLambda` | 0 | $2\bigl(1/(1 + 2\lambda) - B(1 + \lambda, 1 + \lambda)\bigr)/\lambda^2$ | Mean: $\lambda > -1$; variance: $\lambda > -1/2$ |

The variance expression for `TukeyLambda` suffers cancellation as $\lambda \to 0$; for $\lvert\lambda\rvert < 0.05$, it forms the logarithm of $B(1 + \lambda, 1 + \lambda)$ from the series for $\ln\Gamma$ (coefficients $\zeta(k)$), avoids subtraction with `expm1`, and evaluates using `f64` ($\pi^2/3$ at $\lambda = 0$).
The rustdoc states a relative error below $2 \times 10^{-15}$ for this evaluation.

## Other standard-form families

The following 13 families include basic laws such as uniform and inverse Gaussian, and laws whose moments or tails are evaluated by quadrature.
Definitions use constructor parameters; $Z$, $Z_1$, and $Z_2$ are independent standard normals, and $E_1$ and $E_2$ are independent standard exponentials.
`Uniform` takes endpoints; `DoubleParetoLogNormal` takes location and scale through $\mu$ and $\sigma$ in its constructor.

| Constructor | Parameter domain | Support | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Uniform::new(low, high)` | Finite and $\text{low} < \text{high}$ | $[\text{low}, \text{high})$ | $f(x) = 1/(\text{high} - \text{low})$ |
| `Trapezoid::new(c, d)` | $0 \le c \le d \le 1$ | $[0, 1]$ | The density rises on $[0, c]$, is flat at height $2/(1 + d - c)$ on $[c, d]$, and falls on $[d, 1]$. $c = d$ is triangular |
| `Bradford::new(shape)` | $c > 0$ | $[0, 1]$ | $F(x) = \ln(1 + cx)/\ln(1 + c)$ |
| `IrwinHall::new(n)` (`with_options`) | Integer $n \ge 1$ ($n = 0$ is `InvalidInput`) | $[0, n]$ | Sum of $n$ independent standard uniforms |
| `GeneralizedNormal::new(shape)` (`with_options`) | $\beta > 0$ | $\mathbb{R}$ | $f(x) = \beta e^{-\lvert x \rvert^\beta}/\bigl(2\Gamma(1/\beta)\bigr)$. $\beta = 1$ is Laplace, $\beta = 2$ is normal with variance 1/2 |
| `GeneralizedGamma::new(a, c)` (`with_options`) | $a > 0$ and $c \ne 0$ finite | $(0, \infty)$ | $f(x) = \lvert c \rvert x^{ac - 1}e^{-x^c}/\Gamma(a)$. $X^c \sim \mathrm{Gamma}(a, 1)$ |
| `InverseGaussian::new(mu)` (`with_options`) | $\mu > 0$ | $(0, \infty)$ | Mean $\mu$ and shape 1. $F(x) = \Phi\bigl((x/\mu - 1)/\sqrt{x}\bigr) + e^{2/\mu}\Phi\bigl(-(x/\mu + 1)/\sqrt{x}\bigr)$ |
| `ExponentialPower::new(shape)` (`with_options`) | $b > 0$ | $[0, \infty)$ | $S(x) = \exp(1 - e^{x^b})$. A lifetime law distinct from `GeneralizedNormal` |
| `GaussHypergeometric::new(a, b, c, z)` (`with_options`) | $a, b > 0$ and $c$ finite, $z > -1$ | $[0, 1]$ | $f(x) \propto x^{a-1}(1 - x)^{b-1}(1 + zx)^{-c}$. $c = 0$ is beta |
| `JohnsonSB::new(a, b)` (`with_options`) | $a$ finite, $b > 0$ | $(0, 1)$ | $a + b\,\operatorname{logit}(X)$ is standard normal |
| `Rice::new(shape)` (`with_options`) | $b \ge 0$ | $[0, \infty)$ | $X = \sqrt{(Z_1 + b)^2 + Z_2^2}$. $b = 0$ is Rayleigh |
| `DoubleParetoLogNormal::new(mu, sigma, alpha, beta)` (`with_options`) | $\mu$ finite, $\sigma, \alpha, \beta > 0$ | $(0, \infty)$ | $X = \exp(\mu + \sigma Z + E_1/\alpha - E_2/\beta)$ |
| `RelativisticBreitWigner::new(rho)`（`with_options`） | $\rho > 0$ | $[0, \infty)$ | $f(x) \propto 1/\bigl((x^2 - \rho^2)^2 + \rho^2\bigr)$ |

All 13 families implement `Mean` and `Variance`; only `Uniform` implements `Entropy` ($\ln(\text{high} - \text{low})$).

| Family | Quantile | Sampling | Mean and variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Uniform` | Closed form $(1 - p)\,\text{low} + p\,\text{high}$ | Inverse transform | $(\text{low} + \text{high})/2$, $(\text{high} - \text{low})^2/12$ |
| `Trapezoid` | Closed form (piecewise square roots) | Inverse transform | Closed-form raw moments |
| `Bradford` | Closed form $\bigl((1 + c)^p - 1\bigr)/c$ | Inverse transform | Mean $1/\ln(1 + c) - 1/c$ and closed-form variance (20-term series avoiding cancellation for $c \le 1/8$) |
| `IrwinHall` | Numerical inversion | Sum of $n$ uniforms | $n/2$, $n/12$ |
| `GeneralizedNormal` | Assign a sign to $Q^{-1}(1/\beta, 2\min(p, 1 - p))^{1/\beta}$, using the inverse upper regularized incomplete gamma function | Sign and power of a gamma variate | 0, $\Gamma(3/\beta)/\Gamma(1/\beta)$ |
| `GeneralizedGamma` | Power $1/c$ of a gamma-distribution inverse | Power of a gamma variate | $\mathrm{E}[X^r] = \Gamma(a + r/c)/\Gamma(a)$ (exists only for $a + r/c > 0$) |
| `InverseGaussian` | Numerical inversion | Michael–Schucany–Haas method | $\mu$, $\mu^3$ |
| `ExponentialPower` | Closed form $\bigl(\ln(1 - \ln(1 - p))\bigr)^{1/b}$ | Inverse transform | Quantile quadrature |
| `GaussHypergeometric` | Numerical inversion | Inverse transform (numerical inversion per sample) | Density quadrature |
| `JohnsonSB` | Closed form $\operatorname{sigmoid}\bigl((\Phi^{-1}(p) - a)/b\bigr)$ | Normal-variate transformation | Quantile quadrature |
| `Rice` | Square root of a noncentral chi-squared quantile (2 degrees of freedom, noncentrality $b^2$) | Two normal variates | Mean: Poisson-mixture series (12-term asymptotic expansion for $b \ge 32$); variance: $2 + b^2 - (\text{mean})^2$ for $b < 32$, the same asymptotic expansion for $b \ge 32$ |
| `DoubleParetoLogNormal` | Numerical inversion | A normal and two exponential variates | $\mathrm{E}[X^r] = e^{r\mu + r^2\sigma^2/2}\frac{\alpha}{\alpha - r}\frac{\beta}{\beta + r}$ ($r < \alpha$) |
| `RelativisticBreitWigner` | Numerical inversion | Inverse transform (numerical inversion per sample) | Mean: closed form, $\mathrm{E}[X^2] = \rho\sqrt{1 + \rho^2}$. Orders 3 and higher do not exist because the density decays as $x^{-4}$ |

Quantile quadrature integrates $\mathrm{E}[X^r] = \int_0^1 Q(u)^r\,du$ within the tolerances and work limit of `LawOptions`; the mean and variance are estimates.
Numerical inversion is the bisection method described in [Quantile and sampling methods](oxref:random-families-quantiles-and-sampling), consuming the work in `LawOptions`.
Numerical considerations for each family are collected in [Numerical considerations for the basic families](oxref:random-families-other-numerics).

## Summary

Classical families are provided in standard form with location 0 and scale 1; add location and scale using `Affine::new(law, μ, σ)`.
Constructors return `DomainError` for out-of-domain parameters, so check argument ranges in the parameter-domain column before constructing a law.
Existence of a mean and variance depends on the family and parameters; nonexistent moments give `DomainError` with reason `Undefined`, while five families including `Alpha` implement neither `Mean` nor `Variance`.
Treat means and variances of families using quantile quadrature, such as `ExponentialPower` and `JohnsonSB`, as estimates obtained within the tolerances of `LawOptions`.
When `Entropy` is needed, only `Uniform` among these families returns it directly.
