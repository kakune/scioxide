These tables cover integer-word generators and ways to advance, partition, and save them.
Every generator is for statistical simulation and unsuitable for cryptography; supply seeds through `SeedableRng::from_seed` or caller-provided entropy.
See [Conversion to uniform words and floating-point values](oxref:random-algorithms-uniform-conversion) for native versus uniform words and [Scope of reproducibility](oxref:random-algorithms-reproducibility) for reproducibility guarantees.

## Generators

Every generator implements `BitGenerator`, `Rng` (`f32` and `f64`), `SeedableRng`, `SerializableState`, and `SnapshotGenerator`, and can be duplicated at the same position with `Clone`.
