These tables cover low-discrepancy sequences and designs and paths constructed from sequences or random numbers.
Plans (`QmcPlan`, `QmcReplicatePlan`, `BrownianBridgePlan`) and design `PoissonDisk` are immutable and `Send + Sync + Clone`; callers own cursors and workspace.
Latin hypercubes have no Plan and are constructed with functions `latin_hypercube`, `latin_hypercube_with_options`, and `latin_hypercube_into`.
Sequence theory appears in [Halton and Sobol sequence construction and discrepancy](../../algorithms/quasi-random-and-brownian-bridges/), [QMC positions, randomization, and error estimation](../../algorithms/qmc-positions-and-randomization/), and [Path construction with Brownian bridges](../../algorithms/brownian-bridge-construction/); designs are explained in [Latin hypercube designs and discrepancy](../../algorithms/latin-hypercube-and-discrepancy/) and [Poisson-disk sampling](../../algorithms/poisson-disk-sampling/).
