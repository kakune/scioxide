These tables cover finite-set sampling and types controlling failures during batch generation.
Batch output and state policies are explained in [Batch sampling and state handling](oxref:random-algorithms-batches-and-state); the workspace procedure appears in [Querying workspace and requirements](oxref:random-algorithms-workspaces).

## Sampling indices and permutations

| Item | Kind | Role | Contract essentials | Explanation | Executable example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`uniform_index`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-random/0.1.0/scioxide_random/fn.uniform_index.html) | Function | Unbiased index in $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$ | `uniform_index(n, rng)`. Exactly uniform through Lemire rejection. Uses a 32-bit uniform word for $n \le 2^{32}$, otherwise 64 bits. Exhausting 1,024 words gives `ConvergenceFailure`; $n = 0$ gives `InvalidInput`. No allocation. Words read before failure remain consumed | [Modulo bias and Lemire rejection](oxref:random-sampling-lemire), [Simulation and Wilson intervals](oxref:random-birthday-wilson) | [Comparing collision probabilities through exact formulas, approximations, and simulation](oxref:random-birthday-probability), [Counting randomly assigned seed collisions and extrapolating to 64 bits](oxref:random-birthday-seeds) |
| [`shuffle`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-random/0.1.0/scioxide_random/fn.shuffle.html) | Function | Fisher–Yates permutation | `shuffle(slice, rng)`. Every permutation is equally likely. Calls `uniform_index` $n - 1$ times without allocating or cloning elements. On failure, the generator remains advanced by consumed words and the array retains an intermediate rearrangement. Use `shuffle_in_place` for all-or-nothing output or generator rollback | [Permutations and sampling without replacement](oxref:random-sampling-permutation) | - |
| [`shuffle_in_place`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-random/0.1.0/scioxide_random/fn.shuffle_in_place.html) | Function | Fisher–Yates permutation under policies | `shuffle_in_place(slice, rng, policy, workspace)`. Draw sequence and successful permutation match `shuffle`. `Atomic` clones and rearranges elements in workspace equal to the array length (zero below length 2), leaving the array unchanged on failure. `Incremental` uses no workspace and retains completed swaps. The generator follows the state policy in `policy`. Failure to grow workspace gives `AllocationFailure` | [Permutations and sampling without replacement](oxref:random-sampling-permutation) | - |
| [`choose`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-random/0.1.0/scioxide_random/fn.choose.html) | Function | Select one element uniformly | `choose(slice, rng)`. An empty array gives `None` without consuming words. Returns generator failures and rejection-limit errors | [Permutations and sampling without replacement](oxref:random-sampling-permutation) | [Comparing biased urn draws with Fisher and Wallenius probability masses](oxref:random-noncentral-hypergeometric-urn) |
| [`sample_indices`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-random/0.1.0/scioxide_random/fn.sample_indices.html) | Function | Sample $k$ distinct indices without replacement | `sample_indices(n, k, rng)` returns an owned `Vec`. For $k \ge \lfloor n/16 \rfloor$, uses partial Fisher–Yates ($O(n)$ work and memory, uniformly random order); otherwise uses Floyd's method with a set of at most $4k$ slots (expected $O(k)$, unspecified order). $k > n$ gives `InvalidInput`. Allocates storage before the first draw; allocation failure gives `AllocationFailure` without consuming words. Failure during sampling returns nothing and leaves the generator advanced | [Permutations and sampling without replacement](oxref:random-sampling-permutation) | - |
| [`WeightedSampler`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-random/0.1.0/scioxide_random/struct.WeightedSampler.html) | Struct | Alias table for index probabilities proportional to weights | `new(&weights)` divides finite nonnegative weights (at least one positive) by their maximum, normalizes with compensated summation, and constructs an $O(n)$ table. Each draw uses `uniform_index` and `next_float` once each. `sample_index(rng)` and `sample(rng, &values)` do not allocate; `sample` reports length mismatches as `DimensionMismatch` before advancing the generator. `sample_n(rng, n)` allocates an owned `Vec` once; `sample_n_into(rng, policy, output, workspace)` writes caller output according to `SamplingPolicy`. Zero-weight indices are never returned. Empty, negative, or all-zero weights give `InvalidInput`; nonfinite values give `NonFinite`; allocation failure gives `AllocationFailure` | [Weighted indices and the alias method](oxref:random-discrete-alias-method), [From weights to probabilities](oxref:random-weighted-probabilities), [Why the alias method reproduces category probabilities](oxref:random-weighted-alias-reason) | [Varying rare weights, draw counts, and seeds in alias sampling](oxref:random-weighted-sampling) |

## Batch-generation policies

| Item | Kind | Role | Contract essentials | Explanation | Executable example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [`SamplingPolicy`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-random/0.1.0/scioxide_random/struct.SamplingPolicy.html) | Struct | Policies for batch generation into caller output | A `Copy` value constructed with `SamplingPolicy::rollback(output)` and `advance_on_error(output)`, without a default. Output and state policies are independent. Caller-output forms (`sample_n_into`, `shuffle_in_place`, `points_into`) take it (immediately after the generator when one is passed); owned forms do not. Validation and workspace allocation precede snapshots and sampling; empty batches neither save nor draw. Successful batches read the same words in the same order as the same number of individual samples, including rejected candidates. Policies apply to returned errors, not panics. Provides `output_policy()`, `workspace_requirements(request)`, and `workspace(request)` | [Batch state policies](oxref:random-prng-batch-state-policy), [Batch sampling and state handling](oxref:random-algorithms-batches-and-state), [Minimal example: three forms and state policies](oxref:random-api-minimal-examples) | [Comparing what failed batches leave in output and generators under each policy](oxref:random-batch-state-policy), [Measuring error slope −1/2 with independent repetitions](oxref:random-buffon-convergence), [Estimating π and confidence intervals across sample counts](oxref:random-buffon-estimate), [Refining trajectories with Brownian bridges and checking variance](oxref:random-colloid-bridge) |
| [`OutputPolicy`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-random/0.1.0/scioxide_random/enum.OutputPolicy.html) | Enum | Unit of output publication | `Atomic` publishes the whole output only on success; `Incremental` publishes completed samples (rows for vector values, swaps for permutations) in order, retaining them after later failure, even with `Rollback` | [Batch state policies](oxref:random-prng-batch-state-policy), [Batch sampling and state handling](oxref:random-algorithms-batches-and-state), [Minimal example: three forms and state policies](oxref:random-api-minimal-examples) | [Comparing what failed batches leave in output and generators under each policy](oxref:random-batch-state-policy), [Measuring error slope −1/2 with independent repetitions](oxref:random-buffon-convergence), [Estimating π and confidence intervals across sample counts](oxref:random-buffon-estimate), [Refining trajectories with Brownian bridges and checking variance](oxref:random-colloid-bridge) |
| [`Rollback`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-random/0.1.0/scioxide_random/struct.Rollback.html) | Struct | State policy restoring the generator on failure | Available only for generators implementing `SnapshotGenerator`. Selected by `SamplingPolicy::rollback` and passed as a value to `latin_hypercube_into` and `PoissonDisk::generate_into`. Owned forms take no state policy | [Batch state policies](oxref:random-prng-batch-state-policy), [Batch sampling and state handling](oxref:random-algorithms-batches-and-state), [Sample forms and state after failure](oxref:random-choosing-sample-shape), [Minimal example: three forms and state policies](oxref:random-api-minimal-examples) | [Comparing what failed batches leave in output and generators under each policy](oxref:random-batch-state-policy), [Checking all-pairs distances and output bounds of accepted sampling points](oxref:random-poisson-disk-separation), [Checking that Latin-hypercube swaps still use every stratum once](oxref:random-lhs-marginal-invariants), [Generating uniform samples with three forms and two state policies](oxref:random-api-batch-forms) |
| [`AdvanceOnError`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-random/0.1.0/scioxide_random/struct.AdvanceOnError.html) | Struct | State policy retaining consumed words on failure | Requires no snapshot capability. Selected by `SamplingPolicy::advance_on_error`. Matches the generator position left by a failed owned form | [Batch state policies](oxref:random-prng-batch-state-policy), [Batch sampling and state handling](oxref:random-algorithms-batches-and-state), [Sample forms and state after failure](oxref:random-choosing-sample-shape), [Minimal example: three forms and state policies](oxref:random-api-minimal-examples) | [Checking Poisson-disk termination reasons and bounds on point counts](oxref:random-poisson-disk-termination), [Comparing what failed batches leave in output and generators under each policy](oxref:random-batch-state-policy), [Generating uniform samples with three forms and two state policies](oxref:random-api-batch-forms) |
| [`StatePolicy`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-random/0.1.0/scioxide_random/trait.StatePolicy.html) | Trait | Sealed trait for state policies | Implemented only by `Rollback` and `AdvanceOnError`. `Rollback` is restricted to `SnapshotGenerator` generators. Used as the type argument of `SamplingPolicy<P>` and the state-policy argument of `latin_hypercube_into` and `PoissonDisk::generate_into` | [Batch state policies](oxref:random-prng-batch-state-policy) | [Comparing what failed batches leave in output and generators under each policy](oxref:random-batch-state-policy), [Generating uniform samples with three forms and two state policies](oxref:random-api-batch-forms) |
| [`SnapshotGenerator`](https://docs.rs/scioxide-random/0.1.0/scioxide_random/trait.SnapshotGenerator.html) | Trait | Capture and restore in-memory state | Capture does not advance the generator; restoration to the same generator cannot fail and does not allocate. Implemented by built-in generators, `BoundedSubstream`, `QmcSequence`, and `&mut G`. To restore after owned-form failure, capture `snapshot` before the call and restore with `restore_snapshot`. Portable storage uses `SerializableState` | [Batch state policies](oxref:random-prng-batch-state-policy) | [Comparing what failed batches leave in output and generators under each policy](oxref:random-batch-state-policy) |
