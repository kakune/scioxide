Wiener process $W(t)$ is a zero-mean Gaussian process, so its distribution is determined by covariance $\mathrm{Cov}(W(s), W(t)) = \min(s, t)$ (variance $t$ when $s = t$).
One path gives only one value at each time, so these quantities are estimated across many paths.
This example generates $R$ paths using `BrownianBridgePlan` and compares sample variances and covariances with theory and standard errors.
Path construction and derivation of $\min(s, t)$ are in [Hierarchical generation of stochastic paths with a Brownian bridge process](../brownian-bridge/); coordinate assignment is in [Assigning coordinates with a Brownian bridge](oxref:random-bridge-coordinate-assignment).

## Standard errors of sample variance and covariance

Write the values at time $t$ across $R$ paths as $W_r(t)$ ($r = 1, \dots, R$), with sample mean $\overline{W}(t)$.
Sample variance and covariance are

$$
\hat{v}(t) = \frac{1}{R-1}\sum_{r=1}^{R} \bigl(W_r(t) - \overline{W}(t)\bigr)^2, \qquad
\hat{c}(s, t) = \frac{1}{R-1}\sum_{r=1}^{R} \bigl(W_r(s) - \overline{W}(s)\bigr)\bigl(W_r(t) - \overline{W}(t)\bigr)
$$

both unbiased estimators.
Standard errors (SE) follow from normality of $W(t)$.
The unbiased sample variance of a normal sample is distributed as $t \cdot \chi^2_{R-1}/(R-1)$; chi-squared variance is twice its degrees of freedom, giving SE $t\sqrt{2/(R-1)}$ for $\hat{v}(t)$.
For zero-mean normal variables $X$ and $Y$, Isserlis' formula gives $\mathrm{E}[X^2Y^2] = \mathrm{Var}X\,\mathrm{Var}Y + 2\,\mathrm{Cov}(X, Y)^2$, so their product has variance $\mathrm{Var}(XY) = \mathrm{Var}X\,\mathrm{Var}Y + \mathrm{Cov}(X, Y)^2$.
Substituting $X = W(s)$ and $Y = W(t)$ and neglecting the small mean-estimation correction gives approximate SE $\sqrt{(st + \min(s, t)^2)/R}$ for $\hat{c}(s, t)$.
At $t = s$, this becomes $s\sqrt{2/R}$, nearly matching the variance SE.

## Computing the path ensemble

The following cell divides the time span ending at $T = 2$ s into 64 intervals and generates $R$ paths, reusing one plan and workspace with `apply_into`.
`apply_into` constructs paths in workspace before copying to output, leaving output unchanged on failure.
At each time $t$, sample variance $\hat{v}(t)$ and covariance $\hat{c}(s, t)$ against fixed time $s$ are computed from sums and squared sums.
Inputs are path count $R$, fixed time $s$ [s], and random seed.

The upper graph overlays sample variance markers on theoretical variance $t$ (dashed) and its $\pm 2$ SE band.
The lower graph similarly overlays theoretical covariance $\min(s, t)$ and sample covariance, with a vertical line at $t = s$.
Time axes of both graphs are linked.
The table compares terminal variance $\mathrm{Var}\,W(T)$ and covariance $\mathrm{Cov}(W(s), W(T))$ against theoretical $T$ and $s$ using SE.

```rust
//| id: random-brownian-ensemble
//| ref: random-brownian-ensemble
//| title: "Compare Brownian ensemble variance and covariance with theory"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-random]
//| inputs:
//|   path_count: {"type":"range","label":"Path count R [paths]","description":"Standard errors shrink as 1/√R.","min":64,"max":2048,"step":64,"value":512}
//|   anchor_time: {"type":"range","label":"Fixed time s [s]","description":"Moves the kink of covariance min(s, t).","min":0.25,"max":1.75,"step":0.25,"value":0.5}
//|   ensemble_seed: {"type":"range","label":"Random seed","min":1,"max":1000,"step":1,"value":271}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"few-paths","label":"R = 64","description":"Variance standard error widens to about 18% of theory.","inputs":{"path_count":64}}
//|   - {"id":"other-seed","label":"Seed 1","description":"Seed giving terminal variance slightly above theory at the same R.","inputs":{"ensemble_seed":1}}
//|   - {"id":"late-anchor","label":"s = 1.5 s","description":"The kink moves later, increasing theoretical Cov(W(s), W(T)) and its SE.","inputs":{"anchor_time":1.5}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"comparison","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":1.7229,"atol":2e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"comparison","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":0.12512,"atol":1e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"comparison","path":"/rows/0/4","range":{"min":-4,"max":4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"comparison","path":"/rows/1/1","approx":{"value":0.3968,"atol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"comparison","path":"/rows/1/3","approx":{"value":0.049411,"atol":1e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"comparison","path":"/rows/1/4","range":{"min":-4,"max":4}}
//|   - case: few-paths
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"comparison","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":0.35635,"atol":1e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"comparison","path":"/rows/0/4","range":{"min":-4,"max":4}}
//|   - case: other-seed
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"comparison","path":"/rows/0/4","range":{"min":-4,"max":4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"comparison","path":"/rows/1/4","range":{"min":-4,"max":4}}
//|   - case: late-anchor
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"comparison","path":"/rows/1/2","approx":{"value":1.5,"atol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"comparison","path":"/rows/1/3","approx":{"value":0.10126,"atol":1e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"comparison","path":"/rows/1/4","range":{"min":-4,"max":4}}
use scioxide_core::RealScalar;
use scioxide_random::{BrownianBridgePlan, Distribution, Normal, SeedableRng, SplitMix64};

// Divide terminal time T = 2 s into 64 intervals.
let (r, steps, horizon) = (path_count.round() as usize, 64_usize, 2.0_f64);
let rf = r as f64;
let times: Vec<f64> = (0..=steps)
    .map(|i| horizon * i as f64 / steps as f64)
    .collect();
let plan = BrownianBridgePlan::new(&times).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
// Reuse the plan and workspace for every path.
let mut workspace = plan.workspace(()).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let standard = Normal::<f64>::standard();
let mut rng = SplitMix64::from_seed(ensemble_seed.round() as u64);
let (mut z, mut path) = (vec![0.0_f64; steps], vec![0.0_f64; steps]);
// path[i] is W(t_{i+1}), so the fixed time s is path[anchor].
let anchor = (anchor_time * steps as f64 / horizon).round() as usize - 1;
let mut sums = vec![0.0_f64; steps];
let mut squares = vec![0.0_f64; steps];
let mut cross = vec![0.0_f64; steps];
for _ in 0..r {
    for value in &mut z {
        *value = standard.sample(&mut rng).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    }
    plan.apply_into(&z, &mut path, &mut workspace)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    for (i, &w) in path.iter().enumerate() {
        sums[i] += w;
        squares[i] += w * w;
        cross[i] += path[anchor] * w;
    }
}
let mean = |i: usize| sums[i] / rf;
let variance = |i: usize| (squares[i] - rf * mean(i) * mean(i)) / (rf - 1.0);
let covariance = |i: usize| (cross[i] - rf * mean(anchor) * mean(i)) / (rf - 1.0);
// Independent reference: Var W(t) = t, Cov(W(s), W(t)) = min(s, t), and their SE values.
let s = times[anchor + 1];
let variance_se = |t: f64| t * RealScalar::sqrt(2.0 / (rf - 1.0));
let covariance_se = |t: f64| RealScalar::sqrt((s * t + s.min(t) * s.min(t)) / rf);
let grid = &times[1..];
let variance_band: Vec<[f64; 4]> = grid
    .iter()
    .map(|&t| [t, t, t - 2.0 * variance_se(t), t + 2.0 * variance_se(t)])
    .collect();
let covariance_band: Vec<[f64; 4]> = grid
    .iter()
    .map(|&t| {
        let c = s.min(t);
        [t, c, c - 2.0 * covariance_se(t), c + 2.0 * covariance_se(t)]
    })
    .collect();
let variance_points: Vec<[f64; 2]> = (0..steps).map(|i| [grid[i], variance(i)]).collect();
let covariance_points: Vec<[f64; 2]> = (0..steps).map(|i| [grid[i], covariance(i)]).collect();
let variance_theory: Vec<[f64; 2]> = grid.iter().map(|&t| [t, t]).collect();
let covariance_theory: Vec<[f64; 2]> = grid.iter().map(|&t| [t, s.min(t)]).collect();
emit_chart!(serde_json::json!({
    "kind": "xy",
    "link": { "group": "bridge-ensemble-time" },
    "dataZoom": true,
    "tooltip": true,
    "xLabel": "Time t [s]",
    "yLabel": "Path-ensemble variance [s]",
    "layers": [
        { "id": "band", "kind": "band", "name": "Theory ± 2 SE", "points": variance_band },
        {
            "id": "theory",
            "kind": "line",
            "name": "Theoretical Var W(t) = t",
            "style": { "lineType": "dashed" },
            "points": variance_theory
        },
        { "id": "measured", "kind": "scatter", "name": "Sample variance", "points": variance_points }
    ]
}));
emit_chart!(serde_json::json!({
    "kind": "xy",
    "link": { "group": "bridge-ensemble-time" },
    "dataZoom": true,
    "tooltip": true,
    "xLabel": "Time t [s]",
    "yLabel": "Covariance Cov(W(s), W(t)) [s]",
    "layers": [
        { "id": "band", "kind": "band", "name": "Theory ± 2 SE", "points": covariance_band },
        {
            "id": "theory",
            "kind": "line",
            "name": "Theoretical min(s, t)",
            "style": { "lineType": "dashed" },
            "points": covariance_theory
        },
        { "id": "measured", "kind": "scatter", "name": "Sample covariance", "points": covariance_points }
    ],
    "annotations": [{ "kind": "line", "axis": "x", "value": s, "label": "Fixed time s" }]
}));
let digits4 = serde_json::json!({ "significantDigits": 4 });
let fixed2 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 2 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Quantity" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Measured [s]", "type": "number", "format": digits4 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Theoretical [s]", "type": "number", "format": digits4 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "SE [s]", "type": "number", "format": digits4 },
    { "key": "4", "label": "z = difference / SE [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed2 }
]);
let last = steps - 1;
let rows = vec![
    ("Var W(T)", variance(last), horizon, variance_se(horizon)),
    (
        "Cov(W(s), W(T))",
        covariance(last),
        s,
        covariance_se(horizon),
    ),
];
let rows: Vec<(&str, f64, f64, f64, f64)> = rows
    .into_iter()
    .map(|(name, value, exact, se)| (name, value, exact, se, (value - exact) / se))
    .collect();
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "comparison" })
);
```

Sample variance markers scatter around the dashed line, mostly inside the band.
Neighboring-time variances come from the same paths and are strongly correlated, so markers form a smooth curve rather than scattering independently.
Thus a curve crossing outside the band may stay outside for a while; do not count outside markers as independent trials.
Covariance markers rise along $t$ for $t \le s$ and plateau at height $s$ for $t > s$.
This kink represents $\min(s, t)$ and expresses independence of increments after $s$ from $W(s)$.
Variance markers curving away from proportionality to $t$ or shifting by a constant factor suggest incorrect bridge coefficients or coordinate scales.

With the defaults ($R = 512$, $s = 0.5$ s, seed 271), terminal variance is $1.7229$ s, differing from theoretical $2$ s by $-2.21$ times SE $0.125$ s.
Covariance $\mathrm{Cov}(W(0.5), W(2))$ is $0.3968$ s, differing from theoretical $0.5$ s by $-2.09$ times SE $0.0494$ s.
Normal-approximation two-sided probabilities are about 2.7% for $|z| \ge 2.21$ and 3.7% for $|z| \ge 2.09$; fluctuations of either size occur every few dozen seeds.
The two values use the same ensemble and are not independent, tending to shift in the same direction.
With the same $R$ and seed 1, terminal variance is $2.0577$ s ($0.46$ times) and covariance $0.5153$ s ($0.31$ times).
At $R = 64$, variance SE widens to about 18% of theory; seed 271 gives terminal variance $1.4953$ s ($-1.42$ times).
Setting $s = 1.5$ s moves the kink later, increasing theoretical covariance to $1.5$ s and SE to $0.101$ s, with measured $1.3295$ s ($-1.68$ times).

## One path and an ensemble

[One path's quadratic variation](../brownian-bridge/) and ensemble variance and covariance both have theoretical references derived from Wiener properties independently of path generation.
Quadratic variation averages many increments within one path, so quadratic variation from even one path approaches $T$ as interval count $n$ increases.
Ensemble variance and covariance get only one value per path, so accuracy depends on path count $R$.
Read both comparisons in SE units; under normal approximation, $|z| > 2$ occurs with probability about 4.6% per value.

## Summary

Reusing one `BrownianBridgePlan` and workspace through `apply_into` generates many paths without per-path allocation.
At each time, sample variance fluctuates around $t$ with SE $t\sqrt{2/(R-1)}$ and sample covariance around $\min(s, t)$ with SE $\sqrt{(st + \min(s, t)^2)/R}$.
A covariance kink and plateau at $t = s$ confirm independence of increments after $s$ from $W(s)$.
Quantities from one ensemble are correlated; do not count outside-band markers as independent trials, and interpret each $z$ in SE units.
For greater accuracy, increase path count $R$, using the $1/\sqrt{R}$ decrease in SE.
