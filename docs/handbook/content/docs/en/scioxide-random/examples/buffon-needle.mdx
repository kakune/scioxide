Dropping a needle of length $\ell$ onto parallel lines spaced $d$ apart gives a crossing probability involving $\pi$.
Reversing this relationship, published by G.-L. L. Buffon in 1777, estimates $\pi$ from the crossing fraction.
This page samples needle directions with `UniformDirection` and centers with `Uniform`, deriving standard errors and confidence intervals for $\hat\pi$ by the delta method.
[Error convergence rate for Buffon's needle](../buffon-error-slope/) checks error decay with slope $-1/2$ against sample size $N$ using independent replicates.

## Crossing probability

Draw lines parallel to the $x$ axis, with needle length $\ell \le d$.
Let $y$ be the center's distance from the nearest line and unit vector $\mathbf u = (u_x, u_y)$ the needle direction.
For random drops, $y$ is uniform on $[0, d/2]$ and $\mathbf u$ uniform on the unit circle, independently.
Needle endpoints are displaced by $(\ell/2)|u_y|$ perpendicular to the lines, so a crossing occurs when

$$
y \le \frac{\ell}{2}\,|u_y|
$$


Writing $\mathbf u = (\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$, angle $\theta$ is uniform on $[0, 2\pi)$ and $|u_y| = |\sin\theta|$ has mean $2/\pi$.
Independence of $y$ and $\mathbf u$ allows fixing $\mathbf u$ and averaging over $y$ first, giving crossing probability

$$
p = \mathrm E\!\left[\frac{\ell |u_y| / 2}{d/2}\right] = \frac{\ell}{d}\,\mathrm E|u_y| = \frac{2\ell}{\pi d}
$$


The assumption $\ell \le d$ excludes needles crossing two lines simultaneously.

## Estimator and standard error

Suppose $K$ of $N$ needles cross a line.
Needles are independent, so $K$ is binomial $\mathrm{Binomial}(N, p)$, and fraction $\hat p = K/N$ has standard error (SE) $\sqrt{p(1-p)/N}$.
Solving $p = 2\ell/(\pi d)$ for $\pi$ gives estimator

$$
\hat\pi = g(\hat p) = \frac{2\ell}{d\,\hat p}
$$


$g$ is the reciprocal of $\hat p$ and nonlinear, so the distribution of $\hat\pi$ is not a simple scaling of the distribution of $\hat p$.
Use the first-order Taylor expansion $g(\hat p) \approx g(p) + g'(p)(\hat p - p)$ (delta method).
Since $g'(p) = -2\ell/(d p^2) = -\pi/p$, replacing $p$ by $\hat p$ gives SE

$$
\mathrm{SE}(\hat\pi) \approx |g'(\hat p)|\sqrt{\frac{\hat p(1-\hat p)}{N}} = \hat\pi\sqrt{\frac{1-\hat p}{K}}
$$


The asymptotic 95% confidence interval (CI) is $\hat\pi \pm 1.96\,\mathrm{SE}(\hat\pi)$.
Using true values, $\mathrm{SE} = \pi\sqrt{(1-p)/(pN)}$ is proportional to $N^{-1/2}$.

A second-order expansion also gives bias approximately $\pi(1-p)/(pN)$ for $\hat\pi$, with bias-to-SE ratio $\sqrt{(1-p)/(pN)}$.
For $N \ge 256$, this ratio is at most 0.24 even at $\ell/d = 0.1$ and about 0.06 at most for default $\ell/d = 0.8$, making bias largely negligible for the CI.
At $K = 0$, $\hat\pi$ is undefined and the cell returns an error.

## Sampling needle directions

`UniformDirection::<f64>::new(2)` represents the uniform unit-circle distribution.
Sampling draws two independent standard normal components and divides by their length computed with `hypot`.
The two-dimensional standard normal is rotationally invariant, so normalized vector directions are uniform.
Zero-length pairs are rejected and redrawn, but this is extremely rare.
This method calls no trigonometric functions.
Drawing angle $\theta$ uniformly on $[0, \pi)$ would use the sought $\pi$ as input; direction vectors keep $\pi$ out of the estimate calculation.
General-dimensional spherical properties are explained in [Uniform spherical directions](oxref:random-circular-uniform-direction).

Row-wise `sample_n_into` writes $(u_x, u_y)$ coordinate pairs for $N$ needles into one row-major array.
Centers use `sample_n_into` of `Uniform::new(0.0, 0.5)` ($[0, d/2)$ for $d = 1$).
Both receive `SamplingPolicy::rollback(OutputPolicy::Incremental)`, restoring the generator to its pre-call state on failure.
The two laws draw sequentially from the same generator: $N$ directions first, then $N$ positions.

## Estimates across sample sizes

The following cell drops needles for each $N = 2^8, 2^9, \dots, 2^{k}$, tabulating $\hat\pi$, delta-method SE, 95% CI, and difference from the true value divided by SE, $z$.
Inputs are ratio $\ell/d$ (0.1–1), maximum exponent $k$, and random seed, with line spacing $d = 1$.
Each row's needles use a separate stream selected by `split(exponent)` of `Pcg64`.
`split` selects numbered streams from the root generator; distinct numbers give congruential streams with different increments.
Nonoverlap conditions are explained in [Stream spacing and overlap](oxref:random-prng-stream-spacing).
Thus row estimates can be treated as independent.

The graph plots $\hat\pi$ and 95% CI error bars against logarithmic $N$, with a horizontal true-value $\pi$ reference.
Error-bar lengths should approximately halve whenever $N$ quadruples.

```rust
//| id: random-buffon-estimate
//| ref: random-buffon-estimate
//| title: "Estimate π and confidence intervals across sample sizes"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-random]
//| inputs:
//|   needle_ratio: {"type":"range","label":"Needle-length / line-spacing ratio ℓ/d [-]","description":"Changes crossing probability p = 2ℓ/(πd) and standard error.","min":0.1,"max":1,"step":0.05,"value":0.8}
//|   max_exponent: {"type":"range","label":"Maximum sample-size exponent k (N = 2ᵏ) [-]","description":"Sample size in the final table and graph row.","min":10,"max":18,"step":1,"value":16}
//|   needle_seed: {"type":"range","label":"Random seed [-]","description":"Pcg64 seed. Each row uses a split-selected stream from this seed.","min":1,"max":1000,"step":1,"value":42}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"short-needle","label":"Short needles ℓ/d = 0.2","description":"Crossing probability falls to 0.127, increasing theoretical standard error about 2.7 times at fixed N.","inputs":{"needle_ratio":0.2}}
//|   - {"id":"long-run","label":"Through N = 2¹⁸","description":"The final row's standard error is about 0.0060; interval widths shrink as N^{-1/2}.","inputs":{"max_exponent":18}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/8/0","equals":65536}
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/8/1","equals":33446}
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/8/3","range":{"min":3.10,"max":3.18}}
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/8/4","range":{"min":0.0110,"max":0.0130}}
//|   - case: short-needle
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/8/2","range":{"min":0.12,"max":0.135}}
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/8/4","range":{"min":0.029,"max":0.035}}
//|   - case: long-run
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/10/0","equals":262144}
//|       - {"artifact":"estimates","path":"/rows/10/4","range":{"min":0.0055,"max":0.0065}}
use scioxide_core::RealScalar;
use scioxide_random::{
    Distribution, OutputPolicy, Pcg64, SamplingPolicy, SamplingRequest, SeedableRng,
    SplittableRng, Uniform, UniformDirection,
};

let pi = std::f64::consts::PI; // Reference (true value)
let last = max_exponent.round() as usize;
let n_max = 1_usize << last;
let direction = UniformDirection::<f64>::new(2).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
// Center-to-nearest-line distance y is uniform on [0, d/2) (d = 1).
let offset = Uniform::new(0.0_f64, 0.5).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let policy = SamplingPolicy::rollback(OutputPolicy::Incremental);
let mut direction_workspace = direction
    .workspace(policy, n_max)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mut offset_workspace = policy
    .workspace(SamplingRequest::scalars(n_max))
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let (mut uv, mut y) = (vec![0.0_f64; 2 * n_max], vec![0.0_f64; n_max]);
let root = Pcg64::from_seed(needle_seed.round() as u64);
let (mut rows, mut bars) = (Vec::new(), Vec::new());
for exponent in 8..=last {
    let n = 1_usize << exponent;
    // Select a separate stream for each sample size.
    let mut rng = root.split(exponent as u64).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    direction
        .sample_n_into(&mut rng, policy, &mut uv[..2 * n], &mut direction_workspace)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    offset
        .sample_n_into(&mut rng, policy, &mut y[..n], &mut offset_workspace)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    // Crossing condition y ≤ (ℓ/2)|u_y| (uv is row-major (u_x, u_y)).
    let hits = y[..n]
        .iter()
        .zip(uv.chunks_exact(2))
        .filter(|&(&y, u)| y <= 0.5 * needle_ratio * u[1].abs())
        .count();
    if hits == 0 {
        return Err(format!("π̂ is undefined because no needles crossed at N = {n}"));
    }
    let p_hat = hits as f64 / n as f64;
    let pi_hat = 2.0 * needle_ratio / p_hat;
    // Delta method: SE(π̂) = π̂ √((1 − p̂)/K).
    let se = pi_hat * RealScalar::sqrt((1.0 - p_hat) / hits as f64);
    let (low, high) = (pi_hat - 1.96 * se, pi_hat + 1.96 * se);
    bars.push([n as f64, pi_hat, low, high]);
    rows.push((n, hits, p_hat, pi_hat, se, low, high, (pi_hat - pi) / se));
}
emit_chart!(serde_json::json!({
    "kind": "errorbar",
    "dataZoom": true,
    "tooltip": true,
    "xLabel": "Sample size N [needles]",
    "yLabel": "Estimated π [-]",
    "xType": "log",
    "yMin": 2.0,
    "yMax": 4.5,
    "series": [{ "name": "π̂ and 95% confidence interval", "points": bars }],
    "annotations": [{ "kind": "line", "axis": "y", "value": pi, "label": "π" }]
}));
let fixed5 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 5 });
let digits3 = serde_json::json!({ "significantDigits": 3 });
let fixed2 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 2 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "N [needles]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Crossing count K [needles]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "p̂ [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed5 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "π̂ [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed5 },
    { "key": "4", "label": "SE(π̂) [-]", "type": "number", "format": digits3 },
    { "key": "5", "label": "95% CI lower bound [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed5 },
    { "key": "6", "label": "95% CI upper bound [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed5 },
    { "key": "7", "label": "z = (π̂ − π)/SE [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed2 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "estimates" })
);
```

For default $\ell/d = 0.8$, crossing probability is $p = 1.6/\pi = 0.50930$.
With seed 42, row $N = 65536$ gives $K = 33446$, $\hat\pi = 3.13513$, and $\mathrm{SE} = 0.0120$; 95% CI $[3.11162, 3.15864]$ contains $\pi$, with difference $z = -0.54$.
Across nine rows, $z$ ranges from $-1.33$ to $1.55$, and error bars approximately halve for every quadrupling of $N$.
Setting $k = 18$ gives $\hat\pi = 3.14152$ and $\mathrm{SE} = 0.00602$ in row $N = 262144$.

Setting $\ell/d = 0.2$ reduces it to $p = 0.12732$.
Theoretical SE $\pi\sqrt{(1-p)/(pN)}$ is about 2.67 times default; measured SE $0.0311$ in row $N = 65536$ is 2.59 times default.
This row has $\hat\pi = 3.07861$ and $z = -2.02$, outside the 95% CI.
Each row has about 5% chance of falling outside the CI, so at least one exclusion among nine independent rows has probability $1 - 0.95^9 \approx 37\%$; such an outlier is not unusual.
Relative SE $\sqrt{(1-p)/(pN)}$ decreases with increasing $p$, so within $\ell \le d$, needles with $\ell = d$ achieve equal accuracy with the fewest samples.

## Summary

Buffon estimates $\hat\pi = 2\ell/(d\hat p)$ from the reciprocal of crossing fraction $\hat p$; the delta method gives SE $\hat\pi\sqrt{(1-\hat p)/K}$ and 95% CI $\hat\pi \pm 1.96\,\mathrm{SE}$.
`UniformDirection` normalizes two normal components, giving uniform unit-circle directions without trigonometry or $\pi$.
Separate streams chosen with `split` at each sample size allow treating row estimates as independent and counting excluded CIs as 5% events.
Reducing $\ell/d$ lowers $p$ and raises SE at fixed $N$, so $\ell = d$ is most efficient for this estimator.
