[The von Mises distribution for wind direction](../von-mises-wind-direction/) calculated the population mean resultant length $A(\kappa) = I_1(\kappa)/I_0(\kappa)$.
Only finitely many wind directions can be observed, and the resulting mean resultant length $\bar{R}$ and mean wind direction $\bar{\theta}$ fluctuate between observation sets.
This page simulates wind directions with `VonMises`, compares 24-sector frequencies with the density, and checks the upward bias of $\bar{R}$ and standard error (SE) of $\bar{\theta}$ against theory.
Azimuth conventions are the same as on the preceding page: north at 0°, clockwise, with unit vector $(\sin\theta, \cos\theta)$.

## Sample mean resultant length

From $N$ observations, compute mean east component $\bar{S}$ and mean north component $\bar{C}$, then sample mean resultant length $\bar{R} = \sqrt{\bar{S}^2 + \bar{C}^2}$ and mean direction $\bar{\theta} = \operatorname{atan2}(\bar{S}, \bar{C})$.
$\bar{R}$ estimates $A(\kappa)$ but is biased upward for finite $N$.
For the average $\bar{u}$ of independent unit vectors $u_i$, decomposing variance about the mean gives the exact identity

$$
E[\bar{R}^2] = \lVert E[u]\rVert^2 + \frac{E\lVert u - E[u]\rVert^2}{N} = A^2 + \frac{1 - A^2}{N}
$$

because $\lVert u \rVert = 1$ implies $E\lVert u - E[u]\rVert^2 = 1 - A^2$.
$A(\kappa)$ is the limit as $N \to \infty$; even at $\kappa = 0$, $\bar{R}$ is of order $1/\sqrt{N}$ rather than 0.

The component variances of $\theta - \mu$ follow from recurrence $I_2 = I_0 - (2/\kappa)I_1$ as $\operatorname{Var}\cos(\theta - \mu) = 1 - A/\kappa - A^2$ and $\operatorname{Var}\sin(\theta - \mu) = A/\kappa$.
$\bar{R}$ is approximately the average of $\cos(\theta - \mu)$, while $\bar{\theta} - \mu$ is approximately the average of $\sin(\theta - \mu)$ divided by $A$, so the delta method gives

$$
\operatorname{SE}(\bar{R}) \approx \sqrt{\frac{1 - A/\kappa - A^2}{N}},
\qquad
\operatorname{SE}(\bar{\theta}) \approx \frac{1}{\sqrt{N\kappa A}}
$$


Both are approximations when $\bar{R}$ is sufficiently far from 0 ($A\sqrt{N}$ is much larger than 1); at $\kappa = 0$ the mean direction itself is undefined.

The following two cells use the same formulas for $A(\kappa)$, finite-$N$ $E[\bar{R}^2]$, and $\operatorname{SE}(\bar{\theta})$, collected in a definitions block.
Compute $A(\kappa)$ as the ratio of `bessel_i` in scioxide-special.

```rust
//| id: random-von-mises-sample-model
//| kind: definitions
//| title: "Theoretical mean resultant lengths and approximate standard errors"
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-special]
use scioxide_core::RealScalar;

/// Population mean resultant length A(κ) = I₁(κ)/I₀(κ).
pub(crate) fn resultant_length(kappa: f64) -> Result<f64, String> {
    let i1 = scioxide_special::bessel_i(1.0, kappa).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let i0 = scioxide_special::bessel_i(0.0, kappa).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    Ok(i1 / i0)
}

/// For a sample of N observations, E[R̄²] = A² + (1 − A²)/N.
pub(crate) fn expected_square_length(a: f64, n: usize) -> f64 {
    a * a + (1.0 - a * a) / n as f64
}

/// Mean-direction SE ≈ 1/√(NκA) [rad]. None at κ = 0 because mean direction is undefined.
pub(crate) fn direction_se(kappa: f64, a: f64, n: usize) -> Option<f64> {
    (kappa > 0.0).then(|| 1.0 / RealScalar::sqrt(n as f64 * kappa * a))
}
```

## Wind rose and circular statistics

The handbook's chart types do not include polar plots, so unfold the wind rose, a radial plot of frequencies by direction, onto an azimuth horizontal axis.
The following cell takes mean wind direction $\mu$ [degrees] (0–345°), concentration $\kappa$ (0–10), observation count $N$ (100–5000), and a seed, simulating $N$ wind directions.
Group observations into 24 sectors of width 15°, centered at 0°, 15°, …, 345°, and plot each sector's proportion as a symbol.
The band adds binomial $\pm 2\sqrt{p(1-p)/N}$ to each sector probability $p$; $p$ is computed exactly as the `winding_cdf` difference $F_w(\theta + 7.5°) - F_w(\theta - 7.5°)$.
`winding_cdf` extends the principal-interval distribution function to the real line by increasing it by 1 per revolution, so differences give sector probabilities directly even across 0°.
The dashed line is the density times the 15° width, $f(\theta)\,\Delta\theta$, approximating sector probabilities with midpoint densities.
The midpoint approximation has relative error of order $(\Delta\theta)^2\,|f''/f|/24$; at $\Delta\theta = 15°$ it is about 1% at the center of the $\kappa = 3$ peak, while at $\kappa = 10$ it is about 3% at the peak center and 11% at sectors 45° from it.

The first table compares sample $\bar{R}$ with the $N \to \infty$ value $A$ and the finite-$N$ value $\sqrt{A^2 + (1 - A^2)/N}$.
The second table shows deviations of $\bar{R}$ and mean wind direction $\bar{\theta}$ in SE units.
At $\kappa = 0$ mean direction is undefined and $\bar{R}$ near 0 is not approximately normal, so SE and difference / SE fields are blank.

```rust
//| id: random-von-mises-wind-rose
//| ref: random-von-mises-wind-rose
//| title: "Group wind-direction samples into 24 sectors and compare von Mises density and circular statistics"
//| run: reactive
//| uses: [random-von-mises-sample-model]
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-random, scioxide-special]
//| inputs:
//|   mean_bearing: {"type":"range","label":"Mean wind direction μ [degrees] (north 0, clockwise)","min":0,"max":345,"step":15,"value":315}
//|   kappa: {"type":"range","label":"Concentration κ [-]","description":"0 gives uniform directions; larger values concentrate them around the mean direction.","min":0,"max":10,"step":0.25,"value":3}
//|   count: {"type":"range","label":"Observation count N [observations]","min":100,"max":5000,"step":100,"value":1000}
//|   seed: {"type":"range","label":"Random seed [-]","min":1,"max":1000,"step":1,"value":42}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"uniform","label":"κ = 0 (uniform)","description":"A = 0 gives density 1/24 in every sector. R̄ = 0.037 is nonzero and on the scale of √(1/N) = 0.032; mean direction is undefined, so SE fields are blank.","inputs":{"kappa":0}}
//|   - {"id":"weak","label":"κ = 0.5","description":"A = 0.2425, and the mean-direction SE widens to 5.2°.","inputs":{"kappa":0.5}}
//|   - {"id":"strong","label":"κ = 10, N = 5000","description":"A = 0.9486 and circular variance 0.0514; mean-direction SE is 0.26°.","inputs":{"kappa":10,"count":5000}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"resultant","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"min":0.77,"max":0.85}}
//|       - {"artifact":"resultant","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":0.8101975686518561,"atol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"deviations","path":"/rows/0/2","approx":{"value":0.008598181528014727,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"deviations","path":"/rows/1/2","approx":{"value":1.1623139175716448,"atol":1e-9}}
//|   - case: uniform
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"resultant","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":0.03162277660168379,"atol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"resultant","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"min":0,"max":0.12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"deviations","path":"/rows/1/1","equals":null}
//|   - case: weak
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"resultant","path":"/rows/0/2","approx":{"value":0.2424996125808019,"atol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"deviations","path":"/rows/1/2","approx":{"value":5.203338812841106,"atol":1e-9}}
//|   - case: strong
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"resultant","path":"/rows/0/2","approx":{"value":0.9485998259548459,"atol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"resultant","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"min":0.94,"max":0.956}}
use scioxide_core::RealScalar;
use scioxide_random::{Density, Distribution, SeedableRng, SplitMix64, VonMises};

let mu = RealScalar::to_radians(mean_bearing);
let law = VonMises::<f64>::new(mu, kappa).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let n = count as usize;
let mut rng = SplitMix64::from_seed(seed as u64);
// Azimuth θ (north 0, clockwise) has unit vector (east, north) = (sin θ, cos θ).
let (mut east, mut north) = (0.0_f64, 0.0_f64);
let mut bins = [0_usize; 24];
for _ in 0..n {
    let theta = law.sample(&mut rng).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    east += RealScalar::sin(theta);
    north += RealScalar::cos(theta);
    // Group into 15° sectors centered at 0°, 15°, …, 345°.
    let k = RealScalar::round(RealScalar::to_degrees(theta) / 15.0) as i64;
    bins[k.rem_euclid(24) as usize] += 1;
}
let (s_bar, c_bar) = (east / n as f64, north / n as f64);
let r_bar = RealScalar::hypot(s_bar, c_bar);
let width = RealScalar::to_radians(15.0);
let mut theory = Vec::new();
for k in 0..=144 {
    let bearing = 2.5 * k as f64;
    let mass = law
        .pdf(RealScalar::to_radians(bearing))
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?
        * width;
    theory.push([bearing, mass]);
}
let (mut observed, mut band) = (Vec::new(), Vec::new());
for (k, &c) in bins.iter().enumerate() {
    let bearing = 15.0 * k as f64;
    // Compute bin probabilities from winding_cdf differences and add binomial ±2 SE bands.
    let lower = RealScalar::to_radians(bearing - 7.5);
    let upper = RealScalar::to_radians(bearing + 7.5);
    let p = law.winding_cdf(upper).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?
        - law.winding_cdf(lower).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let se = RealScalar::sqrt(p * (1.0 - p) / n as f64);
    observed.push([bearing, c as f64 / n as f64]);
    band.push([bearing, p, p - 2.0 * se, p + 2.0 * se]);
}

    serde_json::json!({
        "kind": "xy",
        "xLabel": "Wind direction (north 0°, clockwise) [degrees]",
        "yLabel": "Proportion in a 15° sector [-]",
        "xMin": 0,
        "xMax": 360,
        "yMin": 0,
        "dataZoom": true,
        "tooltip": true,
        "layers": [
            {"id": "band", "kind": "band", "name": "Bin probability ± 2 SE", "points": band},
            {
                "id": "density",
                "kind": "line",
                "name": "von Mises density × 15°",
                "style": {"lineType": "dashed"},
                "points": theory
            },
            {"id": "sample", "kind": "scatter", "name": "Sample proportion", "points": observed}
        ]
    }),
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "rose" })
);
let a = resultant_length(kappa)?;
let biased = RealScalar::sqrt(expected_square_length(a, n));
let fixed4 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 4 });
let fixed2 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 2 });
let none = "Undefined";
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "The statistic" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Sample value [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "N → ∞ value A [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Finite-N value [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    vec![("Mean resultant length R̄", r_bar, a, biased)],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "resultant" })
);
// For κ > 0, SE(R̄) ≈ √((1 − A/κ − A²)/N), SE(θ̄) ≈ 1/√(NκA). Undefined at κ = 0.
let se_r = (kappa > 0.0).then(|| RealScalar::sqrt((1.0 - a / kappa - a * a) / n as f64));
let se_dir = direction_se(kappa, a, n).map(RealScalar::to_degrees);
// Wrap the difference between mean direction θ̄ and μ into (−180°, 180°]. There is no mean direction at κ = 0.
let theta_bar = RealScalar::atan2(s_bar, c_bar);
let diff = (kappa > 0.0).then(|| {
    RealScalar::to_degrees(RealScalar::atan2(
        RealScalar::sin(theta_bar - mu),
        RealScalar::cos(theta_bar - mu),
    ))
});
let ratio = |value: Option<f64>, se: Option<f64>| value.zip(se).map(|(v, s)| v / s);
let deviations = vec![
    (
        "R̄ − finite-N value [-]",
        Some(r_bar - biased),
        se_r,
        ratio(Some(r_bar - biased), se_r),
    ),
    ("θ̄ − μ [degrees]", diff, se_dir, ratio(diff, se_dir)),
];
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Difference" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Value", "type": "number", "format": fixed4, "nullText": none },
    { "key": "2", "label": "SE", "type": "number", "format": fixed4, "nullText": none },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Difference / SE [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed2, "nullText": none }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    deviations,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "deviations" })
);
```

With the default inputs ($\mu = 315°$, $\kappa = 3$, $N = 1000$ observations, seed 42), plotted proportions are symmetric about northwest at 315°, and all 24 sectors lie inside the band.
Sample $\bar{R} = 0.8047$ differs from the finite-$N$ value 0.8102 by $-0.0055$, or $-0.64$ SE for SE 0.0086.
Mean wind direction differs from $\mu$ by $-1.02°$, or $-0.88$ SE for SE 1.16°.
Sample circular variance $1 - \bar{R}$ is 0.1953, 0.0053 above the population value 0.1900.

In the “κ = 0 (uniform)” case, $A = 0$ and the dashed line is horizontal at $1/24 = 0.0417$ for every sector.
Sample $\bar{R} = 0.037$ differs from $A = 0$ and is on the same scale as $\sqrt{1/N} = 0.032$.
Mean direction is undefined, so SE and difference / SE are blank in the second table.
In the “κ = 0.5” case, $A = 0.2425$; $\bar{R} = 0.2699$ differs from the finite-$N$ value 0.2444 by $+1.19$ SE, and mean-direction SE widens to 5.2°.
In the “κ = 10, N = 5000” case, $A = 0.9486$ and circular variance is 0.0514, with mean-direction SE 0.26°.
In this case, midpoint approximation error is comparable to the binomial SE; the difference between the dashed line and the band center (the `winding_cdf` difference) is 1.4 SE at the peak center 315° and 1.1 SE at sectors ±45° from it.
Two of 24 sectors fall outside the band, comparable to the expected 1.1 from the probability 0.046 of each sector exceeding $\pm 2$ SE.

## Bias in the mean resultant length

The upward bias of $\bar{R}$ increases as $N$ and $\kappa$ decrease.
The following cell generates $R$ groups (200–4000) of $N = 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200, 500$ observations with $\mu = 0$ and concentration $\kappa$ (0.25–10), computing means of $\bar{R}$ and $\bar{R}^2$ at each $N$.
Because $E[\bar{R}^2] = A^2 + (1 - A^2)/N$ is exact, check its difference from the mean of $\bar{R}^2$ divided by SE (sample standard deviation across groups / $\sqrt{R}$).
Jensen's inequality bounds the mean of $\bar{R}$ above by $\sqrt{E[\bar{R}^2]}$.
The final column gives the fraction of groups satisfying $|\bar{\theta} - \mu| \le 1.96\,\operatorname{SE}(\bar{\theta})$, close to 0.95 if the normal approximation holds.
The plot shows means of $\bar{R}$ as symbols joined by a dotted line, $\sqrt{A^2 + (1 - A^2)/N}$ as a dashed line, and $A(\kappa)$ as a horizontal reference line, with a logarithmic horizontal axis.
The SE of the mean of $\bar{R}$ is smaller than the symbols, so it is tabulated.

```rust
//| id: random-von-mises-resultant-bias
//| ref: random-von-mises-resultant-bias
//| title: "Examine mean resultant length bias across observation counts"
//| run: reactive
//| uses: [random-von-mises-sample-model]
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-random, scioxide-special]
//| inputs:
//|   kappa: {"type":"range","label":"Concentration κ [-]","min":0.25,"max":10,"step":0.25,"value":3}
//|   replicates: {"type":"range","label":"Replicates R [groups]","min":200,"max":4000,"step":200,"value":2000}
//|   seed: {"type":"range","label":"Random seed [-]","min":1,"max":1000,"step":1,"value":5}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"weak","label":"κ = 0.25","description":"For A = 0.124, the mean R̄ at N = 5 is 0.417. At N = 5 and 10, 1.96 SE exceeds π and the proportion is 1, dropping to 0.89 at N = 50.","inputs":{"kappa":0.25}}
//|   - {"id":"strong","label":"κ = 10","description":"At A = 0.9486, bias at N = 5 shrinks to about 0.01, and mean-direction interval proportions range from 0.940 to 0.963 for all N.","inputs":{"kappa":10}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"bias","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":0.7248609411406439,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"bias","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"min":0.825,"max":0.86}}
//|       - {"artifact":"bias","path":"/rows/6/1","range":{"min":0.805,"max":0.816}}
//|       - {"artifact":"bias","path":"/rows/6/6","range":{"min":0.92,"max":0.98}}
//|   - case: weak
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"bias","path":"/rows/0/6","range":{"min":0.999,"max":1.0}}
//|       - {"artifact":"bias","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"min":0.38,"max":0.45}}
//|       - {"artifact":"bias","path":"/rows/3/6","range":{"min":0.85,"max":0.93}}
//|   - case: strong
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"bias","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":0.9198733038412512,"atol":1e-9}}
use scioxide_core::RealScalar;
use scioxide_random::{Distribution, SeedableRng, SplitMix64, VonMises};

let law = VonMises::<f64>::new(0.0, kappa).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let a = resultant_length(kappa)?;
let r = replicates as usize;
let mut rng = SplitMix64::from_seed(seed as u64);
let (mut rows, mut mean_points, mut theory) = (Vec::new(), Vec::new(), Vec::new());
for n in [5_usize, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200, 500] {
    let (mut sum_r, mut sum_r2, mut sum_r4, mut covered) = (0.0_f64, 0.0_f64, 0.0_f64, 0_usize);
    // Input κ is at least 0.25, so mean-direction SE is defined.
    let se_dir = direction_se(kappa, a, n).ok_or("Mean direction is undefined at κ = 0")?;
    for _ in 0..r {
        let (mut s, mut c) = (0.0_f64, 0.0_f64);
        for _ in 0..n {
            let theta = law.sample(&mut rng).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
            s += RealScalar::sin(theta);
            c += RealScalar::cos(theta);
        }
        let r_bar = RealScalar::hypot(s, c) / n as f64;
        sum_r += r_bar;
        sum_r2 += r_bar * r_bar;
        sum_r4 += r_bar * r_bar * r_bar * r_bar;
        // With μ = 0, atan2(S, C) directly gives the mean-direction error.
        let error = RealScalar::atan2(s, c);
        if -1.96 * se_dir <= error && error <= 1.96 * se_dir {
            covered += 1;
        }
    }
    let rf = r as f64;
    let mean_r = sum_r / rf;
    let se_mean = RealScalar::sqrt((sum_r2 / rf - mean_r * mean_r) / (rf - 1.0));
    // Independent check: for a mean of unit vectors, E[R̄²] = A² + (1 − A²)/N.
    let mean_r2 = sum_r2 / rf;
    let expected_r2 = expected_square_length(a, n);
    let se_r2 = RealScalar::sqrt((sum_r4 / rf - mean_r2 * mean_r2) / (rf - 1.0));
    let z = (mean_r2 - expected_r2) / se_r2;
    let coverage = covered as f64 / rf;
    rows.push((n, mean_r, se_mean, mean_r2, expected_r2, z, coverage));
    let nf = n as f64;
    mean_points.push([nf, mean_r]);
    theory.push([nf, RealScalar::sqrt(expected_r2)]);
}
emit_chart!(
    serde_json::json!({
        "kind": "line",
        "xLabel": "Observation count N [observations]",
        "yLabel": "Mean resultant length [-]",
        "xType": "log",
        "yMin": 0,
        "yMax": 1,
        "dataZoom": true,
        "tooltip": true,
        "annotations": [{"kind": "line", "axis": "y", "value": a, "label": "A(κ)"}],
        "series": [
            {"name": "√(A² + (1 − A²)/N)", "points": theory, "style": {"lineType": "dashed"}},
            {
                "name": "Mean R̄",
                "points": mean_points,
                "style": {"lineType": "dotted", "showSymbol": true, "symbol": "circle"}
            }
        ]
    }),
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "bias-curve" })
);
let fixed4 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 4 });
let sci2 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 2 });
let fixed2 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 2 });
let fixed3 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 3 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "N [observations]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Mean R̄ [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Its SE [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Mean R̄² [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "4", "label": "A² + (1 − A²)/N [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "5", "label": "R̄² difference / SE [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed2 },
    { "key": "6", "label": "Proportion with |θ̄ − μ| ≤ 1.96 SE [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed3 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "bias" })
);
```

With the default inputs ($\kappa = 3$, $R = 2000$ groups, seed 5), the mean of $\bar{R}$ at $N = 5$ is 0.8423, 0.032 above $A = 0.8100$.
At $N = 500$ it falls to 0.8102, with bias comparable to SE 0.0003.
The mean of $\bar{R}^2$ differs from $A^2 + (1 - A^2)/N$ by between $-1.81$ and $+1.87$ SE over seven $N$ values.
Mean-direction interval proportions range from 0.942 to 0.958; at $\kappa = 3$ the normal-approximation SE is usable even for $N = 5$.

In the “κ = 0.25” case, the mean of $\bar{R}$ at $N = 5$ is 0.417 against $A = 0.124$, exceeding three times $A$.
At $N = 5, 10$, $1.96\,\operatorname{SE}(\bar{\theta})$ exceeds $\pi$, making the proportion 1; at $N = 50$ ($A\sqrt{N} = 0.88$), it drops to 0.89.
The $\operatorname{SE}(\bar{\theta})$ approximation fails in this range, and intervals do not maintain nominal 95% coverage.
Even here, the mean of $\bar{R}^2$ remains within 1.33 SE of $A^2 + (1 - A^2)/N$; the exact formula applies regardless of $N$ and $\kappa$.
In the “κ = 10” case, bias at $N = 5$ shrinks to about 0.01, and proportions range from 0.940 to 0.963 at every $N$.

## Circular statistics of observed data

This page generated samples with known parameters.
Functions computing mean direction, mean resultant length, and circular variance from observed wind directions are available in `statistic` and `Statistic::circular` of scioxide-stats.
Estimating von Mises $\kappa$ from $\bar{R}$ requires solving $A(\hat{\kappa}) = \bar{R}$, but at small $N$ the bias above directly produces overestimation of $\hat{\kappa}$.

## Summary

Sample $\bar{R}$ is upward-biased as $E[\bar{R}^2] = A^2 + (1 - A^2)/N$ shows, so compare it with $\sqrt{A^2 + (1 - A^2)/N}$ rather than $A$ when $\kappa$ is small or $N$ is a few tens or fewer.
Even uniform wind directions ($\kappa = 0$) give $\bar{R}$ of order $1/\sqrt{N}$, so a small $\bar{R}$ is not evidence of concentration.
Use mean-direction SE $1/\sqrt{N\kappa A}$ only when $A\sqrt{N}$ is much larger than 1; mean direction is undefined at $\kappa \to 0$.
Compare 24-sector frequencies with the density using bin probabilities and ranges from their binomial SEs.
