The discount rate making net present value (NPV) zero is called the internal rate of return (IRR) when assessing investments.

With initial investment $C_0 < 0$ (outflow) and period-$t$ end cash flow $C_t$ (inflow or outflow), NPV at annual rate $r$ is defined as follows.

$$
\operatorname{NPV}(r) = \sum_{t=0}^m \frac{C_t}{(1 + r)^t} = C_0 + \frac{C_1}{1 + r} + \frac{C_2}{(1 + r)^2} + \dots + \frac{C_m}{(1 + r)^m} \qquad (r > -1)
$$

Multiplying IRR equation $\operatorname{NPV}(r) = 0$ by $q^m$ ($q = 1 + r$) produces a degree-$m$ polynomial in $q$.
$r = -1$ ($q = 0$) is an NPV singularity; cash-flow signs can give multiple real roots with $r > -1$.

## Conventional and nonconventional cash flows

### Conventional: a unique IRR

Initial investment followed only by positive returns ($C_0 < 0$, with $C_t > 0$ for $t \ge 1$) is conventional.
Its derivative is

$$
\frac{d \operatorname{NPV}}{dr} = -\sum_{t=1}^m \frac{t C_t}{(1 + r)^{t+1}} < 0 \qquad (r > -1)
$$

so NPV strictly decreases over $r > -1$.
Moreover, $r \to -1^+$ gives $\operatorname{NPV} \to +\infty$ and $r \to +\infty$ gives $\operatorname{NPV} \to C_0 < 0$; continuity and monotonicity yield exactly one IRR in $r > -1$.
It is positive when $\operatorname{NPV}(0) = \sum_t C_t > 0$, meaning total returns exceed investment.

### Nonconventional: possibly multiple IRRs

Flows with a large final outflow $C_m = -D < 0$, such as removal or environmental-restoration costs (for example $(-, +, +, -)$), are nonconventional.

For initial investment 100, receipts $A$ after each of years one and two, and removal cost $D$ after year three, all amounts in 10,000 yen, NPV is

$$
\operatorname{NPV}(r) = -100 + \frac{A}{1+r} + \frac{A}{(1+r)^2} - \frac{D}{(1+r)^3}
$$

Setting $q = 1 + r > 0$ and multiplying by $q^3$ gives this cubic.

$$
P(q) = -100 q^3 + A q^2 + A q - D = 0
$$

For removal $D > 0$, coefficient signs $-100 \to +A \to -D$ change twice; Descartes' rule gives either two or zero positive real roots $q > 0$ counting multiplicity.
For $D = 0$, $P(q) = q(-100q^2 + Aq + A)$ gives one positive root after excluding $q = 0$, hence one IRR as in conventional flows.

| Rate range | NPV with removal cost $D > 0$ |
|---|---|
| $r \to -1^+$ | Removal term $-D/q^3$ dominates, diverging to $-\infty$ |
| Intermediate rates | Positive if receipts $A$ are large enough |
| $r \to +\infty$ | Discounted terms tend to zero, approaching initial investment $-100$ [10,000 yen] |

Sufficiently large receipts $A$ let NPV rise from negative to positive and fall negative again, giving two IRRs $r_1 < r_2$.

### Removal cost where two IRRs coincide

Fixing $A$ and increasing $D$ shifts $P$ downward; two positive roots approach and coincide at cost $D^*$.
A repeated root satisfies both $P(q) = 0$ and $P'(q) = -300 q^2 + 2 A q + A = 0$.
The positive root of $P'(q) = 0$ and its removal cost are:

$$
q^* = \frac{A + \sqrt{A^2 + 300 A}}{300}, \qquad D^* = -100 (q^*)^3 + A (q^*)^2 + A q^*
$$

There are two IRRs for $0 < D < D^*$ and none for $D > D^*$.
At $A = 70$ [10,000 yen], these are $D^* \approx 49.75$ [10,000 yen] and $r^* = q^* - 1 \approx -23.0$%; at $A = 100$ [10,000 yen], $q^* = 1$ and $D^* = 100$ [10,000 yen] ($r^* = 0$).
As $D$ approaches $D^*$, root separation shrinks proportionally to $\sqrt{D^* - D}$; NPV does not change sign near repeated roots, making sign searches difficult.

## Logarithmic scanning and Brent solves

Uniform rate $r$ scanning poorly resolves rapid changes near singularity $r = -1$.
Instead, uniformly divide dimensionless logarithmic growth rate:

$$
u = \ln(1 + r) \iff 1 + r = e^u
$$

Scan from $r = -0.95$ ($u \approx -3.0$) to $r = 4$ ($u \approx 1.6$), keeping ratios of adjacent compound-growth factors $1 + r$ constant.

Apply Brent to each scanned subinterval with opposite NPV signs, finding each interval's root.
Exact-zero scan points are also accepted as endpoint roots by `Brent`, so those intervals are solved too.
Brent safeguards inverse quadratic/secant updates with bracket-preserving bisection; see [Bracketing root-finding methods](../../algorithms/bracketing-methods/).
Scanning can miss roots closer than the spacing and repeated roots without sign changes; choose resolution for root separation.

The following block provides definitions shared by two cells.
`npv` gives NPV, `log_grid` scan points $r = e^u - 1$ dividing $u$ into $n$ intervals, and `sign_change_intervals` selects Brent subintervals.
Compute $\ln(1 + r)$ and $e^u - 1$ with `RealScalar::ln_1p` and `RealScalar::exp_m1` to avoid cancellation near zero $r$ and $u$.

```rust
//| id: rootfind-cashflow-model
//| kind: definitions
//| title: "NPV and logarithmic scanning"
//| crates: [scioxide-core]
use scioxide_core::RealScalar;

/// NPV(r) = −100 + A/(1 + r) + A/(1 + r)² − D/(1 + r)³ [10,000 yen]. A receipts, D removal costs [10,000 yen].
pub(crate) fn npv(inflow: f64, cost: f64, rate: f64) -> f64 {
    let q = 1.0 + rate;
    -100.0 + inflow / q + inflow / (q * q) - cost / (q * q * q)
}

/// Divide u = ln(1 + r) on [ln(1 + r_min), ln(1 + r_max)] into n intervals; scan r = e^u − 1.
pub(crate) fn log_grid(r_min: f64, r_max: f64, n: usize) -> Vec<f64> {
    let (u_min, u_max) = (RealScalar::ln_1p(r_min), RealScalar::ln_1p(r_max));
    (0..=n)
        .map(|i| RealScalar::exp_m1(u_min + (u_max - u_min) * i as f64 / n as f64))
        .collect()
}

/// Left-endpoint indices of Brent subintervals: select opposite-sign intervals and
/// intervals with exact-zero left endpoints (also the final right endpoint).
pub(crate) fn sign_change_intervals(values: &[f64]) -> Vec<usize> {
    let last = values.len() - 2;
    (0..=last)
        .filter(|&j| {
            let (left, right) = (values[j], values[j + 1]);
            left == 0.0
                || (left < 0.0 && right > 0.0)
                || (left > 0.0 && right < 0.0)
                || (j == last && right == 0.0)
        })
        .collect()
}
```

## IRRs with removal costs

The next cell varies receipts $A$, removal $D$, and upper scan rate, dividing $u$ into 640 intervals and solving sign-changing intervals with `Brent`.
Use `with_residual_tolerance` to set absolute reference $\lvert \operatorname{NPV} \rvert \le 10^{-11}$ [10,000 yen] ($10^{-7}$ yen).
This adds monetary residual acceptance to default interval-width threshold $4\varepsilon(1 + \max(\lvert a \rvert, \lvert b \rvert))$.
The table gives interval endpoints, returned `Brent` points, their NPV, evaluations, and termination.
Unsuccessful interval points are not counted as IRRs; the final outcome shows whether every interval succeeded.
Without sign-changing intervals, Brent is not run; outcome `failed` displays the scan point minimizing $\lvert \operatorname{NPV} \rvert$.

```rust
//| id: rootfind-cashflow-rate
//| ref: rootfind-cashflow-rate
//| title: "Find removal-cost cash-flow IRRs in each sign-changing interval"
//| run: reactive
//| uses: [rootfind-cashflow-model]
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-rootfind]
//| inputs:
//|   annual_inflow: {"type":"range","label":"Each year-one/two receipt A [10,000 yen]","description":"Changes positive intermediate cash flows.","min":45,"max":100,"step":1,"value":70}
//|   decommissioning_cost: {"type":"range","label":"Year-three removal cost D [10,000 yen]","description":"Changes final negative flow and IRR counts.","min":0,"max":180,"step":5,"value":40}
//|   maximum_rate_percent: {"type":"range","label":"Upper scan rate [%/year]","description":"Changes the high-rate scan/display range.","min":100,"max":800,"step":25,"value":400}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"no-decommissioning","label":"D = 0 (no removal)","description":"Conventional flow gives only one IRR, 25.69%.","inputs":{"decommissioning_cost":0}}
//|   - {"id":"large-inflow","label":"A = 100 (large receipts)","description":"Two IRRs separate to −67.22% and 49.08%.","inputs":{"annual_inflow":100}}
//|   - {"id":"no-sign-change","label":"D = 180 (large removal cost)","description":"No sign changes in range; display only the scan point minimizing |NPV|.","inputs":{"decommissioning_cost":180}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"irr","path":"/rows/0/2","approx":{"value":-50.0,"atol":5e-11}}
//|       - {"artifact":"irr","path":"/rows/1/2","approx":{"value":0.0,"atol":5e-11}}
//|       - {"artifact":"irr","path":"/rows/0/3","range":{"min":-1e-11,"max":1e-11}}
//|       - {"artifact":"irr","path":"/rows/0/4","equals":2}
//|       - {"artifact":"irr","path":"/rows/1/4","equals":5}
//|       - {"artifact":"irr","path":"/rows/0/5","equals":"ResidualTolerance"}
//|       - {"artifact":"irr","path":"/rows/1/5","equals":"ResidualTolerance"}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/2/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: no-decommissioning
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"irr","path":"/rows/0/2","approx":{"value":25.69,"atol":5e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"irr","path":"/rows/0/5","equals":"ResidualTolerance"}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/2/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: large-inflow
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"irr","path":"/rows/0/2","approx":{"value":-67.22,"atol":5e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"irr","path":"/rows/1/2","approx":{"value":49.08,"atol":5e-3}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/2/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: no-sign-change
//|     expect:
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/state","equals":"failed"}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/diagnostics/0/value","approx":{"value":94.80,"atol":5e-3}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/1/diagnostics/1/value","approx":{"value":-69.97,"atol":5e-3}}
use scioxide_core::{EvaluationRole, Interval, NumericScalar, ResidualTolerance};
use scioxide_rootfind::{Brent, ScalarFn, ScalarRootOptions, ScalarRootSolver};

let (a, d) = (annual_inflow, decommissioning_cost);
let function = ScalarFn::new(|r: f64| Ok(npv(a, d, r)));
// Also accept |NPV| ≤ 1e-11 [10,000 yen] (relative part zero).
let options = ScalarRootOptions::default()
    .with_residual_tolerance(ResidualTolerance::new(1e-11, 0.0).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?);
let grid = log_grid(-0.95, maximum_rate_percent / 100.0, 640);
let values: Vec<f64> = grid.iter().map(|&r| npv(a, d, r)).collect();
let (mut rows, mut found, mut unsuccessful) = (Vec::new(), Vec::new(), 0_usize);
for j in sign_change_intervals(&values) {
    let interval = Interval::new(grid[j], grid[j + 1]).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let result = Brent
        .solve(&function, interval, &options)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    // Count as IRR only with successful termination.
    if result.is_success() {
        found.push(result.point());
    } else {
        unsuccessful += 1;
    }
    rows.push((
        100.0 * grid[j],
        100.0 * grid[j + 1],
        100.0 * result.point(),
        result.residual(),
        result.counts().provider(EvaluationRole::Function),
        result.termination().to_string(),
    ));
}
// Show IRRs vertically and NPV = 0 horizontally.
let zero_line =
    serde_json::json!({ "kind": "line", "axis": "y", "value": 0.0, "label": "NPV = 0" });
let mut marks = vec![zero_line];
marks.extend(found.iter().map(|&r| {
    serde_json::json!({ "kind": "line", "axis": "x", "value": 100.0 * r, "label": "IRR" })
}));
let curve: Vec<[f64; 2]> = grid
    .iter()
    .zip(&values)
    .map(|(&r, &v)| [100.0 * r, v])
    .collect();
emit_line_chart!(
    &vec![serde_json::json!({ "name": "NPV(r)", "points": curve })],
    options = &serde_json::json!({
        "xLabel": "Rate r [%/year]",
        "yLabel": "Net present value NPV [10,000 yen]",
        "yMin": -150.0,
        "yMax": 150.0,
        "annotations": marks
    })
);
if rows.is_empty() {
    // Without sign changes, show minimum-|NPV| scan points (not proof of no roots).
    let (r, v) = grid
        .iter()
        .zip(&values)
        .fold((grid[0], values[0]), |best, (&r, &v)| {
            if NumericScalar::abs(v) < NumericScalar::abs(best.1) {
                (r, v)
            } else {
                best
            }
        });
    emit_outcome!(
        Outcome::failed("No sign-changing subintervals in range; IRRs were not computed")
            .explanation("This does not prove absence of roots. Check all cubic roots.")
            .status("No sign change")
            .diagnostic("Rate at minimum-|NPV| scan point", 100.0 * r, Some("%"))
            .diagnostic("NPV there", v, Some("10,000 yen"))
    );
} else {
    let percent = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 4 });
    let fixed10 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 10 });
    let sci2 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 2 });
    let columns = serde_json::json!([
        { "key": "0", "label": "Left subinterval endpoint [%]", "type": "number", "format": percent },
        { "key": "1", "label": "Right subinterval endpoint [%]", "type": "number", "format": percent },
        { "key": "2", "label": "Brent point [%]", "type": "number", "format": fixed10 },
        { "key": "3", "label": "NPV [10,000 yen]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 },
        { "key": "4", "label": "Function evaluations [count]", "type": "integer" },
        { "key": "5", "label": "Termination reason" }
    ]);
    let intervals = rows.len();
    emit_table_with_columns!(
        columns,
        rows,
        metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "irr" })
    );
    let outcome = if unsuccessful == 0 {
        Outcome::satisfied("Brent accepted IRRs in every sign-changing subinterval")
    } else {
        Outcome::partial("Some Brent subintervals stopped before acceptance")
            .explanation("Points with unsuccessful termination are excluded from IRR counts.")
    };
    emit_outcome!(
        outcome
            .status(format!("Successful {}/{intervals}", intervals - unsuccessful))
            .diagnostic("Sign-changing subintervals", intervals as f64, None)
            .diagnostic("Unaccepted subintervals", unsuccessful as f64, None)
    );
}
```

At defaults $A = 70$ and $D = 40$ [10,000 yen], factorization $-100q^3 + 70q^2 + 70q - 40 = -10(q - 1)(2q - 1)(5q + 4)$ gives IRRs $r = -50$% and 0% ($q = -0.8$ lies outside $r > -1$).
`Brent` accepts both with `ResidualTolerance`, giving NPV $1.1 \times 10^{-13}$ and $1.4 \times 10^{-14}$ [10,000 yen] and closed-form discrepancies $1.4 \times 10^{-14}$% and $1.6 \times 10^{-14}$%.
At default upper 400%, root $-50$% mathematically coincides with the middle scan point ($u = \ln 0.5$), requiring only two endpoint evaluations.
The $0$% root requires five evaluations.
At $D = 0$ there is only IRR 25.69%; at $A = 100$ there are $-67.22$% and 49.08%.

The fixed vertical range $\pm 150$ [10,000 yen] hides rapid changes where $r$ approaches $-95$%.
NPV is positive between the IRRs ($-50$% < $r$ < 0%) and negative outside.
With two IRRs, the rule “accept if IRR exceeds the cost of capital” cannot be used.
Use another criterion, such as the sign of $\operatorname{NPV}(r_c)$ at cost of capital $r_c$.

At $D = 180$ or $A = 45$ no sign changes occur in range; outcome is `failed`.
At $D = 180$, maximum NPV remains negative; even minimum-$\lvert \operatorname{NPV} \rvert$ scan point $r = 94.80$% gives NPV $-69.97$ [10,000 yen].
Scanning alone cannot show no roots; next inspect all cubic roots for IRR existence.

## Checking all cubic roots

Because NPV converts to polynomial $P(q) = 0$, all roots through `polynomial_roots_with_options` supply every IRR candidate from real $q > 0$ roots.
This independent computation checks roots potentially missed by scanning.
The next cell fixes receipts $A$ and sweeps removal $D$ from 0 to 180 in 0.5 steps [10,000 yen], comparing cubic IRR counts with preceding scan-selected brackets at each $D$.
Control scan subdivisions $N$ to inspect ranges where coarse scans miss nearby roots.
Compare only roots in scan range $-95$% $\le r \le 400$%.

Default `PolynomialRootMethod::Auto` generates cubic candidates from companion eigenvalues, improves them with Aberth–Ehrlich, and verifies backward errors.
Use roots of the cubic only with successful `PolynomialRootReport` termination; otherwise stop at that $D$.
Table backward errors are maximum `PolynomialRootReport::backward_error` over swept $D$ values.
The final outcome confirms verification for all 361 $D$ values.

//| ref: rootfind-cashflow-polynomial-roots
//| title: "Sweep removal costs and compare all cubic roots with scan-selected brackets"
//| run: reactive
//| uses: [rootfind-cashflow-model]
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-rootfind]
//| inputs:
//|   sweep_inflow: {"type":"range","label":"Each year-one/two receipt A [10,000 yen]","description":"Changes coalescence cost D*.","min":45,"max":100,"step":1,"value":70}
//|   scan_intervals: {"type":"range","label":"Scan subdivisions N [-]","description":"Coarse scans miss two nearby IRRs.","min":5,"max":640,"step":5,"value":640}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"close-roots-coarse","label":"A = 85, N = 20 (coarse scan)","description":"At three D values from 71–72 [10,000 yen], just before D* = 72.48, scanning misses both nearby IRRs.","inputs":{"sweep_inflow":85,"scan_intervals":20}}
//|   - {"id":"close-roots-fine","label":"A = 85, N = 40 (fine scan)","description":"Refinement makes counts match at every D.","inputs":{"sweep_inflow":85,"scan_intervals":40}}
//|   - {"id":"double-root","label":"A = 100 (repeated root at D* = 100)","description":"At D = 100 [10,000 yen], repeated q = 1 has no sign change and scanning misses it.","inputs":{"sweep_inflow":100}}

//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/0","approx":{"value":49.7497,"atol":5e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":-23.0217,"atol":5e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":0}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/3","equals":null}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/5","range":{"max":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"branches","path":"/spec/layers/0/points/0/0","approx":{"value":4.0,"atol":0.25}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/2/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|   - case: close-roots-coarse
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/0","approx":{"value":72.48,"atol":5e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":3}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/3","equals":71}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/4","equals":72}
//|   - case: close-roots-fine
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":0}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/3","equals":null}
//|   - case: double-root
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/0","approx":{"value":100.0,"atol":5e-5}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":1}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/3","equals":100}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/2/state","equals":"satisfied"}

use scioxide_rootfind::{PolynomialRootOptions, polynomial_roots_with_options};

let (a, n) = (sweep_inflow, scan_intervals as usize);
let (r_min, r_max) = (-0.95_f64, 4.0_f64);
let grid = log_grid(r_min, r_max, n);
// Default settings, method Auto.
let options = PolynomialRootOptions::default();
// Closed form: positive P′(q*) = 0 root q* coalesces IRRs at removal D*.
let q_star = (a + RealScalar::sqrt(a * a + 300.0 * a)) / 300.0;
let d_star = -100.0 * RealScalar::powi(q_star, 3) + a * q_star * q_star + a * q_star;
let (mut lower, mut upper, mut lost) = (Vec::new(), Vec::new(), Vec::new());
let (mut missed, mut backward) = (Vec::new(), 0.0_f64);
for k in 0..=360 {
    let d = 0.5 * f64::from(k);
    // Ascending coefficients of P(q) = −100q³ + Aq² + Aq − D.

        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;

    if !report.is_success() {
        return Err(format!(
            "Unverified cubic roots at D = {d} [10,000 yen]: {}",
            report.termination()
        ));
    }
    backward = report
        .backward_error()
        .map_or(backward, |e| backward.max(e));
    // Among real q > 0 roots, use r = q − 1 inside scan ranges as IRRs.

        .iter()
        .filter(|q| q.im == 0.0 && q.re > 0.0)
        .map(|q| q.re - 1.0)
        .filter(|&r| r_min <= r && r <= r_max)
        .collect();

        let branch = if r < q_star - 1.0 {
            &mut lower
        } else {
            &mut upper
        };
        branch.push([d, 100.0 * r]);
    }
    // Count Brent brackets selected by the preceding cell's rules.
    let values: Vec<f64> = grid.iter().map(|&r| npv(a, d, r)).collect();
    if sign_change_intervals(&values).len() != exact.len() {
        missed.push(d);
        lost.extend(exact.iter().map(|&r| [d, 100.0 * r]));
    }
}
let d_star_line =
    serde_json::json!({ "kind": "line", "axis": "x", "value": d_star, "label": "D*" });
emit_chart!(
    serde_json::json!({
        "kind": "xy",
        "xLabel": "Removal cost D [10,000 yen]",
        "yLabel": "IRR r [%/year]",
        "yMin": -100.0,
        "yMax": 100.0,
        "annotations": [d_star_line],
        "layers": [
            { "id": "lower", "kind": "line", "name": "Cubic roots (lower IRR)", "points": lower },
            { "id": "upper", "kind": "line", "name": "Cubic roots (upper IRR)", "points": upper },
            { "id": "lost", "kind": "scatter", "name": "Roots at mismatched-count D", "points": lost }
        ]
    }),
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "branches" })
);
let fixed4 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 4 });
let fixed1 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 1 });
let sci2 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 2 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "D* (closed form) [10,000 yen]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "1", "label": "IRR at D* [%]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Mismatched D [count]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Minimum such D [10,000 yen]", "type": "number", "format": fixed1, "nullText": "None" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Maximum such D [10,000 yen]", "type": "number", "format": fixed1, "nullText": "None" },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Maximum backward error [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 }
]);
let row = (
    d_star,
    100.0 * (q_star - 1.0),
    missed.len(),
    missed.first().copied(),
    missed.last().copied(),
    backward,
);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    vec![row],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "summary" })
);
emit_outcome!(
    Outcome::satisfied("All cubic roots verified at 361 swept D values")
        .status("Successful 361/361")
        .diagnostic("Maximum backward error", backward, None)
        .diagnostic("Mismatched D counts", missed.len() as f64, None)

At defaults $A = 70$ [10,000 yen] and $N = 640$, branches join at $D^* = 49.7497$ [10,000 yen] ($r^* = -23.02$%), with no IRRs farther right.

## Summary

Cash flows with final costs such as removal may have two IRRs or none.
Log-coordinate $u = \ln(1 + r)$ scanning and bracketed `Brent` find interval IRRs, but can miss roots closer than scan spacing and repeated roots.
If NPV converts to a polynomial, use `polynomial_roots_with_options` for all roots, comparing real-$q > 0$ counts with scan counts.
On mismatches, refine scans or identify boundaries with closed forms such as repeated-root condition $P = P' = 0$.
For two-IRR projects, assess NPV at the cost of capital rather than relying on IRR alone.
