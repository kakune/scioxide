Frequencies of circular membranes fixed around their boundary, such as drums, are determined by Bessel zeros.
The required quantity is the $m$th zero for order $\nu$, not an arbitrary zero, so ordinary root finding alone is insufficient.
This page computes eigenfrequencies using indexed special-zero function `special_zeros_with_options`.
[Cylindrical waves in scioxide-special](../../../scioxide-special/examples/cylindrical-wave/) covers Bessel properties and waves; notation $j_{\nu,m}$ follows it.

## Membrane vibrations and boundaries

Membrane displacement $w(r, \varphi, t)$ [m] at surface density $\sigma$ [kg/m²] and tension $T$ [N/m] obeys wave equation $w_{tt} = c^2 \nabla^2 w$ with $c = \sqrt{T/\sigma}$ [m/s].
Separating $w = R(r)\cos(\nu\varphi)\cos(\omega t)$ makes $R$ satisfy Bessel's equation:

$$
r^2 R'' + r R' + \bigl(k^2 r^2 - \nu^2\bigr) R = 0, \qquad k = \omega / c
$$

Single-valuedness around the circumference gives $\nu = 0, 1, 2, \dots$; the center-finite solution is $R = J_\nu(kr)$ (the other, $Y_\nu$, diverges at $r \to 0$).
Fixed boundary $R(a) = 0$ at radius $a$ [m] gives $k a = j_{\nu,m}$ (the $m$th positive zero of $J_\nu$), hence eigenfrequencies:

$$
f_{\nu,m} = \frac{\omega}{2\pi} = \frac{c\, j_{\nu,m}}{2\pi a}
$$

Mode $(\nu, m)$ has $\nu$ nodal diameters and $m - 1$ nodal circles excluding the boundary.
$j_{0,1} = 2.404826$ determines the fundamental; other ratios $j_{\nu,m}/j_{0,1}$ are noninteger, producing inharmonic overtones in drums.
Consider radius $a = 0.33$ m and density $\sigma = 0.26$ kg/m², varying tension.

## Computing indexed zeros

`special_zeros_with_options(family, branch, start, count, &options)` takes family (such as `SpecialZeroFamily::BesselJ(nu)`), branch (`SpecialZeroBranch::Real` for positive reals), first index (from 1), count, and settings.
Returned `SpecialZeros` is a checked sequence; successful `report().termination()` (`Complete`) means every requested zero was checked.
For values alone, `special_zeros(SpecialZeroFamily::BesselJ(nu), count)` computes `count` zeros from index 1 with defaults, returning values only if every check passes.

Real zeros of $J_\nu$ are indexed as follows.

1. Use table guesses through the eighth zero for orders $\nu = 0, 1, 5/2$.
   Otherwise, sufficiently large $\beta = (m + \nu/2 - 1/4)\pi$ ($\beta > 8$ and $\beta > 2(\nu^2 + 1)$) uses McMahon; for $\nu \ge 32$ near turning points use Olver uniform asymptotics.
2. With initial guesses, check for a sign change in the surrounding interval ($\pm 0.35$ times neighboring-zero spacing).
3. Without guesses or sign changes, move rightward from $x = \max(\nu, 1/8)$ in steps shorter than one-quarter period, selecting sign change $m$ ($j_{\nu,1} > \nu$ ensures no earlier zeros).
4. Refine within the interval using safeguarded Newton, replacing out-of-bracket updates by bisection.
5. Finally reevaluate at rounded coordinates, checking estimated error (function magnitude plus evaluation error, divided by slope, plus rounding displacement) against tolerance.

Sub-quarter-period steps prevent two zeros per step canceling sign changes.
These checks nevertheless use floating-point evaluation, not guaranteed enclosing intervals.
At defaults, small-index $\nu = 2, 3$ zeros are neither tabulated nor within asymptotic ranges, so step 3 counting determines indices.
See [Indexed zeros of special functions](../../algorithms/special-function-zeros/) for details, derivative zeros, complex branches, origin requests, and partial results.

## Modal frequencies

The following cell computes the first $M$ zeros at each order $\nu = 0, \dots, \nu_{\max}$ through `special_zeros_with_options`, sorting by frequency.
Inputs are maximum order $\nu_{\max}$, zeros per order $M$, and tension $T$ [N/m].
Outputs plot frequencies by order and tabulate wave speed/fundamental and up to 12 lowest modes' zeros, frequencies, ratios, and estimated errors.
Compute and pass zeros through order $\nu_{\max} + 1$ for interlacing checks in the next cell.
Unsuccessful sequence termination stops that order at its checked prefix and reports this in the outcome.

```rust
//| id: rootfind-membrane-modes
//| ref: rootfind-membrane-modes
//| title: "Compute circular-membrane eigenfrequencies from Bessel zeros"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-rootfind]
//| exports: [zeros, errors, max_order]
//| inputs:
//|   max_order: {"type":"range","label":"Maximum order ν_max [-]","description":"Maximum nodal-diameter count.","min":0,"max":6,"step":1,"value":3}
//|   zeros_per_order: {"type":"range","label":"Zeros per order M [-]","description":"Maximum nodal-circle count (M − 1).","min":1,"max":6,"step":1,"value":3}
//|   tension: {"type":"range","label":"Tension T [N/m]","description":"Changes wave speed c = √(T/σ).","min":1000,"max":8000,"step":500,"value":4000}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"low-tension","label":"T = 1000 N/m (quarter tension)","description":"Frequencies halve as √T, giving fundamental 71.93 Hz without changing frequency ratios.","inputs":{"tension":1000}}
//|   - {"id":"more-zeros","label":"M = 6 (more indices)","description":"The lowest 12 include (0, 4). Next-cell j₀,₆ differs from McMahon by only 1.3 × 10⁻⁷.","inputs":{"zeros_per_order":6}}
//|   - {"id":"high-order","label":"ν_max = 6 (higher orders)","description":"The lowest 12 include (4, 1), (5, 1), and (6, 1). Next-cell j₆,₁ differs from McMahon by −0.105.","inputs":{"max_order":6}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/0","approx":{"value":124.0347,"atol":5e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":143.8577,"atol":5e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"frequencies","path":"/spec/layers/0/points/0/1","approx":{"value":143.8577,"atol":5e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"modes","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":2.404825557695773,"atol":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"modes","path":"/rows/1/0","equals":"(1, 1)"}
//|       - {"artifact":"modes","path":"/rows/1/3","approx":{"value":1.593341,"atol":5e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"modes","path":"/rows/2/0","equals":"(2, 1)"}
//|       - {"artifact":"modes","path":"/rows/2/3","approx":{"value":2.135549,"atol":5e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"modes","path":"/rows/3/0","equals":"(0, 2)"}
//|       - {"artifact":"modes","path":"/rows/3/3","approx":{"value":2.295417,"atol":5e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"modes","path":"/rows/11/4","range":{"max":2e-15}}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/3/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/3/diagnostics/0/value","equals":12}
//|   - case: low-tension
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":71.9288,"atol":5e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"modes","path":"/rows/1/3","approx":{"value":1.593341,"atol":5e-6}}
//|       - {"artifact":"modes","path":"/rows/3/3","approx":{"value":2.295417,"atol":5e-6}}
//|   - case: more-zeros
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"modes","path":"/rows/11/0","equals":"(0, 4)"}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/3/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/3/diagnostics/0/value","equals":24}
//|   - case: high-order
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"modes","path":"/rows/6/0","equals":"(4, 1)"}
//|       - {"artifact":"modes","path":"/rows/11/0","equals":"(6, 1)"}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/3/state","equals":"satisfied"}
//|       - {"path":"/outputs/3/diagnostics/0/value","equals":21}
use scioxide_core::RealScalar;
use scioxide_rootfind::{
    RootTermination, SpecialZeroBranch, SpecialZeroFamily, SpecialZeroOptions,
    special_zeros_with_options,
};
use std::f64::consts::PI;

// Radius a [m], surface density σ [kg/m²]. Wave speed c = √(T/σ) [m/s], frequency f = c j / (2πa) [Hz].
let (radius, density) = (0.33_f64, 0.26_f64);
let speed = RealScalar::sqrt(tension / density);
let frequency = |j: f64| speed * j / (2.0 * PI * radius);
let (orders, count) = (max_order as usize, zeros_per_order as usize);
// zeros[ν][m − 1] = j_{ν,m}. Unsuccessful orders retain only checked prefixes.
let (mut zeros, mut errors) = (Vec::new(), Vec::new());
let mut termination = RootTermination::Complete;
for nu in 0..=orders + 1 {
    let result = special_zeros_with_options(
        SpecialZeroFamily::BesselJ(nu as f64),
        SpecialZeroBranch::Real,
        1,
        count,
        &SpecialZeroOptions::default(),
    )
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    zeros.push(result.roots().iter().map(|z| z.point().re).collect::<Vec<_>>());
    errors.push(result.roots().iter().map(|z| z.estimated_error()).collect::<Vec<_>>());
    if !result.report().is_success() {
        termination = result.report().termination();
        break;
    }
}
// Collect (ν, m, j_{ν,m}, estimated error) for ν ≤ ν_max and sort by increasing frequency.
let mut modes: Vec<(usize, usize, f64, f64)> = Vec::new();
for (nu, (row, error)) in zeros.iter().zip(&errors).enumerate().take(orders + 1) {
    for (m, (&j, &e)) in row.iter().zip(error).enumerate() {
        modes.push((nu, m + 1, j, e));
    }
}
modes.sort_by(|x, y| x.2.total_cmp(&y.2));
let j01 = zeros[0].first().copied().ok_or("Could not obtain j₀,₁")?;
// Plot frequencies versus order ν in series indexed by m.
let layers: Vec<_> = (1..=count)
    .map(|m| {
        let points: Vec<[f64; 2]> = modes
            .iter()
            .filter(|mode| mode.1 == m)
            .map(|&(nu, _, j, _)| [nu as f64, frequency(j)])
            .collect();
        serde_json::json!({
            "id": format!("m{m}"),
            "kind": "scatter",
            "name": format!("m = {m}"),
            "points": points
        })
    })
    .collect();
emit_chart!(
    serde_json::json!({
        "kind": "xy",
        "xLabel": "Order ν (nodal diameters) [-]",
        "yLabel": "Eigenfrequency f [Hz]",
        "xMin": -0.5,
        "xMax": 6.5,
        "yMin": 0.0,
        "tooltip": true,
        "layers": layers
    }),
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "frequencies" })
);
let fixed2 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 2 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Wave speed c [m/s]", "type": "number", "format": fixed2 },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Fundamental f₀₁ [Hz]", "type": "number", "format": fixed2 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    vec![(speed, frequency(j01))],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "summary" })
);
let sci2 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 2 });
let rows: Vec<_> = modes
    .iter()
    .take(12)
    .map(|&(nu, m, j, error)| (format!("({nu}, {m})"), j, frequency(j), j / j01, error))
    .collect();
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "(ν, m)" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Zero j_ν,m [-]", "type": "number", "format": { "fractionDigits": 10 } },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Frequency f [Hz]", "type": "number", "format": { "fractionDigits": 1 } },
    { "key": "3", "label": "f / f₀₁ [-]", "type": "number", "format": { "fractionDigits": 4 } },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Estimated error [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(columns, rows, metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "modes" }));
export!(zeros, zeros);
export!(errors, errors);
export!(max_order, orders);

let largest = modes.iter().map(|mode| mode.3).fold(0.0_f64, RealScalar::max);
let outcome = if termination.is_success() {
    Outcome::satisfied("All requested zeros at every order passed original-function checks")
} else if matches!(termination, RootTermination::Unverified) {
    Outcome::failed("A zero failed checks; stopped at that order")
} else {
    Outcome::partial("Zero search reached a limit; stopped at that order")
};
emit_outcome!(
    outcome
        .status(termination.to_string())
        .diagnostic("Computed modes", modes.len() as f64, None)
        .diagnostic("Maximum estimated error", largest, None)
);
```

At default $T = 4000$ N/m, wave speed is 124.03 m/s and fundamental $f_{0,1} = 143.86$ Hz.
Next modes $(1, 1)$, $(2, 1)$, and $(0, 2)$ have ratios 1.5933, 2.1355, and 2.2954, exactly the zero ratios $j_{\nu,m}/j_{0,1}$.
Estimated errors of 12 zeros (`SpecialZero::estimated_error`) are at most $1.7 \times 10^{-15}$; the outcome is `Complete`.
Tension scales frequencies as $\sqrt{T}$ without changing zeros or ratios (fundamental 71.93 Hz at $T = 1000$ N/m).
At fixed index $m$ frequencies rise with order, with $f_{\nu+1,m}$ between $f_{\nu,m}$ and $f_{\nu,m+1}$ (zero interlacing).
At $\nu_{\max} = 6$, modes with many nodal diameters such as $(4, 1)$, $(5, 1)$, and $(6, 1)$ enter the lowest 12.
Real-drum frequencies differ through air loading and membrane bending stiffness.

## Independent zero checks

Results from `special_zeros_with_options` are estimates checked in the original function.
Their evaluations share scioxide-special Bessel paths, so reevaluation with `bessel_j` is not independent.
The next cell checks prior zeros through three relationships separate from root finding.

1. Double-precision sums of power series $J_\nu(x) = \sum_{k \ge 0} (-1)^k (x/2)^{2k+\nu} / \bigl(k!\,(k+\nu)!\bigr)$ ([DLMF 10.2.2](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.2.E2)).
   Sum of absolute terms $A = \sum_k |t_k|$ equals $I_\nu(x)$; rounding error is approximately a few multiples of $\varepsilon A$.
   Large $x$ increases $A \approx e^x/\sqrt{2\pi x}$ and cancellation, so compare series values against $\varepsilon A$.
2. Differences from three-term McMahon asymptotics ([DLMF 10.21.19](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.21.E19)).

   $$
   j_{\nu,m} \sim \beta - \frac{\mu - 1}{8\beta} - \frac{4(\mu - 1)(7\mu - 31)}{3(8\beta)^3}, \qquad \beta = \left(m + \frac{\nu}{2} - \frac14\right)\pi, \quad \mu = 4\nu^2
   $$

   This fixes $\nu$ with $m \to \infty$; differences reflect truncation error.
3. Violations of interlacing $j_{\nu,m} < j_{\nu+1,m} < j_{\nu,m+1}$ ([DLMF 10.21.2](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.21.E2), $\nu \ge 0$).
   This checks indices rather than value accuracy; a mistaken zero appears as a violation.

```rust
//| id: rootfind-membrane-zero-checks
//| ref: rootfind-membrane-zero-checks
//| title: "Check Bessel zeros with power series, McMahon expansions, and interlacing"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core]
//| dependsOn:
//|   modes: rootfind-membrane-modes
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"checks","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":0}
//|       - {"artifact":"checks","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":12}
//|       - {"artifact":"zeros","path":"/rows/0/2","range":{"min":-1e-15,"max":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"zeros","path":"/rows/11/0","equals":"(3, 3)"}
//|       - {"artifact":"zeros","path":"/rows/11/2","range":{"min":-7.8e-12,"max":7.8e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"zeros","path":"/rows/11/3","approx":{"value":7.79e-12,"rtol":0.01}}
//|       - {"artifact":"zeros","path":"/rows/0/4","approx":{"value":1.751e-3,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"zeros","path":"/rows/1/4","approx":{"value":4.036e-5,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"zeros","path":"/rows/2/4","approx":{"value":4.678e-6,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"zeros","path":"/rows/9/4","approx":{"value":-1.245e-2,"rtol":1e-3}}
use scioxide_core::RealScalar;
use std::f64::consts::PI;

let zeros: Vec<Vec<f64>> =
    serde_json::from_value(modes["zeros"].clone()).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let errors: Vec<Vec<f64>> =
    serde_json::from_value(modes["errors"].clone()).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let orders = modes["max_order"].as_u64().ok_or("Missing maximum order")? as usize;
let mut rows = Vec::new();
for (nu, (row, error)) in zeros.iter().zip(&errors).enumerate().take(orders + 1) {
    for (index, (&j, &e)) in row.iter().zip(error).enumerate() {
        let m = index + 1;
        // Check 1: sum Σ t_k with t_k = (−1)^k (x/2)^{2k+ν} / (k! (k+ν)!) in f64.
        let half = j / 2.0;
        let mut term = RealScalar::powi(half, nu as i32);
        for k in 1..=nu {
            term /= k as f64;
        }
        let (mut sum, mut magnitude, mut k) = (term, term.abs(), 0.0_f64);
        while term.abs() > f64::EPSILON * magnitude * 1e-3 || k < half {
            k += 1.0;
            term *= -half * half / (k * (k + nu as f64));
            sum += term;
            magnitude += term.abs();
        }
        // Check 2: three-term McMahon (DLMF 10.21.19).
        let beta = (m as f64 + nu as f64 / 2.0 - 0.25) * PI;
        let mu = 4.0 * (nu * nu) as f64;
        let mcmahon = beta
            - (mu - 1.0) / (8.0 * beta)
            - 4.0 * (mu - 1.0) * (7.0 * mu - 31.0) / (3.0 * RealScalar::powi(8.0 * beta, 3));
        rows.push((format!("({nu}, {m})"), j, sum, f64::EPSILON * magnitude, j - mcmahon, e));
    }
}
// Check 3: count interlacing violations j_{ν,m} < j_{ν+1,m} < j_{ν,m+1}.
let at = |nu: usize, m: usize| zeros.get(nu).and_then(|row| row.get(m)).copied();
let (mut pairs, mut violations) = (0_usize, 0_usize);
for nu in 0..=orders {
    for m in 0..zeros.get(nu).map_or(0, Vec::len) {
        let (Some(low), Some(next)) = (at(nu, m), at(nu + 1, m)) else {
            continue;
        };
        pairs += 1;
        let above = at(nu, m + 1).is_none_or(|high| next < high);
        if !(low < next && above) {
            violations += 1;
        }
    }
}
emit_table_with_columns!(
    serde_json::json!([
        { "key": "0", "label": "Interlacing violations [count]", "type": "integer" },
        { "key": "1", "label": "Checked pairs [count]", "type": "integer" }
    ]),
    vec![(violations, pairs)],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "checks" })
);
let sci2 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 2 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "(ν, m)" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Zero j_ν,m [-]", "type": "number", "format": { "fractionDigits": 10 } },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Power-series J_ν(j) [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Series rounding indicator εA [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 },
    { "key": "4", "label": "j − McMahon [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Estimated error [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(columns, rows, metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "zeros" }));
```

For 12 default zeros ($\nu_{\max} = 3$, $M = 3$), every power-series $\lvert J_\nu(j_{\nu,m}) \rvert$ is below rounding indicator $\varepsilon A$.
The largest is $5.8 \times 10^{-13}$ at $(3, 3)$, where $\varepsilon A$ is $7.8 \times 10^{-12}$.
Series values grow with $x$, explained by cancellation indicator $\varepsilon A$ without contradicting zero positions.
Twelve pairs show zero interlacing violations.
McMahon differences decrease with index at fixed order: at $\nu = 0$, indices $m = 1, 2, 3$ give $1.8 \times 10^{-3}$, $4.0 \times 10^{-5}$, and $4.7 \times 10^{-6}$.
Setting $M = 6$ in the preceding cell reduces discrepancy from $j_{0,6}$ to $1.3 \times 10^{-7}$.
At fixed index, differences grow with order; at $\nu_{\max} = 6$, discrepancy from $j_{6,1}$ is $-0.105$.
McMahon fixes $\nu$ with $m \to \infty$, giving only coarse references where $m$ is small relative to $\nu$.
At $\nu_{\max} = 6$ and $M = 6$, $\varepsilon A$ at $j_{6,6} \approx 26.8$ rises to $3.9 \times 10^{-6}$, reducing power-series checking sensitivity.
This cell has no inputs; vary the preceding cell to change checked ranges.

## Summary

Eigenfrequencies depend on indexed zeros $m$ of $J_\nu$; wrong indices select different vibration modes.
`special_zeros_with_options` determines indices from table/asymptotic guesses or outward sign-change counts, refines with safeguarded Newton, and checks the original function before returning.
Before using zeros, verify successful `SpecialZeroReport` termination; partial results permit only the prefix through `completed()`.
Results are residual-checked estimates, not proofs; verify independently using alternative evaluations such as power series, asymptotics, and interlacing.
Airy, Kelvin, error-function, and Fresnel zeros use the same `special_zeros_with_options` with different families.
