Particles confined in finite-depth wells, such as semiconductor quantum wells, have discrete energies (levels).
Place a width-$L$ well at $|x|<L/2$ with potential zero inside and $V_0>0$ outside.
Assume the same effective mass $m^*$ inside and outside ($m^*=0.067\,m_e$ for GaAs conduction electrons).
The stationary Schrödinger equation is

$$
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m^*}\psi''(x)+V(x)\psi(x)=E\psi(x)
$$

with bound states satisfying $0<E<V_0$.
Wavefunctions decay exponentially outside and are trigonometric inside.
Reflection symmetry separates even and odd states.

## Matching and removing poles

Using interior wavenumber $k=\sqrt{2m^*E}/\hbar$, set $z=kL/2$, $z_0=(L/2)\sqrt{2m^*V_0}/\hbar$, and $q=\sqrt{z_0^2-z^2}$.
$q$ is external decay constant $\kappa=\sqrt{2m^*(V_0-E)}/\hbar$ times $L/2$.
Continuity of $\psi$ and $\psi'$ at well edges gives $z\tan z=q$ for even states and $-z\cot z=q$ for odd states.
Direct sign searches in these forms can mistake sign changes across $\tan$ or $\cot$ poles for roots.
For root finding multiply by $\cos z$ or $\sin z$, using pole-free equations.

$$
f_{\rm even}(z)=z\sin z-q\cos z,\qquad
f_{\rm odd}(z)=z\cos z+q\sin z
$$

Even solutions lie in intervals $\tan z\ge0$, odd solutions in $\cot z\le0$.
Define interval $k$ as $[k\pi/2,\min((k+1)\pi/2,z_0)]$ ($k=0,1,\dots$), solving even states for even $k$ and odd states for odd $k$.
Endpoint $f$ signs are opposite in each interval.
Furthermore, $z\tan z-q$ (or $-z\cot z-q$) is strictly increasing inside; $f$ multiplies it by fixed-sign $\cos z$ (or $-\sin z$), giving exactly one bound state per interval.
Thus the bound-state count is $\lceil2z_0/\pi\rceil$.

Solutions at $z=z_0$ have zero decay constant and cannot normalize, so are excluded.
Crossing just above depths where $2z_0/\pi$ is integer creates a new state at $E\approx V_0$.
The odd equation's zero at origin $z=0$ is trivial $\psi\equiv0$; odd intervals begin at $z=\pi/2$.

Compute energies with $E=V_0(z/z_0)^2$.
Deepening wells gives $z\to n\pi/2$ ($n=k+1$), approaching infinite-well values:

$$
E_n^\infty=\frac{\hbar^2\pi^2n^2}{2m^*L^2}
$$

For low deep-well levels $q\approx z_0$; near $z=n\pi/2$ approximate $\tan z\approx-1/(z-n\pi/2)$ for even states, giving $z_n\approx(n\pi/2)\,z_0/(z_0+1)$.
Odd states have the same approximation in $-\cot z$.
Consequently $E_n/E_n^\infty\approx[z_0/(z_0+1)]^2$, and relative infinite-well discrepancies shrink slowly as $2/z_0\propto V_0^{-1/2}$.

The following block provides definitions shared by three cells.
`HBAR2_OVER_2ME` gives conversion constant $\hbar^2/(2m_e)$; `matching_condition` gives $f_{\rm even}$/$f_{\rm odd}$; `solve_level` solves interval $k$.
The conversion rounds CODATA value $\hbar^2/(2m_e)=0.0380998212\,\mathrm{eV\,nm^2}$.

```rust
//| id: rootfind-finite-well-model
//| kind: definitions
//| title: "Pole-free matching and bracketed roots"
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-rootfind]
use scioxide_core::{SciOxideError, Interval, RealScalar};
use scioxide_rootfind::{Brent, RootResult, ScalarFn, ScalarRootOptions, ScalarRootSolver};

/// ħ²/(2mₑ) [eV nm²].
pub(crate) const HBAR2_OVER_2ME: f64 = 0.0380998212;

/// Interval k matching: even k uses f_even = z sin z − q cos z; odd k f_odd = z cos z + q sin z.
pub(crate) fn matching_condition(z: f64, z0: f64, k: usize) -> f64 {
    // Compute q = √(z₀² − z²) from (z₀ − z)(z₀ + z), avoiding cancellation as z → z₀
    let q = RealScalar::sqrt((z0 - z) * (z0 + z));
    let (sin, cos) = RealScalar::sin_cos(z);
    if k % 2 == 0 {
        z * sin - q * cos
    } else {
        z * cos + q * sin
    }
}

/// Solve [kπ/2, min((k+1)π/2, z₀)] with default Brent.
pub(crate) fn solve_level(z0: f64, k: usize) -> Result<RootResult<f64>, SciOxideError> {
    let lower = k as f64 * core::f64::consts::FRAC_PI_2;
    let upper = RealScalar::min((k + 1) as f64 * core::f64::consts::FRAC_PI_2, z0);
    let condition = ScalarFn::new(|z: f64| Ok(matching_condition(z, z0, k)));
    Brent.solve(&condition, Interval::new(lower, upper)?, &ScalarRootOptions::default())
}
```

## Bracketed levels and convergence to infinite wells

The following cell inputs depth $V_0$ [eV], width $L$ [nm], and effective-mass ratio $m^*/m_e$, solving each interval with `Brent`.
Default `ScalarRootOptions` acceptance stops at widths of a few ulp or exactly zero function values.

First table: roots $z$, termination, final widths, function counts.
Second: energies, margins to threshold $V_0-E$, and infinite-well ratios.
The third fixes $L$ and $m^*$, multiplying depths by 1, 4, 16, …, 1024 to compare ground-state $E_1/E_1^\infty$ with approximation $[z_0/(z_0+1)]^2$.
The final outcome is satisfied if every level has `is_success()`.
This cell exports well parameters, $z_0$, roots, and energies for two later cells.

All three stop at width criterion `BracketTolerance`, with widths at most $4.4\times10^{-16}$ and 8–10 evaluations.
Energies are 34.2, 132.6, and 271.5 meV, respectively 0.61, 0.59, and 0.54 times infinite-well values.
Higher levels approach threshold (third margin $V_0-E=28.5$ meV) and penetrate farther outside, reducing infinite-well ratios.

In the depth table, each quadrupling of $V_0$ doubles $z_0$, roughly halving $1-E_1/E_1^\infty$ from 0.39 to 0.017.

At $V_0=1$ eV, five levels exist and exact-zero function values stop the first two with `ResidualTolerance`.
At $V_0=0.02$ eV, $z_0=0.94<\pi/2$ leaves only the even ground state.

## Finite-difference Hamiltonian checks

Independently discretize the Schrödinger equation with centered differences on a grid of spacing $h$, then compute eigenvalues of the symmetric tridiagonal matrix.
The domain is centered on the well with approximate full width $wL$ ($w=4$ or 8), setting $\psi=0$ immediately outside the first and last points.
Offset grid points $x_i$ by $h/2$ so edges $\pm L/2$ fall midway between points.
Matrix diagonals are $2c/h^2+V(x_i)$ and adjacent entries $-c/h^2$ ($c=\hbar^2/2m^*$).

This approximation has two errors.
First, grid errors: centered truncation is $O(h^2)$.
Midpoint potential steps give observed orders near two in this example despite discontinuities (checked below).
Second, finite-domain truncation shifts eigenvalues upward unless the domain is wide relative to exterior decay length $1/\kappa=L/(2q)$.
This does not decrease with smaller $h$; first change domain width from fourfold to eightfold and verify unchanged values, then refine grids.

Using first-cell levels as references, the next cell shows errors at $h=L/N$ and $h/2$ and observed order $p=\log_2(e_h/e_{h/2})$.
$N$ is grid points per well width.
Negative error ratios (errors changing sign between $h$ and $h/2$) make observed order undefined, displayed blank.
Compute at most six lowest eigenvalues with scioxide-linalg's `HermitianEigenvaluesResult::tridiagonal_with_options`.

use scioxide_core::RealScalar;
use scioxide_linalg::{EigenIndexRange, HermitianEigenOptions, HermitianEigenvaluesResult};

let read = |name: &str| levels[name].as_f64().ok_or(format!("Missing {name}"));
let (depth, width, kinetic) = (read("depth")?, read("width")?, read("kinetic")?);
let reference: Vec<f64> =
    serde_json::from_value(levels["energies"].clone()).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let extent: usize = if extent == "4" { 4 } else { 8 };
let grid = grid as usize;
// Compare up to six lowest levels
let count = reference.len().min(6);
// Independent check: centered-difference eigenvalues [eV] on full domain wL with per_width points per well width L
let finite_difference = |per_width: usize| -> Result<Vec<f64>, String> {
    let n = extent * per_width;
    let h = width / per_width as f64;
    let t = kinetic / (h * h);
    // x_i = (i + 1/2 − n/2) h puts edges ±L/2 between grid points
    let diagonal: Vec<_> = (0..n)
        .map(|i| {
            let x = (i as f64 + 0.5 - n as f64 / 2.0) * h;
            let inside = -0.5 * width < x && x < 0.5 * width;
            let potential = if inside { 0.0 } else { depth };
            2.0 * t + potential
        })
        .collect();
    let off_diagonal = vec![-t; n - 1];
    let range = EigenIndexRange::new(0, count).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let eigen_options = HermitianEigenOptions::default().with_index_range(range);
    let result = HermitianEigenvaluesResult::tridiagonal_with_options(
        &diagonal,
        &off_diagonal,
        &eigen_options,
    )
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    Ok(result.eigenvalues().to_vec())
};
let coarse = finite_difference(grid)?;
let fine = finite_difference(2 * grid)?;
let mut rows = Vec::new();
for k in 0..count {
    let error_h = coarse[k] - reference[k];
    let error_half = fine[k] - reference[k];
    // Define observed order only for positive error ratios
    let ratio = error_h / error_half;
    let order = (ratio > 0.0).then(|| RealScalar::log2(ratio));
    rows.push((
        k + 1,
        reference[k] * 1e3,
        coarse[k] * 1e3,
        fine[k] * 1e3,
        error_h * 1e3,
        error_half * 1e3,
        order,
    ));
}

let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let fixed2 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 2 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Level n [-]", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Root-found E [meV]", "type": "number", "format": fixed3 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Differences h [meV]", "type": "number", "format": fixed3 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Differences h/2 [meV]", "type": "number", "format": fixed3 },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Error e_h [meV]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Error e_h/2 [meV]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 },
    { "key": "6", "label": "Observed order p [-]", "type": "number", "format": fixed2, "nullText": "Undefined" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(columns, rows, metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "orders" }));
```

At defaults $N=32$ and $w=4$, first/second errors are $-0.10$/$-0.44$ meV at $h$ and $-0.025$/$-0.11$ meV at $h/2$, giving order 2.00.
Difference eigenvalues lie below root-found values, approaching from below under refinement.
Centered differences underestimate second-derivative magnitudes of $\sin$ waves as $(2-2\cos kh)/h^2<k^2$, shifting eigenvalues downward.
Third-level order 2.45 differs from two, but becomes 2.01 with $w=8$.
This near-threshold level decays slowly, so full width $4L$ leaves nonnegligible truncation.
At $N=64$ and $w=4$, third-level errors change sign between $h$ and $h/2$, leaving order undefined.

Changing the first cell to shallow depth $V_0=0.02$ eV gives $w=4$ error $+0.32$ meV independently of $h$, with order near zero.
Difference eigenvalues exceed root-found values because boundary $\psi=0$ confines the state; at $w=8$, error falls to order $10^{-3}$ meV.
The sole level at $L=1$ nm and $V_0=0.3$ eV has decay length about 4 nm; even $w=8$ is insufficient, leaving error 37 meV.
When orders depart from two, question domain width before grid resolution.

## Graphical solution

Plot matching as intersections of left sides $z\tan z$ (even)/$-z\cot z$ (odd) and right side $q=\sqrt{z_0^2-z^2}$ versus $z$.
The right side is a quarter-circle of radius $z_0$; increasing $z_0$ (deeper/wider wells or heavier particles) enlarges it and creates intersections.
The next cell receives $z_0$ and roots from the first, plotting interval branches solid, the circular arc dashed, and roots as markers.
Separate branches into series by interval to avoid lines across poles.

//|       - {"artifact":"graphical","path":"/spec/layers/4/id","equals":"roots"}
//|       - {"artifact":"graphical","path":"/spec/layers/4/points/0/0","range":{"min":0,"max":1.5708}}
//|       - {"artifact":"graphical","path":"/spec/layers/4/points/1/0","range":{"min":1.5707,"max":3.1416}}
//|       - {"artifact":"graphical","path":"/spec/layers/4/points/2/0","range":{"min":3.1415,"max":3.6317}}
use core::f64::consts::FRAC_PI_2;
use scioxide_core::RealScalar;

let z0 = levels["z0"].as_f64().ok_or("Missing z0")?;
let roots: Vec<f64> = serde_json::from_value(levels["roots"].clone()).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let circle = |z: f64| RealScalar::sqrt((z0 - z) * (z0 + z));
let mut layers = Vec::new();
for k in 0..roots.len() {
    let left = k as f64 * FRAC_PI_2;
    let right = RealScalar::min((k + 1) as f64 * FRAC_PI_2, z0);
    let even = k % 2 == 0;
    // Left branches: even z tan z, odd −z cot z. Plot only interval interiors
    let branch: Vec<_> = (1..60)
        .map(|j| {
            let z = left + (right - left) * j as f64 / 60.0;
            let tan = RealScalar::tan(z);
            [z, if even { z * tan } else { -z / tan }]
        })
        .collect();
    let name = format!("{}-state branch n = {}", if even { "Even" } else { "Odd" }, k + 1);
    layers.push(serde_json::json!({
        "id": format!("branch-{k}"),
        "kind": "line",
        "name": name,
        "points": branch
    }));
}
let arc: Vec<_> = (0..=120)
    .map(|j| {
        let z = z0 * j as f64 / 120.0;
        [z, circle(z)]
    })
    .collect();
let marks: Vec<_> = roots.iter().map(|&z| [z, circle(z)]).collect();
layers.push(serde_json::json!({
    "id": "arc",
    "kind": "line",
    "name": "Right side √(z₀² − z²)",
    "style": { "lineType": "dashed" },
    "points": arc
}));
layers.push(serde_json::json!({
    "id": "roots",
    "kind": "scatter",
    "name": "Computed roots",
    "points": marks
}));
emit_chart!(
    serde_json::json!({
        "kind": "xy",
        "xLabel": "z = kL/2 [-]",
        "yLabel": "Matching-condition sides [-]",
        "xMin": 0.0,
        "xMax": z0 * 1.05,
        "yMin": 0.0,
        "yMax": z0 * 1.1,
        "layers": layers
    }),
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "graphical" })
);
```

At defaults the arc intersects three branches, with root markers at intersections.
First even branch diverges at $z\to\pi/2$; second odd branch starts at zero at $z=\pi/2$ and diverges at $z\to\pi$.
The third is cut off at $z_0=3.63<3\pi/2$ and intersects the arc before its pole.
Lowering first-cell $V_0$ to $z_0<\pi/2$ makes the arc intersect only the first branch, leaving the even ground state.
The first branch starts at zero at $z=0$ and then diverges, always intersecting the arc for $z_0>0$.
Thus one-dimensional symmetric wells always have at least one even ground state, however shallow.

## Summary

Multiplying away $\tan$/$\cot$ poles gives matching $f_{\rm even}$/$f_{\rm odd}$ with exactly one sign change per interval $[k\pi/2,\min((k+1)\pi/2,z_0)]$.
Construct $\lceil2z_0/\pi\rceil$ bound-state brackets and solve with `Brent`, avoiding missing or duplicate roots.
Relative infinite-well discrepancies shrink only as approximately $2/z_0\propto V_0^{-1/2}$, making infinite formulas coarse even for deep wells.
For difference-eigenvalue checks, first verify stable values under domain widening, then observed order near two between $h$ and $h/2$.
