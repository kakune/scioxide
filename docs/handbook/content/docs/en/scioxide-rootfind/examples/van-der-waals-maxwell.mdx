Dividing by critical temperature $T_c$, pressure $p_c$, and volume $V_c$ gives the substance-independent reduced van der Waals equation:

$$
p(V,T)=\frac{8T}{3V-1}-\frac3{V^2},\qquad V>\frac13
$$

Every $p,T,V$ on this page denotes dimensionless reduced quantities.
Isotherms at $T<1$ have mechanically unstable intervals $\partial p/\partial V>0$ (compression lowers pressure), replaced at equilibrium by liquid–gas coexistence.
Maxwell's equal-area rule determines coexistence pressure (saturation vapor pressure) $p_{\rm sat}(T)$.
CO₂ conversion uses the same $T_c=304.13\,\mathrm K$ and $p_c=7.3773\,\mathrm{MPa}$ as differentiation and AD examples.

## Volume cubic

Fix pressure $p>0$ and clear denominators to obtain:

$$
3pV^3-(p+8T)V^2+9V-3=0
$$

For $0<V<1/3$ or $V<0$, $p(V,T)<0$, and neither $V=0$ nor $V=1/3$ is a root of the cubic; thus all real roots for $p>0$ lie in physical domain $V>1/3$.
With three real roots, sort them as $V_l<V_m<V_g$.
Use extreme roots $V_l$ and $V_g$ for phases, excluding intermediate unstable $V_m$ from coexistence volumes.

Pressure ranges with three real roots are bounded by isotherm extrema (spinodal points).
Their volumes are two $V>1/3$ roots of $\partial p/\partial V=0$, equivalently $4TV^3=(3V-1)^2$; small volume gives the minimum, large volume the maximum.
At $T<27/32$, minimum pressure is negative, so use only positive-pressure range $(0,p_{\max})$.
At extrema, two roots coalesce, increasing conditioning.

The following block provides definitions shared by three cells.
`isotherm` gives $p(V,T)$; `cubic_coefficients` cubic coefficients; `volumes` three volumes at $p$; `equal_area_residual` next-section $G(p)$; `saturation_pressure` nested Maxwell-pressure solves.
Coefficients use ascending order $[-3,\,9,\,-(p+8T),\,3p]$.
`volumes` errors unless `PolynomialRootReport` succeeds.

```rust
//| id: rootfind-maxwell-model
//| kind: definitions
//| title: "Reduced van der Waals equation and nested root finding"
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-rootfind]
use scioxide_core::{SciOxideError, Interval, NumericScalar, RealScalar, ResidualTolerance};
use scioxide_rootfind::{
    Brent, PolynomialRootOptions, RootResult, ScalarFn, ScalarRootOptions, ScalarRootSolver,
    polynomial_roots_with_options,
};

/// Reduced equation p(V, T) = 8T/(3V − 1) − 3/V².
pub(crate) fn isotherm(v: f64, t: f64) -> f64 {
    8.0 * t / (3.0 * v - 1.0) - 3.0 / (v * v)
}

/// Return ascending coefficients of 3pV³ − (p + 8T)V² + 9V − 3.
pub(crate) fn cubic_coefficients(p: f64, t: f64) -> [f64; 4] {
    [-3.0, 9.0, -(p + 8.0 * t), 3.0 * p]
}

/// Return root real parts at p in ascending order, plus maximum |Im V| and conditioning estimates.
pub(crate) fn volumes(p: f64, t: f64) -> Result<([f64; 3], f64, Option<f64>), SciOxideError> {
    let options = PolynomialRootOptions::default();
    let roots = polynomial_roots_with_options(&cubic_coefficients(p, t), &options)?;
    let report = roots.report();
    if !report.is_success() {
        return Err(SciOxideError::numerical_instability(
            scioxide_core::NumericalIssue::Breakdown,
            "Cubic roots failed backward-error checks",
        ));
    }
    let z = roots.roots();
    let mut v = [z[0].re, z[1].re, z[2].re];
    v.sort_by(f64::total_cmp);
    let imaginary = z
        .iter()
        .fold(0.0, |m, z| RealScalar::max(m, NumericScalar::abs(z.im)));
    Ok((v, imaginary, report.max_root_condition().value()))
}

/// Equal-area residual G(p) from antiderivatives.
pub(crate) fn equal_area_residual(p: f64, t: f64) -> Result<f64, SciOxideError> {
    let ([liquid, _, gas], _, _) = volumes(p, t)?;
    Ok(
        8.0 * t / 3.0 * RealScalar::ln((3.0 * gas - 1.0) / (3.0 * liquid - 1.0))
            + 3.0 * (1.0 / gas - 1.0 / liquid)
            - p * (gas - liquid),
    )
}

/// Use Brent with |G| ≤ 10⁻¹² on a bracket inset by 1% of pressure-extrema width.
pub(crate) fn saturation_pressure(t: f64) -> Result<RootResult<f64>, SciOxideError> {
    let options =
        ScalarRootOptions::default().with_residual_tolerance(ResidualTolerance::new(1e-12, 0.0)?);
    // Extremum volumes: roots of 4TV³ = (3V − 1)² on [1/3, 1] and [1, 20]
    let extrema =
        ScalarFn::new(|v: f64| Ok(4.0 * t * v * v * v - RealScalar::powi(3.0 * v - 1.0, 2)));
    let mut bounds = [0.0; 2];
    for (bound, (a, b)) in bounds.iter_mut().zip([(1.0 / 3.0, 1.0), (1.0, 20.0)]) {
        let extremum = Brent.solve(&extrema, Interval::new(a, b)?, &options)?;
        *bound = isotherm(extremum.point(), t);
    }
    let lower = RealScalar::max(bounds[0], 0.0);
    let margin = 0.01 * (bounds[1] - lower);
    let equation = ScalarFn::new(|p: f64| equal_area_residual(p, t));
    Brent.solve(&equation, Interval::new(lower + margin, bounds[1] - margin)?, &options)
}
```

## All-root computation and PolynomialRootReport

The following cell inputs reduced temperature $T$ and pressure $p$, finding all three roots with `polynomial_roots_with_options`.
Per-root tables give real/imaginary parts, independently computed state-equation $p(V,T)-p$ for real roots, and `PolynomialRootDiagnostic` backward-error and condition-number estimates.
Backward error $\eta(z)$ is the smallest componentwise relative coefficient perturbation making $z$ an exact root.
Condition estimates are first-order absolute conditioning; root errors are approximately conditioning times backward error.
The report table gives `PolynomialRootReport` termination, actual algorithms, and maximum backward/conditioning values.
The isotherm chart marks selected pressure horizontally and real roots vertically.
The outcome uses report `is_success()`, meaning all roots passed backward checks.

## Equal-area equation

Maxwell expresses equal liquid/gas chemical potentials (molar Gibbs energies) through the isotherm integral:

$$
G(p)=\int_{V_l(p)}^{V_g(p)}\bigl[p(V,T)-p\bigr]\,dV=0
$$

The isotherm lies below horizontal $p$ over $[V_l,V_m]$ and above over $[V_m,V_g]$, so both component areas must match.
Using antiderivatives gives:

$$
G(p)=\frac{8T}{3}\ln\frac{3V_g-1}{3V_l-1}
+3\left(\frac1{V_g}-\frac1{V_l}\right)-p(V_g-V_l)
$$

The integrand vanishes at both endpoints, so endpoint movement contributes nothing to $G$ derivatives, giving $G'(p)=-(V_g-V_l)<0$.
Thus $G$ strictly decreases over three-real-root pressures; opposite endpoint $G$ signs give unique equal-area pressure.
This relies on $V_l$/$V_g$ being smallest/largest roots, so sort the roots of the cubic first.

## Nested roots and adaptive-quadrature checks

The next cell inputs reduced $T$ and computes Maxwell $p_{\rm sat}$ through nested `saturation_pressure` above.
First find the two roots of $4TV^3-(3V-1)^2$ with `Brent` to determine the pressure range between the extrema, passing outer `Brent` a bracket inset by 1% of its width from each end.
Endpoints themselves have repeated roots and inaccurate inner cubic roots.
When $T<27/32$ sets the lower end to $p=0$, insetting also avoids zero leading cubic coefficient $3p$ and diverging gas roots.
Each outer evaluation solves the cubic with `polynomial_roots_with_options`, computing $G(p)$ using the antiderivative only when `PolynomialRootReport` succeeds.
Outer tolerance is $|G|\le10^{-12}$.

Independent checks use scioxide-integrate adaptive Gauss–Kronrod without antiderivatives, separately integrating $[V_l,V_m]$ and $[V_m,V_g]$.
Explicit quadrature tolerances are absolute $10^{-14}$ and relative $10^{-13}$.
Default `SubdivisionOptions` uses both $\sqrt\varepsilon\approx1.5\times10^{-8}$, insufficient to check equal areas at $10^{-12}$.
The table gives integrals, estimated errors, evaluations, terminations, and their sum alongside $G(p_{\rm sat})$ computed using the antiderivative.
Quadrature error estimates are internal estimates, not certified bounds.
The outcome uses `is_success()` of outer `Brent`, exporting temperature and coexistence to the next cell.

## Coexistence curves and criticality

At critical point $T\to1$, both volumes approach 1 and the three roots of the cubic coalesce at $V=1$.
Conditioning grows, turning small coefficient errors into large volume errors.
Tightening only outer pressure tolerance does not reduce inner-root errors.
The coexistence tangent at criticality satisfies $dp_{\rm sat}/dT\to4$.
This equals $\partial p/\partial T=8/(3V-1)=4$ at critical volume $V=1$.

The next cell computes coexistence at 23 $T=0.55,0.57,\dots,0.99$ points and plots curves, marking critical $(V,T)=(1,1)$ separately.
Each temperature's $p_{\rm sat}$ uses `saturation_pressure`; rejected temperatures return errors.
Compute critical secant slope $(1-p_{\rm sat}(1-\delta))/\delta$ at $\delta=0.02,0.01,0.005,0.0025$ and compare theoretical 4.
As $\delta\to0$, secants approach tangents, but finite $\delta$ retains curvature bias.
Prior exported temperatures/volumes appear as horizontal lines and markers, converted to CO₂ temperature/pressure.

## Summary

Volume-cubic real roots at $p>0$ all lie in $V>1/3$; `polynomial_roots_with_options` obtains liquid, unstable, and gas volumes together.
Maxwell pressure is unique by $G'(p)=-(V_g-V_l)<0$; outer `Brent` slightly inside extremum pressures avoids inner near-multiplicity.
Outer acceptance thresholds remain as area discrepancies; use explicit quadrature tolerances resolving the same digits and check both areas separately.
Near criticality, monitor worsening inner accuracy through `PolynomialRootReport::max_root_condition` as conditioning grows.
