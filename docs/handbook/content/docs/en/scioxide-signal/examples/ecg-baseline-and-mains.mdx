An electrocardiogram (ECG) measures the potential from the heart muscle's electrical activity at the body surface, with an amplitude of about 1 mV.
The record includes slow baseline wander below 1 Hz from breathing and body motion, a 50 Hz sinusoid from mains power (60 Hz in some regions), and broadband noise from muscle activity.
The ST segment used in diagnosis (between the S and T waves) contains low-frequency components, so phase distortion by the high-pass filter that removes baseline wander changes the waveform's shape.
This page adds these three noises to a synthetic ECG and applies an IIR cascade of second-order sections with zero phase to check waveform error and R-wave timing.

The APIs used are `design_notch_peak` for notch design, `design_iir` for Butterworth design, causal `filter` and forward–backward `forward_backward` processing of `SecondOrderSections`, `detrend` for detrending, and `find_peaks_with_options` for peak detection.
Design theory is covered in [IIR filter design](../../algorithms/iir-design/), and execution and endpoint handling in [IIR filtering and zero phase](../../algorithms/iir-filtering-and-zero-phase/).

## Synthetic ECG

Use a sampling rate of $f_s = 500$ Hz and a 10 s record (5000 samples).
The heart rate is 72 beats/min, with R waves at $t_j = 0.4 + j \cdot (60/72)$ s ($j = 0, \dots, 11$).
Represent each beat as a sum of five Gaussian functions with offsets $\mu$ from the R-wave time, amplitudes $a$, and widths $\sigma$:

$$
s(t) = \sum_{j} \sum_{w} a_w \exp\!\left(-\frac{(t - t_j - \mu_w)^2}{2\sigma_w^2}\right)
$$


The parameters below define the model for this page; they do not reproduce a real recording.

| Wave | Amplitude $a$ [mV] | Offset $\mu$ [s] | Width $\sigma$ [s] |
|---|---|---|---|
| P | 0.15 | −0.200 | 0.025 |
| Q | −0.10 | −0.025 | 0.008 |
| R | 1.20 | 0 | 0.010 |
| S | −0.25 | 0.025 | 0.008 |
| T | 0.30 | 0.250 | 0.040 |

Add the following three components to obtain the recorded signal.

- Baseline wander: $b \sin(2\pi \cdot 0.3\,t + 0.7)$ (models a respiratory period of 3.3 s)
- Mains interference: $m \sin(2\pi \cdot 50\,t)$
- EMG noise: white Gaussian noise with standard deviation $\sigma_n$ (change the sequence with the random seed)

## Filter configuration

Apply three cascaded filters to address the three noises.
Pass frequencies to the design functions as ratios to the Nyquist frequency of 250 Hz.

- 50 Hz notch: `design_notch_peak(0.2, Q, ResonanceKind::Notch)`.
  Its −3 dB bandwidth is $50/Q$ Hz; a larger $Q$ preserves more ECG components near 50 Hz.
- High-pass filter for baseline wander: second-order Butterworth (`IirBand::Highpass`).
  The default cutoff is 0.5 Hz, below the 1.2 Hz heartbeat fundamental and above the 0.3 Hz respiration frequency.
- Low-pass filter for EMG noise: fourth-order Butterworth (`IirBand::Lowpass`).
  The default cutoff is 40 Hz.

Combining the second-order-section rows from all three designs (1 + 1 + 2 = 4 rows) into one `SecondOrderSections` applies the entire cascade in a single call.
Causal `filter` has amplitude response $|H|$ and frequency-dependent group delay.
Forward–backward `forward_backward` has amplitude response $|H|^2$ and leaves phase unchanged.
With $|H|^2$, stopband attenuation doubles in dB and the gain at cutoff becomes $1/2$ (−6 dB).

Before filtering, subtract a least-squares line from the record with `detrend(record, DetrendKind::Linear)`.
This brings the endpoint values and slope closer to 0, reducing endpoint transients in forward–backward processing.

### Cascade frequency response

The next cell constructs the cascade from the notch $Q$ and the high-pass and low-pass cutoffs, and tabulates responses at key frequencies.
The table frequencies represent baseline wander (0.3 Hz), the heartbeat fundamental (1.2 Hz), the main QRS components (10 Hz), and mains power (50 Hz).
It shows the causal amplitude $|H|$ and group delay [ms], and the forward–backward amplitude $|H|^2$.
As an independent check, evaluate the closed-form notch expression using `f64` complex arithmetic and report its difference from `SecondOrderSections::response`.
Group delay is undefined at the notch zero at 50 Hz, so leave it blank.
This cell passes the cascade rows to the next cell.

```rust
//| id: signal-ecg-filter-response
//| ref: signal-ecg-filter-response
//| title: "Frequency response of the cascade for ECG preprocessing"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-signal]
//| exports: [rows]
//| inputs:
//|   quality: {"type":"range","label":"Notch Q factor [-]","description":"Ratio of 50 Hz to the −3 dB bandwidth.","min":5,"max":60,"step":5,"value":30}
//|   highpass_hz: {"type":"range","label":"High-pass cutoff [Hz]","description":"Second-order Butterworth filter for baseline wander.","min":0.1,"max":1.5,"step":0.1,"value":0.5}
//|   lowpass_hz: {"type":"range","label":"Low-pass cutoff [Hz]","description":"Fourth-order Butterworth filter for EMG noise.","min":20,"max":100,"step":5,"value":40}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"aggressive-highpass","label":"High-pass 1.5 Hz","description":"Bringing the cutoff closer to the heartbeat fundamental reduces the 1.2 Hz component.","inputs":{"highpass_hz":1.5}}
//|   - {"id":"wide-notch","label":"Q = 5","description":"The notch bandwidth becomes 10 Hz.","inputs":{"quality":5}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"response","path":"/rows/0/2","approx":{"value":0.1147,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"response","path":"/rows/1/2","approx":{"value":0.9707,"rtol":1e-4}}
//|       - {"artifact":"response","path":"/rows/2/3","approx":{"value":11.74,"atol":0.01}}
//|       - {"artifact":"response","path":"/rows/3/1","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"response","path":"/rows/3/3","equals":null}
//|       - {"artifact":"response","path":"/rows/0/5","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-13}}
//|       - {"artifact":"response","path":"/rows/2/5","range":{"min":0,"max":1e-13}}
//|   - case: aggressive-highpass
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"response","path":"/rows/0/2","approx":{"value":1.597e-3,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"response","path":"/rows/1/2","approx":{"value":0.2906,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|   - case: wide-notch
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"response","path":"/rows/2/4","approx":{"value":0.99907,"rtol":1e-4}}
use scioxide_core::Complex;
use scioxide_signal::{
    IirBand, IirDomain, IirFamily, ResonanceKind, SecondOrderSections, design_iir,
    design_notch_peak,
};

let nyquist = 250.0;
let center = 50.0 / nyquist;
let notch =
    design_notch_peak(center, quality, ResonanceKind::Notch).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let butterworth = |order, band| {
    design_iir(order, IirFamily::Butterworth, band, IirDomain::Digital)
        .and_then(|design| design.to_sos())
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())
};
let highpass = butterworth(2, IirBand::Highpass(highpass_hz / nyquist))?;
let lowpass = butterworth(4, IirBand::Lowpass(lowpass_hz / nyquist))?;
// Combine the rows of the three designs into one cascade
let rows: Vec<[f64; 6]> = notch
    .sections()
    .iter()
    .chain(highpass.sections())
    .chain(lowpass.sections())
    .copied()
    .collect();
let cascade = SecondOrderSections::new(IirDomain::Digital, &rows).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;

// Independent check: closed-form notch g(1 − 2cos(πf₀)z⁻¹ + z⁻²)/(1 − 2g cos(πf₀)z⁻¹ + (2g − 1)z⁻²)
let pi = std::f64::consts::PI;
let gain = 1.0 / (1.0 + (0.5 * pi * center / quality).tan());
let c0 = (pi * center).cos();
let closed_notch = |f: f64| {
    let z1 = Complex::new((pi * f).cos(), -(pi * f).sin());
    let z2 = z1 * z1;
    let one = Complex::new(1.0, 0.0);
    let numerator = (one - z1 * Complex::new(2.0 * c0, 0.0) + z2) * Complex::new(gain, 0.0);
    let denominator = one - z1 * Complex::new(2.0 * gain * c0, 0.0)
        + z2 * Complex::new(2.0 * gain - 1.0, 0.0);
    (numerator / denominator).norm()
};

let mut rows_out = Vec::new();
for hz in [0.3, 1.2, 10.0, 50.0] {
    let f = hz / nyquist;
    let h = cascade.response(f).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?.norm();
    // Convert group delay [samples] to ms. Omit it at the notch zero (|H| ≈ 0), where it is undefined
    let delay = if h > 1e-8 {
        cascade.group_delay(f).ok().map(|d| 1000.0 * d / 500.0)
    } else {
        None
    };
    let notch_gain = notch.response(f).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?.norm();
    rows_out.push((
        hz,
        h,
        h * h,
        delay,
        notch_gain,
        (notch_gain - closed_notch(f)).abs(),
    ));
}
let sci2 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 2 });
let fixed2 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 2 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Frequency [Hz]", "type": "number" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Causal |H| [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Zero-phase |H|² [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Group delay [ms]", "type": "number", "format": fixed2, "nullText": "Undefined" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Notch |H| [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Difference from closed form [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows_out,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "response" })
);

// Sample 0.05–245 Hz at logarithmically uniform intervals and plot |H|² (omit zero values)
let mut curve = Vec::new();
for i in 0..=400 {
    let hz = 0.05 * (245.0_f64 / 0.05).powf(i as f64 / 400.0);
    let power = cascade
        .response(hz / nyquist)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?
        .norm_sqr();
    if power > 0.0 {
        curve.push([hz, power]);
    }
}
emit_line_chart!(
    &vec![serde_json::json!({ "name": "Zero-phase |H|²", "points": curve })],
    options = &serde_json::json!({
        "xLabel": "Frequency [Hz]",
        "yLabel": "Squared amplitude |H|² [-]",
        "xType": "log",
        "yType": "log",
        "yMin": 1e-12,
        "yMax": 2.0,
        "annotations": [
            { "kind": "line", "axis": "x", "value": 0.3, "label": "Baseline wander" },
            { "kind": "line", "axis": "x", "value": 50.0, "label": "Mains power" }
        ]
    })
);
export!(rows, &rows);
```

At the defaults $Q = 30$, high-pass 0.5 Hz, and low-pass 40 Hz, the zero-phase $|H|^2$ is 0.115 at 0.3 Hz, 0.971 at 1.2 Hz, and 0.99993 at 10 Hz.
Baseline wander is reduced to about one ninth, but is not eliminated.
At 50 Hz, $|H|$ is $1.4 \times 10^{-15}$, with the notch zero exactly at the mains frequency.
The difference from the closed-form notch expression is at most $4 \times 10^{-15}$ at every frequency.

Causal group delay varies strongly with frequency: 552 ms at 0.3 Hz, 99 ms at 1.2 Hz, and 11.7 ms at 10 Hz.
Lower frequencies are delayed more, disrupting the relative positions of the P, QRS, and T waves.
Raising the high-pass cutoff to 1.5 Hz reduces $|H|^2$ at 0.3 Hz to $1.6 \times 10^{-3}$, but also scales the 1.2 Hz heartbeat fundamental by 0.29, changing the waveform.
Setting $Q = 5$ widens the notch bandwidth to 10 Hz; even at 10 Hz the notch alone reduces the gain to $|H| = 0.99907$.

## Time-domain processing and checks

The next cell processes the synthetic record with the cascade from the previous cell.
Inputs are the random seed, baseline-wander amplitude $b$, mains-interference amplitude $m$, EMG-noise standard deviation $\sigma_n$, and whether to use zero phase.
There are three checks.

- Compare RMS errors against the noise-free ECG (with its mean subtracted) before and after processing.
  Measure over 1–9 s to exclude endpoint transients.
- Also show the RMS error against the noise-free ECG subjected to the same processing.
  This measures only residual noise, excluding waveform changes caused by the filter itself.
  Predict this value from the frequency response as an independent check.
  A sinusoid of amplitude $A$ leaves RMS $A|H|^p/\sqrt2$, and white noise leaves variance $\sigma_n^2 \int_0^1 |H(f)|^{2p}\, df$ ($p = 2$ for zero phase, $p = 1$ for causal processing; use the trapezoidal rule with 1000 intervals for integration).
- Detect R waves in the processed waveform with `find_peaks_with_options` (height at least 0.6 mV, spacing at least 200 samples), and report their shifts from the true R-wave times in ms.

The charts show the recorded, processed, and noise-free ECG waveforms over 2–4 s, and the processed error over the full record, with linked time axes.

```rust
//| id: signal-ecg-cleaning
//| ref: signal-ecg-cleaning
//| title: "Remove noise from a synthetic ECG and check R-wave timing"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-random, scioxide-signal]
//| dependsOn:
//|   filters: signal-ecg-filter-response
//| inputs:
//|   seed: {"type":"integer","label":"Random seed","description":"Change the EMG-noise sequence.","min":1,"max":9999,"value":11}
//|   baseline: {"type":"range","label":"Baseline-wander amplitude b [mV]","description":"Amplitude of the 0.3 Hz sinusoid.","min":0,"max":1.5,"step":0.1,"value":0.5}
//|   mains: {"type":"range","label":"Mains-interference amplitude m [mV]","description":"Amplitude of the 50 Hz sinusoid.","min":0,"max":0.5,"step":0.05,"value":0.2}
//|   noise: {"type":"range","label":"EMG noise σ_n [mV]","description":"Standard deviation of white Gaussian noise.","min":0.005,"max":0.1,"step":0.005,"value":0.02}
//|   zero_phase: {"type":"checkbox","label":"Zero phase (forward–backward)","description":"Clear to process with the causal filter.","value":true}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"causal","label":"Causal processing","description":"Group delay delays the R waves and increases error against the original ECG.","inputs":{"zero_phase":false}}
//|   - {"id":"strong-noise","label":"b = 1.5 mV, m = 0.5 mV, σ_n = 0.1 mV","description":"R-wave timing remains unchanged even with strong noise.","inputs":{"baseline":1.5,"mains":0.5,"noise":0.1,"seed":2024}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"cleaning","path":"/rows/0/0","approx":{"value":0.3796,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"cleaning","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":0.04255,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"cleaning","path":"/rows/0/2","approx":{"value":0.04188,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"cleaning","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":0.04125,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"cleaning","path":"/rows/0/4","equals":12}
//|       - {"artifact":"cleaning","path":"/rows/0/5","range":{"min":-1e-9,"max":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"cleaning","path":"/rows/0/6","range":{"min":0,"max":1.01}}
//|   - case: causal
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"cleaning","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":0.1824,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"cleaning","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":0.1200,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"cleaning","path":"/rows/0/5","approx":{"value":10.67,"atol":0.01}}
//|   - case: strong-noise
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"cleaning","path":"/rows/0/0","approx":{"value":1.113,"rtol":1e-3}}
//|       - {"artifact":"cleaning","path":"/rows/0/4","equals":12}
//|       - {"artifact":"cleaning","path":"/rows/0/5","range":{"min":-1e-9,"max":1e-9}}
use scioxide_core::Interval;
use scioxide_random::{Distribution, Normal, SeedableRng, SplitMix64};
use scioxide_signal::{
    DetrendKind, IirDomain, PeakOptions, SecondOrderSections, detrend, find_peaks_with_options,
};

let rows: Vec<[f64; 6]> =
    serde_json::from_value(filters["rows"].clone()).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let cascade = SecondOrderSections::new(IirDomain::Digital, &rows).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let (fs, n) = (500.0, 5000_usize);
let pi = std::f64::consts::PI;
// Synthetic ECG model (synthesized with f64 inherent methods). (Amplitude [mV], offset [s], width [s])
let waves = [
    (0.15, -0.200, 0.025),
    (-0.10, -0.025, 0.008),
    (1.20, 0.0, 0.010),
    (-0.25, 0.025, 0.008),
    (0.30, 0.250, 0.040),
];
let beats: Vec<f64> = (0..12).map(|j| 0.4 + j as f64 * 60.0 / 72.0).collect();
let ecg = |t: f64| -> f64 {
    let wave = |tr: f64| -> f64 {
        waves
            .iter()
            .map(|&(a, mu, s)| a * (-0.5 * ((t - tr - mu) / s).powi(2)).exp())
            .sum()
    };
    beats.iter().map(|&tr| wave(tr)).sum()
};
let normal = Normal::new(0.0, noise).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mut rng = SplitMix64::from_seed(seed as u64);
let clean: Vec<f64> = (0..n).map(|k| ecg(k as f64 / fs)).collect();
// Recorded signal = ECG + baseline wander + mains interference + EMG noise
let mut recorded = Vec::with_capacity(n);
for (k, &s) in clean.iter().enumerate() {
    let t = k as f64 / fs;
    let interference =
        baseline * (2.0 * pi * 0.3 * t + 0.7).sin() + mains * (2.0 * pi * 50.0 * t).sin();
    let emg = normal.sample(&mut rng).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    recorded.push(s + interference + emg);
}

// After detrending, apply the cascade forward–backward or causally
let process = |x: &[f64]| -> Result<Vec<f64>, String> {
    let detrended = detrend(x, DetrendKind::Linear).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let y = if zero_phase {
        cascade.forward_backward(&detrended)
    } else {
        cascade.filter(&detrended)
    };
    y.map_err(|e| e.to_string())
};
let output = process(&recorded)?;
let reference = process(&clean)?;
let mean = clean.iter().sum::<f64>() / n as f64;
let rms = |a: &[f64], b: &dyn Fn(usize) -> f64| -> f64 {
    let sum: f64 = (500..4500).map(|k| (a[k] - b(k)).powi(2)).sum();
    (sum / 4000.0).sqrt()
};
let before = rms(&recorded, &|k| clean[k] - mean);
let after = rms(&output, &|k| clean[k] - mean);
let residual = rms(&output, &|k| reference[k]);

// Independent check: predict residual-noise RMS from the frequency response.
// Sinusoid: amplitude × |H|^p / √2; white noise: σ_n² ∫₀¹ |H(f)|^(2p) df (p = 2 for zero phase)
let p = if zero_phase { 2 } else { 1 };
let gain = |hz: f64| -> Result<f64, String> {
    Ok(cascade
        .response(hz / 250.0)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?
        .norm()
        .powi(p))
};
let mut white = 0.0;
for i in 0..=1000 {
    let weight = if i == 0 || i == 1000 { 0.5 } else { 1.0 };
    let h = cascade
        .response(i as f64 / 1000.0)
        .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?
        .norm();
    white += weight * h.powi(2 * p) / 1000.0;
}
let predicted = ((baseline * gain(0.3)?).powi(2) / 2.0
    + (mains * gain(50.0)?).powi(2) / 2.0
    + noise * noise * white)
    .sqrt();

// Detect R waves and measure shifts from true times (nearest beat) [ms]
let options = PeakOptions::new()
    .with_height(Interval::new(0.6, 10.0).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?)
    .with_distance(200);
let peaks = find_peaks_with_options(&output, options).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let shifts: Vec<f64> = peaks
    .iter()
    .map(|p| {
        let t = p.index() as f64 / fs;
        let nearest = beats.iter().fold(f64::INFINITY, |m, &tr| {
            if (t - tr).abs() < m.abs() { t - tr } else { m }
        });
        1000.0 * nearest
    })
    .collect();
let mean_shift = shifts.iter().sum::<f64>() / shifts.len().max(1) as f64;
let max_shift = shifts.iter().fold(0.0_f64, |m, s| m.max(s.abs()));

let fixed3 = serde_json::json!({ "significantDigits": 3 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "RMS error before processing [mV]", "type": "number", "format": fixed3 },
    { "key": "1", "label": "RMS error after processing [mV]", "type": "number", "format": fixed3 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "RMS difference from identically processed ECG [mV]", "type": "number", "format": fixed3 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Predicted RMS difference [mV]", "type": "number", "format": fixed3 },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Detected R waves", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Mean R-wave shift [ms]", "type": "number", "format": fixed3 },
    { "key": "6", "label": "Maximum R-wave shift [ms]", "type": "number", "format": fixed3 }
]);
let row = (
    before,
    after,
    residual,
    predicted,
    peaks.len(),
    mean_shift,
    max_shift,
);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    vec![row],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "cleaning" })
);

// Waveforms over 2–4 s and full-record error
let window =
    |y: &[f64]| -> Vec<[f64; 2]> { (1000..2000).map(|k| [k as f64 / fs, y[k]]).collect() };
let clean_points: Vec<[f64; 2]> = (1000..2000)
    .map(|k| [k as f64 / fs, clean[k] - mean])
    .collect();
emit_line_chart!(
    &vec![
        serde_json::json!({ "name": "Recorded signal", "points": window(&recorded) }),
        serde_json::json!({ "name": "Processed", "points": window(&output) }),
        serde_json::json!({
            "name": "Noise-free ECG (mean subtracted)",
            "points": clean_points,
            "style": { "lineType": "dashed" }
        }),
    ],
    options = &serde_json::json!({
        "xLabel": "Time t [s]",
        "yLabel": "Potential [mV]",
        "link": { "group": "ecg-time" }
    })
);
let error: Vec<[f64; 2]> = (0..n)
    .step_by(2)
    .map(|k| [k as f64 / fs, output[k] - (clean[k] - mean)])
    .collect();
emit_line_chart!(
    &vec![serde_json::json!({ "name": "Processed − noise-free ECG", "points": error })],
    options = &serde_json::json!({
        "xLabel": "Time t [s]",
        "yLabel": "Error [mV]",
        "link": { "group": "ecg-time" }
    })
);
```

At the default inputs, the RMS error over 1–9 s decreases from 0.380 mV before processing to 0.0425 mV afterward.
The difference from the identically processed noise-free ECG is 0.0419 mV, indicating that the filter itself causes little waveform change.
This residual closely matches the 0.0413 mV predicted from the frequency response, and consists mainly of residual 0.3 Hz baseline wander (amplitude $0.5 \times 0.115$ mV).
The prediction is the steady-state response for an infinitely long record; it differs from the measurement because the 8 s interval contains only 2.4 cycles of baseline wander and because of noise-sample variability.
All 12 R waves are detected, with a mean timing shift of 0 ms and a maximum of 0.67 ms.
The true R-wave times (beat spacing 416.67 samples) do not lie on the sampling grid, so shifts smaller than the 2 ms sample interval arise from rounding to the grid.

Switching to causal processing increases the RMS error after processing to 0.182 mV.
The difference from the identically processed ECG is 0.118 mV (prediction 0.120 mV), mainly because a larger fraction $|H| = 0.339$ of the baseline wander remains.
The remaining difference is waveform delay and distortion due to group delay; R waves are delayed by a mean of 10.7 ms and a maximum of 11.3 ms.
This is close to the table's 11.7 ms group delay at 10 Hz and can be explained as delay of the main QRS components.
Even with stronger noise ($b = 1.5$ mV, $m = 0.5$ mV, $\sigma_n = 0.1$ mV), zero-phase processing gives a mean R-wave timing shift of 0 ms.

## Summary

ECG preprocessing can remove mains interference with a notch, baseline wander with a high-pass filter, and EMG noise with a low-pass filter, applying all three as one cascade of second-order sections.
When the full record is available, use `forward_backward` for zero phase to preserve R-wave timing and ST-segment shape.
The amplitude response becomes $|H|^2$, so account for cutoff attenuation and residual baseline wander during design.
Residual noise can be predicted from the frequency response; agreement between prediction and measurement checks the processing effect.
When only causal processing is possible, as in real-time monitoring, estimate group-delay latency using the method in the [group-delay example](../iir-group-delay-zero-phase/).
