When applying signal-processing functions to measured data, use them in a form that lets you check results through a separate computation.
Every example in this chapter places closed-form values, direct computations from definitions, or statistics from simulated observations with varying seeds on the same page and displays them alongside SciOxide results.
Changing inputs and observing how agreement changes also reveals where method assumptions fail.

| Example | Topic | APIs used | Independent check |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Checking smoothing, peaks, and outliers](smoothing-peaks-and-outliers/) | Gaussian-kernel smoothing, plateau peaks, spike removal in sensor sequences | `convolve`, `power_spectrum`, `find_peaks_with_options`, `LocalFilterPlan`, `LocalFilterStream` | Direct double-loop sums, Parseval's relation and sum preservation, manual prominence and width, batch/stream agreement |
| [Estimating a variable star's period](variable-star-period/) | Period and daily aliases of irregularly observed RR Lyrae variables ($P = 0.567$ days) | `lomb_scargle`, `LombScargle` | Comparison with the true period; approximate false-alarm probability and exceedance fractions in noise-only simulations, with standard errors |
| [Removing ECG baseline wander and mains noise](ecg-baseline-and-mains/) | Add 0.3 Hz baseline wander and 50 Hz mains noise to a synthetic ECG and remove them with notch and high-pass filters | `design_notch_peak`, `design_iir`, `SecondOrderSections`, `detrend`, `find_peaks_with_options` | Closed-form notch response, residual noise RMS predicted from frequency response, R-wave timing shifts |
| [IIR group delay and zero-phase filtering](iir-group-delay-zero-phase/) | Low-pass group delay and pulse distortion for Butterworth, Chebyshev, and Bessel filters | `design_iir`, `SecondOrderSections`, `group_delay`, `forward_backward` | Group delay from zeros and poles and numerical phase differentiation, output centroid shifts, zero-phase symmetry |
| [Chirp spectrograms and time–frequency uncertainty](chirp-spectrogram-uncertainty/) | Measure the time/frequency resolution tradeoff with window length in a linear-chirp STFT | `waveform`, `window`, `StftPlan` | Closed-form ridge width and optimal window width, theoretical instantaneous frequency, inverse reconstruction error |
| [Wavelet denoising and perfect reconstruction](wavelet-denoising/) | Shrink a noisy piecewise smooth test signal with a MAD-estimated universal threshold | `WaveletPlan`, `WaveletFamily` | Zero-threshold round-trip error, error improvement against the noise-free signal, means and standard errors over repeated seeds |
| [44.1 kHz and 48 kHz conversion and aliasing](audio-sample-rate-conversion/) | Passband error and alias suppression for resampling ratio 160/147 | `resample`, `PolyphasePlan`, `resample_periodic` | Theoretical sinusoid amplitude and phase, alias measurements, round-trip error |
| [Estimate seismic arrival-time differences by cross-correlation](seismic-delay-correlation/) | Estimate sub-sample delay between two stations with correlation and parabolic interpolation | `correlate`, `waveform` | Error against true delay, linearized error formula and standard deviation over repeated seeds |
| [An LTI model of an RLC circuit](rlc-circuit-lti/) | Series RLC Bode plots, step response, and discretization errors | `StateSpace`, `frequency_response`, `step_response`, `discretize` | Closed-form resonant magnitude and phase, step response, and overshoot |
| [Synthesize acoustic reflections by linear convolution with an impulse response](echo-convolution/) | Synthesize direct sound and multiple wall reflections using a prepared convolution plan | `ConvolutionNdPlan`, `convolve` | Direct weighted delay sums, energy formula from autocorrelation cross terms |
| [Generate analytic signals and recover instantaneous envelopes with the Hilbert transform](hilbert-envelope/) | Recover amplitude-modulated envelopes and inspect breakdown when carrier frequency falls below modulation frequency | `hilbert`, `envelope` | Closed-form analytic signal and theoretical envelope |

The mathematical pages also contain cells with independent checks.
For example, [Windows and DPSS](../algorithms/windows-and-dpss/) compares Hann gain and ENBW with closed forms, [FIR filter design and minimum phase](../algorithms/fir-design-and-minimum-phase/) counts Remez equiripples on a dense grid, and [State-space models and LTI systems](../algorithms/lti-systems/) measures discretization error order.

## Summary

Examples cover local processing (smoothing, peaks, outliers), period and spectrum estimation (variable stars, chirps), filter design and application (ECG, group delay), wavelet denoising, resampling, correlation-based delay estimation, LTI models, convolution synthesis, and envelope recovery.
Every example displays SciOxide computations alongside independent checks, letting you examine how agreement changes with inputs.
Begin with the example closest to your task, then check its mathematical methods in [Mathematics and algorithms](../algorithms/).
