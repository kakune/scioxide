For curved boundaries or complex domains, finite-element and finite-volume methods use simplex meshes of intervals, triangles, or tetrahedra instead of structured grids.
Connectivity is supplied through input vertex indices, allowing errors such as reversed orientations, degenerate cells, and overlapping faces.
Wrong orientations reverse face-flux signs, and degenerate cells make element-map determinants zero, so these must be detected before constructing a discretization.
This page explains orientation and connectivity conditions checked during `SimplexMesh` construction, face and edge numbering and orientation signs, boundary tags, and interval meshes.
[Foundations of points, ranges, grids, and meshes](../geometry-grids-and-meshes/) covers point types, field locations, and mesh tokens.

## Simplex-mesh orientations and boundary tags

`SimplexMesh<S, D>` is a mesh of intervals, triangles, or tetrahedra, with aliases `IntervalMesh`, `TriangleMesh`, and `TetrahedronMesh`.
Each cell is supplied as $D + 1$ vertex indices and must have positive orientation.
Interval $[a, b]$ requires $x_a < x_b$; triangle $[a, b, c]$ must be counterclockwise with `orient2d(a, b, c)` equal to `Positive`; tetrahedron $[a, b, c, d]$ requires $\det(b - a, c - a, d - a) > 0$.
This tetrahedral orientation makes `orient3d(a, b, c, d)` equal to `Negative` (see [Exact geometric predicates](../exact-predicates/) for sign conventions).
Orientation uses exact predicate signs rather than rounded areas or volumes, preventing rounding from confusing degeneracy (zero area) with clockwise orientation.

Construction (such as `TriangleMesh::new(points, cells)`) checks the following and returns `InvalidInput` on failure.
`from_coordinates(&coordinates, cells)` accepts a sequence of `[S; D]` coordinates instead of `Point`, rejects non-finite coordinates with `NonFinite`, then performs the same checks.

- Each cell's vertices are distinct and its orientation is positive.
- No cell occurs twice (equal vertex sets mean the same cell even with different ordering).
- At most two cells share a face, and those cells use it in opposite directions.
- No vertex is unused, and cells around each vertex form a manifold (in two dimensions, one disk or fan around the vertex).
  In three dimensions, cells around each edge must also form a ring around the edge or one chain from boundary to boundary.

A nonexistent vertex index gives `OutOfBounds`.
Global embedding is not checked, so self-overlapping meshes such as a fan winding twice around an interior vertex are accepted; summed cell measures count overlapped area.

Vertices and cells retain input order; faces and edges receive indices during construction and are stored with increasing vertex indices.
`cell_faces(c)` returns cell $c$'s faces as a sequence of `OrientedEntity`; face $i$ is opposite local vertex $i$.
`OrientedEntity` holds face index `index()` and sign `sign()`: $+1$ when the cell-induced orientation agrees with increasing-index orientation, or $-1$ when opposite.
Internal faces are used in opposite directions by their two cells, so their signs sum to 0.
These signs can be used directly to distribute finite-volume face fluxes with opposite signs or construct incidence matrices (discrete boundary operators).
`cell_edges` returns edges in local vertex-pair order $(0, 1), (0, 2), \ldots$; `face_cells`, `edge_cells`, and `vertex_cells` return incident cells in increasing order.
Connectivity tables are stored at construction, making each query $O(1)$ per result.

`boundary_faces()` returns faces belonging to only one cell in increasing order.
`SimplexBoundaryTags::new(&mesh, pairs)` assigns `u32` tags to boundary faces, allowing boundary conditions to be selected by face groups.
Tag values have no special meaning (0 is ordinary), and untagged boundary faces are allowed.
Tagging an internal or duplicate face gives `InvalidInput`; an out-of-range face gives `OutOfBounds`.
`tag(&mesh, face)` uses binary search among $t$ tags at cost $O(\log t)$; supplying a different mesh revision from that used for tags (including generation changes through `with_points` and sibling revisions from the same parent) returns `InvalidInput`.

For triangles and tetrahedra, `cell_measure` accumulates the edge-vector determinant exactly and rounds once, then divides by 2 for triangles or 6 for tetrahedra.
Coordinate differences are not rounded before multiplication, so cancellation does not lose area or volume digits even in thin cells.
Triangle areas in `f64` are correctly rounded unless subnormal; tetrahedron volumes incur one further rounding when divided by 6.
Interval length is a rounded coordinate difference.
A measure unrepresentable in the scalar type (overflowing or rounding to 0) gives `NumericalInstability`.

`with_points` replaces coordinates while preserving connectivity, rechecks only cell orientations, and advances the generation by one.
A changed point count gives `DimensionMismatch`; unchanged coordinates return a clone with the same token.
`with_cells` replaces connectivity while preserving coordinates, repeats construction checks, and advances the generation by one.

The next cell constructs a mesh with vertices $0 = (0, 0)$, $1 = (1, 0)$, $2 = (1, 1)$, $3 = (t, 1)$ [m] and triangles $[0, 1, 2]$, $[0, 2, 3]$.
As input $t$ approaches 1, triangle $[0, 2, 3]$'s area $(1 - t)/2$ approaches 0; at $t = 1$ it degenerates and at $t > 1$ it becomes clockwise.
The first table compares the sign of `orient2d` from $[0, 2, 3]$ with manually computed signed area $(1 - t)/2$ (an independent check), shows construction results, and compares total area with closed form $1 - t/2$.
The signed area's sign should agree with `orient2d`.
When construction is accepted, the second table shows every face (edge)'s vertices, incident cells, signs from each cell, boundary flag, and tag.
The bottom edge $0$–$1$ receives tag 1 and other boundary edges tag 2.
The third table uses fixed square $(0, 0), (1, 0), (1, 1), (0, 1)$ independently of $t$, showing inputs with one connectivity fault at a time and their results.

```rust
//| id: spatial-simplex-mesh-orientation
//| ref: spatial-simplex-mesh-orientation
//| title: "Triangle-mesh orientations, face signs, boundary tags, and rejected connectivity"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-spatial]
//| inputs:
//|   apex_x: {"type":"range","label":"Vertex 3 x coordinate t [m]","min":-1,"max":1.5,"step":0.25,"value":0}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"degenerate","label":"t = 1 (degenerate)","description":"Vertices 0, 2, and 3 are collinear; orient2d gives Zero and construction gives InvalidInput.","inputs":{"apex_x":1}}
//|   - {"id":"clockwise","label":"t = 1.5 (clockwise)","description":"Triangle [0, 2, 3] becomes clockwise and construction gives InvalidInput.","inputs":{"apex_x":1.5}}
//|   - {"id":"wide","label":"t = −1","description":"Total area increases to 1.5 m²; connectivity and signs stay unchanged.","inputs":{"apex_x":-1}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"construction","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"Positive"}
//|       - {"artifact":"construction","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":0.5,"atol":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"construction","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":1.0,"atol":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"faces","path":"/rows/1/2","equals":-1}
//|       - {"artifact":"faces","path":"/rows/1/3","equals":1}
//|       - {"artifact":"faces","path":"/rows/1/5","equals":null}
//|       - {"artifact":"faces","path":"/rows/0/5","equals":1}
//|       - {"artifact":"rejected","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":"InvalidInput"}
//|       - {"artifact":"rejected","path":"/rows/2/1","equals":"OutOfBounds"}
//|       - {"artifact":"rejected","path":"/rows/3/1","equals":"InvalidInput"}
//|   - case: degenerate
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"construction","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"Zero"}
//|       - {"artifact":"construction","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":0.0,"atol":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"construction","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":"InvalidInput"}
//|       - {"artifact":"construction","path":"/rows/0/3","equals":null}
//|   - case: clockwise
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"construction","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"Negative"}
//|       - {"artifact":"construction","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":-0.25,"atol":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"construction","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":"InvalidInput"}
//|   - case: wide
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"construction","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":1.0,"atol":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"construction","path":"/rows/0/3","approx":{"value":1.5,"atol":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"faces","path":"/rows/1/2","equals":-1}
use scioxide_spatial::{Point, SimplexBoundaryTags, TriangleMesh, orient2d};

let corners = [[0.0, 0.0], [1.0, 0.0], [1.0, 1.0], [apex_x, 1.0]];
let points: Vec<Point<f64, 2>> = corners
    .iter()
    .map(|&c| Point::new(c))
    .collect::<Result<_, _>>()
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let sign = orient2d(&corners[0], &corners[2], &corners[3]).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let built = TriangleMesh::new(points, vec![[0, 1, 2], [0, 2, 3]]);
let (result, area) = match &built {
    Ok(mesh) => {
        let total = (0..mesh.cell_count())
            .map(|c| mesh.cell_measure(c))
            .sum::<Result<f64, _>>()
            .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
        ("Accepted".to_string(), Some(total))
    }
    Err(error) => (format!("{:?}", error.kind()), None),
};
// Independent check: signed area (1 − t)/2 for triangle [0, 2, 3] and total area 1 − t/2 when accepted.
let signed_area = 0.5 * (1.0 - apex_x);
let exact_area = (apex_x < 1.0).then(|| 1.0 - 0.5 * apex_x);
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let fixed4 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 4 });
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "orient2d of [0, 2, 3]" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Signed area (1 − t)/2 [m²]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Construction result" },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Total area [m²]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4, "nullText": "—" },
    { "key": "4", "label": "Closed form [m²]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4, "nullText": "—" },
    { "key": "5", "label": "Difference [m²]", "type": "number", "format": sci3, "nullText": "—" }
]);
let difference = area.zip(exact_area).map(|(a, b)| (a - b).abs());
let sign = format!("{sign:?}");
let row = (sign, signed_area, result, area, exact_area, difference);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    vec![row],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "construction" })
);

// Each face (edge) of an accepted mesh: vertices, incident cells, signs from each cell, boundary, and tag.
if let Ok(mesh) = &built {
    let boundary: Vec<(usize, u32)> = mesh
        .boundary_faces()
        .map(|face| {
            let bottom = mesh.face_vertices(face).is_ok_and(|v| v == [0, 1]);
            (face, if bottom { 1 } else { 2 })
        })
        .collect();
    let tags = SimplexBoundaryTags::new(mesh, boundary).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let mut rows = Vec::new();
    for face in 0..mesh.face_count() {
        let vertices = mesh.face_vertices(face).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
        let cells = mesh.face_cells(face).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
        let mut signs = [None, None];
        for (slot, cell) in signs.iter_mut().zip(0..mesh.cell_count()) {
            let faces = mesh.cell_faces(cell).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
            *slot = faces.iter().find(|f| f.index() == face).map(|f| f.sign());
        }
        let on_boundary = mesh.is_boundary_face(face).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
        let tag = tags.tag(mesh, face).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
        let (vertices, cells) = (format!("{vertices:?}"), format!("{cells:?}"));
        rows.push((vertices, cells, signs[0], signs[1], on_boundary, tag));
    }
    let face_columns = serde_json::json!([
        { "key": "0", "label": "Face vertices" },
        { "key": "1", "label": "Incident cells" },
        { "key": "2", "label": "Sign in cell 0", "type": "integer", "nullText": "—" },
        { "key": "3", "label": "Sign in cell 1", "type": "integer", "nullText": "—" },
        { "key": "4", "label": "Boundary", "type": "boolean" },
        { "key": "5", "label": "Tag", "type": "integer", "nullText": "None" }
    ]);
    emit_table_with_columns!(
        face_columns,
        rows,
        metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "faces" })
    );
}

// Introduce one connectivity fault at a time on the fixed square and inspect construction or tagging results.
let square: Vec<Point<f64, 2>> = [[0.0, 0.0], [1.0, 0.0], [1.0, 1.0], [0.0, 1.0]]
    .iter()
    .map(|&c| Point::new(c))
    .collect::<Result<_, _>>()
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let classify = |cells: Vec<[usize; 3]>| match TriangleMesh::new(square.clone(), cells) {
    Ok(_) => "Accepted".to_string(),
    Err(error) => format!("{:?}", error.kind()),
};
let valid =
    TriangleMesh::new(square.clone(), vec![[0, 1, 2], [0, 2, 3]]).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let diagonal = (0..valid.face_count())
    .find(|&f| valid.is_boundary_face(f).is_ok_and(|boundary| !boundary))
    .ok_or("No internal edge")?;
let interior_tag = match SimplexBoundaryTags::new(&valid, vec![(diagonal, 5)]) {
    Ok(_) => "Accepted".to_string(),
    Err(error) => format!("{:?}", error.kind()),
};
let same_direction = classify(vec![[0, 1, 2], [0, 1, 3]]);
let unused = classify(vec![[0, 1, 2]]);
let missing = classify(vec![[0, 1, 4], [0, 2, 3]]);
let rejected = vec![
    ("Two triangles use edge 0–1 in the same direction", same_direction),
    ("Vertex 3 unused", unused),
    ("Reference to nonexistent vertex 4", missing),
    ("Tagging internal edge 0–2", interior_tag),
];
emit_table_with_columns!(
    ["Faulty input", "Result"],
    rejected,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "rejected" })
);
```

At default $t = 0$, triangle $[0, 2, 3]$ is counterclockwise and total area is 1 m² as predicted by the closed form.
Diagonal $0$–$2$ is shared by two triangles, used with sign $-1$ from cell 0 and $+1$ from cell 1, summing to 0.
The four boundary edges each belong to one cell, with tag 1 on the bottom and 2 elsewhere.
At $t = 1$, the three points are collinear, signed area is 0, `orient2d` returns `Zero`, and construction gives `InvalidInput`.
At $t = 1.5$, signed area is $-0.25$ m² and sign `Negative`, again rejected with `InvalidInput`.
In the fixed-square table, a shared edge with matching direction, an unused vertex, and tagging an internal edge give `InvalidInput`; a nonexistent vertex gives `OutOfBounds`.

## Interval meshes

One-dimensional `IntervalMesh` aliases `SimplexMesh<S, 1>`, using the same connectivity and boundary representations as triangles and tetrahedra for intervals on the real line.
Each interval $[a, b]$ is oriented as $x_a < x_b$; coordinate comparison involves no rounding, so orientation tests are exact.
In one dimension, faces are vertices, with face measure `face_measure` equal to 1 and interval measure `cell_measure` equal to length $x_b - x_a$.
Each interval is its own sole edge, so edge count equals interval count.
At most one interval can end and at most one begin at any vertex.
Reversed, degenerate, or duplicate intervals, unused vertices, or other sharing patterns such as two intervals branching from one vertex are rejected with `InvalidInput`.

The next cell divides a boundary layer of length $L = 0.01$ m into $n$ intervals refined near wall $x = 0$.
Nodes are $x_i = L\,(i/n)^p$ ($i = 0, \ldots, n$); at $p > 1$, intervals nearer the wall are shorter.
Total length is $L$, the shortest interval is the first, $L\,n^{-p}$, and the longest is the last, $L\,\bigl(1 - (1 - 1/n)^p\bigr)$; values computed with `powf` of `f64` provide an independent check.
The first table compares total, minimum, and maximum lengths with closed forms; the second shows boundary-face counts and measures and edge count.
The third table constructs inputs with one connectivity fault at a time and shows error classifications when rejected.

```rust
//| id: spatial-interval-mesh-grading
//| ref: spatial-interval-mesh-grading
//| title: "Lengths and rejected connectivity of a graded interval mesh"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-spatial]
//| inputs:
//|   cells: {"type":"range","label":"Interval count n","min":2,"max":40,"step":1,"value":8}
//|   grading: {"type":"range","label":"Grading exponent p [-]","min":1,"max":3,"step":0.25,"value":2}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"uniform","label":"Uniform spacing (p = 1)","description":"All eight intervals have length 1.25×10⁻³ m.","inputs":{"grading":1}}
//|   - {"id":"strong-grading","label":"n = 40, p = 3","description":"The first interval is 1.5625×10⁻⁷ m and the last 7.31×10⁻⁴ m, a length ratio around 4681.","inputs":{"cells":40,"grading":3}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"lengths","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":0.01,"atol":1e-17}}
//|       - {"artifact":"lengths","path":"/rows/1/1","approx":{"value":1.5625e-4,"rtol":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"lengths","path":"/rows/2/1","approx":{"value":2.34375e-3,"rtol":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"entities","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":2}
//|       - {"artifact":"entities","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":1}
//|       - {"artifact":"entities","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":8}
//|       - {"artifact":"rejected","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":"InvalidInput"}
//|       - {"artifact":"rejected","path":"/rows/1/1","equals":"InvalidInput"}
//|       - {"artifact":"rejected","path":"/rows/2/1","equals":"InvalidInput"}
//|       - {"artifact":"rejected","path":"/rows/3/1","equals":"OutOfBounds"}
//|   - case: uniform
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"lengths","path":"/rows/1/1","approx":{"value":1.25e-3,"rtol":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"lengths","path":"/rows/2/1","approx":{"value":1.25e-3,"rtol":1e-14}}
//|   - case: strong-grading
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"lengths","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":0.01,"atol":1e-17}}
//|       - {"artifact":"lengths","path":"/rows/1/1","approx":{"value":1.5625e-7,"rtol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"lengths","path":"/rows/2/1","approx":{"value":7.3140625e-4,"rtol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"entities","path":"/rows/0/2","equals":40}
use scioxide_spatial::{IntervalMesh, Point};

// Nodes x_i = L (i/n)^p are caller-supplied input; p > 1 refines near wall x = 0.
let length = 0.01;
let n = cells as usize;
let points = (0..=n)
    .map(|i| Point::new([length * (i as f64 / n as f64).powf(grading)]))
    .collect::<Result<Vec<_>, _>>()
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let intervals: Vec<[usize; 2]> = (0..n).map(|i| [i, i + 1]).collect();
let mesh = IntervalMesh::new(points.clone(), intervals.clone()).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;

// Read interval lengths with cell_measure and compare their sum, minimum, and maximum with closed forms (checked using f64 powf).
let measures = (0..mesh.cell_count())
    .map(|cell| mesh.cell_measure(cell))
    .collect::<Result<Vec<f64>, _>>()
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let total: f64 = measures.iter().sum();
let shortest = measures.iter().copied().fold(f64::INFINITY, f64::min);
let longest = measures.iter().copied().fold(0.0, f64::max);
let first = length * (1.0 / n as f64).powf(grading);
let last = length * (1.0 - (1.0 - 1.0 / n as f64).powf(grading));
let sci6 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 6 });
let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let length_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Quantity" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Computed [m]", "type": "number", "format": sci6 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Closed form [m]", "type": "number", "format": sci6 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Relative difference [-]", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);
let lengths: Vec<(&str, f64, f64, f64)> = [
    ("Total interval length", total, length),
    ("Shortest interval", shortest, first),
    ("Longest interval", longest, last),
]
.into_iter()
.map(|(label, value, exact)| (label, value, exact, (value - exact).abs() / exact))
.collect();
emit_table_with_columns!(
    length_columns,
    lengths,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "lengths" })
);

// Boundary faces are endpoint vertices with zero-dimensional measure 1.
let boundary: Vec<usize> = mesh.boundary_faces().collect();
let boundary_measure = mesh.face_measure(boundary[0]).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let entity_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Boundary-face count", "type": "integer" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Boundary-face measure [-]", "type": "number" },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Edge count", "type": "integer" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    entity_columns,
    vec![(boundary.len(), boundary_measure, mesh.edge_count())],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "entities" })
);

// Introduce one connectivity fault at a time and inspect error classifications for rejected construction.
let mut branching = intervals.clone();
branching.push([0, 2]);
let mut inverted = intervals.clone();
inverted[0] = [1, 0];
let unused = intervals[..n - 1].to_vec();
let mut outside = intervals;
outside[0] = [0, n + 1];
let attempts = [
    ("Two intervals begin at vertex 0", branching),
    ("First interval reversed", inverted),
    ("Final vertex unused", unused),
    ("Nonexistent vertex index n + 1", outside),
];
let rejected: Vec<(&str, String)> = attempts
    .into_iter()
    .map(
        |(label, cells)| match IntervalMesh::new(points.clone(), cells) {
            Ok(_) => (label, "Accepted".to_string()),
            Err(error) => (label, format!("{:?}", error.kind())),
        },
    )
    .collect();
emit_table_with_columns!(
    ["Faulty connectivity", "Construction result"],
    rejected,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "rejected" })
);
```

With defaults ($n = 8$, $p = 2$), total length agrees with $L = 0.01$ m and shortest $1.5625 \times 10^{-4}$ m and longest $2.34375 \times 10^{-3}$ m agree with closed forms within rounding error.
At $n = 40$ and $p = 3$, the first interval is $1.5625 \times 10^{-7}$ m and the last $7.3140625 \times 10^{-4}$ m, giving a length ratio around 4681.
Increasing $p$ shrinks the shortest interval as $n^{-p}$, widening the length ratio.
In the third table, `InvalidInput` denotes connectivity-validation rejection and `OutOfBounds` rejection of an out-of-range vertex index.

## Summary

Supply simplex-mesh cells with positive orientation.
Construction checks orientation with exact predicates and duplicates, shared-face orientations, and vertex-manifold conditions combinatorially from vertex indices, returning `InvalidInput` on failure.
Global embedding is not checked; the caller must ensure that the mesh does not overlap itself.
Faces and edges store increasing vertex indices; `OrientedEntity` signs sum to 0 on internal faces and can be used directly for flux directions or incidence-matrix signs.
Assign `u32` tags to boundary faces with `SimplexBoundaryTags` and select boundary conditions by face group.
Interval meshes use the same representation; read graded-node interval lengths with `cell_measure`.
