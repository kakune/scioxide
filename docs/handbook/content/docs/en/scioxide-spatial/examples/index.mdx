Each example uses `scioxide-spatial` for a concrete problem in astronomy, hydrology, statistics, chemistry, aerospace engineering, machining, or robotics and checks results against an analytic solution, known values, exhaustive search, or another method.

| Example | Subject | Main APIs | Independent check |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Angular-distance neighbor search in a star catalog](celestial-neighbor-search/) | Angular neighbors of 30 bright stars and a spherical Voronoi diagram of the celestial sphere | `KdTree`, `radius_into`, `SphericalVoronoi` | Angular distances from the haversine formula, exhaustive search over all points, and total region area $4\pi$ |
| [Station Thiessen regions and area-weighted rainfall](voronoi-territory/) | Thiessen regions of six rain gauges and basin-average rainfall | `Voronoi`, `HalfspaceIntersection`, `Delaunay` | Sum of areas versus basin area and correspondence of Delaunay edges and Voronoi ridges |
| [Checking Thiessen regions at grid points](voronoi-territory-grid-check/) | Station regions containing basin grid points | `KdTree`, `nearest_into`, `orient2d` | Exhaustive nearest-neighbor search, polygon membership, and area estimates from grid-point counts |
| [Mass conservation, rotation invariants, and linear-field reproduction](geometric-invariants/) | Mass of a rod on a nonuniform grid, rotation of a circle, and temperature on a square plate | `SegmentMesh`, `CellField`, `Rotation`, `Delaunay::barycentric_into` | Analytic mass, length preservation and inverse rotation, and linear-field reproduction |
| [Nearest-neighbor distance statistics (Clark–Evans)](nearest-neighbor-statistics/) | $k$th-neighbor distances of uniform points on a unit torus | `KdTreeOptions::with_periodic_box`, `nearest_batch_into`, `SearchOptions::with_epsilon` | Closed-form binomial-model distribution and mean (with standard errors), and exhaustive minimum-image distances |
| [Maximizing the minimum angle with Delaunay triangulation](delaunay-min-angle/) | Comparing minimum angles against triangulations with flipped diagonals | `Delaunay`, `TriangleMesh::with_cells`, `incircle`, `orient2d` | Edge counts for local conditions using `incircle` and minimum angles for cocircular points |
| [Delaunay circumcenters and Voronoi vertices](delaunay-circumcenters/) | Circumcenters for uniform, circular, and integer-grid point configurations | `Delaunay`, `Voronoi`, `input_mapping` | Circumcenters from an independent formula, triangle count $2N - h - 2$, and vertices shared by cocircular triangles |
| [Molecular superposition and RMSD](molecular-superposition/) | Superposition of benzene, methane, ethanol, and carbon dioxide | `estimate_rigid_transform_with_options`, `RigidTransform`, `Rotation::from_matrix` | Linearized expected RMSD and repetition averages, rotation-error angles, and errors for linear molecules |
| [Spacecraft orientation interpolation and angular velocity](attitude-interpolation/) | Interpolating key orientations specified by Euler angles | `EulerSequence`, `Slerp`, `RotationSpline` | Slerp angular velocity versus relative angle divided by time difference, continuity at spline knots, and Euler-angle round trips |
| [Convex-hull vertex counts of random point clouds](convex-hull-random-points/) | Convex hulls of uniform points in squares and disks | `ConvexHull` | Rényi–Sulanke formulas and standard errors, and slopes of vertex-count growth |
| [Reconstructing convex-hull boundaries and area deficits](convex-hull-perimeter/) | Convex-hull boundary polygons and areas | `ConvexHull`, `neighbors`, `orient2d` | Shoelace areas and Efron's identity, containment of all points, and agreement between `build` and `new` |
| [Hausdorff distance of polygonal approximations](hausdorff-polygon/) | Maximum discrepancy when approximating circular arcs by an inscribed regular $n$-gon | `directed_hausdorff`, `pairwise` | Sagitta $R(1-\cos(\pi/n))$, exhaustive search, and order −2 convergence in $n$ |
| [Forward kinematics of a planar three-link robot arm](robot-arm-kinematics/) | Joint and end-effector positions of a planar three-link arm | `RigidTransform`, `Rotation` | Closed-form positions, reversing composition order, and $T^{-1} \circ T = I$ |

## Summary

All 13 examples include a check on the same page using a method distinct from the main computation.
Read the nearest-neighbor statistics and star-catalog examples to examine neighbor search and statistics; read Delaunay minimum angles and circumcenters, convex-hull vertex counts and boundaries, and Thiessen regions and their grid-point comparisons to examine tessellation properties.
Molecular superposition, orientation interpolation, and robot-arm examples demonstrate rotations and rigid transformations.
Varying each example's controls away from the defaults reveals discrepancies from theory and configurations that produce errors.
The mathematics of the methods appears in the pages beginning with [Mathematics and shared conventions](../algorithms/).
