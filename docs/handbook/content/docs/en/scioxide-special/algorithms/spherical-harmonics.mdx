Spherical harmonic analysis of gravitational and geomagnetic fields, orbital angular momentum eigenstates in quantum mechanics, and expansions of functions on a sphere use spherical harmonics $Y_l^m$ orthonormal on the unit sphere.
$Y_l^m$ is the associated Legendre function $P_l^m$ multiplied by normalization factor $\sqrt{(l - m)!/(l + m)!}$; computing the associated function first and then multiplying can take intermediate values outside binary64 at high degree.
This page explains definitions and phase conventions of complex and real spherical harmonics, the addition theorem, recurrence including normalization factors, estimated-error models, and calling forms.
[Legendre functions](oxref:special-legendre-associated) covers the definition of $P_l^m$ and the Condon–Shortley phase.

## Definitions and phase conventions

For polar angle $\theta \in [0, \pi]$ and azimuth $\phi$ (both in radians), `spherical_harmonic(l, m, theta, phi)` returns the complex spherical harmonics orthonormal on the unit sphere (DLMF §14.30):

$$
Y_l^m(\theta, \phi) = \sqrt{\frac{2l + 1}{4\pi}\frac{(l - m)!}{(l + m)!}}\, P_l^m(\cos\theta)\, e^{im\phi} \quad (0 \le m \le l), \qquad
Y_l^{-m} = (-1)^m \overline{Y_l^m}
$$


$P_l^m$ is a Ferrers function including the Condon–Shortley phase $(-1)^m$ (the same convention as `legendre_p_assoc`), so $Y_1^1 = -\sqrt{3/(8\pi)}\sin\theta\, e^{i\phi}$.
The return type is `Complex<S>`.
`spherical_harmonic_real(l, m, theta, phi)` returns the real (tesseral) spherical harmonics:

$$
Y_{lm} = \begin{cases}
\sqrt{2}\,(-1)^m \operatorname{Re} Y_l^m & (m > 0) \\
Y_l^0 & (m = 0) \\
\sqrt{2}\,(-1)^{|m|} \operatorname{Im} Y_l^{|m|} & (m < 0)
\end{cases}
$$

The factor $(-1)^m$ cancels the Condon–Shortley phase.
The real forms are also orthonormal on the unit sphere.

For angle $\gamma$ between directions $\Omega_1$ and $\Omega_2$, spherical harmonics satisfy the addition theorem:

$$
\sum_{m=-l}^{l} Y_l^m(\Omega_1)\,\overline{Y_l^m(\Omega_2)} = \sum_{m=-l}^{l} Y_{lm}(\Omega_1) Y_{lm}(\Omega_2) = \frac{2l + 1}{4\pi} P_l(\cos\gamma)
$$

At $\Omega_1 = \Omega_2$, this becomes Unsöld's theorem, $\sum_m |Y_l^m|^2 = (2l + 1)/(4\pi)$.
The angles satisfy $\cos\gamma = \cos\theta_1\cos\theta_2 + \sin\theta_1\sin\theta_2\cos(\phi_1 - \phi_2)$.

$|m| > l$ gives `InvalidInput` (`OutOfRange`), $\theta < 0$ or $\theta > \pi$ gives `DomainError` (`OutsideDomain`), and non-finite angles give `NonFinite`; $\phi$ accepts any finite real value.

## Evaluation by normalized recurrence

`spherical_harmonic` applies recurrence to $\bar P_l^m(\theta) = \sqrt{\frac{2l+1}{4\pi}\frac{(l-m)!}{(l+m)!}}P_l^m(\cos\theta)$, including the normalization factor, rather than to $P_l^m$ itself.
Starting from $\bar P_0^0 = 1/\sqrt{4\pi}$, advance diagonally with $\bar P_k^k = -\sin\theta\sqrt{(2k + 1)/(2k)}\,\bar P_{k-1}^{k-1}$; after $\bar P_{m+1}^m = \sqrt{2m + 3}\cos\theta\,\bar P_m^m$, increase the degree using

$$
\bar P_k^m = a_k \cos\theta\, \bar P_{k-1}^m - b_k \bar P_{k-2}^m, \qquad
a_k = \sqrt{\frac{4k^2 - 1}{k^2 - m^2}}, \quad
b_k = \sqrt{\frac{(2k + 1)\bigl((k - 1)^2 - m^2\bigr)}{(2k - 3)(k^2 - m^2)}}
$$


Normalized values have magnitude at most $\sqrt{(2l + 1)/(4\pi)}$, avoiding overflow of $(l + m)!/(l - m)!$.
Near poles, underflow of $\sin^m\theta$ below binary64 is handled by scaling, storing a significand and separate power-of-two exponent.
$\sin\theta$ is passed separately rather than formed from $\cos\theta$, preserving accuracy near poles.
$e^{im\phi}$ is formed by repeated squaring of $e^{i\phi}$, avoiding rounding in the product $m\phi$.
Work is about $l + \log_2|m|$ units, with the limit set by `LegendreOptions`.
The estimated error in the form with options multiplies term magnitudes by the normalized-recurrence rounding model $32\varepsilon(1 + l + |m|)^2$; it is not an enclosing interval.

## Accuracy guarantees

`spherical_harmonic` and `spherical_harmonic_real` have no uniform ULP specification.
Reference data (independently generated reference values) checks values and estimated errors in both binary64 and binary32.
The estimated error in the form with options uses the rounding model above and is not an enclosing interval.
To check accuracy at high degree, inspect residuals of the addition theorem or Unsöld's theorem as in the experiment below.

## Functions and calling forms

Both functions accept angles of real type `S` (`f32`, `f64`); the complex form returns `Complex<S>`, and the real form `S`.

| Function | Arguments | Form with options | Scoped API | Options |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `spherical_harmonic` | `degree: usize`, `order: i32`, `theta: S`, `phi: S` | `spherical_harmonic_with_options` | `spherical_harmonic_scoped` | `LegendreOptions` |
| `spherical_harmonic_real` | `degree: usize`, `order: i32`, `theta: S`, `phi: S` | `spherical_harmonic_real_with_options` | `spherical_harmonic_real_scoped` | `LegendreOptions` |

The form with options `*_with_options(.., options)` returns `SpecialFunctionResult`, containing an estimated error, termination status, work counts, and algorithm identifier.
If high-order values near poles fall below binary64, the standard form returns zero, while the form with options retains scaled and logarithmic values ($Y_l^m \approx 1.96 \times 2^{-9966}$ for $l = m = 1000$, $\theta = 10^{-3}$).
The scoped API `*_scoped(.., options, scope)` returns the same result as the form with options within a solve scope.
`LegendreOptions` contains only a work limit (`Budget`), defaulting to 65536 units; it does not change accuracy.
An insufficient limit returns no value and gives `ConvergenceFailure` (`IterationLimit`).
No function returns $Y_l^m$ for every $m$ at once; call the function for $m = -l, \dots, l$ when computing expansion coefficients.

## Spherical harmonic addition theorem

The next cell selects degree $l$ and directions $\Omega_1 = (\theta_1, 0.3)$ and $\Omega_2 = (\theta_2, 0.3 + \Delta\phi)$, checking the addition theorem and Unsöld's theorem by summing over $2l + 1$ orders.
The right side's $P_l(\cos\gamma)$ is computed with `legendre_p`, a separate path from the normalized recurrence of `spherical_harmonic`.
The complex and real sums should both match the same right side, and the imaginary part of the complex sum should vanish.
The final row compares the real part of $Y_1^1$ with a closed form including the Condon–Shortley phase.

```rust
//| id: special-spherical-harmonics-addition-theorem
//| ref: special-spherical-harmonics-addition-theorem
//| title: "Check the spherical harmonic addition theorem and phase conventions"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-special, scioxide-core]
//| inputs:
//|   degree: {"type":"range","label":"Degree l","description":"Sum over 2l + 1 orders, m = −l, …, l.","min":0,"max":200,"step":1,"value":3}
//|   theta_deg: {"type":"range","label":"Direction 1 polar angle θ₁ [deg]","min":0,"max":180,"step":5,"value":50}
//|   second_theta_deg: {"type":"range","label":"Direction 2 polar angle θ₂ [deg]","min":0,"max":180,"step":5,"value":120}
//|   azimuth_gap_deg: {"type":"range","label":"Azimuth difference φ₂ − φ₁ [deg]","min":0,"max":360,"step":5,"value":75}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"high-degree","label":"l = 200","description":"The addition theorem holds to rounding level even with 401 terms.","inputs":{"degree":200}}
//|   - {"id":"pole","label":"θ₁ = 0","description":"At a pole, terms with m ≠ 0 vanish.","inputs":{"theta_deg":0}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"addition","path":"/rows/0/3","range":{"min":-1e-15,"max":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"addition","path":"/rows/1/1","approx":{"value":0.12040426179377522,"rtol":1e-14}}
//|       - {"artifact":"addition","path":"/rows/1/3","range":{"min":-1e-15,"max":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"addition","path":"/rows/2/3","range":{"min":-1e-15,"max":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"addition","path":"/rows/3/3","range":{"min":-1e-15,"max":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"addition","path":"/rows/4/3","range":{"min":-1e-15,"max":1e-15}}
//|   - case: high-degree
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"addition","path":"/rows/0/3","range":{"min":-1e-12,"max":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"addition","path":"/rows/1/3","range":{"min":-1e-13,"max":1e-13}}
//|   - case: pole
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"addition","path":"/rows/1/3","range":{"min":-1e-15,"max":1e-15}}
use scioxide_core::Complex;
use scioxide_special::{legendre_p, spherical_harmonic, spherical_harmonic_real};
use std::f64::consts::PI;

let sci3 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 3 });
let digits16 = serde_json::json!({ "significantDigits": 16 });
let l = degree as usize;
let (theta1, theta2) = (theta_deg.to_radians(), second_theta_deg.to_radians());
let phi1 = 0.3_f64;
let phi2 = phi1 + azimuth_gap_deg.to_radians();
// Angle γ between two directions (spherical law of cosines; reference computed with f64 intrinsic methods).
let cos_gamma = theta1.cos() * theta2.cos() + theta1.sin() * theta2.sin() * (phi2 - phi1).cos();
let mut unsold = 0.0_f64;
let mut complex_sum = Complex::new(0.0_f64, 0.0);
let mut real_sum = 0.0_f64;
for m in -(degree as i32)..=(degree as i32) {
    let first = spherical_harmonic(l, m, theta1, phi1).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let second = spherical_harmonic(l, m, theta2, phi2).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    unsold += first.norm_sqr();
    complex_sum += first * second.conj();
    let real_first = spherical_harmonic_real(l, m, theta1, phi1).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let real_second = spherical_harmonic_real(l, m, theta2, phi2).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    real_sum += real_first * real_second;
}
let weight = (2.0 * l as f64 + 1.0) / (4.0 * PI);
let addition = weight * legendre_p(l, cos_gamma.clamp(-1.0, 1.0)).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
// Condon–Shortley phase: Y_1^1 = −√(3/(8π)) sin θ e^{iφ}.
let y11 = spherical_harmonic(1, 1, theta1, phi1).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let y11_exact = -(3.0 / (8.0 * PI)).sqrt() * theta1.sin() * phi1.cos();
let rows = vec![
    ("Σ |Y_l^m(Ω₁)|²", unsold, weight),
    ("Re Σ Y_l^m(Ω₁) Y_l^m(Ω₂)*", complex_sum.re, addition),
    ("Im Σ Y_l^m(Ω₁) Y_l^m(Ω₂)*", complex_sum.im, 0.0),
    ("Σ Y_lm(Ω₁) Y_lm(Ω₂) (real form)", real_sum, addition),
    ("Re Y_1^1(Ω₁)", y11.re, y11_exact),
];
let rows: Vec<_> = rows
    .into_iter()
    .map(|(name, value, reference)| (name, value, reference, value - reference))
    .collect();
let columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Quantity" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "SciOxide value", "type": "number", "format": digits16 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Reference", "type": "number", "format": digits16 },
    { "key": "3", "label": "Difference", "type": "number", "format": sci3 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    columns,
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "addition" })
);
```
At the default $l = 3$, $\sum_m |Y_3^m|^2 = 0.5570423008216336$ agrees with $7/(4\pi)$ and the addition-theorem sum $0.1204042617937752$ with $\frac{7}{4\pi}P_3(\cos\gamma)$ to order $10^{-16}$.
Even at $l = 200$, relative differences of the 401-term sums are $1.3 \times 10^{-15}$ for Unsöld's theorem and $4.6 \times 10^{-15}$ for the addition theorem, showing the normalized recurrence preserves normalization at high degrees.
At $\theta_1 = 0$ or $180^\circ$, terms with $m \ne 0$ vanish at the pole, and the sum is determined solely by $m = 0$.

## Summary and further reading

`spherical_harmonic` returns complex $Y_l^m$ including the Condon–Shortley phase; `spherical_harmonic_real` returns the real form $Y_{lm}$ with that phase cancelled; both are orthonormal on the unit sphere.
The polar angle $\theta$ is in radians within $[0, \pi]$, the azimuth $\phi$ is any finite real number, and $|m| > l$ gives `InvalidInput` (`OutOfRange`).
Recurrence includes normalization factors, avoiding intermediate overflow at high degrees with work of order $l + \log_2|m|$.
There is no uniform ULP specification, so check accuracy using estimated errors in the form with options and residuals of the addition theorem or Unsöld's theorem.
See [Legendre functions](../legendre-functions-and-spherical-harmonics/) for associated-function values and conventions, [Angular momentum coupling coefficients and Wigner symbols](../angular-momentum-coupling/) for integrals of three spherical harmonics (Gaunt coefficients), and the [Legendre, angular momentum, and spectral function APIs](../../api/legendre-angular-spectral/) for individual specifications.
