This page covers descriptive statistics for sample location, spread, shape, and information, together with distances between two distributions.
One-shot functions such as `mean`, `sample_variance`, `median`, and `quantile` accept unweighted samples and use divisor $n-1$, Hyndman–Fan type 7 quantiles, and rejection of missing and nonfinite values by default.
For scalar statistics, `statistic` computes sums, means, variances, extrema, circular statistics, and entropy without workspace; other statistics allocate workspace once.
Axis statistics offer increasingly configurable one-shot functions `statistics` and `rolling_statistics`, the detailed form `…_with_options` accepting `StatisticsOptions`, and the borrowed form `…_into` accepting output, workspace, and execution context.
Borrowed forms accept `BatchView` and `StatisticsOptions` and write output only after every batch succeeds.
Undefined quantities such as mean direction use `None` rather than zero or NaN.
Divergent information is represented by infinity in `InformationValue`.
Scalar and weight combinations for which a statistic is undefined have no `Statistic` constructor and fail to compile.
