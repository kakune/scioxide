This page collects procedures for estimating coefficients from a design matrix and response and for comparing group means.
No intercept column is added implicitly; include a column of ones for an intercept.
`PreparedRegression` copies and factorizes the design; `PreparedGlm` copies the design and row information.
`PreparedOperatorRegression`, `PreparedOperatorGlm`, and `PreparedHuber` borrow the design.
Every prepared type accepts a byte limit constraining preparation and the output and workspace created from it.
With sufficient workspace, `fit_into` allocates nothing during numerical computation and leaves caller-owned output unchanged on error.
One-shot functions `ordinary_least_squares(design, response)` and `glm_fit(design, family, response)` fit unweighted designs with default settings.
`glm_fit` and `huber_location` accept only successful termination and convert other terminations to errors (see the [API guide](oxref:stats-api-termination-reasons) for the mapping from `StatsTermination` to errors).
`fit` on prepared types allocates and returns results through sequential native execution.
`fit_scoped` is the scoped form accepting an execution context or parent `SolveContext`; `fit_into` is the borrowed form writing to caller-owned output and workspace.
Iterative procedures (GLM IRLS, Huber regression, and operator Krylov methods) follow limits and cancellation in scioxide-core's `SolveContext`.
Even after a limit or cancellation, they return the last accepted iterate in `Ok` and convey success or failure only through the termination reason.
GLMs collect the termination reason and accepted-iteration count in a per-attempt report `GlmReport`; attempts accepting no iterations also return reports and leave output unchanged.
P-values and intervals assume the conditions stated for each item; those assumptions are not validated from data.
