`scioxide-stats` estimates statistics from observations and reports their uncertainty.
Its computations fall into two broad styles.
**Online aggregation** retains no sample: it updates means, variances, higher central moments, and covariances observation by observation, and merges independently aggregated results.
**Empirical distribution** computations retain samples, using sorting or selection to obtain quantiles, ranks, tail means, empirical distribution functions, and kernel densities.

These support mean confidence intervals, classical and distribution tests, contingency tables and multiple testing, regression and generalized linear models, resampling and power, distribution fitting, parameter inversion from tail probabilities and exact confidence limits, conjugate posteriors and MCMC diagnostics, survival analysis, time series, multivariate estimation, and global sensitivity analysis.
Probability laws and random numbers use scioxide-random, matrix factorizations and iterative methods scioxide-linalg, root refinement scioxide-rootfind, and spectral estimation scioxide-signal.

## First calculation: summarizing measurements

Summarize 12 measurements of a nominal 100 Ω resistor.
The input controls the twelfth measurement, showing how one outlying observation affects means and quantiles.
The next cell uses the online accumulator `OnlineMoments` for the mean, standard deviation, and 95% mean interval, and the empirical distribution `EmpiricalDistribution` for the median, interquartile range, and upper 10% tail mean.
For a single summary of a slice, the one-shot functions `mean`, `median`, and `mean_confidence_interval` return the same values directly.
The second table independently checks the median against the average of the middle two sorted values (a comparison path using `f64` arithmetic) and shows differences from the one-shot functions.

```rust
//| id: stats-first-summary
//| ref: stats-first-summary
//| title: "Summarize resistance measurements with online aggregation and an empirical distribution"
//| run: reactive
//| crates: [scioxide-stats]
//| inputs:
//|   last_reading: {"type":"range","label":"Twelfth measurement [Ω]","description":"Examine the effect of one outlying measurement.","min":99.5,"max":110,"step":0.5,"value":100}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"outlier","label":"Twelfth measurement 110 Ω","description":"One outlier changes the mean and standard deviation substantially, while the median and interquartile range barely change.","inputs":{"last_reading":110}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":100.0,"atol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/1/1","approx":{"value":0.165144564768953,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/2/1","approx":{"value":99.8950721495184,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/3/1","approx":{"value":100.104927850482,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/4/1","approx":{"value":100.0,"atol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/5/1","approx":{"value":0.2,"atol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/6/1","approx":{"value":100.283333333333,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"checks","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"max":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"checks","path":"/rows/1/1","range":{"max":1e-12}}
//|   - case: outlier
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/0/1","approx":{"value":100.833333333333,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/1/1","approx":{"value":2.89147126228259,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/2/1","approx":{"value":98.9961801414913,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/3/1","approx":{"value":102.670486525175,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/4/1","approx":{"value":100.0,"atol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/5/1","approx":{"value":0.225,"atol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"summary","path":"/rows/6/1","approx":{"value":108.383333333333,"atol":1e-9}}
//|       - {"artifact":"checks","path":"/rows/0/1","range":{"max":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"checks","path":"/rows/1/1","range":{"max":1e-12}}
use scioxide_stats::{
    EmpiricalDistribution, MomentOrder, OnlineMoments, QuantileMethod, QuantileProbability,
    QuantileRule, TailSide, Unweighted,
};

let readings = [
    99.8_f64,
    100.1,
    100.0,
    99.9,
    100.2,
    100.0,
    99.7,
    100.3,
    100.1,
    99.9,
    100.0,
    last_reading,
];
// Online aggregation: add observations individually and read the mean, standard deviation, and interval from the summary.
let mut moments = OnlineMoments::new(MomentOrder::Second);
for &r in &readings {
    moments.observe(r).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
}
let s = moments.snapshot();
let undefined = || "A quantity expected to be defined for 12 observations was undefined".to_string();
let mean = s.mean().map_err(|e| e.to_string())?.ok_or_else(undefined)?;
let sd = s
    .standard_deviation(1)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?
    .ok_or_else(undefined)?;
// The default interval is two-sided 95%, a Student t interval with n − 1 degrees of freedom.
let ci = s
    .confidence_interval_of_mean()
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?
    .ok_or_else(undefined)?;
// Empirical distribution: copy and sort the sample, then obtain type 7 quantiles and the upper tail mean.
let law = EmpiricalDistribution::new(&readings, Unweighted).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let q = |p: f64| -> Result<f64, String> {
    let p = QuantileProbability::new(p).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
    let rule = QuantileRule::new(QuantileMethod::Linear);
    Ok(law.quantile(p, rule).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?.value())
};
let p90 = QuantileProbability::new(0.9).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let tail = law
    .tail_mean(p90, TailSide::Upper)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let median = q(0.5)?;
// One-shot functions: compute the same quantities directly from the slice.
let one_shot_mean = scioxide_stats::mean(&readings).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let one_shot_median = scioxide_stats::median(&readings).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let one_shot_ci =
    scioxide_stats::mean_confidence_interval(&readings).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
// Independent check: average the middle two of 12 sorted values (f64 arithmetic).
let mut sorted = readings;
sorted.sort_by(f64::total_cmp);
let median_by_hand = (sorted[5] + sorted[6]) / 2.0;
let one_shot_gap = [
    (one_shot_mean - mean).abs(),
    (one_shot_median - median).abs(),
    (one_shot_ci.bounds().lower() - ci.bounds().lower()).abs(),
    (one_shot_ci.bounds().upper() - ci.bounds().upper()).abs(),
]
.into_iter()
.fold(0.0, f64::max);
let fixed4 = serde_json::json!({ "fractionDigits": 4 });
let sci2 = serde_json::json!({ "notation": "scientific", "significantDigits": 2 });
let summary_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Quantity" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Value [Ω]", "type": "number", "format": fixed4 },
    { "key": "2", "label": "Computational style" }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    summary_columns,
    vec![
        ("Mean", mean, "Online aggregation"),
        ("Sample standard deviation", sd, "Online aggregation"),
        ("Lower endpoint of the 95% mean interval", ci.bounds().lower(), "Online aggregation"),
        ("Upper endpoint of the 95% mean interval", ci.bounds().upper(), "Online aggregation"),
        ("Median (type 7)", median, "Empirical distribution"),
        ("Interquartile range Q(0.75) − Q(0.25) (type 7)", q(0.75)? - q(0.25)?, "Empirical distribution"),
        ("Upper 10% tail mean", tail.mean(), "Empirical distribution"),
    ],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "summary" })
);
let check_columns = serde_json::json!([
    { "key": "0", "label": "Check" },
    { "key": "1", "label": "Difference [Ω]", "type": "number", "format": sci2 }
]);
emit_table_with_columns!(
    check_columns,
    vec![
        ("Median minus hand calculation", (median - median_by_hand).abs()),
        ("Maximum difference of mean, median, and interval endpoints from one-shot results", one_shot_gap),
    ],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({ "id": "checks" })
);
```

With the default twelfth measurement of 100.0 Ω, both mean and median are 100.0000 Ω, the sample standard deviation is 0.1651 Ω, the 95% mean interval is $[99.8951, 100.1049]$ Ω, and the interquartile range is 0.2000 Ω.
The upper 10% tail mean averages mass equivalent to the largest 1.2 observations, giving 100.2833 Ω.
The median matches the hand calculation, and differences from one-shot functions are 0.

Setting the twelfth measurement to 110 Ω (the “Twelfth measurement 110 Ω” case) increases the mean to 100.8333 Ω and standard deviation to 2.8915 Ω; the mean interval widens to $[98.9962, 102.6705]$ Ω, straddling the nominal value.
The median remains 100.0000 Ω and the interquartile range changes little to 0.2250 Ω; the upper tail mean of 108.3833 Ω concentrates the outlier's effect.
A single outlier strongly pulls the mean and variance, while the order-based median and interquartile range are less affected.
Which to report depends on whether the outlier is treated as part of the population, a decision the computational method alone cannot make.

## Reading results

Each quantity in an accumulator summary returns `Result<Option<S>, SciOxideError>`.
Mathematically undefined quantities are `Ok(None)`, including a one-point sample variance or a constant-series correlation.
One-shot functions such as `mean` and `sample_variance` return `Result<S, SciOxideError>`, treating undefined quantities (such as one-point sample variance) as `Err`.
Invalid input, numerical breakdown, and values unrepresentable in `S` return `Err`; read the error classification with `kind()`.

Confidence intervals and p-values are statistical inferences under the recorded assumptions.
Check interval construction and assumptions with `MeanConfidenceInterval::method()` and `assumptions()`.
Quantile and tail-mean results retain the definition conventions used (`QuantileResult::convention()`, `TailMeanResult::convention()`).
Align these conventions when comparing with other statistical software.

## Summary

`scioxide-stats` supports two computational styles: online aggregation updates without retaining samples, while empirical distributions retain the full sample for sorting or selection.
For a single calculation with defaults, use one-shot functions accepting only data (such as `mean`, `median`, `t_test_independent`, and `bootstrap`); use detailed forms `_with_options` to change settings.
Means and variances are strongly affected by outliers; medians and interquartile ranges are less affected.
Summary queries return `Result<Option<S>, SciOxideError>`, distinguishing mathematical absence (`None`) from computational failure (`Err`).
Confidence intervals and p-values record calibration conventions and assumptions; interpret them together with those premises.
