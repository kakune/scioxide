## Batch verification of Kepler-equation roots


The next cell checks seven proposals about roots of Kepler's equation $f(E) = E - e\sin E - M = 0$ through batch verification with `ScalarRootVerifier`.
The derivation and solver comparison appear in [Kepler's equation and eccentricity](../../../scioxide-rootfind/examples/kepler-equation/); the defaults match at $e = 0.6$ and $M = 1.2$ rad.

The value extension combines the sine enclosure into one interval with `hull()`, and constructs derivative $f'(E) = 1 - e\cos E$ similarly.
$e$ and $M$ take the input binary64 values themselves as exact constants.
Proposal center $E^*$ is a Newton approximation using f64's inherent methods and is not itself verified.
The proposals claim uniqueness over the full domain $[0, 2\pi]$, uniqueness in intervals centered at $E^*$ with half-widths $10^{-3}$, $10^{-9}$, $10^{-15}$, and 0, and absence in intervals excluding and containing the root.
Reserve the workspace with `ProofRequest::proofs(7)` and set the verifier byte limit to 1 MiB.

The workspace-requirements table uses no rounding and appears in every environment.
Decision and established-fact columns are populated only in native environments; wasm32 displays “Not executed”.

```rust
//| id: validated-api-kepler-proof
//| ref: validated-api-kepler-proof
//| title: "Enclose Kepler-equation roots through batch interval Newton verification"
//| run: button
//| uses: [validated-api-environment]
//| crates: [scioxide-core, scioxide-validated]
//| inputs:
//|   eccentricity: {"type":"range","label":"Eccentricity e [-]","min":0,"max":0.99,"step":0.01,"value":0.6}
//|   mean: {"type":"range","label":"Mean anomaly M [rad]","min":0,"max":6.28,"step":0.01,"value":1.2}
//| cases:
//|   - {"id":"circular","label":"e = 0 (circular orbit)","description":"The root is exactly E* = M = 1.2, and f is exactly linear. Uniqueness is Proved even in a point interval of half-width 0.","inputs":{"eccentricity":0}}
//|   - {"id":"near-two-pi","label":"M = 6.28 (just before 2π)","description":"At E* ≈ 6.275222, 1 ulp is approximately 8.9×10⁻¹⁶, so rounding of endpoints prevents proof in an interval of half-width 10⁻¹⁵, giving Inconclusive(Reason(Inequality)).","inputs":{"mean":6.28}}
//|   - {"id":"periapsis","label":"e = 0.99, M = 0 (periapsis)","description":"The root E* = 0 is Proved even in a point interval, but absence on [0.01, 0.1] gives Inconclusive(Reason(Inequality)) because dependency from two occurrences of x makes the enclosure include 0.","inputs":{"eccentricity":0.99,"mean":0}}
//| tests:
//|   - case: default
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"table-0","path":"/rows/0/1","equals":7}
//|       - {"artifact":"table-0","path":"/rows/1/1","range":{"min":1000,"max":20000}}
//|       - {"artifact":"table-0","path":"/rows/2/1","equals":1048576}
//|       - {"artifact":"table-1","path":"/rows/0/0","equals":"Unique"}
//|       - {"artifact":"table-1","path":"/rows/1/2","approx":{"value":1.785141603274313,"atol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"table-1","path":"/rows/4/2","approx":{"value":1.786141603274313,"atol":1e-12}}
//|       - {"artifact":"table-1","path":"/rows/5/0","equals":"Empty"}
//|       - {"artifact":"table-1","path":"/rows/5/3","approx":{"value":1.886141603274313,"atol":1e-12}}
//|   - case: circular
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"table-1","path":"/rows/4/2","approx":{"value":1.2,"atol":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"table-1","path":"/rows/4/3","approx":{"value":1.2,"atol":1e-15}}
//|   - case: near-two-pi
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"table-1","path":"/rows/3/2","approx":{"value":6.275222165469160,"atol":1e-12}}
//|   - case: periapsis
//|     expect:
//|       - {"artifact":"table-1","path":"/rows/4/2","approx":{"value":0,"atol":1e-15}}
//|       - {"artifact":"table-1","path":"/rows/5/2","approx":{"value":0.01,"atol":1e-15}}
use scioxide_core::{ExecutionContext, ExtendedInterval, SciOxideError};
use scioxide_validated::{
    DecoratedInterval as D, ProofRequest, RootClaim, RootVerificationReport,
    ScalarRootCertificate, ScalarRootVerifier, ValidatedOptions,
};

// 1. Interval extensions of f(E) = E − e sin E − M and f'(E) = 1 − e cos E (e and M are input binary64 values)
let ecc = D::point(eccentricity).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let anomaly = D::point(mean).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let one = D::point(1.0).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let value = |x: D<f64>| x.sub(&ecc.mul(&x.sin()?.hull())?)?.sub(&anomaly);
let derivative = |x: D<f64>| one.sub(&ecc.mul(&x.cos()?.hull())?);
// 2. Proposal center E*: Newton's method using f64's inherent methods (E₀ = π). This approximation is not verified.
let mut center = std::f64::consts::PI;
for _ in 0..60 {
    center -=
        (center - eccentricity * center.sin() - mean) / (1.0 - eccentricity * center.cos());
}
// 3. Claims and intervals. Half-width 0 gives a point interval; the last two claim absence in intervals excluding and containing the root.
let tau = std::f64::consts::TAU;
let proposals = [
    (RootClaim::Unique, 0.0, tau, "[0, 2π]"),
    (RootClaim::Unique, center - 1e-3, center + 1e-3, "E* ± 10⁻³"),
    (RootClaim::Unique, center - 1e-9, center + 1e-9, "E* ± 10⁻⁹"),
    (
        RootClaim::Unique,
        center - 1e-15,
        center + 1e-15,
        "E* ± 10⁻¹⁵",
    ),
    (RootClaim::Unique, center, center, "E* ± 0"),
    (
        RootClaim::Empty,
        center + 1e-2,
        center + 1e-1,
        "E* + [10⁻², 10⁻¹]",
    ),
    (RootClaim::Empty, center - 1e-3, center + 1e-3, "E* ± 10⁻³"),
];
let certificates = proposals
    .iter()
    .map(|&(claim, lo, hi, _)| {
        let region = ExtendedInterval::new(Some(lo), Some(hi))?;
        let center = scioxide_core::checked_midpoint(lo, hi)?;
        Ok(ScalarRootCertificate {
            region,
            center,
            claim,
        })
    })
    .collect::<Result<Vec<_>, SciOxideError>>()
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
// 4. The verifier owns its configuration. Reserve workspace from batch-verification requirements (without rounding).
let options = ValidatedOptions::new().with_byte_limit(1 << 20);
let verifier = ScalarRootVerifier::new(&value, &derivative, options);
let request = ProofRequest::proofs(certificates.len());
let requirements = verifier
    .workspace_requirements(request)
    .map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
let mut workspace = verifier.workspace(request).map_err(|e| e.to_string())?;
emit_table_with_columns!(
    ["Workspace requirement", "Value"],
    vec![
        ("Independent proofs [count]", requirements.proofs()),
        (
            "New workspace allocation [B]",
            requirements.required_bytes()
        ),
        ("Maximum retained storage [B]", requirements.byte_limit()),
    ],
    metadata = serde_json::json!({"id":"table-0"})
);
// 5. Batch verification. Initialize report entries with NotRun reports; verification overwrites them.
let mut proof = || -> Result<_, SciOxideError> {
    let mut reports = vec![RootVerificationReport::not_run(); certificates.len()];
    let scope = ExecutionContext::sequential();
    verifier.verify_batch_into(&certificates, &mut reports, &mut workspace, scope)?;
    Ok(reports)
};
let reports = match proof() {
    Ok(reports) => Some(reports),
    Err(error) if unsupported(&error) => None,
    Err(error) => return Err(error.to_string()),
};
// 6. Read the decision from report status and established facts from evidence
let rows: Vec<_> = proposals
    .iter()
    .enumerate()
    .map(|(index, &(claim, lo, hi, label))| {
        let (status, evidence) = match &reports {
            Some(reports) => (
                format!("{:?}", reports[index].status()),
                reports[index]
                    .evidence()
                    .map_or("None".to_string(), |fact| format!("{fact:?}")),
            ),
            None => ("Not executed".to_string(), "Not executed".to_string()),
        };
        (format!("{claim:?}"), label, lo, hi, status, evidence)
    })
    .collect();
emit_table_with_columns!(
    [
        "Claim",
        "Interval",
        "Lower endpoint [rad]",
        "Upper endpoint [rad]",
        "Decision",
        "Established fact"
    ],
    rows,
    metadata = serde_json::json!({"id":"table-1"})
);
emit_outcome!(match &reports {
    None => Outcome::unavailable("Proof computation not executed in this environment"),
    Some(reports) => Outcome::satisfied("Executed verification of each claim; consult table decisions for success or failure")
        .diagnostic("Claim count", reports.len() as f64, None),
});
```

In an x86_64 native environment, defaults give $E^* \approx 1.786141603274$ rad, and uniqueness over the full domain and half-widths $10^{-3}$, $10^{-9}$, and $10^{-15}$ all yield `Proved`.
Since $f'(E) = 1 - e\cos E \ge 1 - e > 0$, $f$ is monotonically increasing.
Over the full domain $[0, 2\pi]$, opposite signs of $f$ at the endpoints establish existence, and $0 \notin f'(X)$ establishes uniqueness, proving the claim without narrowing the root location.
In the point interval of half-width 0, the enclosure of $f(E^*)$ contains 0 but is not exactly zero; neither an endpoint sign change nor Newton-map inclusion occurs, giving `Inconclusive(Reason(Inequality))`.
This does not negate the claim.
Absence on $[E^* + 10^{-2}, E^* + 10^{-1}]$ gives `Proved`; absence claimed in an interval containing the root gives `Refuted` with established fact `Unique`.
Workspace requirements are 7 proofs, 6664 B of new workspace allocation (the x86_64 value; wasm32 uses 3752 B because `usize` is 32-bit), and a limit of 1048576 B.

At $e = 0$, $f$ is exactly linear and the enclosure of $f(E^*)$ is exactly zero, so uniqueness in the point interval $E^* = M$ is `Proved`.
At $M = 6.28$, 1 ulp near $E^* \approx 6.275222$ rad is approximately $8.9 \times 10^{-16}$, so half-width $10^{-15}$ is about 1 ulp.
Endpoint values of $f$ are obscured by rounding widths and their signs cannot be determined, giving uniqueness status `Inconclusive(Reason(Inequality))` for this interval.
For $e = 0.99$ and $M = 0$, the root is at $E^* = 0$ and can be proved even in a point interval, but absence on $[0.01, 0.1]$ yields `Inconclusive(Reason(Inequality))`.
The true $E - 0.99\sin E$ is positive on this interval, but dependency from two occurrences of $E$ makes its enclosure contain 0; the small $f'(X) \approx [0.010, 0.015]$ also makes Newton image $N(X) \approx [-0.002, 0.016]$ intersect $X$, preventing an absence proof.


## Summary

Strict monotonicity and endpoint signs establish root existence and uniqueness separately.
A point interval merely containing an approximate root need not permit a uniqueness proof.
See [root mathematics](../../algorithms/root-certificates/) for the decision conditions.
